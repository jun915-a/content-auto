## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
USB Type-C is a high-speed, reversible, and compact USB connector designed to replace the traditional USB-A port. It supports multiple protocols, including USB, DisplayPort, and Power Delivery (PD), making it an ideal solution for modern devices.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Supports speeds of up to 40 Gbps.
- **Point 2**: Can deliver up to 100W of power.
- **Point 3**: Compatible with multiple protocols.
