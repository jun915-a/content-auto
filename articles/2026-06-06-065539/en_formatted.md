## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
pg_durable is Microsoft's in-database durable execution system, now open-sourced, ensuring data consistency and reliability.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Reliability**: Ensures data consistency across transactions
- **Performance**: Optimized for high-performance databases
- **Open-Source**: Community-driven development and feedback

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Database Transactions**
pg_durable manages database transactions to prevent data loss and inconsistencies.

**Durable Execution**
pg_durable ensures that database operations are executed reliably, even in the event of failures.

> 💡 Insight: By open-sourcing pg_durable, Microsoft aims to collaborate with the community to further enhance database reliability.
