The sheet’s innovation lies in its **integrated resistive sensors**, which change electrical resistance when bent or twisted. Unlike traditional sensors that require bulky wiring or external cameras, this system **self-contains its circuitry**, allowing for seamless integration into fabrics, plastics, or other flexible substrates. The team at MIT’s **Department of Mechanical Engineering** designed it to mimic natural materials, ensuring durability while maintaining sensitivity.
