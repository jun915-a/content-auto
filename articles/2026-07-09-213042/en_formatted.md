# Rethinking AI: Infrastructure Over Models

*Insert header image here*

The future of AI isn't just about developing better models, it's about building a robust infrastructure to support them.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The current AI landscape is built around individual models, with each having its own strengths and weaknesses. However, this approach is no longer sustainable as the complexity of AI systems grows.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Individual models are not enough for complex AI systems.
- **Point 2**: A robust infrastructure is needed to support AI development.
- **Point 3**: This infrastructure will enable more efficient and effective AI development.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Model Fragmentation**
The current model-based approach to AI development leads to model fragmentation, where each model is developed in isolation. This makes it difficult to share knowledge, integrate models, and leverage the strengths of multiple models.

**Infrastructure-First Approach**
An infrastructure-first approach, on the other hand, focuses on developing a robust foundation that can support multiple models and applications. This approach enables more efficient and effective AI development, as well as better integration of models and applications.

> 💡 Insight: By focusing on infrastructure, we can create more sustainable and scalable AI systems.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Improved model sharing and integration
- Enhanced AI application development
- Better scalability and sustainability of AI systems

## ✨ Conclusion
The next era of AI is not just about developing better models, but about building a robust infrastructure to support them. By taking an infrastructure-first approach, we can create more efficient, effective, and sustainable AI systems that can tackle complex problems and drive innovation.
