## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Leap seconds are adjustments made to Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) to keep it in sync with Earth's rotation. This has been done 27 times since 1972, but never in a negative direction. The reason for this is that our planet's rotation is slowing down slightly over time, resulting in a longer day. The International Earth Rotation and Reference Systems Service (IERS) is responsible for monitoring Earth's rotation and deciding when a leap second is needed.
