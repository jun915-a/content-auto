## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The core issue with USB-C cable labels is that they often misrepresent the cable's actual capabilities, which can lead to damage or safety issues.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Incorrect Labeling**: Labels may not reflect the cable's true specs
- **Performance Variance**: Cables with the same label can perform differently
- **Safety Risks**: Mislabeling can lead to overheating or electrical shock

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The main issue with USB-C cables is the lack of standardization, leading to inconsistent labeling.

**Element 2**: Testing cables with a meter can reveal their true capabilities and help avoid potential safety hazards.
> 💡 Insight: Always test your USB-C cables before using them to ensure safety and optimal performance.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Inconsistent cable performance can lead to frustration and wasted time
- Incorrect labeling can cause safety issues, such as electrical shock or fires
- Reliable testing methods can help build trust in USB-C cables

## ✨ Conclusion
By being aware of the potential issues with USB-C cable labels and taking the time to test them, you can ensure a safer and more reliable experience.
