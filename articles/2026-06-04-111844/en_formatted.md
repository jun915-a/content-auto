# Anti-NMDA Receptor Encephalitis

*Insert header image here*

A rare autoimmune disease causing memory loss and seizures, learn about anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis and its effects on the brain

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis is a rare autoimmune disease where the body's immune system mistakenly attacks the brain's NMDA receptors, leading to severe symptoms.
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Autoimmune Disease**: The body's immune system attacks the brain
- **NMDA Receptors**: Crucial for memory and learning
- **Rare Condition**: Affects approximately 1 in 100,000 people
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Causes and Triggers**
Anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis can be triggered by tumors, viruses, or other medical conditions.
**Symptoms and Diagnosis**
Symptoms include memory loss, seizures, and hallucinations, and diagnosis involves a combination of blood tests and brain scans.
> 💡 Insight: Early diagnosis is crucial for effective treatment
## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Memory loss and cognitive impairment
- Seizures and other neurological symptoms
- Significant impact on daily life and relationships
## ✨ Conclusion
Anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis is a complex and rare disease requiring prompt medical attention and support
