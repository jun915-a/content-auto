## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Azulejo is a type of Portuguese decorative art that dates back to the 13th century, characterized by intricately painted ceramic tiles. 
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Origin**: From the Arabic word 'al-zulayj', meaning 'polished stone'
- **Technique**: Hand-painted using a unique method called 'cobalt oxide'
- **Influence**: Reflects Moorish, Gothic, and Renaissance styles
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**History**: Azulejo has a long and storied history, with early examples found in Portuguese monasteries and churches.
**Designs**: The tiles often feature floral patterns, geometric shapes, and mythological creatures.
> 💡 Insight: Azulejo tiles were not only decorative but also used to tell stories and convey messages.
## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Used in architectural decoration, from palaces to churches
- Influenced other art forms, such as painting and sculpture
- Continues to inspire modern artists and designers
## ✨ Conclusion
Azulejo is a testament to the power of art to transcend time and culture, a true marvel of human creativity
