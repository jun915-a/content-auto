# Visualizing London Trains in 3D

Explore the TFL Api and National Rails with a deck.gl based visualiser, tracking trains in 3D with minimal drift.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Experience the thrill of tracking trains in 3D, from any platform in London to the nearest airports. This innovative deck.gl visualiser combines the power of TFL Api and National Rails to bring you an immersive experience.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Real-time train tracking with minimal drift.
- **Point 2**: Visualisation of train routes and stations.
- **Point 3**: Integration with TFL Api and National Rails.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The visualiser uses a combination of TFL Api and National Rails data to accurately track train movements. With real-time updates, you can stay on top of train schedules and plan your journey accordingly.
**Element 2**: The 3D visualisation allows you to see the train routes and stations in detail, making it easier to navigate the complex London train network.
