## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1: The Booker Prize's Significance**
The Booker Prize, one of the most prestigious literary awards, has historically favored literary fiction over genre fiction. Harrison's win challenges this norm, showcasing the power of speculative fiction.

**Element 2: Harrison's Impact**
M. John Harrison's unique style, blending philosophical themes with speculative fiction, has garnered critical acclaim. His win solidifies his place in literary history and encourages a broader appreciation for sci-fi literature.
