## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
SSH tunnels are a powerful tool for securely accessing remote servers and services, even when they are behind a firewall or VPN. By creating a tunnel, you can route your internet traffic through a secure SSH connection, encrypting your data and protecting your identity.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Establish a secure connection between your local machine and a remote server.
- **Point 2**: Use local and remote port forwarding to access services and websites.
- **Point 3**: Bypass firewalls and VPN restrictions with SSH tunnels.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Local port forwarding allows you to access a remote service as if it were running on your local machine. This is useful for accessing web servers, databases, and other services that require a specific port or protocol.

**Element 2**
Remote port forwarding, on the other hand, allows you to access a service on your local machine from a remote location. This is useful for accessing services that are behind a firewall or VPN, or for accessing services that require a specific port or protocol.

> 💡 Insight: SSH tunnels are not just for accessing remote servers - they can also be used to secure your online activities and protect your identity.
