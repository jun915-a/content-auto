## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Incident**
Samuel Tunick deleted data from his phone at the U.S. border. He was subsequently charged with a felony, accused of obstructing a federal investigation by destroying evidence.

**Legal Interpretation**
Prosecutors argued that deleting data, even on a personal device, could be seen as an attempt to hide information from lawful border searches, thus constituting a crime.

> 💡 Insight: The case underscores the tension between an individual's right to privacy and the government's security mandates at international borders.

**Border Search Powers**
U.S. Customs and Border Protection agents have the authority to search electronic devices entering the country, a power that has been subject to legal challenges.
