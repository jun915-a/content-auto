Forestiere's underground world features a series of interconnected tunnels, rooms, and gardens, all carefully designed to harness natural light and ventilation. He excavated the soil himself, using only hand tools, and constructed the walls and ceilings using limestone, concrete, and brick.

**The Inspiration**
Forestiere's vision was to create a self-sustaining underground world where he could live, work, and grow his own food. He was inspired by the natural beauty of the earth and the potential for human ingenuity to shape it.
