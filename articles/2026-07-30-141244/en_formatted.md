## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Carolina Cloud pays interest on unused prepaid credits based on the Secured Overnight Financing Rate (SOFR) to ensure fair treatment of customers.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Unused prepaid credits accrue interest based on SOFR.
- **Point 2**: This policy promotes fair treatment of customers.
- **Point 3**: SOFR-based interest rates are transparent and competitive.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Prepaid Credits and Interest**
Unused prepaid credits are subject to interest accrual based on the SOFR rate, which is a widely accepted benchmark for short-term interest rates. This approach ensures that customers are not penalized for not using their prepaid credits immediately.

**SOFR and Fair Treatment**
By using SOFR-based interest rates, Carolina Cloud promotes fair treatment of customers. This policy eliminates the risk of arbitrary or unfair interest rates, providing customers with a transparent and predictable experience.

> 💡 Insight: Using SOFR-based interest rates demonstrates Carolina Cloud's commitment to fairness and transparency in its business practices.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- **Reduced complexity**: SOFR-based interest rates simplify the prepaid credit process.
- **Increased transparency**: Customers can easily understand how interest is calculated.
- **Improved customer trust**: Fair treatment and transparent interest rates foster trust in the organization.

## ✨ Conclusion
Carolina Cloud's decision to pay interest on unused prepaid credits based on SOFR rates sets a new standard for fairness and transparency in the industry. This approach benefits customers by providing a predictable and competitive experience.
