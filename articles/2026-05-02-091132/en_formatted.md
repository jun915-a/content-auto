## The Core of This Topic
WhatCable is a tiny menu bar app for inspecting USB-C cables, solving the problem of identical-looking cables with different capabilities.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Identify Charging Speeds**: Determine the charging speed of your USB-C cable
- **Check Thunderbolt 4**: Verify if your cable supports Thunderbolt 4
- **Simplify Cable Management**: Easily identify and organize your USB-C cables
## Detailed Breakdown
**Cable Inspection**
WhatCable reads the cable data your Mac already detects, providing you with essential information about your USB-C cables.
**Cable Organization**
With WhatCable, you can easily identify and label your cables, making it simpler to manage your collection.
> Insight: WhatCable saves you time and frustration by providing a straightforward way to inspect and manage your USB-C cables.
## Real-World Impact
- Reduce cable clutter and confusion
- Increase productivity by quickly identifying the right cable for the task
- Make informed purchasing decisions when buying new cables
## Conclusion
WhatCable is a convenient and useful tool for anyone struggling to keep track of their USB-C cables and their various capabilities.
