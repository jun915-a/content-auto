# AstroHelm: Your Phone Camera Becomes a Telescope Guide

Imagine using your smartphone to effortlessly align a telescope or telephoto lens with celestial objects—no more guesswork in the night sky. AstroHelm turns your phone’s sensors into a precision aiming tool, simplifying astronomy for urban stargazers.

**🔑 The Core of This Topic**
AstroHelm is a groundbreaking app that leverages your smartphone’s built-in gyroscope, magnetometer, and camera to act as a real-time alignment system for telescopes or telephoto lenses. Designed for amateur astronomers—especially those in light-polluted cities like New York—it eliminates the frustration of manually tracking stars, planets, or deep-sky objects. By projecting visual guides onto your lens or screen, AstroHelm bridges the gap between modern tech and traditional stargazing.
