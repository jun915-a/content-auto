# LocalWiki: Discover Your Neighborhood's Hidden Stories

Explore Wikipedia entries focused exclusively on landmarks and history within a half-hour walk from your current location. Uncover the untold stories right around the corner.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
LocalWiki redefines Wikipedia by filtering content to showcase only places and historical facts discoverable within a 30-minute walking radius from your GPS location. It transforms your immediate surroundings into a rich tapestry of local knowledge.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Hyperlocal Focus**: Content is strictly limited to your walking distance.
- **Discovery Tool**: Unearths nearby points of interest and history.
- **Interactive Map**: Visualizes accessible Wikipedia entries around you.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Geographic Filtering**
This is the foundational element. LocalWiki uses your location to draw a circle representing a 30-minute walk, and only Wikipedia articles tagged within that geofence are displayed. This ensures relevance to your immediate physical environment.

**Content Curation**
It leverages existing Wikipedia data but applies a strict locality filter. This means you won't see articles about distant cities or abstract concepts, but rather about that old building, the local park's history, or a notable event that happened nearby.

> 💡 Insight: It makes Wikipedia a tool for immediate, physical exploration rather than just passive information retrieval.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Encourages local exploration and appreciation of one's surroundings.
- Provides tourists and residents with context-rich walking tours.
- Fosters a deeper connection to the history and culture embedded in everyday places.

## ✨ Conclusion
LocalWiki turns your neighborhood into an open-air museum, revealing the fascinating stories hidden just a short walk away. It's a powerful reminder that history and culture are all around us, waiting to be discovered.
