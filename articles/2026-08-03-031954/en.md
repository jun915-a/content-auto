# California Data Deletion Law

Californians' data deletion requests become enforceable on Aug 1, giving them more control over their personal data

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
California's data deletion law allows residents to request the deletion of their personal data from companies, giving them more control over their information.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Data Deletion**: Californians can request data deletion from companies
- **Enforceable Law**: The law becomes enforceable on August 1
- **Personal Data**: The law applies to personal data collected by companies

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**What is Covered**
The law applies to personal data collected by companies, including online data. 
**How to Request**
Californians can request data deletion by contacting the company directly.

> 💡 Insight: This law gives Californians more control over their personal data and how it is used by companies.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Companies must comply with data deletion requests
- Californians have more control over their personal data
- The law may set a precedent for other states

## ✨ Conclusion
The California data deletion law is a significant step towards giving individuals more control over their personal data. With the law becoming enforceable on August 1, Californians can now request the deletion of their data from companies, marking a new era in data privacy.
