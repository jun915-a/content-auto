## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The preemptive draw and grip are techniques used by cash guards to protect themselves and their money from potential threats during cash collections and deliveries. By establishing control and dominance at the beginning of an interaction, cash guards can minimize the risk of violence and theft.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Establish control and dominance at the beginning of an interaction.
- **Point 2**: Use verbal and non-verbal cues to deter potential threats.
- **Point 3**: Anticipate and respond to potential risks.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The preemptive draw involves drawing a weapon, usually a baton or a pepper spray, as a visible display of authority. This can be an effective way to deter potential threats and establish control. However, it requires a high degree of skill and training to execute safely and effectively.

**Element 2**
The preemptive grip involves establishing physical control over a potential threat, such as a person attempting to grab the cash. This can be done using various techniques, including joint locks and pressure points. The key is to anticipate and respond quickly to potential risks.

> 💡 Insight: The preemptive draw and grip are not just about physical self-defense; they are also about establishing control and dominance in a situation.
