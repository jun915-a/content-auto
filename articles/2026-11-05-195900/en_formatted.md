## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
DNS hijacking by ISPs is a stealthy practice where your internet service provider manipulates your DNS (Domain Name System) requests, redirecting you to alternative websites or servers. This can impact your online privacy, security, and even your web browsing experience.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **ISPs Redirect DNS**: Your internet provider can change where your web requests go.
- **Privacy & Security Risks**: It exposes potential vulnerabilities.
- **Alternative Routes**: You might be sent to different websites than intended.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**DNS Functionality:** The DNS is like a phonebook for the internet, translating website names into IP addresses. When you type a URL, your ISP's DNS server is consulted first.

**Hijacking Process:** ISPs can modify these DNS responses, sending you to different IP addresses. This could be for various reasons, from ad injection to censorship.

> 💡 Insight: DNS hijacking is a powerful tool for ISPs to control your online journey, often without your knowledge or consent.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- **Privacy Invasion:** Your online activities are potentially exposed.
- **Malware Risks:** Redirects to unsafe sites can lead to malware infections.
- **Censorship:** Certain websites or content might be inaccessible.

## ✨ Conclusion
Understanding DNS hijacking is crucial for online privacy and security. While ISPs may have their reasons, users should be aware of this practice and take steps to protect their online data and experiences.
