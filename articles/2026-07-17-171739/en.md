# Brain Can Simultaneously Encode Two Speech Streams

Researchers have discovered that the brain can process and encode two speech streams at the same time, challenging previous assumptions about speech processing.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The brain's ability to process speech is a complex and fascinating phenomenon. Recent studies using electroencephalography (EEG) have shown that the brain can simultaneously encode two speech streams, contradicting the traditional view that the brain can only process one stream at a time.

**Element 1**
Previous studies have shown that the brain can process one speech stream at a time, using a process called selective attention. **Element 2**
The EEG studies used a technique called steady-state visually evoked potentials (SSVEP) to measure the brain's response to speech. The results showed that the brain can simultaneously encode two speech streams, even when the streams are competing for attention.
