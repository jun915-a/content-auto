## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Hardware attestation allows companies to control device usage, creating monopolies. 
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Restricts device modification
- **Point 2**: Enables vendor lock-in
- **Point 3**: Threatens user freedom
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: Companies use hardware attestation to limit device compatibility, forcing users into their ecosystem.
**Element 2**: This leads to a lack of innovation and higher prices due to reduced competition.
> 💡 Insight: Users must be aware of these restrictions to make informed choices.
## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Reduced consumer choice
- Increased security risks
- Stifled innovation
## ✨ Conclusion
The risks associated with hardware attestation monopolies must be addressed to protect user rights and promote a healthier tech industry.
