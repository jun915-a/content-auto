## 🔑 The Core of This Topic

Step into a voxel-filled Warsaw and explore its vibrant city center! This project, inspired by Minecraft and MagicaVoxel, offers an immersive 3D map, letting you control time, weather, and even pedestrians, creating a unique, interactive adventure.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Voxel-ized Warsaw**: A Minecraft-like city map.
- **Interactive**: Control time, weather, and pedestrians.
- **Immersive Experience**: Explore Warsaw's heart in a new way.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Central Warsaw in Voxels**: The project presents a detailed, voxel-based representation of Warsaw's central areas, offering a unique visual experience.

**Time and Weather Control**: Users can manipulate time, from day to night, and adjust weather conditions, adding a dynamic element to the map.

> 💡 Insight: The ability to control time and weather enhances the immersive nature of the experience, allowing users to explore Warsaw under different scenarios.

**Pedestrian Control**: A unique feature lets users click on pedestrians and control their movements, adding an interactive and playful element to the map.

**Exploration**: The map encourages users to explore, discover hidden details, and create their own adventures within the voxel-ized city.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact

- **Education**: Can be used to teach history or urban planning.
- **Entertainment**: Provides a fun, interactive experience for all ages.

This project showcases an innovative approach to digital mapping, blending entertainment, education, and tourism. It's a unique, accessible way to experience Warsaw, offering something for everyone.
