## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Bipartite matching is a fundamental concept in computer science, referring to the problem of finding a perfect matching in a bipartite graph. A bipartite graph is a graph whose vertices can be divided into two disjoint sets such that every edge connects a vertex from one set to a vertex from the other set.
