# Romania's Land Registry Wiped

A hacker has wiped Romania's entire land registry database, causing widespread disruption and concern over the security of sensitive information.

## The Core of This Topic
A recent cyber attack has resulted in the deletion of Romania's land registry database, leaving the country's property records in disarray.

## 5-Second Key Points
- **Massive Data Loss**: Entire land registry database wiped
- **Security Breach**: Hacker gained unauthorized access to system
- **National Impact**: Effects felt across Romania's property market

## Detailed Breakdown
**Vulnerability Exploited**
A weakness in the system's security allowed the hacker to gain access and delete the database.

**Lack of Backups**
Insufficient backups meant that the data could not be easily recovered.

> Insight: This incident highlights the importance of robust cybersecurity measures and regular backups.

## Real-World Impact
- Disruption to property sales and purchases
- Potential for fraudulent activity
- Long-term consequences for Romania's economy

## Conclusion
The attack on Romania's land registry database serves as a warning to governments and organizations to prioritize cybersecurity and protect sensitive information.
