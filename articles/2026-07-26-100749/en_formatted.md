## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Performance Architecture**
DskDitto's speed is a direct result of its parallel architecture. Instead of processing files one by one, it intelligently distributes the workload across available CPU cores. This allows for simultaneous hashing and comparison of file groups, drastically cutting down the time required to scan entire drives or vast directories, even those containing millions of files.

**Rust-Powered Efficiency**
The choice of Rust as the implementation language is crucial to DskDitto's robust performance. Rust's focus on memory safety, zero-cost abstractions, and powerful concurrency primitives enables the creation of highly optimized and reliable software. This ensures that DskDitto operates with minimal overhead, maximizing throughput and stability during intensive scanning operations.
