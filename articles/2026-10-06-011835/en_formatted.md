## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
FlattenSF.com is a web-based tool designed to help users find the gentlest gradients between any two points in San Francisco. It leverages elevation data to calculate and visualize routes that minimize uphill climbs, making the city more accessible for cyclists, pedestrians, and those with mobility challenges.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Effortless Navigation**: Discover routes that avoid San Francisco's notorious inclines.
- **Data-Driven**: Uses precise elevation data for accurate route planning.
- **Accessibility Focus**: Empowers cyclists, walkers, and mobility-impaired individuals.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Route Calculation Engine**
The tool's core is a sophisticated algorithm that analyzes street-level elevation data. It considers the grade of each segment of a route, prioritizing those with minimal inclines to construct the 'flattest' possible path between your start and end points.

**Interactive Map Interface**
Users can easily input their desired origin and destination on an interactive map. The system then overlays the recommended flattest route, allowing for visual confirmation and comparison with other potential paths.

> 💡 Insight: This tool democratizes San Francisco's geography, transforming daunting hills into manageable slopes for everyday travel.

**Customization Options**
While the primary function is finding the flattest route, the platform may offer (or inspire) future features like filtering by distance, avoiding major roads, or even finding routes with specific elevation gain targets.
