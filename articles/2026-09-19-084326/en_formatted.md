## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Photon Emission Analysis**
Researchers analyzed the faint light emitted by the RP2350's transistors. This emission pattern acts as a fingerprint, revealing the operational state of specific logic gates, enabling precise targeting for the laser.

**Targeted Laser Fault Injection**
Using the photon emission data, a focused laser beam was directed at critical components during operation. This induced transient faults, disrupting normal execution and triggering unintended states, such as enabling secure debug modes.
