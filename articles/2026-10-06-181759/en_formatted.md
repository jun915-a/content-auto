## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
TDK’s meta-optic mirrors exploit **subwavelength nanostructures** to control light propagation, acting as ultra-thin, high-performance optical elements. Unlike traditional mirrors, these surfaces **diffract and reflect light dynamically**, enabling **adaptive focusing** and **minimal distortion**. This technology is pivotal for **retinal projection**, where clarity and precision are non-negotiable.

**Element 2**
The **direct retinal projection** approach bypasses the need for intermediate screens or projectors, sending light **straight to the retina** for a **seamless, high-fidelity visual experience**. This method reduces **power consumption by up to 50%** compared to conventional AR displays, making it **ideal for extended wear**—critical for smart glasses designed for daily use.
