# Dangerous PyPI Compromise: LiteLLM Backdoor Steals Credentials

A malicious LiteLLM 1.107.0 package on PyPI installs a credential stealer and backdoor, exposing users to data theft and remote control. Discover the attack, its mechanics, and how to stay safe.

**A critical security alert has emerged as a compromised version of LiteLLM, specifically version 1.107.0, was uploaded to PyPI (Python Package Index). This malicious package not only steals credentials but also implants a backdoor, giving attackers full control over compromised systems. The incident underscores the importance of vigilance when installing third-party packages, especially in development environments where sensitive data is frequently handled.**
