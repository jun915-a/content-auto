# Global Workspace in AI

Discover how global workspace in language models revolutionizes AI capabilities, enhancing performance and understanding

The global workspace in language models refers to a centralized system where information from various sources is integrated and processed to generate human-like responses.

- **Point 2**: Improves understanding of context
- **Point 3**: Enables more accurate responses

**Element 1**

**Element 2**
