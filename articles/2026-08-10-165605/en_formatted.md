## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
This project introduces a novel voice-driven murder mystery game. Players can directly speak to AI-powered suspects, asking questions and receiving responses generated in real-time, allowing for an immersive and interactive detective experience.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Voice Interaction**: Speak directly to AI suspects.
- **AI-Powered Storytelling**: Dynamic responses and evolving narrative.
- **Immersive Deduction**: Solve mysteries through natural conversation.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Interactive Dialogue System**
Leveraging advanced AI, the game enables natural language understanding and generation. Your spoken questions are processed, and the AI constructs relevant, in-character responses, making each playthrough unique.

**Dynamic Suspect Personas**
Each AI suspect is designed with distinct personalities, backstories, and potential motives. The AI ensures their responses are consistent with their persona, adding layers of complexity and realism to the investigation.
