## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
TownSquare is a lightweight "presence layer" designed to add a subtle, interactive element to websites. It allows visitors to see who else is currently viewing the site, fostering a sense of community and shared experience without being intrusive.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Community Building**: Creates a sense of shared experience among visitors.
- **Subtle Engagement**: Enhances user interaction without disrupting the browsing experience.
- **Lightweight Design**: Minimal impact on website performance and loading times.
