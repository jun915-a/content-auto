# Guitoro: Play Real Guitar in Your Browser for Free

Discover Guitoro, a free browser-based virtual guitar that mimics real instruments with stunning accuracy. Strum with your keyboard, mouse, or touchscreen and explore acoustic, classical, electric, or bass—all without downloads or plugins.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Guitoro is a browser-based virtual guitar that leverages high-quality recordings of real instruments to create an immersive playing experience. The platform offers a seamless way to learn chords, experiment with different guitar types, and even jam along to music, all while feeling the authenticity of real strings.
