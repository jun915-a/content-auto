## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Pause and Resume Downloads**
Firefox's download manager offers a simple way to pause and resume downloads. This is especially useful for large files that may take a while to transfer.

**Settings for Resumable Downloads**
To enable resumable downloads, go to Firefox's Settings > General > Applications. Look for the 'Save files to' option and ensure it's set to 'Ask me every time.' This setting ensures Firefox prompts you for a location, allowing you to resume downloads easily.

> 💡 Insight: By default, Firefox saves files to a standard location, which may not be ideal for large downloads. Changing this setting gives you more control and ensures you can resume downloads from the same location.
