# Reviving My College Band with AI: A 25-Year Journey

With the power of AI, one man is reviving his college band, Fading Maize, 25 years after its initial formation at Ripon College in Wisconsin.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Fading Maize, the college band I formed 25 years ago, is back from the dead thanks to AI technology. Our music, recorded on low-budget equipment, has been reimagined and revitalized using the latest advancements in AI music production.
