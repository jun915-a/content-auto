## The Core of This Topic
Dav2d is an open-source video decoder that provides high-performance decoding for various video formats. It is designed to be fast, efficient, and compatible with multiple platforms.

## 5-Second Key Points
- **High-Performance Decoding**: Fast and efficient video decoding
- **Multi-Platform Support**: Compatible with Windows, Linux, and macOS
- **Open-Source**: Free and open-source software

## Detailed Breakdown
**Decoder Architecture**
Dav2d's decoder architecture is designed for high-performance and efficiency, allowing for fast video decoding.

**Supported Formats**
Dav2d supports a wide range of video formats, including H.264, H.265, and VP9.
