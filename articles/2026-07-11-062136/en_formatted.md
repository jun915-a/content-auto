## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Ultrasound is a non-invasive technology that uses high-frequency sound waves to create images of the body's internal structures. In the context of silent speech, ultrasound is used to detect the vibrations of the tongue, lips, and vocal cords, allowing for the recognition of spoken words without the need for traditional microphones.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Tongue and Lip Movement**
The tongue and lips play a crucial role in speech production, and ultrasound technology can detect the precise movements of these organs. By analyzing these movements, it is possible to recognize the spoken words and convert them into digital signals.

**Vocal Cord Vibrations**
The vocal cords are responsible for producing the sound waves that carry our voice. Ultrasound can detect the vibrations of the vocal cords, allowing for the recognition of spoken words without the need for traditional microphones.
