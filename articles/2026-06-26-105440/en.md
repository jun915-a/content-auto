# Apple Skips M6 for M7

Apple is skipping high-end M6 Mac chips to focus on the M7 line, which will include the M7 Pro, M7 Max, and M7 Ultra, with a focus on AI capabilities

## The Core of This Topic
Apple is shifting its focus to the M7 line, citing AI capabilities as the reason for skipping the high-end M6 Mac chips.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **New Chips**: M7 Pro, M7 Max, and M7 Ultra
- **AI Focus**: Chips will have enhanced AI capabilities
- **No M6**: High-end M6 Mac chips will not be released
## Detailed Breakdown
**M7 Lineup**
The M7 lineup will have improved performance and efficiency, with a focus on AI tasks.
**AI Enhancements**
The new chips will have dedicated AI processing units, allowing for faster and more efficient AI computations.
> Insight: The M7 line will provide a significant boost to Apple's AI capabilities.
## Real-World Impact
- Improved performance for AI tasks
- Enhanced efficiency for battery life
- Increased competition in the AI chip market
## Conclusion
The shift to the M7 line marks a significant change in Apple's chip strategy, with a focus on AI capabilities and performance.
