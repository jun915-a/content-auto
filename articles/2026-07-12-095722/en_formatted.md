## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Jellyfish Undersea Roundabout is a world-first underwater traffic circle located in the Faroe Islands, Denmark. This innovative infrastructure allows vehicles to pass through a tunnel system while boats and ships navigate the surrounding waters above.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Underwater Tunnel System**
The roundabout features a sophisticated tunnel system that allows vehicles to pass through while boats and ships navigate the surrounding waters above. This innovative design minimizes disruptions to marine life and reduces the risk of accidents.

**Sustainable Infrastructure**
The Jellyfish Undersea Roundabout is a prime example of sustainable infrastructure. By reducing traffic congestion and promoting eco-friendly transportation, the Faroe Islands are leading the way in environmentally responsible development.
