This ability is crucial in its **predatory hunts** and defense against threats like sharks and larger fish. Its **buccal funnel** also allows precise jet propulsion, enabling swift escapes when needed. Unlike shallow-water octopuses, this species has **thicker skin and reduced sensitivity to pressure**, adapting perfectly to its **high-pressure, low-light habitat**.

**Ecological Role**

Its presence also **aerates sediments** through burrowing, benefiting coral reefs and benthic ecosystems.
