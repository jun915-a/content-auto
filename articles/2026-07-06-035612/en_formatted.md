## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Honore de Balzac, a French novelist and playwright, is widely regarded as one of the most prolific writers in history. He is best known for his series of novels, 'La Comedie Humaine', which explores the lives of the French middle class. Balzac's writing career was marked by incredible productivity, with some sources suggesting he wrote up to 5,000 words per day.

**Element 2**
Leo Tolstoy, a Russian novelist and philosopher, is another writer known for his incredible output. He is best known for his novels, 'War and Peace' and 'Anna Karenina', which are considered two of the greatest novels ever written. Tolstoy's writing career was marked by a strong sense of morality and social commentary, which is reflected in his works.
