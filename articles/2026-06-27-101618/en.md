# Paying for Pre-Modern Armies: A Worldbuilder's Guide

Discover the intricacies of funding pre-modern armies and make your world's militaries more believable.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Pre-modern armies were often funded through a mix of taxation, tributes, and plunder. Understanding these funding sources is crucial for creating realistic and immersive worlds.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Taxation was the primary source of funding for many pre-modern armies.
- **Point 2**: Tributes and plunder were also used to supplement army funding.
- **Point 3**: The type of funding used often depended on the army's purpose and the ruler's priorities.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Taxation**
Taxation was the primary source of funding for many pre-modern armies. Taxes were often levied on the population, with the wealthy contributing more than the poor. This system was fair in theory, but in practice, it often led to resentment and rebellion.

**Tributes and Plunder**
Tributes and plunder were also used to supplement army funding. Tributes were payments made by vassals or subject states to their overlords, while plunder was the spoils of war. These sources of funding were often unreliable and could lead to conflicts over resources.

> 💡 Insight: The type of funding used often reflected the ruler's priorities and the army's purpose.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- A well-funded army can be a powerful tool for a ruler's ambitions.
- The type of funding used can impact the army's morale and effectiveness.
- A realistic funding system can add depth and complexity to a world's politics.

## ✨ Conclusion
Understanding how pre-modern armies were funded is essential for creating realistic and immersive worlds. By considering the different sources of funding and their implications, worldbuilders can create militaries that are believable and engaging.
