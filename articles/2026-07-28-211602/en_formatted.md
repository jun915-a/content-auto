## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Statelessness in transport refers to the absence of session persistence between requests. This means each request is treated independently, without relying on stored information from previous interactions.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Reduced latency by eliminating session setup and teardown.
- **Point 2**: Simplified scalability with load balancers and clustering.
- **Point 3**: Improved security through reduced attack surface.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Statelessness in transport is achieved by using connectionless protocols, such as UDP. This allows for faster transmission and reception of data, as there is no need to establish a persistent connection.

The benefits of transport statelessness are numerous. It enables faster and more efficient data transfer, reduces the risk of session hijacking, and simplifies scalability and security.
