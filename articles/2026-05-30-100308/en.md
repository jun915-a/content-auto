# UC Faculty Push for SAT Return

UC math professors demand a return to SAT tests for STEM admissions due to 'severe' math deficits in students

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
UC math professors are calling for the return of SAT tests for STEM admissions, citing severe math deficits in incoming students.
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Severe math deficits in students
- **Point 2**: Demand for SAT return for STEM admissions
- **Point 3**: UC faculty concerned about student preparedness
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The faculty's demand is driven by concerns about the lack of preparedness of incoming students in math.
**Element 2**: Professors believe that the SAT test would provide a more accurate assessment of a student's math abilities.
> 💡 Insight: The SAT test could help identify students who need additional math support.
## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Better student assessment
- Improved math education
- Increased student success
## ✨ Conclusion
The return of the SAT test for STEM admissions could have a positive impact on student success and math education.
