## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**A Model for the Planet**
Gemini 4 Carbon leverages **neural architecture search (NAS)** to identify the most efficient pathways for processing data. By **pruning unnecessary computations** and optimizing memory usage, it achieves **higher throughput per watt**—a critical metric for sustainable AI. The model also employs **dynamic sparsity**, where only the most relevant neural pathways are activated, further slashing energy demands. This isn’t just about speed; it’s about **doing more with less**, a philosophy that aligns with global sustainability goals.

**Carbon-Aware Training**
Training AI models is notoriously energy-intensive, but Gemini 4 Carbon flips the script. Google introduced **carbon-aware training**, where the model dynamically adjusts its workload based on real-time **grid carbon intensity data**. During peak pollution hours, the system **prioritizes less carbon-intensive tasks** or pauses non-critical computations. This adaptive approach ensures that Gemini 4 Carbon’s lifecycle—from training to inference—**contributes minimally to climate change**.
