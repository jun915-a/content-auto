Ghost Font achieves its magic through subtle yet strategic typographic tweaks. Unlike traditional fonts, it incorporates **micro-variations in letter shapes and spacing** that human eyes perceive as natural but trip up AI algorithms. For example, certain strokes might be slightly misaligned or curves adjusted to mimic handwriting inconsistencies—tricks that fool OCR systems into misreading or skipping text entirely. The font’s designers emphasize that it’s not about obscurity but **exploiting the gaps in AI’s training data**, where real-world imperfections often lead to errors.

**Element 2: Why AI Struggles with It
AI OCR systems rely on **pattern recognition trained on vast datasets of clean, standardized text**. Ghost Font disrupts this by introducing **controlled imperfections** that don’t affect human perception but create enough noise for AI to fail. Studies show that even minor deviations—like slightly asymmetrical letters or inconsistent kerning—can reduce OCR accuracy by **30-50%**, depending on the model. This isn’t about making text unreadable; it’s about making it **readable only to the intended audience—humans.**
