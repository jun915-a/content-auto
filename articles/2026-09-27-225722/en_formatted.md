Learn how to replace old batteries in rechargeable bike lights, ensuring optimal performance and visibility on your rides. A simple fix can make a big difference!

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Identify the battery type and size.
- **Point 2**: Source a replacement battery.
- **Point 3**: Follow the manufacturer's instructions for battery replacement.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1: Battery Identification**
Start by checking your bike light's manual or product specifications to identify the exact battery type and size. This information is crucial for finding a compatible replacement.

**Element 2: Sourcing a New Battery**
Once you know the battery type, purchase a new one from a reputable source. Ensure it's a high-quality battery to maintain the performance and longevity of your bike lights.

> 💡 Insight: Consider investing in a spare battery to have a backup for longer rides or unexpected delays.
