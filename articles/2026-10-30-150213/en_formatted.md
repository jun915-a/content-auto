Discover the University of Tennessee’s digital archive of Cormac McCarthy’s unpublished works, letters, and manuscripts—an unparalleled resource for fans and scholars alike. Explore its impact on literature, preservation, and McCarthy’s enduring influence.

**The Cormac McCarthy Digital Archive** is a groundbreaking online repository housed at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville. Launched in 2017, it offers unprecedented access to the Nobel laureate’s unpublished manuscripts, correspondence, drafts, and early works. This digital library isn’t just a collection—it’s a window into the mind of one of America’s most revered writers, preserving his legacy for future generations while revolutionizing how literary scholars engage with primary sources.
