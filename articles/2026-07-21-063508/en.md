- **Point 2**: Simplify complex computations with ease.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The library provides a simple, incremental API that allows developers to focus on the changes in their data, rather than processing entire datasets from scratch. This approach enables significant reductions in computational overhead and memory usage.

**Element 2**: With incremental computations, developers can simplify complex tasks by breaking them down into smaller, more manageable pieces. This approach also enables developers to take advantage of incremental caching, reducing the need for redundant computations.
