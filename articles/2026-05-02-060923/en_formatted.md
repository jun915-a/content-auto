# WhatCable: Simplify USB-C

*Insert header image here*

WhatCable is a menu bar app that reads cable data, helping you identify the capabilities of your USB-C cables, from charging power to Thunderbolt support

## The Core of This Topic
WhatCable is a tiny app that sits in your menu bar, designed to help you make sense of the various USB-C cables you have, by reading the cable data your Mac provides.

## 5-Second Key Points
- **Identify Cable Capabilities**: Determine what your USB-C cable can do
- **Check Charging Power**: Find out if your cable supports 5W, 100W, or more
- **Thunderbolt Support**: Discover if your cable supports Thunderbolt 4

## Detailed Breakdown
**Cable Inspection**
WhatCable provides a straightforward way to inspect your USB-C cables, giving you the information you need to choose the right cable for the task at hand.

**Simplifying Cable Management**
By using WhatCable, you can simplify your cable management, ensuring you're using the correct cable for charging, data transfer, or other purposes.

> Insight: WhatCable helps you avoid the frustration of dealing with multiple, seemingly identical USB-C cables.

## Real-World Impact
- Easier cable management
- Reduced frustration with incorrect cable usage
- Improved productivity by choosing the right cable for the task

## Conclusion
WhatCable is a handy tool for anyone dealing with multiple USB-C cables, providing a simple and effective way to identify their capabilities and simplify your workflow
