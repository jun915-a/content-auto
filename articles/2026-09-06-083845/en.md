# Topology's Twisted Take on Earth's Map

Explore the fascinating concept of a 'Topologist's Map of the World,' where geographical distortions reveal surprising connections and a different way of seeing our planet.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
A Topologist's Map of the World reimagines our planet not by its actual shape or size, but by its connectivity. Distances and borders are stretched or shrunk to emphasize how places are linked, revealing a network of relationships rather than a static geography.
