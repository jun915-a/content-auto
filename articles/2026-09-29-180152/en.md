## 📈 Detailed Breakdown

**Element 1: What is Jevons’ Paradox?**

Named after economist **William Stanley Jevons**, this phenomenon explains why improvements in **energy efficiency** (e.g., fuel-efficient cars) can lead to **higher total consumption**—not lower. The paradox arises because efficiency reduces costs, **freeing up resources** for additional use. Jevstiller **quantifies this dynamic**, turning it into a **predictive model** rather than just a theoretical observation.


**Element 2: The Disagreement Bound Mechanism**

Jevstiller’s standout feature is its **disagreement bound**, a statistical guarantee that limits how far its predictions can stray from reality. Unlike traditional AI models, which may produce **unreliable outliers**, this tool **bounds the error**, making it trustworthy for **policy-making and resource planning**. It achieves this by **localizing the model**—meaning it adapts to specific datasets without overfitting to global trends.
