# Ditch the Batteries: NFC-Powered Smart Business Card with MCU

A groundbreaking NFC business card harvests energy to power an MCU, displaying dynamic messages without batteries—redefining networking in tech innovation.

{
  "## 🔑 The Core of This Topic": "This project transforms a traditional business card into a dynamic, interactive device by integrating NFC energy harvesting with a microcontroller. It eliminates the need for batteries, relying solely on near-field communication to power embedded electronics and display customizable information."
}
