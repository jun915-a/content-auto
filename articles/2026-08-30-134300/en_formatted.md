## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Wide Field Instrument (WFI)**
This instrument provides Roman with a field of view 200 times larger than Hubble's. This allows it to capture enormous areas of the sky in a single observation, speeding up surveys.

**Coronagraph Instrument (CGI)**
The CGI is designed to block out the light from stars, allowing for direct imaging of exoplanets. This capability is crucial for studying planets that might harbor life.

> 💡 Insight: Roman's immense field of view is its most revolutionary feature, enabling it to conduct large-scale surveys that were previously impossible.
