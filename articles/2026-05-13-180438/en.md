# Leaving GitHub for Forgejo: A New Open Source Home

Discover why developers are migrating from GitHub to Forgejo, a community-driven fork. Explore the benefits of self-hosting and true open-source collaboration.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
This article explores the growing movement of developers and projects leaving GitHub for Forgejo. Forgejo is a community-driven fork of Gitea, offering a truly open-source and community-governed alternative to centralized platforms, emphasizing control, transparency, and ethical development practices.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Open Source Governance**: Forgejo is managed by its community, not a single corporation.
- **Data Sovereignty**: Self-hosting provides complete control over your code and data.
- **Ethical Development**: Avoids the concerns associated with corporate-controlled platforms.
- **Community Focus**: Prioritizes collaboration and developer needs.
- **Flexibility**: Offers freedom from platform lock-in and policy changes.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Community Governance**
Forgejo distinguishes itself by being entirely community-governed. Unlike GitHub, which is owned by Microsoft, Forgejo's development and direction are decided by its users and contributors, ensuring alignment with open-source principles.

**Self-Hosting and Control**
One of the primary drivers for migration is the ability to self-host Forgejo. This grants full control over data, infrastructure, and access, liberating projects from the whims and terms of service of large corporations.

> 💡 Insight: Moving to Forgejo offers a path to regain control over your development workflow and data, fostering a more independent and ethical ecosystem.

**Avoiding Vendor Lock-in**
By choosing Forgejo, projects can avoid the risks of vendor lock-in. This ensures long-term stability and freedom to adapt without being subject to sudden policy shifts or potential discontinuation of services.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Projects gain greater autonomy and control over their infrastructure and data.
- Fosters a more collaborative and transparent development environment.
- Encourages the growth of decentralized and community-owned software infrastructure.
- Provides a refuge for projects concerned about corporate influence on open source.

## ✨ Conclusion
As the open-source community seeks greater autonomy and ethical alternatives, Forgejo represents a compelling choice. It empowers developers with control, fosters true collaboration, and champions the core values of open source, offering a sustainable future for projects seeking independence.
