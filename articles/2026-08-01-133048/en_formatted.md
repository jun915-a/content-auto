## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Wi-Fi technology has become an essential part of our daily lives, but it often struggles to provide reliable coverage over long distances. The 2.4 GHz frequency band is particularly challenging due to its limited range and interference from other devices.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
* **Point 1**: Understand the limitations of the 2.4 GHz frequency band.
* **Point 2**: Identify potential sources of interference.
* **Point 3**: Choose the right equipment to maximize your Wi-Fi signal.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Antennas**
Using high-gain antennas can significantly improve your Wi-Fi signal, allowing it to travel further and penetrate obstacles more effectively. However, the effectiveness of an antenna depends on its orientation and placement.

**Router Configuration**
Properly configuring your router is crucial for achieving long range Wi-Fi connectivity. This includes setting the channel to one that minimizes interference and adjusting the transmit power to optimize coverage.

> 💡 Insight: The key to long range Wi-Fi is to find a balance between signal strength and interference.
