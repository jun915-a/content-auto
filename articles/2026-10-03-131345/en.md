ChatGPT’s **Sites** feature is a groundbreaking tool that lets users **create interactive, AI-powered websites** directly within the ChatGPT interface. Unlike traditional web builders, it merges natural language processing with web development, enabling seamless creation of chatbots, FAQs, and dynamic content without writing a single line of code. The innovation lies in its ability to **turn conversational flows into live web experiences**, bridging the gap between AI and user engagement.
