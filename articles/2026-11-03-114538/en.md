# Warsaw in Voxels: Interactive 3D Map Reimagines City Centre

Explore Warsaw's city centre like never before! This interactive 3D voxel map, inspired by Minecraft, lets you change time, weather, and even control pedestrians.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
This project presents an interactive 3D voxel map of Warsaw's city centre, offering a unique, blocky visualization. It allows users to manipulate environmental factors like time of day and weather, and even engage directly with the environment by controlling virtual pedestrians.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Interactive Voxel Map**: A blocky, 3D representation of Warsaw's city centre.
- **Dynamic Environment**: Adjust time of day and weather conditions.
- **Pedestrian Control**: Experience the city from a unique, interactive perspective.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Voxel Visualization**
The city is rendered in a distinctive voxel art style, reminiscent of games like Minecraft and tools like MagicaVoxel. This approach gives familiar landmarks a charming, retro aesthetic and allows for a unique exploration experience.

**Environmental Controls**
Users can dynamically alter the scene by changing the time of day, observing how light and shadows play across the voxel cityscape. The addition of weather effects further enhances immersion and visual variety.

> 💡 Insight: The voxel aesthetic transforms a real-world location into a playful, digital environment, fostering a new way to appreciate urban landscapes.

**Interactive Pedestrians**
A standout feature is the ability to click on a pedestrian and take control of them. This allows for a first-person or third-person perspective exploration of the city, adding a layer of personal engagement.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Enhances digital tourism and engagement with urban heritage.
- Provides a novel platform for artistic expression and visualization.
- Offers a unique tool for architectural and urban planning visualization.

## ✨ Conclusion
Warsaw in Voxels offers a captivating and interactive journey through a beloved city, blending art, technology, and urban exploration into a delightful digital experience.
