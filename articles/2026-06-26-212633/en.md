## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Data Parallelism**
Data parallelism is a technique where multiple GPUs process different parts of the data in parallel, resulting in significant speedups. This approach is particularly useful for large-scale machine learning workloads.

**Model Parallelism**
Model parallelism involves splitting a machine learning model across multiple GPUs, allowing for faster training times and improved scalability. This approach is especially useful for large and complex models.

> 💡 Insight: By leveraging data and model parallelism, developers can achieve unprecedented performance and scalability in their MLSys applications.
