# Introducing textlog: A Minimalist Text-Only Microblogging Platform

Discover a quiet, text-only microblogging platform that's open-source and JavaScript-free, designed for those seeking a distraction-free online space.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
textlog is an open-source microblogging platform that focuses on simplicity and minimalism. It's a text-only platform, devoid of JavaScript, making it a haven for those seeking a distraction-free online experience. The platform is designed to be quiet, allowing users to express themselves without the noise of modern social media.
