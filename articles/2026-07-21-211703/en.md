# Control USB Power with Uhubctl: Smart Hubs at Your Fingertips

Maximize your USB ports' potential with Uhubctl, a powerful tool for smart USB hubs. Learn how to control USB power per-port and unlock new possibilities.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Uhubctl is a command-line utility designed to control USB power per-port on smart USB hubs. By providing fine-grained control over power delivery, Uhubctl enables users to optimize their hub's performance and extend the lifespan of their devices.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Control USB power per-port for optimal performance and device longevity.
- **Point 2**: Compatible with most smart USB hubs, including popular brands.
- **Point 3**: Lightweight and easy to use, with minimal system resource requirements.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Uhubctl works by communicating directly with the smart USB hub, allowing users to adjust power settings for individual ports. This is particularly useful for devices that require precise power control, such as high-powered USB devices or those that are sensitive to power fluctuations.

**Element 2**
One of the key benefits of Uhubctl is its ability to extend the lifespan of devices connected to the hub. By reducing power surges and spikes, users can help prevent device damage and prolong their lifespan.
