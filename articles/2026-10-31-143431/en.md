# Shiny Object Syndrome: Why We Keep Chasing the Next Big Thing

Ever feel distracted by the latest trend, tool, or idea, only to abandon what you started? Shiny Object Syndrome explains why we struggle to focus—and how to break free from the cycle of constant distraction.

**Shiny Object Syndrome** is the tendency to frequently abandon or switch between goals, projects, or interests due to the allure of something new and exciting. Named for the way children (and adults) are drawn to shiny objects, this phenomenon reflects a broader struggle with focus, commitment, and long-term planning. It’s not just a lack of discipline—it’s a cognitive bias that hijacks our motivation, leaving us feeling scattered and unproductive. Whether in business, relationships, or personal growth, this syndrome derails progress by prioritizing novelty over sustainability. The result? Unfinished projects, wasted effort, and a sense of never truly achieving what we set out to do.
