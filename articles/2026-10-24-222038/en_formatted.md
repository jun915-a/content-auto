Ever wondered why cats in boxes can be both alive and dead? Dive into quantum physics—the science of tiny particles that defies logic. Discover superposition, entanglement, and how it shapes our world.

Quantum physics is the branch of science that explores the behavior of matter and energy at the smallest scales—where atoms, electrons, and photons rule the universe. Unlike the predictable world of classical physics, quantum mechanics introduces bizarre yet fascinating phenomena like particles existing in multiple states at once or being instantaneously connected across vast distances. This field, born from early 20th-century revolutions, isn’t just abstract—it powers modern technology from computers to medical imaging. Yet, its counterintuitive rules continue to challenge even the brightest minds. Let’s break it down in simple terms, exploring why it’s both mind-bending and essential to our world today.
