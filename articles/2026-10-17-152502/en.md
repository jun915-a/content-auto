# Florida’s Giant Snail Crisis: An Invasive Menace

Giant African snails are ravaging Florida’s ecosystems, devouring crops and spreading deadly parasites. Discover the ecological threat behind this silent invasion and why scientists are racing to contain it.

**The Atlantic** reports on Florida’s escalating crisis with the **giant African snail (*Achatina fulica*)**, an invasive species wreaking havoc on agriculture, native plants, and public health. Once an exotic pet, these snails have exploded in numbers, threatening Florida’s biodiversity and economy.
