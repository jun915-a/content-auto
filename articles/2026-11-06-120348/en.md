# Pwn.college: Your Gateway to Ethical Hacking Mastery

Discover **Pwn.college**, the free, interactive platform teaching cybersecurity fundamentals through hands-on challenges. Ideal for beginners and pros alike, it bridges theory and practice in a structured, engaging way.

**Pwn.college** is a free, interactive platform designed to teach cybersecurity and offensive computing concepts through hands-on challenges. Created by **Ropnop**, it’s structured like a college course, offering a **step-by-step learning path** from basics to advanced topics like buffer overflows, cryptography, and reverse engineering. The platform’s **gamified approach**—with quizzes, labs, and real-world scenarios—makes complex subjects accessible and engaging for learners of all levels. Whether you're a **beginner** or an **experienced hacker**, Pwn.college provides a structured, self-paced way to sharpen your skills without the cost of traditional education.
