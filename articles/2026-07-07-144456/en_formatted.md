The ability of small language models to operate in areas with unreliable internet connections is largely due to their distributed architecture, which allows them to process information locally and reduce the need for constant cloud connectivity. This is achieved through the use of edge computing, a technology that enables AI models to run on local devices, such as smartphones or laptops.
