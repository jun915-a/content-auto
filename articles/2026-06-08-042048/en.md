# Lathe: Learn with LLMs

Lathe is an experiment in using LLMs to teach new technical topics, generating hands-on tutorials with source-backed information

## The Core of This Topic
Lathe uses Large Language Models (LLMs) to create interactive tutorials for various technical subjects, allowing users to learn by doing rather than just reading.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Personalized Learning**: tailored to individual needs
- **Source-Backed**: reliable information from trusted sources
- **Hands-On**: interactive tutorials for practical experience
## Detailed Breakdown
**LLM Technology**
Lathe leverages advanced LLMs to analyze topics and create customized learning paths.
**Tutorial Generation**
The platform generates step-by-step guides, complete with examples and references.
> Insight: By using LLMs to teach instead of doing the work, Lathe helps users develop a deeper understanding of the subject matter.
## Real-World Impact
- Improved learning outcomes through interactive engagement
- Enhanced retention of complex technical information
- Increased confidence in applying new skills
## Conclusion
Lathe offers a unique approach to learning, empowering users to take control of their technical education and explore new domains with confidence.
