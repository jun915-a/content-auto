## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
A* pathfinding relies on heuristics to estimate distances, guiding the algorithm towards the most efficient path. Improving these heuristics is crucial for optimal performance.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Better Heuristics**: Lead to more accurate distance estimations
- **Smart Algorithms**: Enable faster and more efficient pathfinding
- **Enhanced Performance**: Results in smoother game experiences and better route optimizations

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Heuristic Functions**
Heuristic functions are used to estimate the distance from a given node to the target node, influencing the path the algorithm chooses.

**Differential Heuristics**
Differential heuristics offer a more nuanced approach by considering the differences in distances between nodes, providing a more accurate estimation.

> 💡 Insight: The choice of heuristic function significantly affects the algorithm's performance and the optimality of the path found.
