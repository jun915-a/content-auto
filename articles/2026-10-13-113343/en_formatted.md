## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
This project transforms a standard WiFi 7 router into a powerful Software Defined Radio (SDR) receiver. By leveraging the router's advanced processing capabilities and high-speed data interfaces, it can capture and process radio signals at an impressive 320 Msps, far exceeding typical SDR capabilities.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Router Repurposing**: A WiFi 7 router's hardware is adapted for SDR use.
- **High Throughput**: Achieves a remarkable 320 Msps sampling rate.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**WiFi 7 Router Hardware**
The project utilizes the high-speed Wi-Fi capabilities and processing power inherent in WiFi 7 routers. These devices are designed for rapid data transmission, making them suitable candidates for high-bandwidth signal processing tasks like SDR.

**Software Defined Radio (SDR) Integration**
Specialized software and firmware are developed to interface with the router's RF front-end and processing chips. This allows the router to function as a digital radio receiver, capturing raw radio frequency data.

> 💡 Insight: Repurposing consumer electronics like routers for advanced scientific applications highlights the potential of accessible hardware.

**320 Msps Throughput**
The key achievement is the ability to sample and process radio signals at 320 Mega-samples per second. This extremely high sampling rate enables the capture of very wide bandwidth signals, crucial for advanced radio monitoring and analysis.
