## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Bike lanes are designated paths for cyclists, providing a safe and efficient way to navigate through traffic. However, obstacles like debris, potholes, and illegal parking can render these lanes unusable, putting cyclists at risk.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Report obstructions through the Bike Bureau to ensure cyclist safety.
- **Point 2**: Regular maintenance of bike lanes is crucial to prevent accidents.
- **Point 3**: Your report can help identify and address potential hazards.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Bike Lane Maintenance**
Regular inspections and maintenance of bike lanes are essential to prevent accidents and ensure a smooth ride for cyclists. This includes cleaning debris, repairing potholes, and addressing other hazards that may obstruct the path.

**Cyclist Safety**
When bike lanes are obstructed, cyclists are forced to navigate through traffic, which can lead to accidents and injuries. Reporting obstructions through the Bike Bureau helps ensure that cyclists can use the lanes safely.
