The Starship vehicle executed its planned flight profile, reaching its intended altitude and demonstrating controlled re-entry maneuvers. This phase is critical for understanding atmospheric flight dynamics.

**The "Chopstick" Catch**
During the descent, the innovative "chopstick" mechanism on the launch tower engaged precisely, gripping the Starship booster. This controlled capture prevented a destructive landing and enabled recovery.
