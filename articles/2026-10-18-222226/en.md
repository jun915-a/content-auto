# SIGSALY: Churchill’s Top-Secret WWII Hotline

Discover how Winston Churchill and Franklin D. Roosevelt used the world’s first secure voice encryption system, SIGSALY, to communicate in secret during WWII. A technological marvel that shaped modern secure communications.

**SIGSALY: Churchill’s Top-Secret WWII Hotline** was the first fully encrypted voice communication system ever created. Developed during World War II, it allowed Winston Churchill and Franklin D. Roosevelt to speak privately across the Atlantic, ensuring their strategy discussions remained confidential from enemy interception. This groundbreaking technology laid the foundation for modern secure communications, including encrypted phone calls and digital encryption we rely on today.
