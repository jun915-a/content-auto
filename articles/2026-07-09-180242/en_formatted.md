# Why We're Moving Off Cloudflare Durable Objects

*Insert header image here*

We're leaving Cloudflare Durable Objects behind. Here's why.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic

Cloudflare Durable Objects, a popular and powerful NoSQL database, has been a crucial part of our platform. However, after careful consideration, we've decided to move away from it.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Performance issues and lack of scalability.
- **Point 2**: Inconsistent support and high costs.
- **Point 3**: Limited features and inflexibility.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
As we grew, we encountered performance issues and scalability problems with Durable Objects. It couldn't keep up with our increasing traffic, leading to slower response times and frustrated users.

**Element 2**
We also experienced inconsistent support, with long wait times for responses and a lack of clear guidance on how to resolve issues. The costs were high, and the features were limited, making it difficult to adapt to our changing needs.

> 💡 Insight: We need a more flexible and scalable solution that can grow with us.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Faster page loads and improved user experience.
- Reduced costs and increased efficiency.
- More flexibility and adaptability in our infrastructure.

## ✨ Conclusion
We're excited to explore new options and improve our infrastructure. This change will allow us to better serve our users and stay ahead of the curve in a rapidly changing tech landscape.
