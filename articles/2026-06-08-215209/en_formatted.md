## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The MusicDecoy app is a simple solution that creates a decoy Music app, redirecting the automatic launch to this new app instead, thus preventing the original Apple Music app from opening.

**Element 2**
This approach is useful for those who prefer other music streaming services and find the automatic launch of Apple Music annoying.

> 💡 Insight: By using a decoy, you maintain the functionality of your device while customizing your music experience.
