# Pandoc Turns 20

Celebrating twenty years of Pandoc, a document conversion tool that has revolutionized the way we work with documents, supporting over 40 formats

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Pandoc is a free and open-source document conversion tool that has been around for twenty years, supporting over 40 formats.
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Converts documents between various formats
- **Point 2**: Supports over 40 formats, including Markdown and HTML
- **Point 3**: Free and open-source
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: Pandoc allows users to convert documents from one format to another, making it easy to share and collaborate on documents.
**Element 2**: The tool supports a wide range of formats, including Markdown, HTML, and PDF, making it a versatile solution for document conversion.
> 💡 Insight: Pandoc's flexibility and customizability have made it a popular choice among writers, researchers, and developers.
## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Simplifies document conversion and sharing
- Enhances collaboration and productivity
- Supports a wide range of formats, making it easy to work with different types of documents

Pandoc's twenty-year milestone is a testament to its impact and relevance in the world of document conversion, and its continued development and support ensure it will remain a vital tool for years to come.
