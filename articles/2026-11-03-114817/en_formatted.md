## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**AI Agents vs. Traditional QA Tools**
Most QA tools rely on **scripted test cases**—rigid, brittle, and prone to breaking when UI changes. Momentic’s agents, however, **learn and adapt** by observing user flows, mimicking real-world usage patterns. They don’t just validate predefined paths; they **explore unpredictably**, uncovering bugs in edge cases that manual tests miss. This approach reduces false negatives and catches **latent issues** that slip through traditional validation.

> 💡 **Insight**: The shift from scripted tests to **autonomous exploration** mirrors how AI is transforming other domains—from chatbots to autonomous vehicles. Momentic applies this same adaptability to QA, making it far more resilient to app updates.

**How It Works: The Agent’s Playbook**
Momentic’s agents follow a **three-step process**:
- **Plan**: Analyze the app’s UI and workflows to identify critical paths and potential failure points.
- **Explore**: Interact dynamically with the app, testing inputs, transitions, and error states.
- **Bug-Bash**: Report findings with **reproducible steps**, screenshots, and severity scores—directly to your team or CI/CD pipeline.

The platform supports **multi-device testing**, meaning agents can simulate users on iOS, Android, or web simultaneously, catching cross-platform bugs early.
