The Library of Congress has officially recognized SQLite as a recommended storage format, solidifying its position as a reliable and secure choice for data storage.

SQLite has been recognized by the Library of Congress as a recommended storage format, providing a secure and reliable way to store data. This recognition is a testament to SQLite's simplicity, flexibility, and widespread adoption.

SQLite's design makes it an ideal choice for a wide range of applications, from small personal databases to large-scale enterprise systems. Its self-contained nature eliminates the need for a separate server, making it a lightweight and portable solution.
