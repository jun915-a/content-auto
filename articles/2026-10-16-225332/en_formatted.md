# 911’s Broken Link to Mental Health Care

*Insert header image here*

America’s 911 system was never designed for mental health crises—yet it bears the brunt of the burden. This flawed response system exposes deep failures in crisis intervention, costing lives and resources.

**The 911 system was built for emergencies—fires, shootings, car crashes—not for mental health crises. Yet, it’s the first line of defense for millions of Americans in distress. The result? A system stretched thin, ill-equipped to handle the most vulnerable, and a tragic cycle of preventable deaths.**
