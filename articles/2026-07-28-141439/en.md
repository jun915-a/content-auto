# Japan Hit by 7.1 Earthquake

A 7.1 magnitude earthquake strikes Japan, causing widespread damage and triggering tsunami warnings, with the Japan Meteorological Agency providing updates on the disaster

## The Core of This Topic
A powerful 7.1 magnitude earthquake has struck Japan, causing significant damage and disruption. The quake's epicenter was located off the coast, prompting tsunami warnings to be issued.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Location**: Off the coast of Japan
- **Magnitude**: 7.1
- **Tsunami Warning**: Issued for several coastal areas
## Detailed Breakdown
**Earthquake Details**
The earthquake occurred at a depth of approximately 60 kilometers, with its epicenter located off the coast of Japan. The quake was felt across several prefectures.
**Response Efforts**
Emergency services are on the scene, with rescue teams working to assess the damage and provide aid to those affected.
> Insight: The Japan Meteorological Agency is closely monitoring the situation and providing updates on the disaster.
