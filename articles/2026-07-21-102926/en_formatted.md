The frontier model is a game-changing approach to editing that simplifies the process by focusing on one single edit. It's a more efficient and effective way to make changes, especially when compared to traditional editing methods.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The frontier model works by identifying the most impactful change that needs to be made and then focusing on that one edit. This approach helps to eliminate unnecessary changes and ensures that the most significant improvements are made first.

**Element 2**: By limiting the scope of each edit, the frontier model forces you to think more critically about the changes you're making. This leads to more intentional and effective editing.

> 💡 Insight: The frontier model is not about making many small changes, but about making one significant change that has a lasting impact.
