## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**MicroVMs Architecture**
MicroVMs use a lightweight virtual machine (VM) architecture to provide isolation and security. This architecture is designed to be highly efficient and scalable, making it suitable for a wide range of use cases.

**Benefits of MicroVMs**
MicroVMs offer several benefits, including improved security, increased control, and enhanced collaboration. With MicroVMs, developers can create isolated environments for development, testing, and production, which helps to prevent contamination and ensure that applications are deployed safely.
