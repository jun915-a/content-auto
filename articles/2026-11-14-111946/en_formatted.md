## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**FM Synthesis Fundamentals**
FM synthesis manipulates the frequency of one or more operators (oscillators) with another, creating rich and evolving timbres. The Workbench visualizes these interactions, making it easier to grasp.

**Operator Configuration**
Each operator can be configured with different waveforms, levels, and feedback. The Workbench allows for quick adjustments and real-time previews, speeding up the sound design process.

> 💡 Insight: Understanding operator relationships is key to unlocking unique FM sounds.

**Algorithm Selection**
Algorithms define how operators are interconnected. The Workbench presents these algorithms in an easily digestible format, allowing users to select and modify them with ease.
