# Herdr Joins Y Combinator, Commits to Open-Source Runtime

Herdr, a platform for building AI agents, has been accepted into Y Combinator, a major accelerator. This significant milestone comes with a strong commitment to keeping its core runtime open-source, ensuring continued community collaboration and developer freedom.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Herdr, an innovative platform designed to simplify the creation and deployment of AI agents, has officially announced its acceptance into Y Combinator, one of the world's most prestigious startup accelerators. A cornerstone of this announcement is Herdr's unwavering commitment to maintaining its core runtime as open-source, ensuring transparency, community contribution, and a strong developer-first approach even as it scales.

- **Y Combinator Acceptance**: Herdr joins the highly selective YC program.
- **Open-Source Commitment**: The core runtime remains freely accessible and modifiable.
- **AI Agent Development**: Focuses on empowering developers to build complex AI agents.
