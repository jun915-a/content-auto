# 8-Bit CPU Built from Scratch

2nd year EE students built an 8-bit CPU in Logisim, a significant DIY project that showcases their skills and understanding of computer architecture

## The Core of This Topic
The control unit of this system does not implement the generic microcode ROM or any kind of RAM, a unique approach to CPU design.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Implemented in Logisim
- **Point 2**: 8-bit CPU architecture
- **Point 3**: No microcode ROM or RAM
## Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The CPU was built from scratch, demonstrating a deep understanding of computer architecture and design principles.
**Element 2**: The use of Logisim as the development platform allowed for a purely digital implementation, streamlining the design process.
> Insight: This project highlights the potential for students to create complex systems with limited resources.
## Real-World Impact
- Improved understanding of CPU design
- Development of problem-solving skills
- Demonstration of innovative thinking
## Conclusion
The successful construction of an 8-bit CPU from scratch is a testament to the students' dedication and expertise, paving the way for future innovations in computer engineering.
