## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Red Hat, acquired by IBM in 2019 for a staggering **$34 billion**, has long been synonymous with enterprise Linux, Kubernetes, and open-source innovation. However, recent reports suggest IBM may be **phasing out Red Hat’s standalone brand**, consolidating its offerings under IBM’s umbrella. This raises critical questions: Will Red Hat’s legacy survive, or is this the death knell for a company that redefined enterprise software?
