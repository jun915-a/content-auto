The Navier-Stokes equations comprise a system of partial differential equations, offering a mathematical framework for fluid dynamics. They consider factors like velocity, pressure, and temperature to describe fluid behavior.

**Complex Behaviors**
Despite their elegant form, these equations exhibit challenging behaviors. Solving them accurately remains a significant mathematical and computational endeavor, especially for turbulent flows.
