As hackers refine their tactics to **disrupt physical operations**, businesses must **harden IoT systems, segment networks, and simulate attack scenarios**. The Intermarché incident proves that in an era of **digital-physical convergence**, **prevention is the only defense**—before the next fire starts.\n"
