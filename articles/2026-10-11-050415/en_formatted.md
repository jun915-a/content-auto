## 🔑 Unveiling the Artistic RNN
This project showcases an innovative use of Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) to generate Korean handwriting strokes. The model learns from a dataset of handwritten Hangul characters, capturing the essence of Korean calligraphy.

- **Cultural Preservation**: It offers a modern tool for traditional art.
- **Personalized Calligraphy**: Users can generate unique styles.

## 📈 A Deep Dive
**Dataset**: The model trains on a diverse dataset, ensuring a wide range of handwriting styles.

**Stroke Generation**: It produces strokes with precision, mimicking human writing.
