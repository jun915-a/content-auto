# Why We Deprecate Our LLM Router

The rise of LLM routers sparks a reevaluation of our approach, leading to the deprecation of our own solution.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Everyone in the industry is building their own LLM routers, and we've taken a step back to assess our solution's value in this landscape.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Our LLM router was a pioneering effort, but it's no longer the best solution.
- **Point 2**: The industry's shift towards open-source and collaborative approaches has changed the game.
- **Point 3**: Our resources are better spent on other projects that drive real innovation.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: In the past, our LLM router was a key differentiator, but today it's just one of many options. We've seen a proliferation of alternative solutions that offer superior features and flexibility.

**Element 2**: The industry's move towards open-source and collaborative approaches has created a snowball effect, with more and more organizations contributing to and building upon each other's work. This has led to a rapid acceleration of innovation and a shift away from proprietary solutions like our LLM router.

> 💡 Insight: Our deprecation of the LLM router is a recognition that sometimes, it's better to focus on what you do best and let others take the lead on specific areas of innovation.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- **Impact 1**: By deprecating our LLM router, we're freeing up resources to focus on more pressing projects that have the potential to drive significant impact.
- **Impact 2**: Our decision will likely influence other organizations to reevaluate their own LLM routers and consider more collaborative approaches.
- **Impact 3**: The industry as a whole will benefit from a more open and collaborative ecosystem, leading to faster innovation and better outcomes for all.

## ✨ Conclusion
As we deprecate our LLM router, we're not saying that it wasn't a valuable effort – it was. But in today's landscape, we believe that our resources are best spent on other projects that will drive real innovation and make a meaningful impact.
