# Windows 11 Weather App RAM Issue

Windows 11's built-in Weather app is found to waste over 1 GB of RAM, causing potential performance issues for users, especially those with lower-end hardware

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Windows 11 Weather app is consuming excessive RAM, affecting system performance.
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **High RAM Usage**: Over 1 GB of RAM is being wasted
- **Performance Impact**: Slower system performance and responsiveness
- **Resource Intensive**: Unnecessary resource usage by the app
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**App Design**
The app's design and functionality may be contributing to its high RAM usage.
**System Requirements**
The app's requirements may not be optimized for lower-end hardware.
> 💡 Insight: Optimizing the app's design and system requirements could reduce RAM usage.
## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Increased lag and slowdowns
- Reduced overall system performance
- Potential for system crashes
## ✨ Conclusion
The Windows 11 Weather app's high RAM usage is a significant issue that needs to be addressed to ensure optimal system performance.
