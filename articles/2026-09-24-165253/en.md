# Nokia’s Design Legacy: A Digital Time Capsule (2025)

Dive into the Nokia Design Archive—a treasure trove of 50+ years of iconic mobile design. Explore raw creativity, cultural shifts, and the future of heritage preservation in tech.

**The Nokia Design Archive** stands as a digital testament to the Finnish brand’s revolutionary impact on mobile technology. From the **1980s’ brick phones** to the **2000s’ sleek Symbian designs**, this archive captures the evolution of form, function, and user experience. It’s not just a collection of devices—it’s a mirror reflecting societal trends, technological breakthroughs, and the human touch behind innovation.
