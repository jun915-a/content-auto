# Revolutionary Frog Bacterium Kills 100% of Tumors in Mice

A groundbreaking study reveals a single dose of a frog-derived gut bacterium eradicates 100% of tumors in mice, sparking hope for a new cancer treatment.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
A single dose of a frog-derived gut bacterium has been shown to eradicate 100% of tumors in mice, paving the way for a potential new cancer treatment.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: A simple, non-invasive treatment shows remarkable promise in eliminating cancerous growths.
- **Point 2**: The bacterium, isolated from the gut of a frog, targets and kills tumor cells without harming healthy tissue.
- **Point 3**: This breakthrough opens doors for further research into the therapeutic potential of gut microbiota.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The study, conducted by a team of scientists, involved administering the bacterium to mice with various types of cancer. The results were astonishing, with 100% of tumors disappearing within a short period.

**Element 2**: The bacterium, known as Enterobacter cloacae, was isolated from the gut of a frog and has been found to have potent anti-tumor properties. It works by targeting and killing cancer cells, without harming healthy tissue.

> 💡 Insight: This study highlights the vast potential of gut microbiota in treating diseases, and opens doors for further research into the therapeutic applications of Enterobacter cloacae.
