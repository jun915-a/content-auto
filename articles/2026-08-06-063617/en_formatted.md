## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Distributed Architecture**
Celld's distributed architecture allows it to scale horizontally, handling increasing amounts of data and traffic without compromising performance. Each node in the cluster stores a portion of the data, ensuring that no single point of failure exists.

**Data Consistency and Replication**
Celld ensures data consistency and replication across the cluster, ensuring that all nodes have the same view of the data. This is achieved through a combination of locking mechanisms and conflict resolution algorithms.

> 💡 Insight: By distributing data storage across multiple nodes, Celld provides a high degree of fault tolerance and scalability, making it an ideal solution for modern applications.
