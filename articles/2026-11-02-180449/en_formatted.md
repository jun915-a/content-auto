## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Enhanced Data Coverage**
The most notable update is the significant expansion of data. Users can now access system call information across a broader spectrum of CPU architectures, moving beyond the initial scope. This makes the platform far more useful for cross-platform development and deeper system analysis.

The addition of a kernel selector allows for precise querying, ensuring users get information relevant to their specific Linux environment. Furthermore, the inclusion of syscall descriptions transforms the site from a mere reference into an educational tool, explaining the 'why' behind each call.
