# Nvidia’s Secret Tactics: How GPU Prices Stayed High

Nvidia allegedly used circular financing to artificially inflate GPU prices, locking consumers into costly cycles. New revelations expose the tech giant’s manipulative strategies—read how they kept profits soaring while gamers and miners suffered.

**Nvidia’s Secret Tactics: How GPU Prices Stayed High**

Nvidia’s dominance in the GPU market comes with a controversial strategy: **circular financing**. This practice, revealed in a recent investigation, allowed the company to **artificially inflate prices** by creating false demand through reseller incentives. By manipulating supply chains and rewarding dealers for high markups, Nvidia ensured GPUs remained **unaffordable for most consumers**—even as demand surged.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Nvidia allegedly used **circular financing schemes**—where dealers were paid to sell GPUs at inflated prices—to **artificially sustain high GPU costs**. This tactic, combined with **supply constraints and aggressive reseller incentives**, created a cycle where prices **never dropped**, despite market saturation. The result? **Consumers paid premiums**, while miners and gamers struggled to secure fair deals.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Circular financing**: Dealers were paid to sell GPUs at inflated prices, **artificially boosting demand**.
- **Supply manipulation**: Nvidia **limited production** while rewarding resellers for high markups, **locking in high prices**.
- **Consumer exploitation**: Gamers and miners faced **no price relief**, despite market oversupply.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Circular Financing Mechanism**
Nvidia’s strategy involved **paying resellers to sell GPUs at inflated prices**, creating a **self-perpetuating cycle**. Dealers were incentivized to **mark up prices aggressively**, knowing Nvidia would **subsidize their profits** through circular financing deals. This **distorted market signals**, making it seem like demand was **stronger than it was**, while **suppressing price drops** even when GPUs were **oversupplied**. The effect? **Consumers paid more** because the system **rewarded high prices** rather than fair competition.

**Supply Chain Constraints**
Nvidia **deliberately limited production** during peak demand periods (e.g., 2021–2022), **constraining supply** while **encouraging dealers to hoard stock**. By **controlling inventory**, the company ensured **shortages persisted**, justifying **premium pricing**. Meanwhile, **miners and scalpers** exploited loopholes, **further driving up costs** for end-users. The result? **A rigged market where only the wealthy could afford high-end GPUs**.

> 💡 **Insight**: Nvidia’s tactics **blurred the line between legitimate business practices and anti-competitive behavior**, raising questions about **fair market pricing** in the tech industry.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact

- **Miners and scalpers** benefited from **artificial shortages**, **exploiting demand** for profit.
