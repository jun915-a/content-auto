# arXiv Bans Hallucinated References

arXiv introduces a new policy, banning users for 1 year if they include hallucinated references in their submissions, aiming to improve academic integrity

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
arXiv's new policy targets fake or non-existent references in research papers, ensuring credibility and trust in academic work.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Strict Penalty**: 1-year ban for offenders
- **Academic Integrity**: Ensuring credible sources
- **Research Impact**: Promoting trustworthy research

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Policy Enforcement**
The policy aims to prevent the spread of misinformation through fake references.

**Submission Guidelines**
Authors must verify the accuracy of their references to avoid penalties.

> 💡 Insight: This move strengthens the academic community's commitment to truthful research.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Improved research reliability
- Enhanced academic credibility
- Increased accountability among authors

## ✨ Conclusion
The new policy reinforces arXiv's dedication to upholding academic standards, fostering a culture of transparency and honesty in research.
