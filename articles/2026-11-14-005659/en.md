# Lonewolf 0.3: Rust-Powered XMPP Server Redefines Privacy & Performance

Meet **Lonewolf 0.3**, a lightweight yet powerful XMPP server built in Rust, offering unmatched speed, security, and self-hosting flexibility. Ideal for privacy-conscious users and developers, this release refines its core with modern optimizations and modularity—revolutionizing decentralized communication.

**Lonewolf 0.3** represents a significant leap in the world of XMPP (Extensible Messaging and Presence Protocol) servers. Built entirely in Rust, this standalone solution prioritizes **performance, security, and ease of deployment**, catering to both individual users and organizations seeking self-hosted communication platforms. With a focus on modularity and minimal dependencies, Lonewolf 0.3 empowers users to control their data while benefiting from a robust, extensible architecture.
