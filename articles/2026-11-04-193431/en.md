# AI’s Hidden Debt: How Legacy Tech Now Pays the Price

AI advancements are exposing decades-old vulnerabilities, forcing developers to confront 40 years of unaddressed technical debt. The PHP2Shell flaw proves how outdated systems now threaten modern security.

**AI is forcing us to pay off 40 years of technical debt**—and the bill is due now. As artificial intelligence reshapes industries, it’s also revealing the cracks in legacy systems, forcing developers to reckon with decades of neglected code and outdated security practices. The recent PHP2Shell vulnerability, which exploited a 16-year-old flaw, underscores how AI’s rapid evolution is exposing long-forgotten weaknesses in foundational technologies. The question isn’t just *how* to fix these issues, but whether we can keep up before the damage spreads further.**
