## The Core of This Topic
Google Books, a massive library of book scans, is offering a $200k bounty for its scans in 2025. This initiative aims to make book access more widespread and promote digital literacy.

## 5-Second Key Points
- **Open Access**: Making books accessible to all
- **Digital Literacy**: Promoting online learning and reading
- **Bounty Initiative**: $200k reward for book scans

## Detailed Breakdown
**Google Books Platform**
Google Books is a digital library that allows users to search and read book excerpts. The platform has an extensive collection of books, including rare and out-of-print titles.

**Bounty Program**
The bounty program is designed to incentivize users to contribute to the platform by uploading book scans. The $200k bounty will be awarded to users who contribute high-quality scans.
