Hubble stands out with its open-source nature, allowing developers and organizations to tailor the app to their specific needs. **Element 2**
The app’s AI-readiness is a game-changer. Hubble is designed to work alongside AI agents, enabling automated note-taking, summarization, and even insights generation.
