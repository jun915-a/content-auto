German, as a dominant language in European academia, played a pivotal role in shaping technical terminology. The German phrase *'sonst wenn'* (literally, 'otherwise if') was translated into English as *'else if'*, a structure that remains foundational in conditional logic today. This translation wasn’t arbitrary—it mirrored existing German programming manuals and textbooks, which themselves drew from pre-existing mathematical and logical frameworks.
