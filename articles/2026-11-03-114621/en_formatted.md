## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
NIST's guide is tailored for self-employed individuals and solopreneurs, recognizing their specific vulnerabilities and limited resources. It provides accessible, practical cybersecurity strategies to help protect their businesses from common online threats.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Understand Threats**: Recognize common cyber risks like phishing and ransomware.
- **Secure Devices**: Implement strong passwords and multi-factor authentication.
- **Protect Data**: Back up important information regularly and encrypt sensitive files.
- **Stay Updated**: Keep software and systems patched to prevent vulnerabilities.
- **Plan for Incidents**: Know how to respond if a security breach occurs.
