# Show HN: Reverse Minesweeper - A Mind-Bending Twist on a Classic

Explore Reverse Minesweeper, a clever twist on the beloved puzzle game. Instead of finding mines, you're tasked with placing them strategically. A true test of logic!

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Reverse Minesweeper flips the classic game on its head. Instead of clearing safe squares to find mines, players are given a grid with numbers indicating how many mines are adjacent to that square. The goal is to strategically place all the mines on the board, satisfying the number clues.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Unique Gameplay**: A fresh take on a familiar concept.
- **Logic Puzzle**: Sharpens deductive reasoning skills.
- **Challenging Levels**: Offers increasing difficulty for engaging play.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Strategic Placement**
The core mechanic involves placing mines. Each number on the board acts as a constraint, telling you exactly how many mines must be in its surrounding cells. This requires careful planning and deduction.

**Deductive Reasoning**
Players must deduce where mines can and cannot be placed. If a cell has a '1' and three adjacent cells are already determined to be safe, the mine must be in one of the remaining adjacent cells.

> 💡 Insight: This game forces you to think backwards, using the revealed information to infer the hidden mine locations.

**Increasing Difficulty**
As you progress, the grids become larger and the number of mines increases, demanding more complex strategies and a deeper understanding of the game's logic.
