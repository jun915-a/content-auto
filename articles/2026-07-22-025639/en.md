# Computable Lets You Buy, Sell, and Redeem GPUs by the Week

A new platform from ex-Jump Trading and Coinbase engineers is turning GPUs into a tradable commodity—like energy markets for AI compute. Here’s how Computable is revolutionizing access to high-performance hardware.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Computable is a marketplace that lets users buy, sell, and redeem GPU compute power by the exact week, just like energy trading. Built by alumni from Jump Trading and Coinbase, it aims to bring liquidity and efficiency to AI hardware markets that currently resemble the pre-2000 energy sector.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Expert-built infrastructure**: Developed by former Jump Trading and Coinbase engineers
- **Weekly GPU trading**: Buy and sell compute power for precise timeframes
- **Marketplace liquidity**: Matches supply and demand to optimize hardware usage
- **AI compute revolution**: Treats GPUs like tradable commodities, not static assets
- **On-demand access**: Redeem GPUs instantly when needed, without long-term commitments

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Computable treats GPUs like energy—tradable, liquid, and accessible on demand. Instead of buying hardware outright or relying on fixed cloud contracts, users can now purchase compute power for specific weeks, much like reserving a generator for peak demand periods. This model mirrors how cloud providers operate but with the flexibility of spot markets.

**Element 2**
The platform’s founders saw parallels between AI compute and energy markets before deregulation. Just as energy trading became more efficient post-2000, Computable aims to bring transparency and liquidity to GPU trading. By enabling short-term leases, it reduces waste (GPUs sitting idle) and lowers costs for users who don’t need permanent hardware.

> 💡 Insight: Computable’s model turns GPUs from depreciating assets into liquid, tradable commodities—just like energy became after market reforms.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- **For AI startups**: Access high-end GPUs without the upfront cost of purchasing hardware
- **For researchers**: Rent compute power for specific experiments or projects, paying only for what they use
- **For cloud providers**: Unlock idle GPU capacity by allowing third-party leases, creating new revenue streams

## ✨ Conclusion
Computable isn’t just another cloud provider—it’s a marketplace redefining how AI compute is bought and sold. By making GPUs tradable like energy, it could democratize access to high-performance hardware and unlock new efficiencies in the AI industry.
