# AI-Powered Search for Every Photo & Video Frame on macOS

Meet **SCM**, an open-source tool that lets you search *every* photo and frame of every video on your Mac using AI. No more digging through clutter—just instant, intelligent results. A game-changer for productivity and content discovery.

**AI-Powered Search for Every Photo & Video Frame on macOS**

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
SCM (Search Content Manager) is an open-source macOS tool that leverages AI to index and search *every* image file and *every frame* of every video on your system. Unlike traditional search tools, it doesn’t just scan metadata—it understands *content* via advanced algorithms, making it possible to find moments, objects, or scenes in seconds.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Instant AI-driven searches**: Find photos/videos by *content*, not just keywords.
- **Frame-level precision**: Search *specific moments* in videos, not just clips.
- **Zero setup**: Works with your existing files—no migration required.
- **Open-source & lightweight**: No cloud dependency, runs locally on macOS.
- **Privacy-first**: Your data stays on your machine, never leaving your device.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1: AI-Powered Content Indexing**
SCM doesn’t rely on file names or tags—it uses AI to analyze visual content. Whether it’s a blurry screenshot or a 4K video, the tool extracts embeddings (numerical representations of visual data) to enable *semantic search*. This means you can ask for "images with blue skies" or "videos featuring my cat" and get results based on actual content, not just keywords. The underlying tech is inspired by modern computer vision models, making it surprisingly accurate for such a lightweight tool.

**Element 2: Frame-by-Frame Video Search**
Most video search tools let you scan clips, but SCM goes further. It breaks videos into *individual frames* and indexes them, allowing you to search for *specific moments* (e.g., "find the frame where the ball hits the ground"). This is particularly useful for editors, researchers, or anyone who needs to locate precise visual details in long-form media. The tool’s efficiency comes from smart sampling—it doesn’t process every frame but intelligently selects keyframes for indexing.
