# Border Phone Searches: Your Privacy at Risk Without a Warrant

Returning from vacation? The government can search your phone at the border without a warrant. Learn about the legal battles and your rights.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
US Customs and Border Protection agents assert the right to search electronic devices like smartphones and laptops at international borders without a warrant, citing national security. This practice clashes with privacy advocates who argue it violates the Fourth Amendment.
