- **Point 1**: Identify unnecessary intermediaries
- **Point 2**: Establish direct connections
- **Point 3**: Add value to interactions
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: Direct communication helps in building trust and understanding among individuals.
**Element 2**: Removing intermediaries can simplify processes and reduce misunderstandings.

- Improved communication
- Increased efficiency
