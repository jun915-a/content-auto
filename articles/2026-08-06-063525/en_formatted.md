Blade Runner's title cards were designed by Dan Perri, a renowned title designer. The cards are a masterful blend of visuals, sound, and narrative that sets the tone for the entire film.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Design of the Title Cards**
The title cards were designed to be a visual representation of the film's themes and tone. Perri's use of typography, color, and composition created a sense of depth and complexity that drew the viewer in.

**The Sound Design**
The title cards also featured a unique sound design that added to the overall sense of atmosphere. The use of sounds like the hum of the city and the whir of machinery created a sense of immersion that was unmatched at the time.
