# Ito: Lua-Powered Terminal UI Revolution for Neovim Enthusiasts

Neovim’s terminal UI customization just got a Lua-powered upgrade. Meet Ito—a lightweight framework for building and styling terminal interfaces, letting you rice your terminal like never before.

**Neovim users, listen up.** Your obsession with terminal customization just found a new playground. Ito is a Lua-based framework designed to let you craft and style terminal UIs with the same precision you apply to your editor. Whether you’re tweaking colors, layouts, or widgets, Ito gives you the freedom to turn your terminal into a masterpiece—without sacrificing functionality. Let’s dive into why this tool is a game-changer for terminal nerds like you and your friend.
