# Hormuz Strait Simulation: Visualizing Global Oil Trade

A Columbia University professor's supply chain exercise turns into a powerful tool for simulating oil trade disruptions on real data

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
This visualization tool simulates a closure of the Strait of Hormuz, one of the world's most critical oil trade routes, on real oil trade data. The project started as a pedagogical exercise for a supply chain class at Columbia University but evolved into a comprehensive visualization and research paper on global oil trade.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Visualize the impact of a Hormuz Strait closure on global oil trade.
- **Point 2**: Real oil trade data informs the simulation for accuracy.
- **Point 3**: A Columbia University professor's supply chain class led to the creation of this tool.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Simulating Oil Trade Disruptions**: The tool allows users to explore the consequences of a Hormuz Strait closure by visualizing the flow of oil through major trade routes. This helps to understand the global implications of such an event.
**Real-World Data**: The simulation relies on actual oil trade data, ensuring the accuracy of the results. This data also provides valuable insights into the complexities of global oil trade.
> 💡 Insight: The simulation highlights the interconnectedness of global oil trade and the potential far-reaching consequences of a Hormuz Strait closure.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
