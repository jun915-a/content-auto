## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Mullvad is ending its public encrypted DNS service to focus resources on its core VPN offering. They are now sponsoring Quad9, a reputable privacy-focused DNS provider, to ensure continued support for encrypted DNS queries and improve overall service quality.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **DNS Service Ending**: Mullvad's own public encrypted DNS servers will be shut down.
- **Quad9 Sponsorship**: Mullvad is now sponsoring Quad9.
- **Enhanced Privacy**: This move aims to provide better, more reliable encrypted DNS services.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Transitioning DNS Services**
Mullvad recognized that maintaining its own DNS infrastructure was resource-intensive and could be better served by a specialized provider. By partnering with Quad9, they can leverage a globally distributed, high-performance network.

**Focusing on VPN Core**
This strategic shift allows Mullvad to dedicate more development and operational efforts towards strengthening its VPN services, which remain its primary focus for user privacy and security.

> 💡 Insight: By consolidating efforts and partnering with experts, Mullvad aims to deliver a superior and more sustainable encrypted DNS experience.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Users can switch to Quad9's servers for continued encrypted DNS resolution.

Mullvad's decision to sponsor Quad9 ensures users still benefit from robust, private encrypted DNS services while the company sharpens its focus on its core VPN offering.
