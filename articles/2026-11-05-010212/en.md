# Is Curiosity a Superpower or a Trap?

Curiosity drives discovery but can also fuel anxiety—Adam Phillips’ sharp analysis in *The London Review of Books* dissects its dual nature: a force for growth or a burden of endless questioning. Is it humanity’s greatest asset—or its most exhausting flaw?

The goal isn’t to suppress it but to **harness its power without surrendering to its pitfalls**. Phillips’ insight lingers: the most fulfilling lives aren’t those defined by endless questioning, but by the **courage to ask—and then to act**. In a world drowning in information, the true test of curiosity isn’t how many questions we can ask, but how many we can answer *with purpose*.\n"
