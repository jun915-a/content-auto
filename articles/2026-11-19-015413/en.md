# ExitPath: Your Open-Source Escape Route from Slack, Notion & Microsoft 365

Tired of vendor lock-in? ExitPath offers a free, open-source way to migrate data from Slack, Notion, and Microsoft 365—without losing productivity or control.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
ExitPath is an open-source tool designed to help users break free from proprietary platforms like **Slack, Notion, and Microsoft 365** without losing access to their data or workflows. It bridges the gap between these closed ecosystems and self-hosted alternatives, empowering users to regain autonomy over their digital tools and data.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Open-source freedom**: No hidden costs or vendor restrictions
- **Data migration**: Seamlessly export chats, notes, and documents
- **Self-hosted flexibility**: Integrate with alternatives like Mattermost, Obsidian, or Nextcloud
- **Privacy-first**: Avoid data silos and corporate surveillance
- **Developer-friendly**: Contribute or customize the tool yourself

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Why Vendor Lock-In Matters**
Many teams rely on Slack for communication, Notion for note-taking, and Microsoft 365 for productivity—but these platforms lock users into their ecosystems. ExitPath dismantles this by providing scripts and APIs to extract and reformat data into open standards. This isn’t just about moving data; it’s about **reclaiming control** over your digital life.

**How It Works**
ExitPath leverages automation and open protocols to pull structured data from proprietary APIs. For example, Slack messages can be converted into **Markdown or JSON**, while Notion pages might export as **Obsidian-compatible files**. The tool acts as a middleman, ensuring compatibility with self-hosted alternatives. This reduces reliance on cloud services and lowers long-term costs.

> 💡 Insight: **The real cost of proprietary tools isn’t just subscription fees—it’s the inability to pivot or innovate without breaking workflows.**
