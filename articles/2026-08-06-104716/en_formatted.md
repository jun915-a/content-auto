## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Quantego is an open-source project created by IBM Quantum that uses Lego bricks to build accurate models of their quantum computers. This unique approach aims to make quantum computing more accessible and engaging, especially for students and educators.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The Quantego project allows users to build and customize their own Lego models of IBM quantum computers, from the IBM Quantum System One to the IBM Q System One. This hands-on experience enables a deeper understanding of the underlying architecture and technology.

**Element 2**
The project's open-source nature encourages collaboration and community involvement, with users sharing their creations and ideas on the Quantego website. This fosters a sense of community and drives innovation in the field of quantum computing.
