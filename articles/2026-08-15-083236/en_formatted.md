## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Firefox is now the last major browser that still supports uBlock Origin, a popular ad blocker known for its effectiveness and customization options. It blocks ads, trackers, and malware, improving browsing experience and security.

- **Point 1**: uBlock Origin is highly customizable
- **Point 2**: It blocks ads, trackers, and malware
- **Point 3**: Firefox is the last major browser to support it
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: uBlock Origin's ability to block a wide range of content, including ads and trackers, has made it a favorite among users looking to enhance their online privacy.
**Element 2**: The blocker also has features like dynamic filtering and a comprehensive filter list, making it highly effective against various types of unwanted content.
