# Fighting Scrapers

A website owner shares their experience fighting scrapers on a 1.5 million-page site, with 99% of traffic being bots

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The core issue is the overwhelming traffic from bots, affecting the website's performance. 
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: 99% of traffic is bots
- **Point 2**: Performance issues arise
- **Point 3**: Security concerns
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The website owner has to constantly monitor traffic patterns to identify and block scrapers.
**Element 2**: Implementing security measures such as CAPTCHAs and IP blocking is crucial.
> 💡 Insight: Understanding the motivations of scrapers is key to developing effective countermeasures.
## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Increased server costs
- Decreased user experience
- Potential data breaches
## ✨ Conclusion
The fight against scrapers is ongoing, and website owners must stay vigilant to protect their sites.
