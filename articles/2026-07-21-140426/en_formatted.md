## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Ternary numbers use three distinct symbols, often 0, 1, and 2, to represent values. This system can be more efficient in certain contexts than binary.
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Ternary is base-3, offering a compact way to represent numbers.
- **Point 2**: Each digit in ternary can have three values: 0, 1, or 2.
- **Point 3**: Converting ternary to binary or decimal requires understanding the base-3 system.
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
