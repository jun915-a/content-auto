Major rail disruption in the Netherlands is suspected to be caused by sabotage, affecting thousands of commuters and cargo. Authorities are investigating the incident.

Suspected sabotage has crippled large parts of the Dutch railway network, leading to widespread cancellations and delays. The incident is under intense investigation by authorities, with significant implications for passenger and freight transport.
