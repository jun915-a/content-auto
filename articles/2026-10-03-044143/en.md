# Lean Proofs: A Software Engineer’s Guide

Discover how Lean proofs revolutionize software logic—from formal rigor to practical debugging. Unpack the anatomy of proofs that scale with code, not just math.

**Lean proofs** are reshaping how software engineers think about correctness, blending formal logic with intuitive reasoning. Unlike traditional proofs, Lean’s approach is **interactive, composable, and scalable**—making it a game-changer for verifying complex systems. Here’s how it works and why it matters for developers.
