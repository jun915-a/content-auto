# Phyllotaxis: Nature's Spiral Meets Interactive Light Art

Explore Phyllotaxis, an audio-reactive LED display mimicking plant growth patterns. Discover how math, nature, and technology merge to create stunning visual experiences.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Phyllotaxis is an audio-reactive LED display inspired by the natural arrangement of leaves on a plant stem. It uses mathematical principles, specifically the Fibonacci sequence and the golden angle, to generate mesmerizing spiral patterns that respond dynamically to sound.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Nature's Algorithm**: Mimics the efficient spiral growth patterns found in plants.
- **Audio Reactivity**: LED patterns shift and pulse in sync with ambient sound.
- **Art Meets Tech**: Blends mathematical beauty with modern LED technology for visual art.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Fibonacci Sequence and Golden Angle**
The core of Phyllotaxis lies in using the Fibonacci sequence (1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8...) to determine the placement and spacing of LEDs. This sequence is then applied with the golden angle (~137.5 degrees) to create natural-looking spirals.

**LED Matrix and Control**
A custom-designed LED matrix forms the canvas. Each LED's brightness and color are controlled by a microcontroller, which processes audio input and translates it into visual changes following the phyllotactic pattern.
