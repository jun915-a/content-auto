## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The PD-1 Pathway**
Researchers focused on the PD-1 pathway, a known player in immune evasion by cancer cells. By manipulating specific components of this pathway within pancreatic tumours, they observed a significant impact on tumour growth and spread.

**Immune System Activation**
This manipulation appears to reawaken the patient's own immune system, enabling it to recognize and attack cancer cells more effectively. This suggests a way to overcome the tumour's defenses.

> 💡 Insight: Re-engaging the immune system is key to effectively combating pancreatic cancer.
