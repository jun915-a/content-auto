**The Art of Line Scan Photography**
Line scan photos are a specialized technique that converts analog images into digital form by scanning them line by line. For MUNI’s Heritage Weekend, these images serve as a **digital time capsule**, immortalizing the city’s transit history with striking clarity. The high-contrast aesthetic of line scans—where grayscale tones and shadows create a timeless, almost cinematic feel—makes them perfect for showcasing vintage vehicles like the **Muni cable cars** and **retro buses**, which are often the stars of the event.
