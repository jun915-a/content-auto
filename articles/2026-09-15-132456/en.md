## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Paxos & Raft**: These algorithms address the crucial problem of achieving consensus in a distributed environment, ensuring all nodes agree on a single value despite failures.

**CAP Theorem**: This fundamental theorem states that a distributed data store cannot simultaneously provide Consistency, Availability, and Partition tolerance. You must choose two out of three.

> 💡 Insight: Understanding trade-offs is paramount in designing robust distributed systems.

**Byzantine Fault Tolerance**: Explores how systems can operate correctly even when some nodes behave maliciously or arbitrarily.

**Distributed Transactions**: Covers methods like two-phase commit (2PC) for ensuring atomicity across multiple distributed resources.

> 💡 Insight: Achieving reliability in the face of failures is a central challenge.
