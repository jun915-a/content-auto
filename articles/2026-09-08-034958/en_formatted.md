## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Einstein’s theory of general relativity describes gravity as the curvature of spacetime caused by mass and energy. However, quantum mechanics governs the behavior of particles at the smallest scales. For over a century, reconciling these two frameworks has been a monumental challenge. Recently, researchers at Oxford made a groundbreaking observation: **Einstein’s gravity appears to influence quantum systems**, validating predictions that gravity’s effects extend even into the quantum world.
