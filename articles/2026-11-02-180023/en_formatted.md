# HackerBooks: Discover Books Mentioned on Hacker News

*Insert header image here*

Explore HackerBooks, a curated collection of ~16.5k books frequently discussed by Hacker News commenters. Built with Claude, it's your go-to for discovering popular and recommended reads.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
HackerBooks is a project born from the idea of HackerTube, but for books. It aggregates and presents books that have been mentioned by at least two different commenters on Hacker News, spanning from 2007 to the present.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Curated Collection**: Features ~16.5k books mentioned on Hacker News.
- **Community Driven**: Based on discussions and recommendations from HN users.
- **AI Powered**: Developed with the assistance of Claude (Opus 5.5).

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Data Source**
The project scrapes discussions from Hacker News, identifying books that appear multiple times across different comment threads. This ensures a level of community validation for each book listed.

**AI Assistance**
Claude, specifically Opus 5.5, was instrumental in building the backend infrastructure and processing the vast amount of data required to compile this extensive book list.

> 💡 Insight: Leveraging AI for data aggregation and analysis can unlock unique insights from community-driven platforms like Hacker News.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Helps users discover highly-recommended books within the tech and intellectual sphere.
- Provides a searchable archive of literary discussions from a prominent online community.
- Offers a novel way to gauge popular and influential reading material based on community consensus.

## ✨ Conclusion
HackerBooks offers a fascinating glimpse into the reading habits and recommendations of the Hacker News community, powered by cutting-edge AI. Dive in and find your next great read!
