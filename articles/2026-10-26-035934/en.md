# Overgrown: How Plants Conquer Your Abandoned Browser Tabs

Ever left a browser tab open, only to return and find it overrun by forgotten pages, notifications, and clutter? Meet **Overgrown**, a clever Chrome extension that turns your abandoned tabs into a thriving digital garden—where plants grow from your neglected sessions. Discover how this playful tool transforms chaos into creativity and why it’s a must-try for productivity and nostalgia.

**Overgrown: When plants invade your abandoned tabs** is a whimsical yet functional Chrome extension that transforms your browser’s forgotten tabs into a **living, breathing ecosystem**. By visualizing your inactive tabs as overgrown plants, Overgrown turns digital clutter into a charming, interactive experience—blending productivity with playful design. It’s a reminder to revisit what you’ve left behind, all while adding a touch of nature to your digital life.
