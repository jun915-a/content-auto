Payments Canada unveils the Real-Time Rail (RTR) system, a 24/7 payment infrastructure set to modernize Canada's economy and enhance financial transactions nationwide.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Canada's Real-Time Rail (RTR) is a new, modern payment system designed to process transactions instantly, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. This infrastructure aims to significantly enhance the speed, efficiency, and security of payments across the nation.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Instant Payments**: Transactions processed in real-time.
- **24/7 Availability**: Operates continuously, including holidays.
- **Economic Modernization**: Boosts efficiency and innovation.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Enhanced Speed and Efficiency**
The RTR system enables immediate fund transfers, dramatically reducing the time it takes for payments to clear. This boosts efficiency for businesses and consumers alike, enabling faster access to funds.

**Increased Security and Resilience**
Built with advanced security features, RTR ensures a robust and reliable payment network. It's designed to handle high volumes of transactions securely, minimizing risks.
