## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Xiaomi Mimo 2.6 post-training dashboard is a sophisticated interface designed to provide comprehensive analytics and visualization tools after model training. It empowers developers to understand model behavior, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions for future iterations.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Performance Metrics**: Quickly grasp key evaluation scores.
- **Error Analysis**: Pinpoint common failure points.
- **Visualization Tools**: See model predictions and ground truth.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Performance Metrics**
This section offers a clear overview of your model's success. Key metrics like accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score are presented in an easily digestible format, allowing for rapid assessment of overall performance against established benchmarks.

**Error Analysis**
Dive deeper into where your model struggles. Visualizations and categorized error reports help identify specific types of mistakes, such as misclassifications of certain classes or common patterns in incorrect predictions, guiding targeted improvements.

> 💡 Insight: Understanding error patterns is crucial for effective model refinement.

**Visualization Tools**
Interact with your model's outputs. Tools like confusion matrices, ROC curves, and sample prediction visualizations allow you to compare model predictions with actual outcomes, offering a granular view of its decision-making process.

> 💡 Insight: Visualizing predictions aids in identifying subtle biases or weaknesses.
