## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The web is constantly evolving, with new websites and technologies emerging every day. However, this evolution comes at a cost, as older websites and technologies are often left behind, forgotten, and eventually deleted. This phenomenon is known as the 'dead web,' and it represents a significant loss of cultural and historical heritage.
