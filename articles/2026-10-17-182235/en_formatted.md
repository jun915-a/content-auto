## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Furifont is a **free, open-source Japanese font** designed to display **furigana**—small kana characters placed above or beside kanji to indicate pronunciation—**natively within the font itself**. Unlike traditional methods requiring separate overlays or plugins, Furifont embeds furigana directly into glyphs, ensuring fluid readability for both native speakers and learners. It’s a game-changer for digital Japanese typography, blending aesthetics with functionality.

**Element 1**
Furifont’s **innovative design** solves a long-standing pain point for Japanese typography: furigana often appeared as separate layers or required manual input, disrupting the visual flow. By integrating furigana **directly into kanji glyphs**, Furifont maintains a clean, unified look while ensuring accessibility. This approach is particularly useful for **digital content**, where layered text can cause alignment issues or readability problems. The font’s **smooth, modern aesthetic** also makes it ideal for branding, social media, and educational materials.

**Element 2**
