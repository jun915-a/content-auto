# Hister: Your Private Search Engine for Web & Files

Discover Hister, an open-source search tool that indexes your browser history, saved files, and notes—without tracking or ads. Built for privacy-conscious users who want seamless, local search across their digital life.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Hister is a **privacy-first search engine** designed to index your personal data—browser history, downloaded files, notes, and more—**entirely offline and locally**. Unlike cloud-based search tools, Hister never sends your data to third parties, ensuring full control over your digital footprint. It’s a lightweight alternative for those tired of relying on centralized search engines that prioritize ads and tracking over user privacy.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Local-first indexing**: Scans your browser history, files, and notes **without syncing to the cloud**.
- **Zero-tracking**: No telemetry, ads, or data leaks—your search queries stay private.
- **Cross-platform**: Works with Chrome, Firefox, and other browsers (via extensions) and supports file systems like Dropbox.
- **Open-source**: Transparent codebase means you can audit its security and functionality.
- **Simple setup**: Install via GitHub, configure paths, and start searching instantly.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Local Privacy Over Cloud Dependencies**
Hister eliminates the need for cloud-based search engines by running entirely on your machine. It indexes your **browser history (via extensions)**, **downloaded files**, and **local notes** (e.g., Markdown or text files) into a searchable database. This means no sensitive data leaves your device, protecting you from potential breaches or surveillance. The tool uses a **simple yet effective indexing algorithm** to quickly retrieve results, making it feel snappy despite its lightweight nature.

> 💡 Insight: *Unlike Google or Bing, Hister doesn’t learn from your queries—it’s purely a tool for personal organization, not a data-mining platform.*

**Cross-Platform Flexibility**
Hister supports multiple environments to maximize usability:
- **Browser extensions** for Chrome, Firefox, and Edge (indexes history and bookmarks).
- **File system scanning** (supports local drives, Dropbox, and other cloud storage via symlinks).
- **Customizable configuration**—you define which folders/files to include, ensuring irrelevant data stays out of the index.

**Open-Source Trust**
As an open-source project, Hister’s code is **fully auditable** by anyone. This transparency is critical for privacy tools, as it allows users to verify that no backdoors or hidden data collection exists. The project is maintained by [asciimoo](https://github.com/asciimoo), a developer known for privacy-focused tools like [Privacy Respecting Tools](https://github.com/asciimoo/privacy-respecting-tools).
