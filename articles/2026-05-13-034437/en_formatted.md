Cactus announces the open-sourcing of Needle, a groundbreaking 26M parameter function-calling model, achieving remarkable speeds on consumer devices.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Needle is a distilled version of the Gemini tool calling model, focusing on achieving high performance on consumer devices. By condensing the model's parameters from 13B to 26M, the development team at Cactus has made significant strides in making this technology accessible to a broader audience.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Achieves 6000 tokens per second (tok/s) prefill and 1200 tok/s decode on consumer devices.
- **Point 2**: Open-sourced on GitHub, making the technology more accessible to the community.
- **Point 3**: A potential game-changer for various applications, including AI-assisted tool use.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The development of Needle was motivated by the need to improve the accessibility of Gemini-like models. By reducing the number of parameters, the team at Cactus aimed to create a more efficient and scalable solution.

**Element 2**: The impressive performance of Needle on consumer devices is a testament to the team's expertise and innovative approach. This achievement has the potential to open up new possibilities for various applications.
