## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Riemann zeta function is a complex function that plays a central role in number theory, particularly in the study of prime numbers. It is defined as the infinite series 1 + 1/2^s + 1/3^s + 1/4^s + ..., which converges for Re(s) > 1.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: The Riemann zeta function is closely related to the distribution of prime numbers.
- **Point 2**: The Riemann Hypothesis, a conjecture about the zeta function, has important implications for number theory.
- **Point 3**: The prime number theorem, which describes the distribution of prime numbers, can be proved using the zeta function.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The zeta function is used to study the distribution of prime numbers through the use of Dirichlet series. By analyzing the properties of the zeta function, mathematicians can gain insights into the behavior of prime numbers.

**Element 2**
The Riemann Hypothesis, a conjecture about the zeta function, states that all non-trivial zeros of the zeta function lie on a vertical line in the complex plane. Proving this conjecture has important implications for number theory and cryptography.
