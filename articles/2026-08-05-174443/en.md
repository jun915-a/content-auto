# Erdős Problems Yield to AI as Humans Step Back

Erdős problems, once considered the pinnacle of human ingenuity, are now being solved by AI, raising questions about the future of mathematics.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Erdős problems are a collection of 23 mathematical problems posed by Paul Erdős, a Hungarian mathematician. These problems were considered some of the most challenging in mathematics, and solving them was seen as a badge of honor for mathematicians. However, with the advent of AI, these problems are now being solved by machines, leaving humans to wonder if their role in mathematics is diminishing.
