## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1: How It Works
Whistle Synth operates by **processing audio input** through a **frequency-tracking algorithm**. When you speak, hum, or play an instrument into the microphone, the app isolates the dominant frequency and converts it into a sustained whistle tone. This isn’t just a simple pitch-shifting tool—it **strips away complexity**, leaving behind a pure, resonant note that can be layered or manipulated further. The simplicity of its design makes it **accessible yet powerful**, appealing to both beginners and seasoned sound artists.

**Element 2: Key Features & Flexibility
The app’s charm lies in its **minimalist interface**, which belies its versatility. You can:
- **Adjust the whistle’s octave** to fit your desired range, from sub-bass rumbles to high-pitched squeals.
- **Control decay and sustain** to shape the tone’s envelope, adding warmth or sharpness as needed.
- **Route the output** to your system audio or directly to a DAW (Digital Audio Workstation) via Audio Unit (AU) support.
