**Element 2**

For example, Epic’s launcher may take longer to **render game previews** or load library data, creating frustration during peak usage. Meanwhile, Steam’s minimalist approach ensures **instantaneous feedback**, a critical factor for competitive or casual gamers alike.

> 💡 Insight: **Performance should never be an afterthought**—even the most visually impressive tools fail if they don’t deliver speed and reliability.
