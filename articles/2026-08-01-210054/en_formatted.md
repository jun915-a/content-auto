# 30-Minute Pressure Cooker Pho Ga

Experience the authentic flavors of Vietnamese chicken noodle soup with a twist - a 30-minute pressure cooker recipe that's quick, easy, and aromatic.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Pho Ga is a popular Vietnamese chicken noodle soup, but traditional recipes can take hours to prepare. This pressure cooker recipe cuts the cooking time in half, making it possible to enjoy this authentic dish in just 30 minutes.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Quickly cook chicken and noodles in a flavorful broth.
- **Point 2**: Pressure cooker reduces cooking time and makes prep work a breeze.
- **Point 3**: Serve with fresh herbs and chili sauce for added flavor.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Chicken and Noodles**
This recipe starts with boneless, skinless chicken breast or thighs, which are quickly browned in a pan before being added to the pressure cooker. The chicken cooks in just 5 minutes, and the noodles follow suit, soaking up the rich broth in under 10 minutes.

**Broth and Seasoning**
The broth is the heart of Pho Ga, and this recipe uses a combination of chicken stock, fish sauce, and soy sauce to create a rich, savory flavor. Aromatics like onion, ginger, and cilantro add depth and warmth to the broth.

> 💡 Insight: The key to a great Pho Ga is using high-quality ingredients and allowing the flavors to meld together.
