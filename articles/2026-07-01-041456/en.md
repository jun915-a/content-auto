A 13-year-old's passion project becomes a reality, offering a unique platform for kids to explore and learn about ant colonies.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Teenagers are often encouraged to pursue STEM fields from a young age, but it's not every day that we see one create something as innovative as an ant colony tracker. Formicarium, a web application built by a 13-year-old, has been gaining attention on social media and scientific communities alike for its potential to revolutionize the way we study and interact with ants.
