## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The pull-back car is a classic toy that uses a simple mechanism to create the illusion of speed. It consists of a motor, a spring, and a wheel. When you pull the car back, you store energy in the spring, which is then released when you let go, propelling the car forward.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
* **Point 1**: The car uses a combination of springs and motors to achieve motion.
* **Point 2**: The motion is created by storing energy in the spring and releasing it.
* **Point 3**: The wheel is connected to the motor, allowing it to turn.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Motor**
The motor in a pull-back car is typically a small electric motor that is connected to the wheel. When you pull the car back, the motor is wound up, storing energy in the spring. When you let go, the motor releases this energy, turning the wheel and propelling the car forward.

**Spring**
The spring is a critical component of the pull-back car's mechanism. It stores energy when you pull the car back, and then releases it when you let go. The spring is typically wound up by a small gear or mechanism that is connected to the motor.
