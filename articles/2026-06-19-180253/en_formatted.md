# Hyundai Acquires Boston Dynamics, Atlas Robots for Auto Plants

Hyundai Motor Group has fully acquired Boston Dynamics from SoftBank for $325 million. The advanced Atlas humanoid robots are slated for deployment in Hyundai's vehicle plants by 2028.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Hydraulic Motor Group has finalized its acquisition of Boston Dynamics, gaining full ownership after a previous stake. This strategic move integrates advanced robotics, particularly the sophisticated Atlas humanoid, into Hyundai's automotive manufacturing processes, aiming for enhanced automation by 2028.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Full Acquisition**: Hyundai now fully owns Boston Dynamics.
- **Robotic Integration**: Atlas robots will be utilized in Hyundai's factories.
- **Future Automation**: Goal is to deploy robots by 2028.

**Hyundai's Strategic Investment**
Hydraulic Motor Group's complete takeover of Boston Dynamics signifies a major commitment to integrating cutting-edge robotics into its industrial operations. This acquisition aims to leverage Boston Dynamics' expertise in advanced mobility and manipulation for practical applications.

**Atlas Humanoid Robots in Manufacturing**
The focus is on deploying the highly capable Atlas humanoid robots within Hyundai's vehicle production lines. These robots are expected to perform complex tasks, potentially improving efficiency, safety, and precision in assembly processes.
