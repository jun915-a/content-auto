## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Let's Seal is an open-source, self-hosted document signing solution that puts the power back in your hands. By leveraging the Let's Encrypt technology, this innovative tool offers free and secure document signing without the need for Adobe's Approved Trust List or costly third-party services.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Self-hosted document signing for free.
- **Point 2**: Open-source and community-driven.
- **Point 3**: No Adobe AATL dependency.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
Let's Seal's core functionality is built on top of Let's Encrypt's trusted certificate issuance. This allows for secure document signing without the need for intermediaries like Adobe or Google. By hosting the solution yourself, you maintain full control over your data and signing processes.

The tool's open-source nature fosters a community-driven development process, ensuring that the solution stays up-to-date with the latest security standards and best practices.

- Democratization of secure document signing.

With Let's Seal, you can break free from the shackles of costly doc signing tools and Adobe's grip on the industry. This self-hosted, open-source solution empowers you to take control of your document signing needs, ensuring secure and efficient processes for all your signing requirements.
