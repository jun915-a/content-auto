# Paying for Pre-Modern Armies: The Financial Realities

Discover how pre-modern armies were funded, and uncover the financial realities that shaped their strength and longevity.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Pre-modern armies were a significant burden on their respective societies, requiring substantial financial investments to maintain. The cost of maintaining an army was a major consideration for rulers, often influencing their military strategies and the strength of their forces.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Armies were often a drain on local economies, leading to tax hikes and economic instability.
- **Point 2**: Mercenaries were a common solution for rulers without large standing armies, but their loyalty was always in question.
- **Point 3**: The cost of maintaining an army influenced the size and strength of pre-modern forces.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Manpower Costs**: Maintaining a large army required a significant number of soldiers, which in turn demanded a substantial amount of food, shelter, and other supplies. This placed a heavy burden on local economies, often leading to tax hikes and economic instability.

**Taxes and Tribute**: To fund their armies, rulers often imposed heavy taxes on their subjects. This could include direct taxes, such as the poll tax, or indirect taxes, like the salt tax. Tributes from conquered territories or vassal states were also a common source of revenue.

> 💡 Insight: The financial realities of pre-modern armies often led to a delicate balance between military strength and economic stability.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- The financial constraints of pre-modern armies influenced the development of military strategies, such as the use of mercenaries and the focus on naval power.
- The cost of maintaining an army contributed to the rise of centralized states, as rulers sought to consolidate power and resources.
- The financial burden of pre-modern armies also shaped the social and cultural landscape of pre-modern societies.

## ✨ Conclusion
Understanding the financial realities of pre-modern armies is essential for worldbuilders seeking to create authentic and immersive settings. By acknowledging the costs and challenges associated with maintaining a pre-modern army, you can create more nuanced and realistic depictions of military power and its impact on society.
