# Born Against: The Resistance to LLM Usage in Hobby Programming Communities

Discover why hobby programmers are increasingly rejecting the use of Large Language Models (LLMs) in their projects, and what it means for the future of coding.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Hobby programming communities are rallying against the use of Large Language Models (LLMs) in their projects, citing concerns about ownership, control, and creativity.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: LLMs undermine the value of human coding skills.
- **Point 2**: They stifle innovation and originality in programming.
- **Point 3**: There are concerns about data privacy and security.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The resistance to LLMs is not just about technology; it's about the values and principles that underpin the hobby programming community. Programmers value the process of writing code, learning from mistakes, and creating something from scratch.

**Element 2**
LLMs, on the other hand, represent a shift towards a more 'black-box' approach to programming, where the focus is on inputting data and getting a pre-defined output. This reduces the creative element and makes programming feel more like a formulaic exercise.

> 💡 Insight: The adoption of LLMs in hobby programming would erode the sense of accomplishment and pride that comes from creating something with one's own hands.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- The resistance to LLMs may lead to the development of alternative, more community-driven approaches to programming.
- It could also result in a shift towards more specialized and niche programming communities.
- Ultimately, it may force the LLM industry to re-examine its business model and consider the impact on hobby programmers.

## ✨ Conclusion
The 'Born Against' movement is a call to action for hobby programmers to reclaim their craft and resist the homogenizing influence of LLMs. It's a reminder that programming is not just about getting a job done, but about the joy, creativity, and sense of accomplishment that comes from creating something from scratch.
