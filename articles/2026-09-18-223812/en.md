## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
A **graph sandwich** is a structure that lies *between* two graphs—one that *contains* all possible paths (a *containment graph*) and another that *avoids* certain substructures (an *exclusion graph*). For decades, mathematicians debated whether such a structure could exist. Now, they’ve proven it does.
