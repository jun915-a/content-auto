# HackerRank Open Sources ATS

HackerRank's open-sourced ATS scores resumes, sparking curiosity and debate among job seekers and recruiters alike, with scores ranging from 74 to 90

## The Core of This Topic
HackerRank has made its Applicant Tracking System (ATS) open source, allowing users to score their resumes and gain insight into the recruitment process.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Resume scoring system
- **Point 2**: Open source for transparency
- **Point 3**: Variable score results
## Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The ATS uses algorithms to evaluate resumes, providing users with a percentage score, such as 90, 74, or 88, indicating their suitability for a role.
**Element 2**: This system aims to streamline the hiring process and reduce bias, but its accuracy and effectiveness are still being debated.
> Insight: The open-sourced ATS provides a unique opportunity for users to understand how their resumes are evaluated.
## Real-World Impact
- Improved resume writing
- Enhanced recruitment efficiency
- Increased transparency in hiring decisions
## Conclusion
The open-sourced ATS is a significant development in the recruitment industry, offering a glimpse into the inner workings of resume evaluation and sparking important discussions about the role of technology in hiring.
