## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Lost Reef**
For years, scientists believed the coral reefs in the Gulf of Guinea, particularly off Benin, were devastated by destructive fishing practices, including bottom trawling. The area was considered a marine dead zone.

**Unexpected Survival**
Recent surveys using advanced technology revealed a surprisingly healthy and diverse coral reef system. This discovery challenges previous assumptions about the region's marine ecosystem and highlights its potential for resilience.

> 💡 Insight: The reef's survival suggests that some marine ecosystems may be more robust than previously thought, offering valuable lessons for conservation.
