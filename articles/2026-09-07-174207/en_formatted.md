## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Real-Time Tracking & Alerts**
The platform’s **live map** updates every few seconds, showing delays, cancellations, and alternative routes. Users receive **push notifications** for disruptions, ensuring they stay ahead of schedule. This transparency is especially valuable in cities like Brussels, where tram delays can disrupt entire commutes. The system integrates **weather data**, too—helping riders avoid flooded bus stops or icy train platforms.

**Multi-Modal Integration**
Belgium’s transport network thrives on **interconnections**. Openbaarvervoerbelgie.be lets users combine **trains (NMBS/SNCB) with local buses or trams**, even adding bike rentals via **De Fiets** or **STIB’s bike-sharing**. For example, a trip from Ghent to Antwerp can start with a train, continue by tram, and end with a bike ride—all planned in one go. This **seamless flow** reduces stress and carbon footprints.

> 💡 Insight: **The platform’s ‘Go’ button** simplifies navigation by suggesting the fastest route, even accounting for traffic or construction. It’s a game-changer for first-time visitors who might otherwise get lost in Belgium’s labyrinthine cities.

**Accessibility & Inclusivity**
Belgium prioritizes **inclusive mobility**. The site highlights **wheelchair-accessible stops**, priority seating, and **audio announcements** for visually impaired users. For example, Brussels’ **STIB trams** have tactile paving and priority boarding zones—details clearly marked on the app. This commitment extends to **real-time alerts** for delayed lifts or unavailable ramps, ensuring no one is left stranded.
