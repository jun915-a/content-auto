## 📈 Detailed Breakdown

**NF-kB's Role in Aging:** This protein is a transcription factor, regulating gene expression. It's known for its role in immune response, but its impact on aging is less understood. Recent studies suggest it accelerates aging by promoting cellular senescence.

**Aging Without Inflammation:** Typically, aging is accompanied by chronic inflammation. However, NF-kB's activity seems to induce aging without this inflammatory response. This unique characteristic makes it an intriguing target for research.
