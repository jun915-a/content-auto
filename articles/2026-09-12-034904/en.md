# Claude’s Age Restriction: Why It Matters & What It Means

Claude AI enforces an 18+ age policy to ensure responsible use. Learn why this rule exists, how it’s enforced, and what it means for users—plus the real-world implications of this safeguard.

**Claude’s age assurance policy** is a key feature designed to protect users and maintain ethical AI standards. While Claude is a powerful tool for creativity, research, and problem-solving, its availability is restricted to users aged 18 and older. This isn’t just a technicality—it’s a deliberate measure to balance innovation with accountability.**
