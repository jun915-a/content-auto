## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**AI Object Removal and Cleanup**
This powerful new tool leverages AI to intelligently identify and remove unwanted elements from footage. Whether it's a stray boom mic or a distracting background object, Resolve 21.1 can handle it with remarkable precision, saving hours of manual rotoscoping.

**Intelligent Scene Detection**
Resolve can now automatically detect scene changes within a clip, generating markers that allow for instant segmentation. This dramatically speeds up the process of assembling rough cuts, especially when working with long-form media or screen recordings.

> 💡 Insight: The AI enhancements in Resolve 21.1 are designed to automate tedious tasks, freeing up editors to focus on creative storytelling.

**Performance and Stability Improvements**
Beyond new features, this update brings substantial under-the-hood optimizations. Expect faster playback, quicker rendering times, and improved overall stability, particularly on complex projects and demanding hardware configurations.
