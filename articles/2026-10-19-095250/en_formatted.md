Dive into the **DNS Museum**, a digital treasure trove celebrating the history, evolution, and hidden intricacies of the Domain Name System. Discover how DNS shapes the internet—and why it’s worth preserving.

**The DNS Museum** is an immersive online exhibit dedicated to preserving the history, culture, and technical evolution of the **Domain Name System (DNS)**—the invisible backbone of the internet. Founded by internet pioneers, it offers a nostalgic yet educational journey through DNS artifacts, stories, and interactive exhibits, bridging the gap between past and present for tech enthusiasts and curious minds alike.
