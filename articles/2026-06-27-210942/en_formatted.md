## 🔑 The Core of This Topic

During World War II, the US Army issued ocarinas, small flute-like instruments, to soldiers as a form of musical therapy and stress relief. The ocarinas were introduced as a way to provide a calming influence and boost morale.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Ocarinas provided a calming influence and boosted morale.
- **Point 2**: The ocarinas were used in musical therapy sessions.
- **Point 3**: The unique sound of ocarinas brought comfort to soldiers.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The ocarinas were designed to produce a unique, haunting sound. This distinctive sound was a welcome respite from the chaos and destruction of war. By playing the ocarinas, soldiers could momentarily escape the stresses of combat.

**Element 2**
The use of ocarinas in World War II marked a significant shift in the way armies approached music and mental health. The ocarinas were often used in musical therapy sessions, where soldiers would play the instruments as a means of relaxation and stress relief.
