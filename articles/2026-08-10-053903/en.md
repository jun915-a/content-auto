# Revolutionizing Retro Game Physics

Discover the power of Picophysics, a groundbreaking physics engine for classic game consoles.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Picophysics is a single-file physics engine designed for game development on retro platforms like N64, PSX, and DC. It's an open-source project that simplifies the process of adding realistic physics to classic games, making them more engaging and immersive for players.
