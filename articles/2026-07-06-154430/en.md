# Scan AI Agents for Hidden Dangers

Discover the risks of AI and protect your agents with MakerChecker, an open-source tool that scans for malicious capabilities.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
AI agents are increasingly being used in various applications, from customer service to healthcare. However, these agents can also pose a significant risk to individuals and organizations if they are not properly designed and monitored. MakerChecker is an open-source tool that helps scan AI agents for dangerous capabilities, ensuring that they behave as intended.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Scans AI agents for malicious capabilities
- **Point 2**: Identifies potential risks and vulnerabilities
- **Point 3**: Provides recommendations for mitigation and improvement

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Model Bias**
Many AI agents are trained on biased data, which can lead to unfair outcomes and discrimination. MakerChecker helps identify model bias by analyzing the data used to train the agent.

**Data Leaks**
AI agents often require access to sensitive data, which can be leaked if the agent is not properly secured. MakerChecker scans for data leaks and provides recommendations for secure data storage.

> 💡 Insight: The key to safe AI agent development is not to avoid risk, but to understand and mitigate it.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Protects individuals from biased AI decisions
- Ensures secure data storage and processing
- Helps organizations avoid costly data breaches

MakerChecker is a powerful tool that helps scan AI agents for dangerous capabilities. By using MakerChecker, developers can ensure that their AI agents are safe, secure, and fair. Join the open-source community and contribute to making AI safer for everyone.
