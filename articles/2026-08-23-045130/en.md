## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The Speedrun approach leverages **gradient checkpointing** and **mixed-precision training** to reduce memory usage by up to 60% while maintaining performance. By carefully selecting batch sizes and sequence lengths that maximize GPU utilization, training throughput increases exponentially. The team also implemented **smart data loading** that pre-processes text data on-the-fly, eliminating disk I/O bottlenecks that traditionally slow down small model training.

**Element 2**
At the heart of NanoGPT Speedrun is a **curriculum learning strategy** that gradually increases model complexity. Starting with shorter sequences and simpler patterns, the training process progressively challenges the model with longer dependencies and more nuanced language structures. This approach not only speeds up convergence but also improves model generalization. The researchers found that this method works particularly well for models under 100M parameters, where traditional training often struggles with underfitting.
