CodePen 2.0 boasts an intuitive drag-and-drop interface, allowing users to effortlessly arrange and manage their projects. The platform's revamped UI ensures that essential tools and features are readily accessible, minimizing the time spent on searching and navigating.

**Element 2**
One of the standout features of CodePen 2.0 is its robust collaboration module. This enables multiple developers to work together in real-time, fostering a more agile and responsive development environment.
