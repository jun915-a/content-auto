# Orbit: AR Satellite Tracker

Explore 15,000+ satellites, planets, and constellations in AR, 2D, and 3D with Orbit iOS app

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Orbit is an innovative iOS application that brings the night sky to life, allowing users to visualize and track over 15,000 celestial objects in augmented reality (AR), on a 2D map, and on a 3D globe. This app is designed to educate and fascinate users of all ages about the wonders of our universe.
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Satellite Tracking**: Track over 15,000 satellites in real-time
- **AR Experience**: Explore the night sky in augmented reality
- **Interactive Maps**: View celestial objects on 2D and 3D maps
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Satellite Database**
The app boasts an extensive database of satellites, planets, and constellations, providing a comprehensive view of our solar system and beyond. 
**User Interface**
The user-friendly interface allows for seamless navigation between different viewing modes, making it accessible to both beginners and astronomy enthusiasts.
> 💡 Insight: The combination of AR and interactive maps offers a unique learning experience

- Enhances astronomy education and awareness

- Provides a fun and interactive way to learn about celestial bodies
