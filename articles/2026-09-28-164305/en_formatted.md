# E-Ink Fridge Magnet Shopping List: Sync Your Groceries with PaperMono

*Insert header image here*

Turn your fridge into a smart shopping hub with **PaperMono**, a touchscreen e-ink device synced wirelessly to your phone. Built on an ESP32-S3, this open-source project transforms mundane lists into a sleek, offline-capable system—perfect for families or minimalists.

**A fridge magnet shopping list** isn’t just a relic of the past—it’s evolving. Meet **PaperMono**, an e-ink touchscreen device that turns your fridge into an interactive, wireless grocery manager. No more sticky notes or forgotten items. Just a **tactile, battery-powered** system that syncs with your phone over Wi-Fi and works offline. Built with **~2,400 lines of C++**, this project is a testament to how far hardware hacking has come.
