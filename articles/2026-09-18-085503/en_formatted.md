## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Formal Verification**
Bend embeds a theorem prover, allowing developers to write verifiable code. This means AI algorithms can be proven correct, drastically reducing bugs and unpredictable behavior inherent in complex models.

**Performance Optimization**
Designed for modern hardware, Bend compiles efficiently for both general-purpose CPUs and parallel-processing GPUs. This ensures that provably correct AI can be deployed without sacrificing speed.
