## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The concept of elementary particles has been a cornerstone of physics for centuries. However, recent discoveries have left scientists questioning the very nature of these fundamental building blocks. The Standard Model, which describes the behavior of particles like quarks and electrons, has been incredibly successful. But it's also incomplete, leaving room for new theories and discoveries.

- **Point 2**: The discovery of new particles could revolutionize our understanding of the universe.
- **Point 3**: The search for elementary particles is a fundamental quest to understand the nature of reality.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Quarks**
Quarks are among the most well-known elementary particles. They come in six "flavors" – up, down, charm, strange, top, and bottom – and are the building blocks of protons and neutrons. However, quarks are never found alone in nature, always bound together with other quarks or antiquarks.

**Leptons**
Leptons are another class of elementary particles. They include electrons, muons, and neutrinos, which are not affected by the strong nuclear force and are not part of the atomic nucleus. Leptons are often seen as the "lighter" cousins of quarks.

> 💡 Insight: The distinction between quarks and leptons is not clear-cut, and some theories propose that they are not distinct entities.
