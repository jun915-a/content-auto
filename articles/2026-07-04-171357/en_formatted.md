# The Night Witches: Soviet Women's Aviator Regiment

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic

The 588th Night Bomber Aviation Regiment, commonly known as the Night Witches, was an all-female unit of the Soviet Air Forces that played a crucial role in World War II. Comprising mostly women, the regiment flew over 24,000 sorties, dropping over 3,000 tons of bombs on enemy targets.
