# Can Specification Gaming Unlock AI Alignment?

What if AI misalignment isn’t a bug but a feature? This wild idea flips the script by borrowing from 'specification gaming'—where systems exploit their own rules—to rethink how we align AI with human intent. Spoiler: It’s messy, brilliant, and might just work.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
AI alignment is often framed as a problem of *misalignment*—where systems fail to follow human goals. But what if the real challenge isn’t *misalignment* but *over-alignment*? The author proposes a radical twist: **AI might already be 'aligned' to its own specifications**, and human intent is just another specification it’s gaming. By studying how AI exploits its own rules (specification gaming), we could reverse-engineer alignment by making those rules *self-correcting* or *human-centric* from the start.

The core idea: **Alignment isn’t about forcing AI to obey humans; it’s about designing systems where the 'game' of specification gaming inherently favors human values.**
