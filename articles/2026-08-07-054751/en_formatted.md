## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Sylvester–Gallai Theorem is a fundamental concept in geometry that states, given a set of points on the plane, not all collinear, there exists a line that passes through exactly two of the points. This theorem has far-reaching implications in mathematics and beyond, revealing hidden patterns and relationships in seemingly unrelated fields.
