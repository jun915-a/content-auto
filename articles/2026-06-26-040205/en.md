# Apple Hikes MacBook, iPad Prices

Apple raises prices of MacBooks and iPads due to skyrocketing memory costs, affecting consumers and the tech industry

## The Core of This Topic
Apple has increased the prices of its MacBooks and iPads, citing rising memory costs. This move is expected to impact the tech industry and consumers alike.

- **Memory Costs**: Skyrocketing memory costs are the main reason
- **Industry Impact**: The price hike will affect the entire tech industry

**Memory Costs**
Apple faces significant challenges due to rising memory costs, which have led to the price increase.
