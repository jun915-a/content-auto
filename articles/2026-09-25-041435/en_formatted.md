Explore how WaveDigger deciphers wireless signals to pinpoint their origins, revolutionizing cybersecurity and IoT investigations with open-source ingenuity.

**WaveDigger: Dig into wireless signals to discover their physical locations** is an open-source tool designed to uncover the geographic origins of wireless signals by analyzing their unique fingerprints. Whether it’s a rogue access point, a misconfigured IoT device, or an unauthorized transmission, WaveDigger empowers users to trace signals back to their sources with precision. Built by **Christian Rowlands**, this tool bridges the gap between signal analysis and physical location discovery, offering a powerful resource for security researchers, penetration testers, and IoT enthusiasts alike.
