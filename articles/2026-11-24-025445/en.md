Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia sparks alarm, raising critical questions about national security implications and the company's role in global tech dominance. Is this a scandal in the making?

**Global Implications**
Nvidia's technology is sought after by nations worldwide, including geopolitical rivals. Any perceived compromise or undue influence over its operations could have far-reaching consequences for global power dynamics and technological parity.
