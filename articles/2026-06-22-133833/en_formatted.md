## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
JSON-LD (JavaScript Object Notation for Linked Data) is a format for marking up structured data on the web. It allows search engines like Google to better understand the content of your website and provide more accurate search results.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Improves SEO by providing search engines with clear data about your website.
- **Point 2**: Enhances accessibility by providing alternative text for images and other content.
- **Point 3**: Enables the use of schema markup to provide additional context about your website's content.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**JSON-LD Structure**: JSON-LD uses a specific structure to mark up data, including a context, a type, and properties.
**Types**: There are several types of JSON-LD, including Person, Organization, and Event.
**Properties**: Properties are used to describe the data, such as name, description, and image.

> 💡 Insight: By using JSON-LD, you can provide search engines with the information they need to understand your website and improve its visibility in search results.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- **Improved SEO**: By providing clear data about your website, you can improve its visibility in search results and drive more traffic to your site.
- **Enhanced Accessibility**: By providing alternative text for images and other content, you can make your website more accessible to users with disabilities.
- **Increased User Engagement**: By providing additional context about your website's content, you can increase user engagement and improve the overall user experience.

## ✨ Conclusion
JSON-LD is a powerful tool for improving your website's SEO, accessibility, and user experience. By implementing it effectively, you can improve your website's visibility, drive more traffic, and increase user engagement.
