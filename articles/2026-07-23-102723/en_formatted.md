## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Artificial intelligence has revolutionized many aspects of business, including menu design. But, as we've seen with some high-profile examples, AI-generated menus can be a recipe for disaster. From confusing layouts to unappetizing graphics, AI menus have left many consumers scratching their heads.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
* **Point 1**: AI menus often prioritize functionality over aesthetics, resulting in an unpleasing user experience.
* **Point 2**: The lack of human touch can lead to mistakes and inconsistencies in menu design.
* **Point 3**: AI-generated menus can fail to adapt to the brand's tone and style.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
A key issue with AI menus is the overreliance on generic templates and design elements. This can result in menus that look like they were generated by a machine, rather than a human designer. The lack of personality and flair can make a brand look unappealing and uninviting.

**Element 2**
Another problem with AI menus is the tendency to prioritize functionality over aesthetics. While it's essential to ensure that menus are easy to navigate and understand, it's equally important to make them visually appealing. Unfortunately, AI-generated menus often sacrifice style for substance.

> 💡 Insight: By prioritizing aesthetics and incorporating human touch into AI-generated menus, businesses can create a more engaging and memorable user experience.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
* McDonald's infamous AI-generated menu, which featured awkwardly placed graphics and confusing layout.
* Domino's Pizza's attempt at AI-powered menu design, which resulted in a menu that looked more like a spreadsheet than a menu.
* Subway's AI-generated menu, which featured a confusing layout and unappealing graphics.

## ✨ Conclusion
While AI can be a powerful tool for menu design, it's essential to remember that human touch and creativity are still essential components of a successful menu. By striking a balance between AI-generated design and human input, businesses can create menus that are both functional and visually appealing.
