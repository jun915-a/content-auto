## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The new incremental compilation feature analyzes and compiles only the changed parts of the code, significantly reducing build times.
**Element 2**: The introduction of caching and optimization techniques minimizes unnecessary recompilations, further improving performance.

> 💡 Insight: By focusing on efficient build times, Elm 1.0 enables developers to iterate and refine their code more quickly, leading to better overall development experiences.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Faster build times mean less waiting time, allowing developers to be more productive.

- Enhanced debugging tools make it easier to identify and fix issues.
