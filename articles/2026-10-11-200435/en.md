Nitter is a decentralized, user-friendly Twitter frontend that prioritizes privacy and free speech. Since 2018, it’s grown into a vital alternative for users tired of Twitter’s censorship and data policies. However, recent **DMCA takedowns** and **hosting challenges** have pushed it to the brink of collapse. Now, its developers are **publicly seeking funding and legal aid** to ensure its survival—sparking debates about free speech, corporate power, and open-source sustainability.
