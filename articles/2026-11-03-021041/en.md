While the company remains a powerhouse in AI research, its ability to **adapt and innovate beyond model development** will determine its long-term success. However, if it clings to its current trajectory, it risks joining the ranks of overhyped startups that failed to deliver on promise. The next 12–24 months will be critical in shaping Anthropic’s future—and the broader AI landscape."
