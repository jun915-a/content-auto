## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
A film photographer's DIY project showcases a unique and affordable approach to creating an f-stop timer, utilizing an iPhone and a smart plug to mimic the functionality of expensive dedicated timers.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Turned an iPhone into a remote control for a smart plug.
- **Point 2**: The smart plug acts as a timer, controlling exposure during darkroom printing.
- **Point 3**: This setup offers a cost-effective solution for photographers on a budget.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1: The iPhone as a Remote**
The iPhone serves as the brain of this setup, acting as a remote control for the smart plug. With a simple app, the photographer can control the plug's functionality, setting precise timers for darkroom exposure.

**Element 2: Smart Plug as Timer**
The $20 smart plug is the heart of this DIY project. It receives commands from the iPhone, turning on and off at precise intervals, controlling the exposure time during printing.

> 💡 Insight: By combining the iPhone's versatility with the smart plug's functionality, photographers can achieve professional-grade results without breaking the bank.
