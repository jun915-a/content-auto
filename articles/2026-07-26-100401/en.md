# Unlocking Transistor Secrets with Realistic Animations

Discover the inner workings of transistors through visually stunning animations, carefully crafted using a semiconductor simulation.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Transistors are the building blocks of modern electronics, but their inner workings can be difficult to grasp without a deep understanding of semiconductor physics. To bridge this gap, I created animations of the most common types of transistors, using a custom semiconductor simulation tool.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**BJT Transistor**
The BJT transistor is a widely used device that relies on the flow of charge carriers in a semiconductor material. My animation illustrates how the transistor's base, collector, and emitter interact to control current flow.

**MOSFET Transistor**
The MOSFET transistor is a type of field-effect transistor that uses a voltage-controlled gate to regulate current flow. My animation shows how the MOSFET's gate, source, and drain interact to control the flow of charge carriers.
