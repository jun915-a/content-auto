## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Sandwich Simulator is an interactive web application that allows users to virtually assemble sandwiches. It provides a vast library of ingredients, from various breads and meats to cheeses, vegetables, and sauces, enabling users to experiment and visualize their ideal sandwich creation.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Ingredient Library**
Choose from an extensive selection of breads, proteins, cheeses, vegetables, and condiments. Each ingredient is rendered visually, allowing for a realistic representation of your sandwich.

**Assembly Interface**
A user-friendly drag-and-drop or selection interface makes building your sandwich intuitive and fun. Layer ingredients in any order you desire, from the base to the toppings.
