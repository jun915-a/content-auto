This release brings powerful AI-driven features directly into the editing workflow. Expect intelligent object removal, scene analysis for automatic cuts, and AI-assisted transcription, all designed to save editors significant time and effort.

Colorists will appreciate the refined color management tools, including new LUT processing and improved HDR support. The update focuses on delivering more accurate and creative color grading options with greater control.
