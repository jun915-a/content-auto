## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Layer Overhead Reduction**
LBSE's conditional layers allow for intelligent layer management, eliminating unnecessary layer creation and management. This results in a significant reduction in layer overhead, which can lead to improved performance and efficiency.

**Conditional Layers**
Conditional layers are a game-changer for LBSE. They enable the engine to dynamically create and manage layers based on specific conditions, ensuring that only necessary layers are created and updated.
