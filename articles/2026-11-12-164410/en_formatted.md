# Visualizing systemd Jobs Live on Boot Console

*Insert header image here*

Discover **Bootvine**, an open-source tool that renders a real-time tree of systemd jobs during Linux boot—unlocking deeper insights into your system’s startup process.

**Bootvine** is a command-line tool designed to visualize the **hierarchy of systemd jobs** as they execute during the Linux boot process. Unlike traditional debugging methods, it provides a **live, interactive tree** of dependencies and states, making it easier to diagnose startup delays or misconfigurations. By rendering systemd’s complex job graph in real time, Bootvine bridges the gap between raw logs and intuitive understanding, empowering sysadmins and developers to optimize boot performance effortlessly.
