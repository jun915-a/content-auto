# Threat Modeling 101

Unlock the secrets of threat modeling with Soatok's expert guide. Learn the fundamentals, key takeaways, and real-world impact of threat modeling.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Threat modeling is the process of identifying, analyzing, and prioritizing potential threats to a system or application. It involves understanding the attack surface, identifying vulnerabilities, and developing mitigation strategies to reduce the risk of a successful attack.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Identify potential threats to a system or application.
- **Point 2**: Analyze the attack surface and identify vulnerabilities.
- **Point 3**: Develop mitigation strategies to reduce the risk of a successful attack.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Threat Identification**
Threat modeling begins with identifying potential threats to a system or application. This involves understanding the attack surface, including the entry points, data flows, and system components. Threats can come from various sources, including insider threats, external attackers, and environmental factors.

**Risk Analysis**
Once potential threats have been identified, a risk analysis is conducted to determine the likelihood and potential impact of each threat. This involves assessing the likelihood of a threat occurring, the potential impact of a successful attack, and the potential consequences of a breach.

> 💡 Insight: Threat modeling is not a one-time activity, but an ongoing process that requires continuous monitoring and analysis.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Improved security posture through proactive threat modeling.
- Reduced risk of a successful attack through mitigation strategies.
- Enhanced incident response through threat modeling.

## ✨ Conclusion
Threat modeling is a critical component of a comprehensive security strategy. By identifying potential threats, analyzing the attack surface, and developing mitigation strategies, organizations can reduce the risk of a successful attack and improve their overall security posture.
