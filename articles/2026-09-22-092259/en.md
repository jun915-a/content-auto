# AI Coding Bottleneck? How Linear Streamlined CI

AI coding is accelerating development, but it's overloading traditional CI systems. Discover how Linear transformed their CI to keep pace with modern development workflows and avoid costly delays.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
AI-driven coding has dramatically increased the volume and complexity of code changes, turning Continuous Integration (CI) into a major bottleneck for many development teams. This post details how Linear tackled this challenge by fundamentally rethinking and rebuilding their CI infrastructure to handle the new demands.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **AI Overload**: AI coding tools generate more frequent, smaller changes, overwhelming standard CI.
- **Reimagined CI**: Linear rebuilt their CI from the ground up, focusing on speed and efficiency.
- **Scalability Wins**: The new system handles increased load without compromising developer productivity.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The AI Effect**
AI coding assistants generate code much faster, leading to a surge in commits and pull requests. This increased activity strains traditional CI pipelines, causing longer wait times for feedback and slowing down the development cycle.

**Linear's CI Overhaul**
Linear recognized the need for a CI system that could scale. They moved away from monolithic builds towards a more distributed and efficient architecture, optimizing every step of the process.

> 💡 Insight: Proactive CI redesign is crucial to leverage AI coding advancements without sacrificing development velocity.

**Parallelization and Caching**
Key to their success was aggressive parallelization of tasks and sophisticated caching strategies. This ensures that redundant work is minimized and tests run in the most efficient order possible.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Significantly reduced CI wait times, enabling faster feedback loops.
- Improved developer productivity by minimizing time spent waiting for builds and tests.
- Created a scalable CI system capable of handling future growth and AI-driven development.

## ✨ Conclusion
By strategically overhauling their CI, Linear demonstrates how teams can adapt to the era of AI-assisted coding, transforming potential bottlenecks into accelerators for innovation and efficiency.
