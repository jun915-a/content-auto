# EU Mandates Driver Monitoring

*Insert header image here*

New EU law requires all cars to have a driver monitoring camera to reduce distracted driving, enhancing road safety

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The European Union has introduced a new law that makes it mandatory for all new cars to be equipped with a driver monitoring camera. This system is designed to detect and prevent distracted driving, a major cause of accidents on European roads.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Improved Safety**: Reduced accidents due to distracted driving
- **Driver Monitoring**: Cameras track driver attention and alertness
- **EU Regulation**: Applies to all new cars sold in the EU

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Driver Attention**
Cameras monitor the driver's face and eyes to detect signs of distraction or drowsiness, ensuring the driver stays focused on the road.

**System Alerts**
The system alerts the driver if it detects any signs of distraction, helping to prevent accidents and improve overall road safety.
> 💡 Insight: This technology has the potential to significantly reduce the number of accidents caused by distracted driving.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Reduced number of road accidents
- Improved road safety for all users
- Encourages responsible driving habits

## ✨ Conclusion
The introduction of mandatory driver monitoring cameras in new cars is a significant step towards enhancing road safety in the European Union, promising a safer driving experience for everyone.
