# Silicon Valley Misreads Science Fiction, Undermining Democracy

Historian Jill Lepore accuses the tech industry of misinterpreting science fiction, leading to the erosion of democratic values and institutions.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Silicon Valley's misreading of science fiction has led to a distorted understanding of the relationship between technology and society. This misinterpretation has resulted in the erosion of democratic values and institutions, as the tech industry prioritizes innovation over civic engagement.

**The Distortion of Science Fiction Concepts**
The tech industry's misreading of science fiction concepts has led to a distorted understanding of the relationship between technology and society. Concepts like the 'invisible hand' and 'technological determinism' have been misinterpreted, leading to a neglect of the social and civic implications of technological advancements.

**The Prioritization of Innovation Over Civic Engagement**
The tech industry's focus on innovation has led to a decline in civic engagement and democratic values. The emphasis on speed and efficiency has resulted in a neglect of the social and civic consequences of technological advancements.
