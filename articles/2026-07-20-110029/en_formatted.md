This demo leverages WebGPU to bring highly efficient 1-bit Large Language Models (LLMs) directly into the browser. 1-bit quantization drastically reduces model size and computational needs, making powerful AI accessible on standard devices without server-side processing.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Bonsai WebGPU Demo**
This interactive demo from the WebML community showcases the practical application of 1-bit LLMs. It allows users to experiment with these compact models, demonstrating their surprisingly robust performance despite extreme quantization.

**1-Bit Quantization**
This technique reduces model weights to just a single bit, leading to massive memory savings and computational speedups. It's a key enabler for running complex AI models on resource-constrained environments like web browsers.
