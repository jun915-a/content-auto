# Quick Pho Ga: 30-Min Pressure Cooker Chicken Pho Recipe

Craving authentic Pho Ga but short on time? This Serious Eats recipe uses a pressure cooker to deliver rich, aromatic chicken pho in just 30 minutes. Perfect for weeknight indulgence!

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
This recipe masterfully leverages the speed and flavor-infusing power of a pressure cooker to create a deeply savory and aromatic Pho Ga broth in a fraction of the traditional time. It focuses on building complex flavors quickly through strategic ingredient additions and pressure cooking.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Speed**: Achieve rich Pho Ga broth in just 30 minutes using a pressure cooker.
- **Flavor**: Deep chicken flavor enhanced by aromatics like ginger and star anise.
- **Simplicity**: Streamlined steps make authentic pho accessible on busy weeknights.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Broth Base**
The foundation is a quick-cooking chicken broth boosted with charred aromatics (ginger, onion) and classic pho spices like star anise, cinnamon, and cloves. Pressure cooking melds these flavors rapidly.

**Chicken Preparation**
Chicken pieces are cooked directly in the broth, ensuring they are tender and infuse the liquid with even more chicken essence. The cooked chicken is then shredded or sliced for serving.

> 💡 Insight: Charring the aromatics before pressure cooking significantly deepens their flavor, adding a smoky complexity to the pho broth.

**Assembly**
Once the broth is ready, it's strained and combined with cooked rice noodles, tender chicken, and fresh garnishes like cilantro, scallions, and lime for a complete, satisfying meal.
