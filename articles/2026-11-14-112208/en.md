# Ageing Protein Found: A New Target Without Inflammation

Scientists have identified a protein that drives ageing, offering a potential new therapeutic avenue. Crucially, this discovery avoids triggering inflammation, a common side effect of other anti-ageing interventions.

Unlike previously identified ageing drivers, Factor X's mechanism of action seems to bypass the inflammatory pathways that often complicate therapeutic interventions, offering a cleaner target for anti-ageing strategies.

**Factor X Activation**
This protein becomes more active as cells age. Its presence correlates with a decline in cellular function and tissue regeneration, acting as a key signal that promotes the ageing phenotype across various cell types.

**Inflammatory Bypass**
Previous research often focused on pathways that, while linked to ageing, also induce chronic inflammation. Factor X operates independently of these pro-inflammatory signals, making it a more attractive target for interventions.

> 💡 Insight: Targeting Factor X could offer a way to slow ageing without the common pitfall of increased inflammation.
