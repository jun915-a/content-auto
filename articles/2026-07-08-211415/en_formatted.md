## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Fable is a framework designed to simplify the process of developing machine learning models. However, its limitations and flaws have started to show, making it less useful in real-world applications.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Fable's reliance on pre-defined templates hinders creativity and adaptability.
- **Point 2**: Its inability to handle complex data structures and relationships limits its scope.
- **Point 3**: The lack of transparency and explainability in Fable's output makes it difficult to trust.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
Fable's core issue lies in its rigid structure, which makes it challenging to incorporate new ideas and approaches. This inflexibility leads to a narrow focus on specific problems, without considering the broader context.

Moreover, Fable's inability to handle complex data structures and relationships means that it struggles with real-world data. This limitation makes it less useful in applications where data is nuanced and multi-faceted.

> 💡 Insight: Fable's flaws are a result of its attempt to simplify a complex problem, rather than addressing the root causes.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- **Inaccurate predictions**: Fable's inability to handle complex data leads to inaccurate predictions and poor decision-making.
- **Limited scalability**: Fable's rigid structure makes it difficult to adapt to new data and scenarios, limiting its scalability.
- **Lack of trust**: Fable's lack of transparency and explainability makes it difficult to trust its output, leading to skepticism and mistrust.

## ✨ Conclusion
Fable, once hailed as a revolutionary approach to machine learning, has fallen short of its promise. Its limitations and flaws make it less useful in real-world applications. As we move forward, it's essential to address these issues and develop more robust and flexible models.
