## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Apple Card wasn’t built overnight. Its origins trace back to a **2006 internal experiment** at Apple, where engineers explored how to merge hardware, software, and financial services into a seamless ecosystem. The project, codenamed *Project Marzipan*, aimed to create a **closed-loop payment system**—one where Apple devices, services, and financial tools would operate as a single, frictionless unit. This vision, refined over a decade, became the blueprint for Apple Card, blending Apple’s design ethos with Goldman Sachs’ financial expertise. The key insight? Apple wasn’t just selling a credit card—it was **reimagining money itself**.
