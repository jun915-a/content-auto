# DuckDuckGo Soars 28% After Google's AI Stance

DuckDuckGo saw a significant surge in visits after Google's AI mode statement, with a 28% increase in traffic, as users seek AI-free search alternatives

## The Core of This Topic
DuckDuckGo is a search engine that emphasizes privacy and avoids AI-driven results, offering an alternative to major search engines like Google.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: DuckDuckGo's AI-free approach
- **Point 2**: Google's AI mode statement
- **Point 3**: 28% increase in DuckDuckGo visits
## Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: DuckDuckGo's focus on user privacy has attracted those seeking to avoid AI-driven search results.
**Element 2**: Google's statement on AI mode sparked a reaction, with some users opting for DuckDuckGo's alternative approach.
> Insight: The shift towards DuckDuckGo indicates a growing desire for privacy and transparency in search engines.
## Real-World Impact
- Increased scrutiny of Google's AI mode
- Growing demand for AI-free search alternatives
- Expansion of DuckDuckGo's user base
## Conclusion
The rise of DuckDuckGo is a testament to the evolving landscape of search engines, with users seeking more control over their online experiences.
