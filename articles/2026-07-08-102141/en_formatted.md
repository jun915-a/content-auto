# EU Mandates Driver Monitoring

The European Union now requires all new cars to include a driver monitoring camera to reduce distracted driving, promoting road safety and saving lives.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The European Union has introduced a new rule that makes it compulsory for all new cars to be equipped with a driver monitoring camera. This system is designed to detect and prevent distracted driving, which is a major cause of accidents on the road.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Improved Safety**: Reduces accidents caused by distracted driving
- **Driver Monitoring**: Cameras track the driver's attention and alertness
- **EU Regulation**: Mandatory for all new cars sold in the European Union

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Driver Attention**
The system uses a camera to monitor the driver's face and eyes, detecting signs of distraction or drowsiness. 

**System Alerts**
The system alerts the driver if it detects any sign of distraction or drowsiness, ensuring the driver stays focused on the road.
> 💡 Insight: This technology has the potential to significantly reduce the number of accidents on the road.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Reduced number of accidents caused by distracted driving
- Increased road safety for all users
- Encourages responsible driving habits

## ✨ Conclusion
The introduction of mandatory driver monitoring cameras in new cars is a significant step towards improving road safety in the European Union. This technology has the potential to save lives and reduce the number of accidents on the road.
