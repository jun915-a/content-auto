Modify your `server` block to include a **custom log format** that captures critical metrics like `$request_length`, `$status`, and `$request_time`. For example:\n\n```\nlog_format prometheus '$remote_addr - $remote_user [$time_local] \"$request\" '\n'$status $body_bytes_sent \"$http_referer\" '\n'\"$http_user_agent\" \"$gzip_ratio\" '\n'$request_length $upstream_response_time';\n```\n\n> **Critical Note**: Ensure your Nginx version supports **`$upstream_response_time`** (available since **1.11+**). To expose this to Prometheus:\n\n1. Enable the module in your Nginx config:\n   ```\n   http {\n       stub_status on;\n       server {\n           listen 127.0.0.1:8080;\n           location /status {\n               stub_status;\n           }\n       }\n   }\n   ```\n\n2. **Scrape `/status`** in your Prometheus `scrape_config`:\n   ```yaml\n   scrape_configs:\n     - job_name: 'nginx'\n       static_configs:\n         - targets: ['localhost:8080']\n   ```\n\n> 💡 **Insight**: This method avoids **plugin bloat** while providing **low-overhead** metrics.
