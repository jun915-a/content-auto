## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Fermat's Last Theorem Statement**
The theorem states that for any integer n greater than 2, no three positive integers a, b, and c can satisfy the equation aⁿ + bⁿ = cⁿ. This remained unproven for centuries until Andrew Wiles' work.

**Lean 4 Proof Assistant**
Lean 4 is an interactive theorem prover and programming language. It allows mathematicians to write proofs that are then exhaustively checked by the computer, eliminating human error.
