One of Cube Motion’s most innovative features is its ability to **overlay digital interfaces onto physical objects**. For example, a **tabletop becomes a collaborative whiteboard**, a **book transforms into an interactive e-reader**, or a **room morphs into a dynamic workspace**. This **blurring of physical and digital boundaries** opens up endless possibilities for **education, gaming, and productivity tools**.
