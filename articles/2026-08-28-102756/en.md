Nostalgia meets modernity with Afterglow, a tool that breathes life into vintage After Dark screen savers on modern macOS. Perfect for tech enthusiasts and retro lovers, it bridges the gap between old-school charm and sleek contemporary systems.

It’s a love letter to retro computing, offering a seamless way to enjoy timeless visuals without sacrificing performance or compatibility.
