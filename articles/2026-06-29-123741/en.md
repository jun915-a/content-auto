HackerRank's open-source ATS scores resumes with surprising results, sparking interest in its potential impact on hiring processes

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
HackerRank has made its Applicant Tracking System (ATS) open source, allowing users to test their resumes and receive a score. The author's resume scored 90/100, then 74, and finally 88, highlighting the system's variability.
