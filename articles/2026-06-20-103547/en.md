Discover the art of machine learning research, where intuition meets innovation, and simplicity yields powerful results

The core of Zen in machine learning is about embracing simplicity and focusing on the essence of the problem, rather than getting bogged down in complexity.

- **Point 2**: Simplicity in model design

**Element 1**
Intuition plays a significant role in machine learning research, guiding the development of innovative solutions. 
**Element 2**
