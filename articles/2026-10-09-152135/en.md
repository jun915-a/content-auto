## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Global Anycast DNS & Low-Latency Routing**
Hetzner’s Anycast network distributes DNS queries across 20+ points of presence (PoPs), ensuring users connect to the nearest server. This isn’t just about speed—it’s about **resilience**. If one PoP fails, traffic seamlessly reroutes via redundant paths. The system handles **millions of queries per second** with sub-10ms response times, a feat few providers match.

**Software-Defined Networking (SDN) & Bare-Metal Efficiency**
Unlike traditional cloud providers, Hetzner avoids virtualization layers. Their SDN architecture lets customers **directly control routing** without hypervisor overhead. This means **faster I/O, lower latency**, and **predictable pricing**—no hidden costs for virtualization.

> 💡 Insight: **No middlemen.** Hetzner’s SDN reduces operational complexity while cutting costs by **30-50%** compared to competitors.

**Direct Fiber Backbone & Cost Savings**
Most cloud providers rely on third-party transit networks, adding hidden fees. Hetzner **owns its own fiber backbone**, eliminating transit costs. This isn’t just about savings—it’s about **consistent performance**. Customers benefit from **dedicated bandwidth** without over-provisioning, making it ideal for high-traffic applications.
