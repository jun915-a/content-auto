# Dithered QR Codes

Discover the fascinating world of dithered QR codes, where art meets technology, and explore their unique characteristics and applications

## The Core of This Topic
Dithered QR codes are a type of QR code that uses a dithering technique to create a visually appealing image. 
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Improved aesthetics
- **Point 2**: Increased error correction
- **Point 3**: Enhanced security
## Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Dithering is a process of creating an image from a limited color palette. 
**Element 2**
This technique allows for the creation of detailed images within the confines of a QR code. 
> Insight: The use of dithering in QR codes can make them more engaging and memorable.
