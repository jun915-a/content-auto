## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
This project showcases Hacker News displayed on a simulated train station-style flip board. It combines real-time data fetching with a nostalgic visual and auditory experience, demonstrating a unique way to consume information.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Retro Aesthetics**: Mimics old-school flip boards.
- **Hacker News Integration**: Fetches live headlines.
- **Satisfying Sounds**: Features the iconic flip sound.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Flip Board Simulation**
This element is the heart of the project, visually recreating the mechanical flip-dot displays found in train stations. Each character flips individually, creating a dynamic and engaging presentation of text, complete with the distinct sound.

**Real-Time Data Feed**
The project pulls live data directly from the Hacker News API. This ensures that the displayed headlines are always current, offering a functional yet entertaining way to stay updated on tech and startup news.
