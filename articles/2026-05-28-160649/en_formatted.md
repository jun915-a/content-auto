## The Core of This Topic
DuckDuckGo's search engine saw a significant surge in visits, with a 28% increase in the week following Google's statement on AI mode, highlighting a potential shift in user preferences towards AI-free search options.

## 5-Second Key Points
- **Increased Visits**: 28% more visits to DuckDuckGo
- **User Preference**: Shift towards AI-free search options
- **Google's Statement**: Triggered the increase in visits

## Detailed Breakdown
**User Behavior**
The increase in visits to DuckDuckGo suggests that users are becoming more aware of their search options and are actively seeking out AI-free alternatives.

**Search Engine Preferences**
The surge in visits to DuckDuckGo indicates that users are looking for search engines that prioritize privacy and transparency, rather than relying on AI-driven results.

> Insight: The shift towards AI-free search options may be driven by concerns over data privacy and the potential biases of AI-driven search results.

## Real-World Impact
- Increased competition in the search engine market
- Growing demand for AI-free search options
- Potential changes in the way search engines prioritize user data and privacy

## Conclusion
The surge in visits to DuckDuckGo highlights a significant shift in user preferences and behavior, with a growing demand for AI-free search options and a greater emphasis on data privacy and transparency.
