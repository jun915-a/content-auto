# MIT Researchers Develop Custom OS

MIT researchers built their own operating system to study chip functionality, gaining deeper insights into chip performance and potential improvements

## The Core of This Topic
To better understand how chips work, MIT researchers took a unique approach by building their own operating system from scratch. This allowed them to study chip performance and identify areas for improvement.
