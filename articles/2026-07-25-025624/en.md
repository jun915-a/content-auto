# Sperm Whales Sleep Vertical with Bubbles

Scientists discover that sperm whales blow bubbles to achieve restful, vertical sleep, a unique adaptation to conserve energy while hunting in deep waters.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Sperm whales sleep vertically in the ocean by blowing bubbles around themselves, which helps to conserve energy while they hunt in deep waters.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Sperm whales sleep with their blowholes above the water.
- **Point 2**: They blow bubbles around themselves for rest.
- **Point 3**: This adaptation helps conserve energy.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Depth Pressure**
The pressure at great depths in the ocean is extreme, and it's difficult for mammals to sleep in such conditions. Sperm whales have adapted to this by sleeping in short intervals while still being alert for potential threats.

**Bubble Formation**
Sperm whales blow bubbles around themselves as they sleep, which helps to conserve energy. This is because the bubbles prevent predators from detecting them by making it harder to see their outline.

> 💡 Insight: By sleeping in short intervals and blowing bubbles around themselves, sperm whales are able to conserve energy while still being alert for potential threats.
