# Uncracked City: AI-Powered Detective Game Redefines Urban Exploration

A solo-founded detective game where players interrogate AI-generated suspects to uncover hidden city histories—blending storytelling, AI ethics, and immersive discovery in a fresh twist on interactive narratives.

**Uncracked City** isn’t just another detective game—it’s an AI-driven mystery where players piece together the secrets of a city by questioning suspects whose lies are woven from language models. Created by a solo founder leveraging AI to craft unique urban narratives, this project merges **interactive storytelling**, **AI ethics**, and **exploration** into a genre-defying experience. Here’s why it’s a standout innovation in gaming and beyond.
