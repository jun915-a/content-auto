**AI-Powered Post-Training Insights**
After your workout, the dashboard doesn’t just summarize stats—it **interprets them**. Using machine learning, it identifies patterns, such as **overuse injuries** or plateauing progress, and suggests **evidence-based adjustments**. For example, if your data shows consistent high impact on your knees during squats, the AI might recommend **low-impact variations** or additional mobility drills.
