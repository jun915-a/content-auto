# RotaryCell: Old Phones, New Network - LTE for Your Rotary Dial

Breathe new life into vintage rotary phones! RotaryCell cleverly uses an ESP32-S3 to connect these classic devices to modern LTE networks, preserving their charm.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
RotaryCell bridges the gap between analog and digital by enabling unmodified rotary phones to make calls over LTE. It uses an ESP32-S3 microcontroller to interpret the pulses from the rotary dial and translate them into digital signals for a cellular module.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Retro Tech Revival**: Connects classic rotary phones to modern LTE.
- **ESP32-S3 Power**: The microcontroller handles dialing and communication.
- **Preserves Charm**: Allows enjoyment of vintage devices with current tech.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Rotary Dial Interface**
The project meticulously decodes the pulse trains generated by a traditional rotary dial. Each pulse corresponds to a digit, which the ESP32-S3 then processes to form a phone number for dialing.

**Cellular Module Integration**
A cellular module, controlled by the ESP32-S3, handles the actual communication over the LTE network. It receives the dialed number and initiates the call, making the old phone act like a modern smartphone.
