# Simulating Strait of Hormuz Closure: Unveiling the Global Oil Trade

A Columbia University professor's visualization tool reveals the impact of simulating a closure of the Strait of Hormuz on global oil trade, exposing key takeaways and real-world implications.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
A simulation of closing the Strait of Hormuz, a critical waterway for oil trade, is the focus of a new visualization tool developed by a Columbia University professor. This tool, which leverages real oil trade data, aims to provide insights into the potential effects of such an event on global oil markets.
