## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Benchmark saturation refers to the observed phenomenon where AI benchmarks and evaluation methods plateau, failing to accurately capture the progress of AI systems. This phenomenon is crucial to understanding AI's limitations and the need for new evaluation methods.
