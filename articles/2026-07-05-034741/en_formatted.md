## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Atomic Force Microscopy**: AFM is a powerful tool that uses a physical probe to map the surface topography of materials with atomic resolution. This technology has enabled researchers to study the behavior of materials at the nanoscale, leading to breakthroughs in fields such as materials science and biology.

**Stainless Etching**: Stainless etching is a critical process in the production of medical implants, such as hip replacements and heart valves. AFM has enabled researchers to study the surface topography of stainless steel and develop new etching techniques that improve the biocompatibility of these implants.

**Bacteria Research**: AFM has revolutionized the study of microorganisms by enabling researchers to study bacteria at the nanoscale. This has led to a deeper understanding of bacterial behavior and the development of new treatments for bacterial infections.
