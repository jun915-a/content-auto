# WordPress RCEs Sell for $500k

Exploit brokers pay huge sums for WordPress RCEs, a vulnerability found with GPT5.6 for just $25, sparking concerns about cybersecurity

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Exploit brokers are willing to pay up to $500,000 for a WordPress Remote Code Execution (RCE) vulnerability. Recently, a researcher found one using GPT5.6 for a significantly lower cost of $25, highlighting the disparity between the value of such exploits and the cost of discovery.
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **High Payouts**: Exploit brokers offer large sums for WordPress RCEs.
- **Low Discovery Cost**: A researcher found an RCE using GPT5.6 for $25.
- **Cybersecurity Concerns**: The ease and low cost of finding such vulnerabilities raise concerns about WordPress security.
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Vulnerability Discovery**
The use of GPT5.6 to find a WordPress RCE for $25 underscores the potential for AI in cybersecurity, both for defense and offense.
**Market Demand**
The high payouts for RCEs indicate a strong demand for these exploits, likely for use in various malicious activities.
> 💡 Insight: The intersection of AI and cybersecurity is a double-edged sword, offering both powerful defensive tools and offensive capabilities.
## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Increased risk of WordPress sites being compromised due to RCEs.
- Growing concern over the role of AI in facilitating exploit discovery.
- Potential for significant financial losses for individuals and organizations affected by these exploits.
## ✨ Conclusion
The discovery of a WordPress RCE for a minimal cost using GPT5.6, contrasted with the high payouts exploit brokers are willing to make, highlights critical issues in cybersecurity and the evolving landscape of vulnerability discovery.
