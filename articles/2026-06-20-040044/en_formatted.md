## The Core of This Topic
Hyundai has acquired Boston Dynamics, a leading robotics company, in a deal worth $325 million. This acquisition marks a significant move by Hyundai to expand its presence in the robotics and artificial intelligence sector.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Acquisition Amount**: $325 million
- **Seller**: Softbank
- **Buyer**: Hyundai
## Detailed Breakdown
**Acquisition Details**
Hyundai has taken full control of Boston Dynamics, with the acquisition expected to boost its robotics capabilities.
**Boston Dynamics' Portfolio**
Boston Dynamics is known for its innovative robots, including Spot and Atlas, which have various applications in industries such as manufacturing and logistics.
