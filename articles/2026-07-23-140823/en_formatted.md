## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Pelicanmaxxing refers to the optimization of AI models for reduced computational requirements and environmental impact. This involves various techniques, such as knowledge distillation, pruning, and quantization, to make AI models more energy-efficient and faster to train.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Model Pruning**
Model pruning involves removing unnecessary neural network connections to reduce computational requirements. This technique has been shown to significantly reduce the energy consumption of AI models while maintaining their accuracy.

**Knowledge Distillation**
Knowledge distillation is a process where a larger, complex AI model is distilled into a smaller, more efficient one. This technique allows for the transfer of knowledge from large models to smaller ones, reducing the computational requirements of AI training.
