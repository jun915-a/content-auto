Unlike global platforms, Pushin ensures all repositories, backups, and infrastructure reside within EU borders, addressing concerns around data privacy laws like **GDPR** and **eIDAS**. This makes it an ideal choice for organizations, researchers, and developers who prioritize security without sacrificing ease of use or collaboration features.
