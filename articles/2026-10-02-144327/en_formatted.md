## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The newly unearthed account, written by an anonymous sailor, paints a vivid picture of the dodo’s **gregarious nature**—unlike earlier descriptions that emphasized its docility. The sailor notes that dodos **gathered in large flocks** near coastal areas, a behavior not documented before. This suggests they may have been **highly social**, possibly relying on group dynamics for survival. Such insights could explain why they were **easier targets for human hunters** than previously thought.

**Element 2**
The text also reveals **contradictions** with earlier accounts, including claims that dodos were **not as flightless as believed**. The sailor describes them as **capable of short, awkward flights**, which might explain why they were **less vulnerable to predators** than previously assumed. This challenges the long-held idea that their extinction was solely due to **naivety and lack of defense mechanisms**.

> 💡 Insight: **The dodo’s social structure and mobility may have played a bigger role in its extinction than previously recognized.**
