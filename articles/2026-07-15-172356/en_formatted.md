In his 1982 paper, A. P. Dawid discusses the concept of a well-calibrated Bayesian prediction, proposing a criterion to evaluate the quality of probabilistic predictions.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
A well-calibrated Bayesian prediction is one where the predicted probability of an event reflects its true likelihood. Dawid's work focuses on developing a criterion to assess the quality of such predictions.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Bayesian predictions should be calibrated to reflect true likelihoods.
- **Point 2**: Dawid's criterion evaluates prediction quality based on calibration.
- **Point 3**: Well-calibrated predictions are essential for reliable decision-making.
