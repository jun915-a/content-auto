## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
OneCLI is a game-changing open source credential gateway designed to keep secrets out of AI agents. By utilizing a secure vault, developers can store and manage sensitive information, such as API keys and access tokens, in a centralized and controlled manner.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Eliminate hardcoded credentials for secure AI development.
- **Point 2**: Centralize sensitive information for easy access and management.
- **Point 3**: Improve collaboration and reduce the risk of sensitive data exposure.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: OneCLI's secure vault uses industry-standard encryption to protect sensitive information, ensuring that only authorized personnel can access it. This eliminates the risk of sensitive data exposure and provides an additional layer of security for AI-powered applications.

**Element 2**: With OneCLI, developers can easily manage and rotate credentials, reducing the risk of credential compromise and improving the overall security posture of AI-powered applications.
