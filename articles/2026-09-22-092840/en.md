# How Traffic Signals Control Urban Flow (2019)

Ever wonder how traffic lights decide when to turn green? This 2019 deep dive reveals the science behind signal timing, sensor tech, and adaptive systems that keep cities moving smoothly—while cutting congestion by up to 20%. Discover the hidden logic behind those glowing LEDs.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Traffic signals are **real-time decision-makers**—using sensors, timers, and algorithms to balance conflicting demands of vehicles and pedestrians. Their goal? Maximize flow while minimizing delays, all while adapting to unpredictable rush-hour chaos. At their heart lies a mix of **hardwired logic** and **AI-driven adaptability**, ensuring no single intersection becomes a traffic jam bottleneck.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Sensors detect traffic**: Loops in the road or cameras trigger signal changes based on real-time vehicle counts.
- **Pre-timed vs. adaptive**: Fixed schedules work for steady flows, while adaptive systems adjust dynamically to congestion.
