## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
.self is a new top-level domain designed to support self-hosting, giving individuals control over their digital presence.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Decentralized Identity**: Empowering users to manage their online identity
- **Self-Hosting**: Enabling individuals to host their own data and services
- **Human-Centered**: Focusing on user needs and privacy

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Self-Hosting Benefits**
Self-hosting allows users to have full control over their data, ensuring privacy and security.

**Decentralized Identity**
A decentralized identity system enables users to manage their online presence without relying on centralized authorities.
