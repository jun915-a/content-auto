## 🔑 The Core of This Topic

This research introduces 'Eye of Sauron,' a system designed to detect hidden spy cameras by analyzing their unique radio-frequency (RF) fingerprints. It can identify cameras even when they are powered off, offering a significant advancement in surveillance detection.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **RF Fingerprinting**: Exploits unique radio signals emitted by devices.
- **Offline Detection**: Identifies cameras even when not actively transmitting.
- **Privacy Enhancement**: Offers a robust solution against covert surveillance.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Radio Frequency Analysis**
The system works by analyzing the ambient RF environment. Each electronic device, including cameras, emits distinct RF signals. Eye of Sauron captures these signals and uses machine learning to identify camera-specific patterns.

**Power-Off Detection**
A key innovation is its ability to detect cameras even when they are not powered on. This is achieved by identifying residual electromagnetic signatures or subtle environmental disturbances caused by the camera's presence, such as its materials interacting with ambient RF fields.

> 💡 Insight: Traditional methods struggle with powered-off devices, making this a critical breakthrough for comprehensive security.
