Reversing project seeks skilled programmers to test and stabilize a classic DOS game.

DOS game F-15 Strike Eagle II is a simulation of aerial combat, and its reversing project is looking for experienced test pilots to fly through its digital skies, identifying bugs and issues that need to be addressed. This project aims to bring back a piece of gaming history, making it enjoyable for fans and nostalgic players.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Game Overview**
F-15 Strike Eagle II is a flight simulator that puts players in the cockpit of an F-15 Eagle, tasked with completing various missions and engaging enemy aircraft. The game features realistic graphics and sound effects, immersing players in a digital world of aerial combat.

**Technical Specifications**
The game is written in assembly language and uses a combination of 8088 and 80186 instructions. It is distributed as a zip file, containing the game executable, documentation, and other resources. Test pilots will need to have a good understanding of DOS and classic gaming systems to navigate the game's complexities.
