# GhostLock: A 15-Year Linux Vulnerability

A long-standing stack-UAF vulnerability, known as GhostLock, has been discovered in all Linux distributions, leaving millions of users vulnerable to attacks.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
GhostLock is a stack-use-after-free (UAF) vulnerability that has existed in every Linux distribution for 15 years, making it one of the longest-standing vulnerabilities in the history of Linux. This vulnerability is particularly concerning because it affects every Linux distribution, from small embedded systems to large enterprise servers.
