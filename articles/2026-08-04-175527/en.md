# Wordle Stats Reborn: From WhatsApp Logs to Data Insights

Discover how a Wordle enthusiast meticulously reconstructed four years of game statistics from scattered WhatsApp chat logs, transforming casual messages into a rich data analysis.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
This project details the fascinating process of extracting and analyzing four years of personal Wordle statistics that were previously buried within WhatsApp chat logs. It highlights the power of data recovery and custom analysis when official records are unavailable or insufficient.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Data Reconstruction**: Recovering historical Wordle stats from chat messages.
- **Custom Analysis**: Building personalized insights beyond official game data.
- **Technical Skill Showcase**: Demonstrating data scraping and processing techniques.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Chat Log Extraction**
The initial challenge involved accessing and parsing years of WhatsApp chat history. This required custom scripts to sift through message content, specifically identifying Wordle results and dates.

**Stat Compilation**
Once extracted, the data was organized into a structured format. This involved categorizing wins, losses, guess counts, and streaks to build a comprehensive statistical profile.

> 💡 Insight: Even unstructured chat data can yield valuable historical information with the right tools and approach.

**Visualisation and Analysis**
Finally, the compiled data was visualized to reveal trends and patterns in gameplay over the four-year period, offering a unique look at personal Wordle performance.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Enables personal data archaeology for digital activities.
- Provides a blueprint for analyzing other informal digital communication logs.
- Offers a deeper understanding of personal gaming habits and progress.

## ✨ Conclusion
This endeavor proves that valuable insights can be unearthed from everyday digital footprints, even when data isn't readily available in a structured format. It's a testament to the power of persistent data collection and analysis.
