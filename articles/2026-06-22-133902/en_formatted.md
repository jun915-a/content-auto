## The Core of This Topic
Fine-tuning a local LLM involves adjusting its parameters to fit specific tasks, such as question categorization, allowing for more accurate and relevant results.

## 5-Second Key Points
- **Customization**: Fine-tuning allows for tailored performance
- **Accuracy**: Improved categorization accuracy is achieved
- **Efficiency**: Local LLMs can be more efficient than cloud-based models

## Detailed Breakdown
**Training Data**
Fine-tuning requires a dataset relevant to the task at hand, in this case, question categorization. The quality and quantity of this data significantly impact the model's performance.

**Model Architecture**
The choice of LLM architecture, such as Qwen 3:0.6B, plays a crucial role in fine-tuning. Different architectures may offer better performance for specific tasks.

> Insight: The right combination of training data and model architecture can lead to significantly improved performance in question categorization tasks.
