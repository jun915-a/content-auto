## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Decompilation is the process of reverse-engineering a game's binary code to understand how it works. By doing so, developers can gain insights into the game's mechanics, optimize performance, and even create custom content. The Decomp Academy is an online platform that aims to teach this complex skill in a hands-on and accessible way.
