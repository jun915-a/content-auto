## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Furifont is a **Japanese font** that integrates **furigana** (small kana characters) directly into kanji, making reading Japanese **intuitive for beginners**. Unlike traditional methods requiring separate furigana layers, Furifont embeds them into the font itself, ensuring seamless readability across platforms.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Open-source & free**: No cost, no restrictions—ideal for personal and commercial use.
- **Automatic furigana**: No manual input needed; furigana appears alongside kanji by default.
- **Design flexibility**: Supports **customization** (colors, sizes) for educational or aesthetic projects.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1: What Is Furigana?
Furigana are small **hiragana or katakana characters** placed above or beside kanji to indicate pronunciation. They’re a lifesaver for learners, acting as a visual scaffold. Furifont **bakes these guides into the font**, so every kanji character includes its phonetic helper—no extra effort required.

**Element 2: How Furifont Works
Furifont uses **Unicode’s private-use area** to store furigana data within the font file. When rendered, the system automatically displays both kanji and furigana simultaneously. This avoids the need for plugins or overlays, making it **universally compatible** with any software that supports standard fonts.
