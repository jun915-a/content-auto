# Track Satellites Over Your Location This Week

Discover every satellite that has passed over any location on Earth in the past seven days. Simply enter a place and see the satellite traffic above you, complete with their photos.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
This project, built by a self-proclaimed satellite nerd, allows users to input any location and see a list of all satellites that have passed overhead in the last week. It utilizes SGP4 orbital propagation and data from CelesTrak to provide accurate tracking and visuals.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **See Local Satellite Traffic**: Discover satellites passing over your area.
- **Past 7 Days**: Access historical data for the last week.
- **Visuals Included**: View photos of the satellites identified.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Location Input**
Users can type in any place name, and the system will geocode it to determine the precise coordinates for tracking satellite passes. This makes it accessible for anyone curious about the sky above.

**Satellite Data & Propagation**
The service pulls up-to-date satellite orbital data from CelesTrak and uses the SGP4 model to calculate precise satellite positions for past passes.

> 💡 Insight: Understanding satellite orbits helps visualize the complex network operating above us.

**Photo Integration**
Where available, the system links to or displays photos of the identified satellites, offering a visual confirmation of what's being tracked.
