* **Point 3**: Understanding the model's limitations and biases is crucial for making informed decisions.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown

The study highlighted the importance of choosing the right model for your task. For instance, the BERT model excelled in natural language processing tasks, while the transformer model performed better in image classification. Another key takeaway was the need to carefully curate the input prompt. A well-crafted prompt can significantly improve the model's performance, while a poorly written one can lead to subpar results. This highlights the importance of understanding the model's strengths and weaknesses.
