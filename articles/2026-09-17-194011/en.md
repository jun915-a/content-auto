# 40C3 Invites Model Citizens: A New Era of Civic Tech

The Chaos Communication Congress (CCC) is calling all model citizens to 40C3, where digital rights, civic engagement, and ethical hacking will converge. Discover how this event bridges technology and society for a more transparent future.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The **40th Chaos Communication Congress (40C3)** isn’t just another tech conference—it’s a call to action for **model citizens** who believe in transparency, digital rights, and ethical participation in society. Hosted by the Chaos Computer Club (CCC), this event redefines how technology intersects with civic responsibility, inviting attendees to explore how hackers, activists, and everyday citizens can collaborate to shape a more accountable world. At its heart, 40C3 challenges participants to think critically about their role in safeguarding democracy, privacy, and innovation in an era of digital surveillance and misinformation.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Model citizens matter**: The event emphasizes that **everyone**—not just experts—plays a role in shaping ethical tech policies.
- **Hacking for good**: From **digital rights advocacy** to **civic tech hackathons**, 40C3 redefines hacking as a force for societal progress.
- **Global collaboration**: Attendees will engage with **international activists, researchers, and policymakers** to tackle shared challenges.

The event’s tagline—*‘Model Citizens’*—hints at a shift: **citizenship in the digital age requires technical literacy and activism**.

For decades, ‘hacking’ was synonymous with **cybercrime**, but 40C3 flips the script. Here, hacking is redefined as **problem-solving, innovation, and ethical intervention**. Imagine a **hackathon where participants build tools to combat misinformation** or **develop open-source voting systems**—this is the ethos of 40C3. The event will feature **civic tech projects**, from **blockchain-based transparency tools** to **privacy-preserving data platforms**, proving that hackers can be **catalysts for social change**.

> 💡 Insight: **The line between ‘hacker’ and ‘citizen’ is blurring**—this event is proof that technology isn’t just for experts; it’s a **shared responsibility**.

**Global Voices, Local Impact**
40C3 thrives on **diverse perspectives**. Speakers and attendees will include **activists from repressive regimes**, **open-source developers**, and **policymakers** grappling with digital rights laws. The event will dissect **real-world cases**, like how **AI surveillance affects marginalized communities** or how **digital tools can strengthen democratic processes**. By bringing together **global voices**, 40C3 aims to create **actionable solutions** that transcend borders—whether it’s **protecting whistleblowers** or **fighting algorithmic bias**.
