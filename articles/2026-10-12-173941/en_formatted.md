# Open-Source Routers: Viable Business or Niche Fantasy?

*Insert header image here*

Could a router pre-loaded with open-source firmware like DD-WRT become a profitable venture? We dissect the market, challenges, and why users crave this balance between freedom and convenience.

**The Core of This Topic**

The idea of a router with **DD-WRT pre-installed** taps into a growing demand for customizable, open-source networking hardware. While enthusiasts love the flexibility of flashing their own firmware, most users—especially beginners—prefer plug-and-play solutions. This creates a unique opportunity: a device that bridges the gap between ease of use and technical control. The question is: *Can a small company succeed here?*
