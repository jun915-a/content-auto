## 📈 Detailed Breakdown

**Audio Logging:** LG Smart TVs have been discovered to log audio, which means they are potentially recording your conversations even when the TV screen is turned off. This feature, designed for voice control, can be a privacy concern as it may capture sensitive information.

**Device Snooping:** The TVs also access local devices, such as cameras and other smart home gadgets. This raises concerns about the security of your personal data and the potential for unauthorized access.

> 💡 Insight: While LG claims these practices enhance user experience, the lack of transparency and user control highlights a critical privacy issue.

This issue highlights the need for stricter privacy regulations and better consumer education on smart device security. LG must address these concerns promptly to maintain user trust and ensure future privacy protections.
