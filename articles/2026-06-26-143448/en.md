The USB-C connector was designed to be a versatile, reversible, and compact solution for multiple applications, including data transfer, power delivery, and display connectivity. However, this versatility comes at the cost of increased complexity, making it difficult to achieve high-speed data transfer and reliable power delivery.

**The 10G Ethernet Module's Challenges**
The 10G Ethernet module's use of USB-C creates a host of challenges, including signal integrity issues, power delivery limitations, and heat dissipation problems. These challenges are exacerbated by the module's need to operate at high speeds and deliver high-quality data transfer.
