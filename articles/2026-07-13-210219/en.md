# Benchmarking 15 Tesla GPUs for E-Waste

Discover the real-world performance of 15 Tesla GPUs, repurposed from E-Waste, under modern workloads.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Benchmarking 15 Tesla GPUs, previously discarded as E-Waste, demonstrates their surprising performance under modern workloads. This study sheds light on the potential for repurposed GPUs in the industry, reducing electronic waste and promoting sustainability.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Repurposed GPUs can still deliver high performance.
- **Point 2**: E-Waste reduction is a pressing concern.
- **Point 3**: Sustainable solutions are worth exploring.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The study involved 15 repurposed Tesla GPUs, each tested under various workloads. The results showed that these GPUs can still deliver high performance, making them suitable for certain applications.

**Element 2**
