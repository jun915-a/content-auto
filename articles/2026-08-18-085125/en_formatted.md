The proof leverages modern tools from harmonic analysis, particularly the study of potentials and extremal configurations. By analyzing the interaction between roots and critical points through logarithmic potentials, mathematicians constructed a rigorous framework to demonstrate that the minimal distance between any root and a critical point cannot exceed 1. This approach not only resolves the conjecture but also provides a template for tackling similar problems in polynomial theory.
