Explore the fascinating project where 37,500 people drew their own versions of the world, revealing how culture, geography, and memory shape our perception of global maps.

**The Drawn World: A Global Portrait of Memory and Imagination** is a groundbreaking project where 37,500 individuals across the globe drew their own maps of the world. This initiative, spearheaded by HabibiCode, uncovers the fascinating ways in which personal, cultural, and geographical experiences influence how people visualize the planet. Beyond mere geography, these drawings tell stories of identity, misconceptions, and collective imagination.
