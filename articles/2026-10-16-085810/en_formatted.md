Sill is a revolutionary app that enables **real-time window synchronization** across your Mac, iPhone, and iPad. Whether you're coding, designing, or managing tasks, Sill ensures your workspace stays consistent and accessible, no matter which device you’re using.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: **Unified workspace** – View and control any window from any device.

- **Point 3**: **No app switching** – Work seamlessly without juggling between apps or devices.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Sill’s core feature is its ability to **mirror or overlay windows** from your Mac onto your iPhone or iPad. This means you can **read, edit, or navigate** documents, spreadsheets, or even browser tabs as if they were local. It’s like having a **second screen** for your mobile device, eliminating the need to switch between apps or devices. Perfect for developers who need to debug on mobile while coding on their Mac, or designers who want to preview layouts on different screen sizes.

**Element 2**
Beyond basic window sync, Sill introduces **interactive controls**. You can **pin windows**, adjust layouts, or even **share windows temporarily** with colleagues for quick reviews. This level of flexibility turns your iPhone or iPad into a **dynamic extension** of your Mac, rather than just a secondary device. The app also supports **multi-window setups**, allowing you to stack or arrange windows in ways that suit your workflow.

> 💡 Insight: **Sill isn’t just about syncing—it’s about redefining how you interact with your devices.** By treating your iPhone and iPad as **active participants** in your workflow, Sill reduces context-switching and keeps your focus intact.
