## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Riemann Hypothesis, if true, states that all non-trivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function lie on the "critical line" with real part 1/2. Its verification is a monumental step in understanding prime number distribution.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Riemann Zeta Function**
This function, defined for complex numbers, is central to the hypothesis. Its zeros are points where the function equals zero, and their distribution is key to prime number theory.

**The Hypothesis Itself**
It posits that all non-trivial zeros of the zeta function have a real part of exactly 1/2. Proving this has been a goal for mathematicians for over 160 years.
> 💡 Insight: The precise location of these zeros dictates the error term in the Prime Number Theorem.
