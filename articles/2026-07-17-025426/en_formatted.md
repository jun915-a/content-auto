## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The exoplanet in question is located in the habitable zone of its star, where temperatures are just right for liquid water to exist. However, the loss of helium could disrupt this delicate balance, making it difficult for life to thrive.

**Element 2**
The rate of helium loss is staggering, with estimates suggesting that the exoplanet is losing 10^9 kg/s. This is a significant amount of gas, and it could have a major impact on the planet's atmosphere.

> 💡 Insight: The loss of helium is a clear indication that the exoplanet's atmosphere is not stable, and it's a warning sign that we should take seriously.
