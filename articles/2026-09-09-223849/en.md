# Spammers Exploit ASCII Smuggling to Evade Email Filters

Microsoft reveals email spammers are now using ASCII smuggling—a technique once popular for AI attacks—to bypass spam filters and deliver malicious messages undetected. Learn how this tactic works and why it’s a growing threat.

**Microsoft warns that email spammers are increasingly adopting ASCII smuggling—a method originally used to attack AI systems—to bypass spam filters and deliver malicious content. This technique, which exploits encoding quirks, allows attackers to hide payloads in plain sight, making detection harder than ever.**
