## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Bigos, or Polish Hunter's Stew, is a traditional Polish dish that's a staple in many Polish households. The key to making a great Bigos is the combination of meats. You'll need to use a variety of sausages, including kielbasa and knockwurst, as well as beef and pork. This combination will give your Bigos a rich and savory flavor.
