# Web Protection Fails: Bad Bots Thrive Despite 'Breaking' the Open Web

*Insert header image here*

New web protection measures, while aiming to curb bad bots, risk fragmenting the internet. Discover why this approach is flawed and what it means for the future of the open web.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Efforts to combat bad bots by implementing complex, often proprietary, web access controls are fundamentally flawed. They impose friction on legitimate users and fail to address the root causes of bot activity, potentially harming the open, accessible nature of the internet.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Bot Problem Persists**: Advanced bot mitigation techniques don't eliminate malicious bots.
- **Open Web at Risk**: New methods break the open web's accessibility and interoperability.
- **Ineffective Solutions**: Current strategies create more problems than they solve.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Fragmented Access**
Many new bot protection systems create walled gardens, requiring users to pass through multiple, often intrusive, checks. This fragmented experience hinders seamless navigation and access to information across different sites.

**Circumvention Tactics**
Malicious actors are adept at finding ways around these new barriers. As soon as one gate is erected, sophisticated bots learn to bypass it, meaning the arms race continues without a true resolution.

> 💡 Insight: The focus on blocking access rather than identifying and mitigating malicious intent is a strategic misstep.

**Economic Impact**
These measures can disproportionately affect smaller businesses and non-profits that lack the resources to implement or navigate complex bot defenses, creating an uneven playing field.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Increased friction for legitimate users trying to access content.
- Continued prevalence of scraping, credential stuffing, and other bot-driven attacks.
- Potential for a less accessible and more fragmented internet experience.

## ✨ Conclusion
Instead of breaking the open web, we need smarter, more collaborative solutions that focus on identifying and stopping malicious bots without compromising the accessibility and integrity of the internet for everyone.
