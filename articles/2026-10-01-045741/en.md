In dating apps, this translates to ensuring no two users would rather be with someone else. The algorithm works by having each user rank their preferences, then iteratively proposing until all are matched in a stable configuration. This eliminates jealousy, envy, and instability—key pitfalls in traditional dating.\n\n**Element 2**\nSingapore’s app implements this algorithm by collecting ranked preferences from users (e.g., “I prefer person A over B”). The system then simulates proposals: if Person A rejects Person B’s offer to pair with Person C, Person B must find their next best option. This continues until everyone is matched in a way that no two users would swap for a better alternative. The result? A **stable, efficient** matching process—one that’s mathematically guaranteed to work.\n\n> 💡 Insight: **The algorithm doesn’t just match people randomly; it ensures fairness by preventing any user from being ‘left out’ or ‘unhappy’ in a way that could be improved.
