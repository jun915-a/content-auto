# Unlayer: Embed Email & Document Builders Seamlessly into Your Apps

*Insert header image here*

Unlayer empowers developers to integrate powerful email and document creation tools directly into their applications, streamlining content workflows for users.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Unlayer provides an embeddable toolkit for developers to add robust email and document builders to their existing applications. This allows end-users to create rich content within the app's interface, without needing separate tools.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Seamless Integration**: Add powerful builders to your app with ease.
- **Enhanced User Experience**: Empower users with intuitive content creation tools.
- **Customizable & White-labeled**: Match your brand's look and feel.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Email Builder**
Offers a drag-and-drop interface for creating professional email templates. Users can easily design visually appealing emails for marketing, notifications, and more, directly within your platform.

**Document Builder**
Enables the creation of structured documents, reports, or articles. This feature allows for rich text formatting, layout control, and embedding media, enhancing document generation capabilities.

> 💡 Insight: By embedding these builders, Unlayer drastically reduces the development effort required to offer advanced content creation features.
