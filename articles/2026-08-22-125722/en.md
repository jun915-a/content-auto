## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The app leverages your smartphone’s gyroscope, accelerometer, and GPS to pinpoint the galactic center with remarkable precision. By syncing with the night sky’s coordinates, it draws a glowing path in AR, guiding users’ gaze directly to Sagittarius A*. This is especially useful in light-polluted areas where the Milky Way is invisible to the naked eye.

**Element 2**
Beyond navigation, Galactic Compass 2 doubles as an educational tool. Users can tap on celestial objects to learn about their composition, distance from Earth, and historical significance. The augmented reality mode adds a layer of interactivity, making astronomy feel like an immersive adventure rather than a static lesson.
