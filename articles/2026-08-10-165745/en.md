## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Naming Conventions**
When it comes to boat names, there are no hard and fast rules. However, most boats follow certain naming conventions to make them easier to identify. For example, many boats are named after their size (e.g., 'Bluefin'), location (e.g., 'Island Hopper'), or type (e.g., 'Sea Dragon').

**Creative Boat Names**
If you're looking for something more creative, consider using a phrase or a word that reflects your personality or interests. For example, 'Sea You Later' or 'Merlin's Magic'. You could also use a play on words, like 'Sea-rious Business' or 'Tidal Wave of Fun'.
