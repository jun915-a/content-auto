# Unlocking Geologic Hydrogen with Zapping Rocks

Scientists have discovered a new method to stimulate geologic hydrogen, a game-changer for sustainable energy

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Stimulated geologic hydrogen is a type of hydrogen that is released from the Earth's crust, offering a promising alternative to traditional fossil fuels. Researchers have found a way to unlock this hydrogen by zapping rocks with energy, creating a sustainable and renewable source of energy.
