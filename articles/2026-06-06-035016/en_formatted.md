# Gov.uk Ditches Stripe

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The UK government has made a significant change to its online payment system, replacing Stripe with Adyen. This move is part of a broader effort to enhance the user experience on Gov.uk Pay.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **New Provider**: Adyen, a Dutch company, will handle online payments
- **Simplified Transactions**: The change aims to make payments more straightforward
- **Improved UX**: The update is part of a larger effort to enhance the overall user experience on Gov.uk Pay

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Adyen's Role**
Adyen will provide the infrastructure for online payments, offering a more streamlined experience for users. 

**Stripe's Replacement**
The decision to replace Stripe comes as the UK government seeks to optimize its payment systems and reduce complexity.
