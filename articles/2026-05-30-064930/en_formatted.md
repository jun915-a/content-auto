## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
SQLite is often overlooked as a database solution for complex workflows, but its versatility and reliability make it an ideal choice for durable workflows. With its self-contained design, SQLite eliminates the need for external dependencies and ensures seamless integration with your application.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: SQLite's ACID compliance ensures reliable transactions, even in the presence of failures.
- **Point 2**: Its self-contained nature eliminates the need for external dependencies and reduces complexity.
- **Point 3**: SQLite's flexibility allows for easy customization and integration with various programming languages.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Atomicity and Consistency**
SQLite's atomicity and consistency features ensure that transactions are processed reliably, even in the presence of failures. This means that your data remains intact, and your workflow remains consistent.

**Isolation and Durability**
SQLite's isolation and durability features ensure that concurrent transactions do not interfere with each other, and that data remains available even in the event of a failure.
