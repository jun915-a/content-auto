## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Garamantes were a Berber civilization that thrived in the Sahara desert from the 5th century BCE to the 7th century CE. Their forgotten castles, scattered across the desert, hold the keys to understanding this enigmatic culture.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: The Garamantes built sophisticated irrigation systems to support their agricultural society.
- **Point 2**: Their castles were designed to withstand the harsh desert climate and protect against invaders.
- **Point 3**: Archaeological discoveries have revealed a complex network of trade routes and cultural exchange.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The Garamantes' irrigation systems were a testament to their ingenuity and adaptability. By harnessing the power of the Sahara's underground aquifers, they were able to create fertile oases in the middle of the desert. This allowed them to build a thriving agricultural society, which in turn supported their growing population.

**Element 2**
The castles of the Garamantes were built with defense in mind. Their unique architecture, featuring thick walls and narrow windows, was designed to protect against invading armies and the harsh desert climate. These structures have stood the test of time, with many still intact today.
