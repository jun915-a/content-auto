## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Gaussian splats are a visually striking design element that can add depth and interest to any composition. They are created by printing multiple small dots in a random pattern, resulting in a speckled or blotchy effect.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Understand the principles of Gaussian distribution.
- **Point 2**: Choose the right ink and paper for optimal results.
- **Point 3**: Experiment with different dot sizes and densities.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Gaussian splats can be used to create a sense of movement or energy in a design. By adjusting the dot size and density, you can control the level of texture and visual interest.

**Element 2**
To achieve the perfect Gaussian splat, it's essential to understand the concept of Gaussian distribution. This involves calculating the probability of dots landing in a particular area, resulting in a smooth, even pattern.
