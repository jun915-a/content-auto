The idea behind this tool is to give users an idea of how far they would have to travel in any direction to reach a population of 100,000 people, assuming a uniform population distribution. This can be useful for a variety of purposes, such as planning urban development, estimating market reach, or simply understanding the scale of human settlement.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Population Distribution**
In a uniform distribution, people are spread out evenly across a given area. This is a simplification, as real-world population distributions are often complex and influenced by many factors. However, for the purpose of this tool, we'll assume a uniform distribution to make calculations easier.

**Estimating Distance**
To estimate the distance to 100,000 people, we'll use a rough estimate of the average population density of a given area. This can be a city, a country, or even a continent. By plugging in the population density and the desired population size, we can calculate the area required to reach that population.
