## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**User-Centric Control**
Castor puts you in complete control. Instead of relying on third-party servers prone to outages and content changes, you manage your own media sources, ensuring consistent access and a personalized channel lineup.

**Efficient Streaming Engine**
Built with performance in mind, Castor utilizes efficient protocols and hardware acceleration to deliver smooth, buffer-free playback, even with high-definition content. It's designed to be lighter and more responsive than many commercial solutions.

> 💡 Insight: By shifting the control to the user, Castor bypasses the common pain points of IPTV, such as stream quality degradation and service interruptions.

**Extensible Platform**
With its open-source nature, Castor is highly extensible. Developers and users can create custom plugins and integrations, expanding its functionality to include new features, data sources, and user interfaces, making it a future-proof solution.
