# Miniature Aluminium Rack Build

A step-by-step guide to crafting a 10-inch mini rack from aluminium extrusions, perfect for showcasing small components or DIY projects.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
This article documents the process of creating a 10-inch mini rack from aluminium extrusions, providing a compact and customizable solution for showcasing small components, tools, or other DIY projects.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Compact storage solution for small components.
- **Point 2**: Customizable design and size options.
- **Point 3**: Durable aluminium extrusions for long-lasting use.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The project begins with selecting the necessary aluminium extrusions and cutting them to the required lengths. The extrusions are then assembled using screws and a drill press, ensuring accuracy and precision.

**Element 2**
Once assembled, the mini rack is secured to a vertical surface using wall anchors or screws. The rack can be customized to fit specific needs, such as adding additional shelves or modifying the size.
