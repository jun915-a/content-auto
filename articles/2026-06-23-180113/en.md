# MSG Dossier: Activists Opposing Facial Recognition Targeted

Madison Square Garden Entertainment reportedly compiled dossiers on activists and lawyers protesting their use of facial recognition technology. Uncover how a major entertainment giant tracks its critics and the chilling implications for privacy and free speech.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
MSG Entertainment, led by James Dolan, allegedly created dossiers on attorneys and activists who publicly opposed its controversial use of facial recognition technology. This surveillance targeted individuals associated with legal challenges and protests, raising serious concerns about corporate power and retaliatory tactics against critics.

**Legal and Ethical Backlash**
The alleged dossier creation has sparked significant legal and ethical debate. It highlights a troubling trend of corporations using advanced tech for retaliatory purposes.
