## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Vertex Buffer Objects (VBOs)**
VBOs are a crucial part of modern OpenGL, allowing for efficient rendering of 3D graphics. They enable the transfer of vertices to the GPU, reducing the need for frequent CPU-GPU communication.

**Shaders**
Shaders are small programs that run on the GPU, responsible for transforming and rendering 3D graphics. LearnOpenGL provides an in-depth explanation of shader programming, including vertex and fragment shaders.
