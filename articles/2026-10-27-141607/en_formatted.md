## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
A seasoned Debian developer has declared they will stop submitting new packages to the NEW queue. This decision stems from the perceived excessive time and effort required for package review, which they find unsustainable given current resource constraints.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **No More NEW Uploads**: A developer is withdrawing from submitting packages to the NEW queue.
- **Review Burden**: The decision highlights concerns about the time and effort involved in package review.
- **Process Impact**: This may affect the flow of new packages into Debian's repositories.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Developer's Stance**
The developer expresses frustration with the lengthy and often demanding process of getting new packages accepted into Debian. They feel their time could be better spent on other contributions.

**The NEW Queue**
The NEW queue is the entry point for unproven packages in Debian. It requires thorough review by maintainers and uploaders to ensure quality, compliance, and security before inclusion.

> 💡 Insight: The decision points to potential bottlenecks in Debian's package onboarding process and the volunteer effort required.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Reduced number of new packages entering the Debian ecosystem, potentially slowing innovation.
- Increased pressure on other developers to pick up the review workload.
- May prompt discussions on improving the efficiency and sustainability of the package review process.

## ✨ Conclusion
This developer's decision is a significant signal about the challenges of maintaining Debian's rigorous package management standards in a volunteer-driven environment.
