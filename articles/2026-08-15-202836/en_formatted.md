## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Bézier Spline Curves**
Bézier spline curves are a powerful tool for modeling complex curves and shapes. By applying Gaussian distributions to these curves, we can create a more accurate representation of line art, taking into account the natural variability of human drawing.
 
**Gaussian Splatting**
Gaussian splatting is a technique that uses a Gaussian distribution to weigh the importance of each data point in a dataset. In the context of line art vectorization, this means that we can use the Gaussian distribution to determine the relative importance of each point in the line art, resulting in a more accurate vector representation.
