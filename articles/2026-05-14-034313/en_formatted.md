Discover why we're transitioning from GitHub to Forgejo, a more open and community-driven platform for developers.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
As a community, we've grown dissatisfied with the restrictive policies and commercialization of GitHub. It's time for a change, and we're excited to announce our move to Forgejo, a platform that aligns with our values of openness and community-driven development.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Say goodbye to restrictive policies and hello to a more open platform.
- **Point 2**: Enjoy a more community-driven approach to development.
- **Point 3**: Ditch the commercialization and focus on what matters most – the code.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
We're tired of GitHub's restrictive policies, which stifle innovation and creativity. With Forgejo, we can focus on building and contributing without the burden of corporate interests.

**Element 2**
Forgejo's community-driven approach ensures that developers have a say in the platform's direction. This means more transparent decision-making and a focus on the needs of the community.

> 💡 Insight: By choosing Forgejo, we're not just switching platforms – we're joining a movement towards a more open and collaborative future for developers.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Improved collaboration and knowledge-sharing among developers.
- Increased transparency and community involvement in platform decision-making.
- A more open and inclusive platform for developers from all backgrounds.

## ✨ Conclusion
As we bid farewell to GitHub and hello to Forgejo, we're excited to see the positive impact this change will have on our community. Join us on this journey towards a more open and collaborative future for developers.
