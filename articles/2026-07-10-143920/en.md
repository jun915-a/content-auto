# Unraveling the Late Bronze Age Collapse

A catastrophic event in the 12th century BC led to the downfall of major civilizations, leaving behind a legacy of mystery and intrigue.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Late Bronze Age Collapse was a pivotal event in human history, marking the sudden and mysterious downfall of several major civilizations in the Eastern Mediterranean, including the Mycenaeans, the Hittites, and the New Kingdom Egyptians. This collapse had a profound impact on the development of Western civilization, shaping the course of history for centuries to come.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: A massive migration of the Sea Peoples is believed to have contributed to the collapse.
- **Point 2**: The collapse was likely caused by a combination of factors, including climate change, drought, and over-reliance on trade.
- **Point 3**: The collapse marked the end of the Bronze Age and the beginning of the Iron Age.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Drought and Climate Change**
The collapse is believed to have been triggered by a severe drought that lasted for several decades, causing widespread famine and societal instability. Climate change also played a role, as the region experienced a significant shift in temperature and precipitation patterns.

**The Role of the Sea Peoples**
The Sea Peoples, a loose confederation of naval raiders, are believed to have played a significant role in the collapse. They attacked and conquered several major cities, including the city of Ugarit, which was an important center of trade and culture.

> 💡 Insight: The Late Bronze Age Collapse was a complex event with multiple causes, but it ultimately led to the downfall of several major civilizations and the beginning of a new era in human history.
