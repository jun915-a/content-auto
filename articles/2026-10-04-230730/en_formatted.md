Page tables are the backbone of virtual memory, but their memory footprint can cripple systems. Learn how they work, their hidden costs, and ways to optimize them for better performance.

**Page tables** are the invisible architects of modern computing, translating virtual addresses to physical memory. Yet, their memory consumption can balloon under heavy workloads, impacting performance and scalability. This article dives into their mechanics, trade-offs, and real-world implications—equipping you to balance efficiency and overhead.
