## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
OONI (Open Observatory of Network Interference) provides free, open-source software to investigate internet censorship. It allows users to measure the blocking of websites, apps, and network surveillance practices, contributing to a global understanding of internet freedom.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Detect Censorship**: Identify if websites or services are blocked in your network.
- **Network Insights**: Understand how your internet is being manipulated.
- **Promote Openness**: Contribute data to fight for internet freedom.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**OONI Probe App**
This is the primary tool. It runs tests on your device to check for blocking of specific websites, apps like WhatsApp or Telegram, and even DNS manipulation. It's user-friendly and designed for everyone.

**OONI Explorer**
A public database where all test results are collected. You can explore censorship events globally, see which sites are blocked where, and analyze trends over time.

> 💡 Insight: OONI's decentralized approach empowers individuals to become citizen network investigators, providing crucial data on censorship.

**Network Measurement Techniques**
OONI uses various methods, including HTTP requests, DNS queries, and TCP/IP packet analysis, to detect subtle forms of blocking and surveillance that might otherwise go unnoticed.

**Data Anonymization and Privacy**
While collecting valuable data, OONI prioritizes user privacy. Test data is anonymized, and users have control over what information is shared, ensuring safety.

> 💡 Insight: Transparency in measurement methods builds trust and encourages wider participation in documenting internet censorship.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Exposes censorship tactics used by governments and ISPs.
- Empowers activists, journalists, and researchers with data.
- Drives advocacy for digital rights and internet freedom.

## ✨ Conclusion
OONI is an essential resource for anyone concerned about internet censorship. By using their tools, you contribute to a more transparent and open internet for all.
