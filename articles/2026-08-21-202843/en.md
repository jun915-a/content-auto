# Kagi Hides Paywalled Links in Search Results

Kagi search engine introduces a new setting to filter out paywalled content, enhancing user experience and saving time by prioritizing accessible information.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Kagi Search has implemented a user-requested feature allowing users to hide search results that lead to paywalled content. This aims to streamline the search experience by ensuring users are directed to freely accessible information, reducing frustration with blocked content.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Paywall Filter**: Kagi now lets you block paywalled links.
- **User Control**: Customize search results to your preferences.
- **Better Experience**: Access information faster without hitting walls.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**New Setting Introduced**
Kagi has added a new, opt-in setting within its customization options. This allows users to actively choose whether or not to see links that require a subscription or payment to view their content. It's a direct response to user feedback.

**Enhanced Search Clarity**
By filtering out paywalled content, Kagi aims to provide a cleaner and more efficient search experience. Users can now be more confident that the results they click on will lead to accessible information, saving valuable time and avoiding dead ends.

> 💡 Insight: This feature empowers users to tailor their search results, prioritizing immediate access to information over potentially gated content.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Users save time by avoiding clicks on links that lead to paywalls.
- Search results become more relevant for those seeking free information.
- Kagi further differentiates itself by offering highly customizable search experiences.

## ✨ Conclusion
Kagi continues to prioritize user needs with its latest update, offering a powerful tool to navigate the web more effectively and access information without unnecessary barriers. This setting is a significant step towards a more user-centric search experience.
