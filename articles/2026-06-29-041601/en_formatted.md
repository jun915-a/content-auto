## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Buttolph Collection**
The collection was compiled by William Starke Rosecrans, a Civil War general, in the late 19th century. Rosecrans was fascinated by the dining habits of the time and amassed a vast collection of menus from various restaurants and eateries. Today, the collection is housed at the New York Public Library and is considered a valuable resource for food historians and enthusiasts alike.

**Types of Restaurants**
The collection features menus from a range of restaurants and eateries, including high-end dining establishments, diners, street vendors, and even ships' menus. This diversity of establishments provides a comprehensive snapshot of the culinary landscape during this period.

> 💡 Insight: The Buttolph Collection serves as a testament to the dynamic nature of American cuisine during the Gilded Age, reflecting the cultural, social, and economic changes of the time.
