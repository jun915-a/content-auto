# Australia Requires SMS Sender ID

The Australian Government has introduced a new regulation requiring registration of SMS/MMS sender IDs to combat scams and spam.

## The Core of This Topic
The Australian Government has created a register for SMS/MMS sender IDs to increase security and trust in messages.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Registration Requirement**: Mandatory for all SMS/MMS senders
- **Scam Prevention**: Reduces the risk of phishing and spam messages
- **Compliance**: Ensures senders comply with Australian regulations
## Detailed Breakdown
**Registration Process**
The registration process involves providing details about the sender and the messages being sent.
**Benefits**
The register provides an additional layer of security and helps to build trust in messages.
> Insight: This register is a significant step towards protecting Australians from scams and spam.
## Real-World Impact
- Reduction in phishing attempts
- Increased trust in messages
- Better protection for consumers
## Conclusion
The Australian Government's decision to require SMS/MMS sender ID registration is a positive step towards a safer and more secure messaging environment.
