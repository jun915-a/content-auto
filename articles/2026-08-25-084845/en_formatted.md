# iCloud+ Hide My Email Stays on iCloud.com

*Insert header image here*

Apple confirms that Hide My Email addresses generated via iCloud+ will persist on iCloud.com, offering continued privacy and flexibility for users managing their digital identity.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Apple has clarified that the custom email addresses created using the 'Hide My Email' feature within iCloud+ will remain accessible and manageable directly on iCloud.com. This ensures users have a persistent, centralized location to control these privacy-enhancing aliases.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Persistence**: Hide My Email addresses are now confirmed to be a permanent fixture on iCloud.com.
- **Centralized Management**: Users can manage all their custom addresses in one place.
- **Continued Privacy**: This feature continues to shield your real email from unwanted access.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**iCloud.com Access**
Users can now log in to iCloud.com and find a dedicated section for managing their 'Hide My Email' addresses. This provides a web-based interface for creating, deleting, and viewing existing aliases.

**Feature Continuity**
This confirmation ensures that the privacy benefits of 'Hide My Email' are not tied to specific devices but are available across the web, offering a consistent experience for managing digital privacy.

> 💡 Insight: The commitment to keeping these addresses on iCloud.com enhances user control and reinforces Apple's dedication to privacy as a core service.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- **Enhanced User Control**: Easily manage privacy aliases from any web browser without needing a specific Apple device.
- **Simplified Alias Management**: Streamlines the process of creating and revoking email forwarding addresses.
- **Improved Security Posture**: Reduces exposure of personal email addresses to potential data breaches or spam.

## ✨ Conclusion
With 'Hide My Email' addresses remaining on iCloud.com, Apple empowers users with greater control and a persistent layer of privacy for their online communications, making digital identity management more robust than ever.
