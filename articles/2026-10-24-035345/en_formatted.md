## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Expanding Powers**
This bill proposes to grant new authorities to agencies, potentially enabling them to collect and access personal information more easily, blurring the lines of what constitutes lawful surveillance.

**Privacy Erosion**
The proposed measures in Bill C-22 lack sufficient oversight and could lead to a chilling effect on freedom of expression and association, as individuals may fear their data is being monitored.
