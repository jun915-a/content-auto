## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Why Classless CSS?**
Classless themes eliminate the overhead of bloated CSS libraries. Instead of writing `btn-primary` or `.card`, you define **semantic styles** (e.g., `color: #333;`) and reuse them across projects. This approach **reduces cognitive load** and speeds up prototyping.

**How to Use CSSBed Themes**
Simply copy-paste the CSS into your project. Themes include **typography stacks, color palettes, and interactive elements**—all ready to customize. For example, a theme might offer:
- A **neutral color scheme** with 6 shades for contrast.
- **Responsive grids** using flexbox or CSS Grid.

> 💡 Insight: **Start small, scale fast**. These themes are designed to grow with your project, avoiding the 'big ball of mud' syndrome.
