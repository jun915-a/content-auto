# AI Won't Replace You: Creating Working Products is Still Your Job

The illusion that AI can generate working products has taken hold. But the truth is, AI can only go so far. Creating a working product still requires human effort and creativity.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
> The idea that AI can create working products from scratch is a myth. While AI can generate prototypes, designs, and even code, it still requires human input and oversight to turn these ideas into functional products.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: AI can generate prototypes, but they often lack usability and functionality.
- **Point 2**: Human creativity and problem-solving skills are still essential for creating working products.
- **Point 3**: AI can augment human capabilities, but it can't replace the need for human judgment and expertise.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Limitations of AI**
AI can only process and analyze data, but it can't understand the nuances of human behavior and preferences.

**The Importance of Human Judgment**
Human judgment and expertise are still essential for creating products that meet user needs and expectations.

> 💡 Insight: AI can't replace the human touch in product creation.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- AI-generated products often require significant human input to make them functional.
- Humans need to validate and iterate on AI-generated designs to ensure they meet user needs.
- AI can help streamline the product creation process, but it can't replace human creativity and problem-solving skills.

## ✨ Conclusion
Creating working products still requires human effort and creativity. While AI can augment human capabilities, it can't replace the need for human judgment and expertise. By understanding the limitations of AI, we can work with it to create products that meet user needs and exceed expectations.
