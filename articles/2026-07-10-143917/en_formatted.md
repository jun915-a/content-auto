Good tools are designed to be invisible, allowing users to focus on their tasks without distraction. They provide a seamless experience, making it easy to achieve goals.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: A good tool should have a simple and clean design, reducing cognitive load and making it easy to use.
**Element 2**: Effective tools also provide feedback, guiding users through the process and helping them understand the results.
> 💡 Insight: The best tools are those that become an extension of the user, allowing them to work efficiently and effectively.
