## 🔑 The Core of This Topic

              Quake in 13 Kilobytes is a tiny remake of the classic first-person shooter Quake, developed by a single person within the constraints of a 13 kilobyte limit.

              This project showcases the capabilities of 8-bit graphics and sound, creating an immersive experience within the limitations of an old console.

              ## 📈 Detailed Breakdown

              **Element 1**: The game's core mechanics are intact, with movement, aiming, and shooting all functioning as expected. However, the graphics and sound have been heavily modified to fit within the 13 kilobyte limit.

              **Element 2**: The level design has been simplified to accommodate the limitations of the game engine, with fewer enemies and obstacles than the original Quake.
