# Pi Pod: AI Agents That Run Securely on Your Server

Discover **Pi Pod**, a sandboxed AI agent platform that lets you run Pi’s coding assistant in isolated environments on your own infrastructure—enabling secure, composable, and private AI workflows.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Pi Pod is a cutting-edge platform that allows developers and businesses to deploy **Pi’s AI coding agent** in **isolated, sandboxed environments**—called *pods*—on **self-hosted servers**. Unlike cloud-based AI tools, Pi Pod ensures **privacy, security, and control** by running AI sessions locally, making it ideal for enterprises transitioning to AI-native workflows.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Self-hosted AI agents**: Run Pi’s coding assistant on your own infrastructure, eliminating dependency on third-party cloud services.
- **Isolated sandboxes**: Each AI session operates in a **separate, secure pod**, preventing data leaks or interference between tasks.
- **Composable environments**: Easily integrate Pi Pod with existing tools, APIs, or workflows for seamless AI automation.
- **Privacy-first**: No data leaves your server—perfect for sensitive projects or compliance-heavy industries.
- **Scalable & flexible**: Deploy pods for individual tasks or scale for enterprise-wide AI adoption.
