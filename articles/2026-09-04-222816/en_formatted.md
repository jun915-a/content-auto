## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Quad9 operates on a **decentralized model**, leveraging a global network of servers to resolve DNS queries quickly while filtering harmful content. By integrating threat intelligence from partners like Google and Cisco, it identifies and blocks over **300 million malicious domains** annually—far exceeding what most ISPs or consumer DNS services offer. This proactive filtering ensures users are redirected away from dangerous sites before any harm occurs.

**Element 2**

The service publishes **real-time threat data** and **server statistics** publicly, allowing users and researchers to verify its effectiveness. This openness contrasts sharply with many commercial DNS providers that monetize user data.
