## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Visual 6502 is a JavaScript-based simulator that lets users visualize and interact with a digital model of the 6502 microprocessor. This groundbreaking tool enables a deeper understanding of how the CPU operates, fostering a better grasp of computer architecture and programming principles.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Visualize the CPU's internal workings in real-time.
- **Point 2**: Interact with the simulator to experiment and learn.
- **Point 3**: Improve electronics education and development with this innovative tool.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Instruction Execution**
The Visual 6502 simulator shows how instructions are fetched, decoded, and executed, providing a clear understanding of the CPU's instruction set.

**Memory and I/O**
Users can observe how memory and I/O operations are handled, including data transfer and storage mechanisms.
