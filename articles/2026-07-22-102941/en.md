## 📈 Detailed Breakdown

**Medieval Castles in Europe

Medieval castles in Europe were built to serve as fortifications, residences, and symbols of power. The most famous examples include the Tower of London, Windsor Castle, and Neuschwanstein Castle. These structures showcase the ingenuity of medieval architects and the grandeur of royal power.**

**Ancient Castles in Asia

Ancient castles in Asia date back thousands of years, with examples like the Great Wall of China and the ancient city of Machu Picchu. These structures demonstrate the advanced engineering skills of ancient civilizations and their ability to create lasting legacies.**
