## 📈 Detailed Breakdown

**Element 1**

The package introduces calculus through **Julia’s built-in plotting tools**, allowing users to visualize functions like `f(x) = sin(x)` alongside their derivatives. For instance, hovering over a tangent line dynamically updates its equation, reinforcing the geometric interpretation of derivatives. This hands-on approach contrasts sharply with traditional methods that rely solely on symbolic manipulation, often leaving students disconnected from the *why* behind formulas.

**Element 2**

A standout feature is the **modular structure** of topics, from limits and continuity to multivariable calculus. Each section includes **practice problems with instant feedback**, encouraging iterative learning. For example, solving `∫x² dx` isn’t just about memorizing `x³/3 + C`—it’s about seeing the area under the curve update in real time as parameters change. This aligns with modern pedagogical research emphasizing **active learning** over passive consumption.
