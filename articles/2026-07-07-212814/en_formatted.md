## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The system uses advanced computer vision technology to detect and track the driver's face, allowing it to monitor for signs of distraction. This includes looking away from the road, using a phone, or eating while driving.

**Element 2**
The system will alert the driver if it detects any signs of distraction, prompting them to refocus on the road. It will also provide feedback to the driver if they fail to respond, helping to prevent accidents.
