# The Hidden Crisis: Why Used GPU Clusters Are Priceless (and Worthless)

The market for used GPU clusters is a black box—no pricing benchmarks, no transparency, and no way to know if you're overpaying or stealing a deal. Here’s why.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The used GPU cluster market is plagued by opacity, making it nearly impossible to determine fair market value. Buyers and sellers operate in the dark, leading to unpredictable pricing, distrust, and missed opportunities in an industry racing toward AI dominance.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **No pricing benchmarks**: Unlike cars or real estate, used GPU clusters lack standardized valuation tools.
- **Highly customized assets**: Each cluster’s value depends on specs, age, and software—unique conditions defy simple comparisons.
- **Rapid depreciation**: GPUs lose 50%+ of their value in 18 months due to Moore’s Law and tech obsolescence.
- **Lack of liquidity**: Selling used clusters is slow, often requiring brokers or auctions with unpredictable outcomes.
- **AI gold rush fuels demand**: Yet, the supply side remains chaotic, with no clear way to match buyers and sellers efficiently.
