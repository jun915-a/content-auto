# Voyager 2 Lives On

NASA extends lifespan of 48-year-old Voyager 2 probe with innovative fix.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
NASA engineers have successfully extended the lifespan of the 48-year-old Voyager 2 probe by implementing a new fix to prevent its power source from failing. Voyager 2, launched in 1977, has been traveling through interstellar space since 2018, sending vital data about the outer reaches of our solar system.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Voyager 2's power source was at risk of failing due to radiation damage.
- **Point 2**: NASA engineers developed a new fix to protect the power source.
- **Point 3**: The fix will extend Voyager 2's lifespan by another year.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Power Source Fix**
The new fix involves redirecting power from a redundant instrument to the failing power source, effectively bypassing the damaged component. This solution allows Voyager 2 to continue collecting data without interruption.

**Radiation Protection**
The fix also includes a new radiation shield to protect the power source from further damage. This shield will help prevent any future power failures, ensuring Voyager 2 remains operational for a longer period.
