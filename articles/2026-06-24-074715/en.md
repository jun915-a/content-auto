# The Unassuming AI Behind Elden Ring's Immersive World

Discover the fascinating story of how developers used low-tech AI to bring Elden Ring's vast open world to life, defying expectations and creating a truly immersive experience.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Elden Ring's AI is often overlooked, but it plays a vital role in creating the game's immersive atmosphere. Rather than relying on cutting-edge AI technology, the developers opted for a low-tech approach that focused on clever design and clever use of tools. This approach allowed them to create a world that feels alive and responsive, without the need for complex AI algorithms.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The developers used a combination of procedural generation and manual placement to create the game's terrain and environments. This approach allowed them to create a vast and diverse world, while still maintaining a sense of coherence and consistency.

**Element 2**: The game's AI is also used to create a sense of realism and immersion. For example, enemies will often patrol predetermined routes, and NPCs will interact with each other in a believable way. This creates a sense of depth and richness in the game world, making it feel more alive and responsive.

> 💡 Insight: The key to Elden Ring's success is its ability to balance complexity and simplicity. By using low-tech AI methods, the developers were able to create a game world that feels complex and immersive, without the need for complex algorithms.
