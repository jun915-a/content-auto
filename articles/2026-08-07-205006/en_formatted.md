## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The IBM 604 trigger tube module was a crucial component of the 1948 IBM 604 calculator system, a pioneering machine that paved the way for modern computers. At its core, this module is a vacuum-tube flip-flop, a type of bistable multivibrator that stores a single bit of information.

**The Flip-Flop Circuit**
The flip-flop circuit is the heart of the trigger tube module. It consists of a pair of vacuum tubes, a 6SN7GT and a 12SN7GT, which work together to store a single bit of information. The circuit is designed to be highly reliable and maintainable, with multiple levels of redundancy to prevent data loss.

**The Power Supply**
The module requires a specialized power supply to function, which is generated by a separate component of the IBM 604 system. The power supply is designed to provide a stable and reliable voltage source to the trigger tube module.
