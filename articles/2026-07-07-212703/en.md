# 98% Isn't Enough

Discover why 98% isn't as impressive as it seems and how it can impact real-world scenarios, from business to everyday life.

## The Core of This Topic
98% seems like a high percentage, but it means 2% of cases are not covered, which can be significant in certain contexts.

## 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Small percentages can add up
- **Point 2**: High percentages don't guarantee success
- **Point 3**: Context matters in percentage evaluation

## Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
In many fields, a 98% success rate is considered excellent, but it still leaves room for improvement and potential issues.

**Element 2**
For instance, in medicine, a 98% cure rate is great, but the 2% who don't recover can be a significant concern.

> Insight: Perfection is often the goal, even if it's not always achievable.

## Real-World Impact
- Financial losses from the 2% margin can be substantial
- Reputation damage from failures, no matter how small the percentage
- Potential for improvement is always present

## Conclusion
In conclusion, while 98% may seem like a high achievement, it's the remaining 2% that can make all the difference in various aspects of life.
