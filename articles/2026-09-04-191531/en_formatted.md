## 📈 Detailed Breakdown

**Element 1: Why Use deSEC Over Traditional DNS?**
Most ISPs and commercial DNS providers (like Google or Cloudflare) log user queries or inject ads into search results. deSEC eliminates these risks by **routing all requests through encrypted tunnels**, ensuring no third party—including your ISP—can monitor your online activity. **Element 2: How Does deSEC Ensure Security?**
deSEC leverages **DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) and DNS-over-TLS** protocols, which encrypt your DNS requests end-to-end. This prevents man-in-the-middle attacks and ISP throttling. Additionally, the service **doesn’t log IP addresses or query details**, aligning with strict privacy standards. The open-source nature of deSEC means security flaws are quickly identified and patched by the community, unlike proprietary systems where vulnerabilities might go unaddressed for longer.
