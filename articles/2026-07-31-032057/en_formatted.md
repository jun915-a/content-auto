## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1: Anthem Data Breach**
In 2015, Anthem Inc. suffered a massive data breach, exposing sensitive information of over 79 million customers. The breach was caused by a phishing attack that compromised an IT employee's credentials.

**Element 2: Equifax Breach**
In 2017, Equifax, one of the largest credit reporting agencies, experienced a massive data breach, exposing the sensitive information of over 147 million people. The breach was caused by a vulnerability in Apache Struts, which was exploited by attackers.

> 💡 Insight: Both incidents highlight the importance of regular software updates and employee training in preventing breaches.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
* The Anthem breach led to significant changes in the way Anthem handled employee credentials and data security.
* The Equifax breach led to major changes in the way Equifax handled data security and employee training.
* Both incidents led to increased awareness of the importance of cybersecurity in the business world.

## ✨ Conclusion
Investigating real-world cybersecurity incidents is crucial for learning from the past and improving future security evaluations. By understanding the key takeaways from these incidents, we can develop more effective security strategies and prevent similar breaches from happening in the future.
