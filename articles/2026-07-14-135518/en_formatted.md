## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Acknowledge your flaws**: Recognize your weaknesses and areas for improvement.
- **Challenge your biases**: Be willing to question your assumptions and consider alternative perspectives.
- **Embrace uncertainty**: Understand that not everything is certain and be comfortable with ambiguity.
