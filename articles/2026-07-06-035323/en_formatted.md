# Game Ownership Matters

*Insert header image here*

The debate between physical and digital games goes beyond format, it's about owning your games and having control over your library

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The core issue is not the format of the game, but rather the concept of ownership and what it means to truly own a game.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Ownership gives players control
- **Point 2**: Digital games can be revoked
- **Point 3**: Physical games provide a sense of tangibility

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Owning a physical copy of a game allows players to lend, trade, or sell it as they see fit, giving them a sense of freedom and flexibility.

**Element 2**
Digital games, on the other hand, are often subject to the terms and conditions of the platform they are purchased on, which can lead to restrictions on how they can be used.

> 💡 Insight: The ability to preserve and play games without relying on online servers is a key aspect of game ownership.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Players may lose access to their games if the platform shuts down
- The resale value of physical games can be higher than digital ones
- Game preservation is crucial for the gaming community

## ✨ Conclusion
Ultimately, the debate between physical and digital games comes down to what it means to own a game and have control over your gaming experience.
