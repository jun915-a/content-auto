## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Iconic Design Influence**
The new 2CV concept draws heavily from its predecessor's distinctive, utilitarian charm. Expect rounded shapes, a simple silhouette, and perhaps even some of the original's quirky design elements, reimagined for the electric era.

**Electric Powertrain and Range**
While specifics are scarce, the focus is on an efficient electric powertrain suitable for city driving. It's expected to offer a practical range for daily commutes, prioritizing affordability and low running costs over long-distance capability.

The project emphasizes making electric mobility accessible. By leveraging simpler design principles and focusing on core functionality, Citroën aims to offer a compelling alternative to more expensive EVs.
