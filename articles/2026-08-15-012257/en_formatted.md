- **Point 2**: Increased complexity
- **Point 3**: Lack of customization
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The new interface, while visually appealing, has made it harder for users to navigate and find essential features.
**Element 2**: The removal of certain options has limited the ability of users to personalize their experience.
> 💡 Insight: These changes have resulted in a steeper learning curve.
## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Reduced productivity due to increased time spent learning the new system
- Higher frustration levels among users
- Potential decrease in overall satisfaction
## ✨ Conclusion
Understanding these factors can help in addressing the issues and improving the user experience of Opus 5.
