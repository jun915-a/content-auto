# GrapheneOS Overhauls Default Apps & Introduces Secure Clipboard

GrapheneOS revolutionizes user privacy with a major update: **overhauled default apps** and a **secure clipboard** to combat clipboard hijacking. Discover how these changes redefine Android security.

**GrapheneOS** has just rolled out a groundbreaking update that reshapes the landscape of Android security. By **replacing default apps** with privacy-focused alternatives and introducing a **secure clipboard**, the team has taken a bold step toward protecting users from **malicious clipboard hijacking**—a growing threat in modern mobile ecosystems. This move cements GrapheneOS as a **paragon of privacy-first design** in an era where digital security is increasingly compromised.
