The Gaia spacecraft has been instrumental in providing accurate data on the positions and distances of stars in the Milky Way galaxy. This data is crucial for creating a stellar navigation chart.
**Stellar Navigation**
Stellar navigation is the process of using the positions and distances of stars to navigate through space. This method is essential for interstellar travel, where traditional navigation methods are insufficient.
