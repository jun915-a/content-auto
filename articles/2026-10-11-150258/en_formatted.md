**Element 2**

ExxonMobil **actively lobbied against climate regulations**, contributed to political campaigns opposing climate action, and **funded media campaigns** that cast doubt on the legitimacy of climate science. This coordinated effort helped shape public opinion and policy decisions, ensuring that **fossil fuel dependence remained unchallenged** for far too long.
