# H.264: Unpacking the Magic of Video Compression

Unlock the secrets behind H.264, the ubiquitous video codec. Discover how it achieves incredible compression without sacrificing quality, revolutionizing streaming and video delivery.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
H.264, also known as AVC (Advanced Video Coding), is a video compression standard. Its magic lies in exploiting spatial and temporal redundancies in video streams. It achieves high compression ratios by predicting frames and encoding only the differences, alongside sophisticated techniques for transforming and quantizing residual data.
