# Longer Bulb Life: A Trade-Off for Worse Performance

Discover how extending a bulb's lifespan often comes at the cost of its brightness and efficiency. Explore the surprising science behind this inverse relationship.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Extending a bulb's lifespan is achieved by running it at a lower temperature. This reduces filament evaporation, a primary failure mechanism. However, this lower operating temperature also significantly reduces the bulb's light output and energy efficiency.
