# Show HN: StartupWiki – A Free Alternative to Crunchbase

StartupWiki offers a free startup database designed to simplify the process of discovering and researching companies.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
StartupWiki is an open-source database aimed at providing easy access to company information, serving as a free alternative to Crunchbase. Its primary goal is to make startup research more accessible and user-friendly.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Centralized platform for startup data
- **Point 2**: User-friendly interface and search functionality
- **Point 3**: Free and open-source

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: StartupWiki's data is sourced from public information and user contributions, allowing it to stay up-to-date without relying on paid subscriptions. The platform also offers features for users to contribute and correct existing data, promoting transparency and accuracy.

**Element 2**: The website's design prioritizes ease of use, making it accessible to both tech-savvy users and those new to startup research. Users can browse companies by name, location, or category, and the search functionality is intuitive and efficient.

> 💡 Insight: By providing a free and user-friendly platform, StartupWiki aims to democratize access to startup data and promote innovation.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Facilitates innovation and entrepreneurship by providing access to startup data
- Encourages user engagement and community building through contribution features
- Offers a free alternative to paid startup databases

## ✨ Conclusion
StartupWiki has the potential to revolutionize the way we approach startup research, making it more accessible and user-friendly. With its free and open-source model, it's an exciting development in the startup ecosystem.
