By leveraging AMD's RDNA 3 architecture and a custom silicon design, the MI455X aims to provide a significant boost in performance and energy efficiency compared to its predecessors.

The MI455X features a massive 48 GB of HBM2e memory, providing a significant boost in bandwidth and capacity compared to previous generations. This allows for more complex AI models to be run on the accelerator, and with reduced memory constraints.

**Processing Power**
With a peak performance of up to 5.5 TFLOPS, the MI455X is well-suited for demanding AI workloads such as computer vision, natural language processing, and recommendation systems. Additionally, the accelerator's custom silicon design enables efficient processing and reduced latency.
