## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Pareto principle, or 80/20 rule, states that 80% of results come from 20% of efforts. In Mario's world, this can be seen in how a small portion of power-ups and mushrooms lead to a large portion of progress.
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: 80% of results from 20% of efforts
- **Point 2**: Key power-ups drive progress
- **Point 3**: Efficiency is key to success
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The Pareto principle can be applied to many areas, including business and personal productivity, where focusing on key tasks can lead to significant gains.
**Element 2**: In Mario's world, this principle is evident in the distribution of power-ups and obstacles, where a small number of key items can greatly impact the game's progress.
> 💡 Insight: By identifying and focusing on the key 20% of efforts, individuals can achieve significantly more with less.
