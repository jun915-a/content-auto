## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Scalable Context Without Compromise**
Traditional LLMs struggle with **context decay**—relying on sliding windows that discard older data. Step 5 Preview’s **1M-token window** eliminates this by leveraging MoE’s ability to **distribute memory across experts**, preserving long-term dependencies. This is critical for tasks like **legal document analysis** or **medical record synthesis**, where granular context is non-negotiable. The model’s **token efficiency** ensures responses remain **latency-conscious**, a rare balance in large-context systems.

**MoE’s Hidden Advantage: Sparsity**
Unlike dense models that activate every neuron, MoE **dynamically engages only relevant experts** (e.g., 8 out of 64). This **sparse activation** slashes compute costs while maintaining performance. For example, processing a **1M-token input** might only require **10% of the model’s capacity**, making it viable for cloud deployments without exorbitant infrastructure. The trade-off? **Specialization**: Experts excel in niche domains (e.g., code, math) but may lag in broader tasks—though Step 5 Preview’s **routing mechanism** mitigates this via **ensemble learning**.
