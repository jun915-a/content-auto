## The Core of This Topic
Denmark's new education policy requires high schoolers to verbally defend their written assignments, aiming to enhance critical thinking and public speaking skills.

## 5-Second Key Points
- **Improved Critical Thinking**: Students will learn to articulate their thoughts and ideas
- **Enhanced Public Speaking**: Verbal defenses will boost students' confidence in presenting their work
- **Better Preparedness**: This approach will prepare students for real-world scenarios where they need to defend their ideas

## Detailed Breakdown
**The New Approach**
The policy focuses on developing students' ability to clearly express their thoughts and ideas, making them more effective communicators.

**The Benefits**
This approach will help students become more confident and self-assured in their abilities, leading to better academic performance and personal growth.

> Insight: By defending their assignments, students will learn to think critically and approach problems from different angles.

- Improved ability to articulate thoughts and ideas

Denmark's new policy is a significant step towards preparing high schoolers for success in their future careers and personal lives.
