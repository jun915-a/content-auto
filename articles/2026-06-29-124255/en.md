# Serving Zero-Downtime Kubernetes with Federated Clusters

Learn how Linkerd's federated clusters enable zero-downtime Kubernetes deployments and the benefits this brings.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Federating clusters for zero-downtime Kubernetes is a game-changer for high-availability applications. By enabling seamless traffic routing between clusters, developers can achieve zero-downtime deployments, reducing the risk of service disruptions and improving overall user experience.
