# Migrating CDC to Postgres: Lessons Learned

Discover how we successfully migrated Change Data Capture (CDC) into Postgres, ensuring seamless data replication and mirroring for our customers.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
At Snowflake, we've been pushing the boundaries of data replication and mirroring. One key challenge we faced was migrating Change Data Capture (CDC) into Postgres. In this article, we'll delve into how we achieved this feat and the lessons we learned along the way.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: We leveraged Postgres's built-in CDC capabilities to replicate data.
- **Point 2**: Our team worked closely with the Postgres community to overcome technical hurdles.
- **Point 3**: The migration resulted in significant performance improvements.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Our journey started with a thorough analysis of Postgres's CDC capabilities. We discovered that Postgres offered a robust built-in CDC feature that could handle our high-volume data replication needs. However, we soon realized that this feature wasn't well-documented, which made it challenging to implement.

**Element 2**
To overcome this hurdle, we reached out to the Postgres community for assistance. The community proved to be invaluable, providing us with insights and expertise that helped us navigate the technical complexities of CDC. This collaboration not only accelerated our development process but also ensured that our solution was robust and scalable.

> 💡 Insight: The key to successful migration lies in understanding the strengths and weaknesses of the underlying technology and leveraging community expertise when needed.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Improved data replication and mirroring for our customers.
- Enhanced scalability and performance of our data pipeline.
- Strengthened collaboration with the Postgres community.

## ✨ Conclusion
Migrating CDC to Postgres was a challenging but rewarding experience. By leveraging Postgres's built-in capabilities and tapping into the expertise of the Postgres community, we were able to achieve seamless data replication and mirroring. This journey has taught us the importance of understanding the underlying technology and seeking help when needed. As we continue to push the boundaries of data replication and mirroring, we're confident that our lessons learned will serve as a valuable guide for others embarking on similar journeys.
