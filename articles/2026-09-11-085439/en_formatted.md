- **Countermeasures**: Strategies range from legal frameworks to technical solutions, all aimed at curbing AI misuse and protecting users.

**Automated Scams and Fraud**
AI-powered scams, such as automated phishing and identity theft, are on the rise. The document highlights the importance of developing AI-based solutions to counter these threats, leveraging machine learning for fraud detection.
