## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Electric Propulsion Redefines Aviation**
The aircraft, named the **Alice**, leverages **four electric motors** and **propellers** for silent, vibration-free flight. Unlike traditional planes, it produces **no exhaust emissions**, making it ideal for **urban air mobility and eco-conscious travelers**. The **battery pack**, weighing over **1,600 kg**, provides enough energy for flights up to **270 miles (435 km)**, sufficient for most regional routes. This innovation addresses one of aviation’s biggest challenges: **reducing carbon emissions without sacrificing performance**.

**A Battery Revolution for Longer Flights**
One of the biggest hurdles in electric aviation has been **battery range and weight**. However, Eviation’s team has optimized the **energy density** of their lithium-ion batteries, allowing the Alice to carry passengers while maintaining **efficient cruising speeds**. Future iterations may integrate **solid-state batteries**, further extending range and reducing charging times. This progress is critical for **scaling electric aviation** from experimental prototypes to **commercial fleets**.
