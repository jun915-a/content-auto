## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Nintendo Switch 2, like its predecessor, relies on USB-C's DisplayPort Alternate Mode for video output. This allows a simple USB-C hub, designed for laptops, to act as a video adapter and peripheral hub, bypassing the need for Nintendo's proprietary, and often expensive, dock.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **USB-C PD & DP Alt Mode**: The key is a USB-C hub supporting Power Delivery and DisplayPort Alternate Mode.
- **HDMI Output**: Enables connection to any TV or monitor with an HDMI port.
- **Peripheral Support**: Allows for USB accessories like controllers and Ethernet adapters.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**USB-C Hub Functionality**
A standard USB-C hub leverages DisplayPort Alternate Mode to carry video signals over the USB-C connection. Combined with Power Delivery, it can charge the Switch 2 while simultaneously outputting video.

**HDMI Adapter Integration**
The crucial component is the HDMI port on the hub. This port directly converts the DisplayPort signal into an HDMI signal, making it compatible with virtually any modern display.

> 💡 Insight: The versatility of USB-C means many existing accessories can be repurposed, saving money and reducing e-waste.

**Power Delivery (PD) Passthrough**
Ensuring the hub supports Power Delivery passthrough is vital. This allows you to connect a charger to the hub, which then powers both the Switch 2 and the hub's functions.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- **Cost Savings**: Significantly cheaper than purchasing an official Nintendo dock.
- **Portability**: A compact hub is easier to carry than the bulky official dock.
- **Enhanced Connectivity**: Adds ports like USB-A, Ethernet, and SD card readers to the Switch 2.
