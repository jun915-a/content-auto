Bambu Lab is under fire for allegedly abusing the open source social contract, sparking controversy and debate within the community

Bambu Lab's actions have raised concerns about the exploitation of open source software, with many feeling that the company is not giving back to the community.

- **Financial Gain**: The company is profiting from open source software
- **Community Backlash**: The community is speaking out against Bambu Lab's actions

**Element 1**: Bambu Lab's business model relies heavily on open source software, but the company is not providing adequate support or contributions.

**Element 2**: The open source community is built on a social contract of mutual respect and reciprocity, which Bambu Lab is allegedly violating.

The controversy surrounding Bambu Lab serves as a reminder of the importance of upholding the open source social contract and respecting the community that makes it possible.
