# Streamline Your Report Sharing with Pagecast

Say goodbye to temporary localhost links and hello to permanent Cloudflare Pages shares with Pagecast, a local CLI for publishing Markdown/HTML reports.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Pagecast is a local CLI that helps you publish Markdown/HTML reports directly to Cloudflare Pages, providing a permanent share link for your reports.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Automate report sharing with a single command
- **Point 2**: Publish to Cloudflare Pages for easy sharing
- **Point 3**: Supports Markdown and HTML reports

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
Pagecast was born out of a need to share reports generated by AI tools like Claude and Codex. With Pagecast, you can turn your localhost tunnels into permanent links, making it easier to collaborate and share your findings.

This CLI is designed to be user-friendly and easy to integrate into your workflow. Simply run the command, and Pagecast will take care of the rest.
