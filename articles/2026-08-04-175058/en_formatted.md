Discover how AI-generated images can discourage readers from engaging with your blog content and learn what you can do instead

## The Core of This Topic
AI-generated images can be off-putting due to their lack of authenticity and personal touch. They may come across as insincere or even deceptive, which can erode trust between the blogger and their audience.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Lack of Authenticity**: AI images can seem fake
- **Limited Emotional Connection**: They often fail to evoke emotions
- **Decreased Trust**: Readers may question the blog's credibility
## Detailed Breakdown
**Human Touch**
A blog with human-created images conveys a sense of effort and dedication, making the content more relatable and engaging.
**Originality**
Unique, handmade images can set a blog apart from others and make it more memorable.
> Insight: Using real images can significantly enhance a blog's appeal and credibility.
## Real-World Impact
- Less engagement from readers
- Lower conversion rates
- Negative perception of the blog
## Conclusion
To build a loyal audience, focus on creating authentic, high-quality content that resonates with your readers.
