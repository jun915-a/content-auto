# England's Forgotten Colony in South America

Uncover the fascinating story of Willoughbyland, a short-lived English colony in South America, and its lasting impact on history.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Willoughbyland was a short-lived English colony in the Guiana region of South America, which existed from 1680 to 1689. It was established by Robert Venables and his companion, William Stewart, who had previously led a failed expedition to the region.
