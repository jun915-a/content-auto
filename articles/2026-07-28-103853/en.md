# Japan Hit by 7.1 Earthquake

A 7.1 magnitude earthquake strikes Japan, causing widespread damage and triggering tsunami warnings, with multiple casualties reported

## The Core of This Topic
A powerful 7.1 magnitude earthquake has struck Japan, causing significant damage and disruption. The earthquake's epicenter was located off the coast of Japan, resulting in a tsunami warning being issued for several coastal regions.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Magnitude**: 7.1
- **Location**: Off the coast of Japan
- **Tsunami Warning**: Issued for multiple coastal regions
## Detailed Breakdown
**Earthquake Details**
The earthquake occurred at a depth of approximately 60 kilometers, with its epicenter located off the coast of Japan. The quake was felt across multiple prefectures, with reports of widespread damage and disruption.
**Tsunami Warning**
A tsunami warning was issued for several coastal regions, with residents advised to evacuate immediately. The warning has since been downgraded, but residents are still advised to remain vigilant.
