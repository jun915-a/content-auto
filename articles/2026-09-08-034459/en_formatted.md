## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Audio Surveillance**: LG's smart TVs have been found to continue logging audio even after the screen has been turned off. This means that conversations and sounds in the vicinity of the TV are being recorded and potentially transmitted elsewhere.

**Device Monitoring**: The TVs also access and monitor connected devices, such as smart speakers or other IoT devices, effectively creating a network of surveillance devices within a user's home.
