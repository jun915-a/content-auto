## Detailed Breakdown
**Homomorphic Encryption**
Homomorphic encryption is a form of encryption that enables computations to be performed on ciphertext, generating an encrypted result that, when decrypted, matches the result of operations performed on the plaintext.
**Private AI Models**
Private AI models can be trained using homomorphic encryption, ensuring the security and privacy of sensitive data.
