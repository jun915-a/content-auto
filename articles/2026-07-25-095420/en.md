# Hormuz in Chaos

A simulation of the Strait of Hormuz closure reveals the fragility of global oil trade.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Strait of Hormuz is a crucial waterway connecting the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman, through which approximately 20% of the world's oil passes. The closure of this strait would have catastrophic effects on the global oil market, leading to supply chain disruptions and price hikes.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: The simulation shows a 20% reduction in global oil supply.
- **Point 2**: Prices surge by up to 50% in the first week.
- **Point 3**: The impact is felt across multiple industries, including transportation and manufacturing.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The simulation uses real-world oil trade data to model the effects of a closure. It takes into account the various factors that influence oil prices, including supply and demand, geopolitical tensions, and economic indicators.

**Element 2**
The results of the simulation are stark. In the first week, oil prices rise by up to 50%, leading to a surge in inflation and a decrease in economic activity. The impact is felt across multiple industries, including transportation and manufacturing.

> 💡 Insight: The closure of the Strait of Hormuz highlights the fragility of global oil trade. The simulation shows that even a temporary closure can have far-reaching consequences for the global economy.
