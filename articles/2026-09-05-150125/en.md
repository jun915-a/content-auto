## 📈 Detailed Breakdown

**Element 1: Fretboard as a Number Line**
The guitar fretboard can be seen as a number line, with each fret representing a number. By moving up the frets, you progress through the number sequence, offering a visual representation of arithmetic progression.

**Element 2: Multiplication through Fret Patterns**
When you play a chord, you're essentially creating a pattern of frets. These patterns can be used to demonstrate multiplication. For example, playing a chord with your fingers on frets 3, 5, and 7 is like multiplying by 3, as you're essentially creating a pattern of every third fret.
