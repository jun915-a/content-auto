Cursor has removed cost information from the usage page and CSV export, affecting users who rely on this data to manage their token usage. This change has caused confusion and inconvenience for many users.

* **Point 1**: Cost information is no longer visible on the usage page or in CSV exports.
* **Point 2**: Users are now unable to track their token usage costs.
* **Point 3**: This change affects users who rely on cost data for budgeting and management.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown

**Element 1**
The removal of cost information from the usage page and CSV export has made it difficult for users to track their token usage costs. This is a critical piece of information for users who rely on Cursor for managing their token usage.

**Element 2**
Users are now unable to see the cost of their token usage, making it challenging to budget and manage their resources. This change has caused frustration and inconvenience for many users.
