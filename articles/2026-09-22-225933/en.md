# Discord’s New Age Verification: Safer for All Users

Discord is enhancing age verification to ensure teens and adults enjoy a safer, more tailored experience. Learn how the platform will confirm age groups and adapt features accordingly—without compromising user privacy or fun.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Discord is rolling out updated age verification methods to better protect younger users while keeping the platform engaging for adults. The goal? A safer, more age-appropriate experience for everyone—without sacrificing the community-driven vibe Discord is known for.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Age verification will now use **phone number or email confirmation** (optional but recommended) to assign users to the correct age group.
- **Point 2**: Teens (under 13) and younger adults (13–17) will see **restricted features** like server roles, DMs, and some app integrations.
- **Point 3**: Adults (18+) will retain **full access** to all Discord features while enjoying **enhanced moderation tools** for safer spaces.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Discord’s new system replaces manual age self-reporting with a **two-step verification process**. Users under 13 (or those in regions where age laws differ) will be prompted to confirm their age via a **phone number or email**—a step designed to reduce inaccuracies. This data is **not shared publicly** and is used solely to determine feature access. The platform emphasizes that **no personal info is sold or misused**, focusing instead on creating a safer environment.

**Element 2**
The biggest change? **Feature restrictions for teens**. Younger users (13–17) will have **limited access to server roles, direct messages (DMs) from adults**, and certain app integrations (like some games or bots). This aligns with **COPPA and other child protection laws**, ensuring teens interact in age-appropriate ways. Meanwhile, adults gain **better moderation controls**, such as **automated age-based warnings** in servers with mixed audiences.

> 💡 Insight: **Privacy meets safety**. Discord’s approach balances **user trust** (no forced data sharing) with **legal compliance**, proving that tech platforms can innovate without sacrificing ethics.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- **Safer teen communities**: Fewer risks of adult predation or inappropriate content in teen-friendly spaces.
- **Clearer adult experiences**: No accidental restrictions—adults can fully engage without worrying about feature blocks.
- **Global adaptability**: Features adjust based on **local age laws**, ensuring compliance worldwide while keeping Discord’s core functionality intact.

## ✨ Conclusion
Discord’s update isn’t about control—it’s about **empowering users**. By making age verification **simple, secure, and optional**, the platform ensures teens stay safe while adults enjoy uninterrupted access. The shift reflects Discord’s commitment to **growth without compromise**, proving that safety and community can coexist—without sacrificing the fun.
