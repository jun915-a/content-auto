## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Gameplay**
Fly By's gameplay is simple yet engaging. Players control a biplane, navigating through various challenges and obstacles. The goal is to fly as far as possible, collecting coins and power-ups along the way. 

**Visuals and Audio**
The game boasts a retro, pixelated art style, reminiscent of classic arcade games. The visuals are simple yet effective, with bright colors and smooth animations. The audio complements the visuals, featuring upbeat, nostalgic music and sound effects.
