Yet, this precision creates a **new paradox**: overproduction (e.g., 10% of global food wasted) coexists with **localized shortages** in regions with failed cold chains.\n\n**Element 2: Who Gets Rationed Now?**\nThe old system’s fairness was **arbitrary** (coupons, points). The question isn’t *if* we’ll ration again (climate change will force it), but **who writes the new rules**—and whether they’ll be fair. The fight for the future isn’t about **ending rationing**, but **democratizing access** in a world where **abundance is the new luxury**."
