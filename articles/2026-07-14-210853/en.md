# The Hidden Costs of Open Source in the AI-Driven Era

Open-source software’s ‘zero-cost’ myth crumbles under AI’s agentic demands. Licensing, compliance, and hidden dependencies now demand strategic foresight—especially as autonomy reshapes development.

**The zero-cost fallacy: open-source software in the agentic era**

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Open-source software (OSS) has long been celebrated for its perceived ‘zero-cost’ benefits—freedom from licensing fees, transparency, and community-driven innovation. However, in the **agentic era**, where AI-driven systems operate autonomously, this fallacy becomes dangerously misleading. The true costs—legal, operational, and ethical—are now front and center, forcing organizations to rethink their reliance on OSS in a world where **autonomy, scalability, and compliance** are non-negotiable.
