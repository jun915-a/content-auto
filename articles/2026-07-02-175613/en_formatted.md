# Mail Memories: Rescue Your Gmail Photos with This Desktop App

Rediscover cherished photos buried in your Gmail archive. Mail Memories is a new desktop app designed to help you easily find and save those precious visual memories before they're lost.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Mail Memories is a desktop application that scans your Gmail account to locate and extract photos. It's built for users who have accumulated years of email history and want to preserve the visual content within their inboxes.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Find Lost Photos**: Quickly locate images hidden in your Gmail.
- **Easy Export**: Download your photos in bulk or individually.
- **Preserve Memories**: Safeguard your visual history.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Gmail Photo Discovery**
The app connects securely to your Gmail account, using its search capabilities to identify emails containing image attachments or embedded photos. It filters through years of communication to pinpoint visual content.

**User-Friendly Interface**
Mail Memories offers a clean and intuitive interface. Users can browse found photos, select which ones to keep, and export them to their local storage with a few clicks, making the process straightforward.
