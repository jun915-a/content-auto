Despite the digital age’s rise in fraud, PDF forgeries are shockingly uncommon. This article dives into why tampering with PDFs—once a low-effort scam—has become harder than expected, backed by real-world data and technical hurdles.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
PDF forgeries, the act of altering digital documents to deceive, are far less common than many might assume. In 2022, research revealed that while digital fraud is rampant, tampering with PDFs—once a seemingly easy scam—has proven surprisingly difficult to execute at scale. This phenomenon stems from a mix of technical barriers, user awareness, and the inherent complexity of forging digital signatures and metadata without detection.
