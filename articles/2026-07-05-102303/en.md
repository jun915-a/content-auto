# Logs Aren’t Just Data—they Shape AI’s Future

A radical new paper argues logs are the hidden architects of AI agents. Discover how treating logs as first-class citizens could redefine autonomy, debugging, and trust in AI systems.

{
  "## 🔑 The Core of This Topic": "The paper posits that logs—historical records of an AI system’s actions and decisions—are not mere byproducts but active agents in shaping autonomy, reliability, and self-correction. By embedding logs into the agent’s core, AI can learn from its past in real time, enabling faster adaptation and more transparent decision-making."
