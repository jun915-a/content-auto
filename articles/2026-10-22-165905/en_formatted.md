Ever wonder how your personal data ends up in the wrong hands? *What’s Getting Pwned* tracks breaches in real-time, exposing vulnerabilities and teaching us how to stay safe in a digital world full of risks.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
*What’s Getting Pwned* (pwn.watch) is a real-time database that tracks data breaches, exposing how personal information—like passwords, emails, and financial details—leaks online. It’s a wake-up call for anyone concerned about cybersecurity, offering transparency into how often and why breaches happen.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Tracks **millions of leaked records** from past and ongoing breaches.
- **Point 2**: Helps users check if their data was exposed in **major hacks** (e.g., LinkedIn, Yahoo).
- **Point 3**: Highlights **reusable passwords** and weak security practices.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Pwn.watch aggregates leaked data from public breach databases like Have I Been Pwned (HIBP). It doesn’t just list breaches—it **categorizes them by severity**, showing how many unique emails or passwords were compromised. This makes it easier for users to gauge their risk level.
