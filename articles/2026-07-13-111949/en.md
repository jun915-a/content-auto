# Shackleton's Final Ship, Quest, Discovered After 100 Years

The wreck of the Quest, Sir Ernest Shackleton's final ship, has been found off the coast of Newfoundland. This discovery marks a significant moment for polar exploration history.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The final resting place of the Quest, the ship used by legendary Antarctic explorer Sir Ernest Shackleton on his last expedition, has been located. This discovery is a monumental find for maritime history and polar exploration enthusiasts.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Discovery**: The wreck of the Quest has been found off the coast of Newfoundland.
- **Legacy**: It was Shackleton's final command before his death.
- **Significance**: Offers a new window into early 20th-century polar exploration.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Quest's Final Voyage**
The Quest, a Norwegian-built sealer, was Shackleton's vessel for his ambitious 1921-1922 'Quest Expedition.' Tragically, Shackleton died aboard the ship in South Georgia in January 1922, making it his final command.

**Discovery Mission**
Using advanced sonar technology, the research vessel Atlantic Charger located the Quest resting on the seabed at a depth of 390 meters (1,280 feet). The expedition team confirmed the find after analyzing sonar images.

> 💡 Insight: The discovery of the Quest provides a tangible link to one of history's most celebrated polar explorers and his ill-fated final journey.
