# Overtone: AI-Powered Captions That Fix Errors & Fit Any Screen

Meet Overtone, an AI tool that auto-generates, corrects misheard words, and optimizes captions for any screen shape—saving hours of manual labor for creators and businesses alike.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Overtone is an AI-driven captioning tool designed to automate the tedious process of transcribing, correcting, and formatting video captions. It tackles two major pain points: **misheard words** and **layout inconsistencies**, ensuring captions are accurate and visually polished for any screen size. Built by Lont, a company specializing in turning emails into personalized videos, Overtone bridges the gap between raw transcription and professional-grade captions, reducing manual effort by 90% or more.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **AI-Powered Accuracy**: Corrects misheard words in real-time, improving caption reliability.
- **Dynamic Layout**: Adapts captions to any screen shape or aspect ratio automatically.
- **Time-Saving**: Cuts hours of manual editing for creators, marketers, and businesses.
- **Open-Source**: Free to use and customize via GitHub, with no hidden costs.
- **Seamless Integration**: Works with existing video workflows, from social media to corporate training.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Overtone starts with **automatic transcription**, converting spoken words into text with surprising accuracy. However, no AI is perfect—misheard words, filler sounds, or background noise often slip through. This is where Overtone’s **context-aware correction engine** shines. By analyzing surrounding words and common speech patterns, it intelligently fixes errors like "you’re" → "your" or "their" → "there,” reducing manual proofreading to a fraction of the time. For businesses like Lont, where every word in a personalized video must be flawless, this feature alone saves **dozens of hours per month**.

**Element 2**
The second challenge—**layout optimization**—is equally frustrating. Captions must fit neatly within a video frame, avoiding awkward line breaks or truncated words. Overtone solves this with **adaptive formatting**: it dynamically adjusts line lengths, font sizes, and spacing to ensure captions look polished on **any device**, from smartphones to 4K TVs. This is a game-changer for creators who upload content across platforms like YouTube, TikTok, or LinkedIn, where inconsistent captions can hurt professionalism.
