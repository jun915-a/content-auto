## 🔑 Unveiling Fermat's Last Theorem

Pierre de Fermat's famous conjecture, proposed in the 17th century, has intrigued mathematicians for centuries. It states that no three positive integers a, b, and c can satisfy the equation an + bn = cn for any integer n greater than 2. This theorem, a cornerstone of number theory, has inspired generations of mathematicians.
