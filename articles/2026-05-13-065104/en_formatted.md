## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Sky**
A well-crafted sky is essential for creating a sense of atmosphere and mood in your scene. To achieve a realistic sky, you need to consider the effects of atmospheric scattering, which scatters light in all directions. This can be achieved using various techniques such as ray marching or volumetric rendering.

**Planets and Sunsets**
Planets and sunsets are another crucial aspect of rendering realistic skies. To achieve accurate planet and sunset simulation, you can utilize physically-based rendering (PBR) techniques. PBR takes into account the real-world properties of materials and lighting, resulting in more accurate and realistic visual effects.
