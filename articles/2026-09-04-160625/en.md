# K2 Horizon: Revolutionizing AI with Open Models

IFM’s K2 Horizon introduces a connected fleet of six open models, merging cutting-edge AI with real-world applications. Discover how this innovation is reshaping industries and driving smarter, more efficient solutions.

**K2 Horizon: A Connected Fleet of Six Open Models** is a groundbreaking initiative by IFM AI that aims to democratize advanced AI capabilities through a collaborative ecosystem of open models. This project bridges the gap between theoretical AI research and practical, industry-ready applications, fostering innovation across sectors like healthcare, finance, and logistics.
