## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Hyperbolic Plane**
Imagine a saddle shape that extends infinitely. This is a simplified analogy for the hyperbolic plane, where the sum of angles in a triangle is always less than 180 degrees, unlike our familiar flat world.

**Billiard Ball Dynamics**
When billiard balls move in this space, their paths, which would be straight lines on a flat table, become curved geodesics. The angles of reflection and collision are dramatically altered, making prediction incredibly difficult for anyone used to Euclidean geometry.

> 💡 Insight: Our everyday geometric intuition breaks down in non-Euclidean spaces, leading to counter-intuitive phenomena.
