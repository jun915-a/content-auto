## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Brainstem's Structure**
The brainstem is a slender, cone-shaped structure that extends from the base of the brain to the spinal cord. It plays a crucial role in controlling various autonomic functions, including breathing, heart rate, and blood pressure. The new atlas provides a detailed view of the brainstem's intricate structures, including the midbrain, pons, and medulla oblongata.

**The Significance of the Atlas**
This atlas is a significant breakthrough in the field of neuroscience, as it provides a highly detailed and accurate map of the brainstem.
