US healthcare marketplaces have been found to share sensitive data with ad tech giants.

**Element 1**: The shared data includes citizenship status and race, which can be used for targeted advertising.
**Element 2**: The practice has been ongoing, with millions of records shared without explicit user consent.
> 💡 Insight: This highlights the need for stricter regulations on data sharing in the healthcare sector.

- Increased risk of discrimination
- Potential for targeted manipulation
- Erosion of trust in healthcare services
## ✨ Conclusion
The sharing of sensitive healthcare data with ad tech giants is a concerning trend that requires immediate attention and action to protect user privacy.
