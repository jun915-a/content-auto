# Mole: The Terminal Research Agent That Stays on Budget

Meet Mole, the AI research agent for your terminal that cuts through the noise, stays budget-friendly, and delivers precise answers—no fluff, no overruns.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Mole is a terminal-based deep research agent designed to streamline information gathering without the usual pitfalls of AI agents: cost overruns, source chaos, or vague answers. It focuses on delivering concise, accurate results while keeping you in control of your terminal workflow.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Budget-friendly**: Minimizes token usage with efficient research strategies.
- **Terminal-native**: Runs entirely in your terminal, no clunky UIs.
- **Source-aware**: Keeps track of references and avoids jumbling unrelated data.
- **Precision-focused**: Prioritizes quality over confidence in its answers.
- **Open-source**: Built for transparency and community contributions.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**How Mole Stays Budget-Conscious**
Mole achieves cost efficiency by optimizing its search depth and query complexity. Instead of making broad, expensive queries, it narrows down research paths based on initial inputs, reducing unnecessary token consumption. This is especially useful for users who need to conduct multiple research sessions without breaking the bank.

**Seamless Terminal Integration**
Designed to work within the terminal environment, Mole leverages your existing workflow. It doesn’t require a separate application or browser tab, making it ideal for developers, researchers, or anyone who spends time in the command line. The agent’s output is formatted for easy reading, and its commands are intuitive for terminal users.

> 💡 Insight: Mole proves that AI research agents don’t need to sacrifice efficiency or accuracy for convenience. By focusing on terminal-native design and cost control, it bridges the gap between powerful AI and practical usability.
