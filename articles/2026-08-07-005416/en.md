# Sun's Hidden Process

Scientists discover Kelvin-Helmholtz Instability on the Sun's surface, a major breakthrough enabled by the NSF's Inouye Solar Telescope

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Kelvin-Helmholtz Instability is a process that occurs when there is a velocity shear in a fluid, leading to the formation of waves and turbulence. On the Sun, this process was previously hidden from view.

- **Point 3**: Breakthrough sheds new light on solar dynamics
