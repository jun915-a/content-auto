# Windows 11 Media Player Issues

Windows 11's new media player uses more RAM and charges for popular video codecs, sparking controversy among users

## The Core of This Topic
The new Windows 11 media player has raised concerns due to its increased RAM usage and charges for popular video codecs.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Increased RAM Usage**: 3.5x more RAM used
- **Codec Charges**: Popular video codecs require additional payment
- **Performance Impact**: Potential slowdowns and decreased performance
## Detailed Breakdown
**RAM Usage**
The increased RAM usage can lead to performance issues, especially on lower-end devices.
**Codec Support**
The need to pay for popular video codecs can be frustrating for users who expect them to be included.
> Insight: This move may drive users to alternative media players.
## Real-World Impact
- Increased costs for users
- Potential performance issues
- Frustration with Microsoft's decision
## Conclusion
The new Windows 11 media player's increased RAM usage and codec charges may have significant real-world impacts on users, potentially driving them to alternative solutions.
