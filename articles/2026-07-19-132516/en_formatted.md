## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Self-Registration**
Register your domain name through a low-cost registrar like Namecheap or Gandi. This will cost you around $1-2 per year.

**Free Hosting**
Use a free web hosting service like InfinityFree, 000webhost, or Byet.host. These services will host your website for free, but be aware of their limitations and potential downtime.

**Open-Source Software**
Choose a free and open-source Content Management System (CMS) like WordPress, Ghost, or Plone. These platforms will allow you to create and manage your website's content.

> 💡 Insight: By keeping your website independent, you'll have full control over your online presence and can make changes as you see fit.
