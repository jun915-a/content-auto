## 📈 Detailed Breakdown

Homomorphic encryption (HE) is a cryptographic technique that allows computations to be performed on **encrypted data** without decrypting it first. Traditionally, AI models require plaintext inputs for training or inference, exposing sensitive data. Google’s advancement in HE—dubbed **TEA-1**—overcomes this by enabling **encrypted data streams** to flow through AI pipelines seamlessly. This is particularly critical for industries like healthcare, where patient data must remain confidential while still being analyzed for insights.

**Element 2**
The **TEA-1** framework demonstrates that HE isn’t just a theoretical concept but a **practical solution** for modern AI workloads. By integrating HE into model inference, Google ensures that encrypted data—such as encrypted medical images or financial transactions—can be processed by AI without ever being exposed. This reduces reliance on **trusted third-party servers** and mitigates risks like data breaches or unauthorized access.
