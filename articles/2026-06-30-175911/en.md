# Introducing .self Domain

Discover the new top-level domain designed to support self-hosting, empowering users to reclaim their digital presence

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The .self domain is a new top-level domain that supports self-hosting, giving users control over their digital identity and data.

- **Secure**: Enhanced security through self-hosting

The .self domain offers numerous benefits, including increased security and customization options.
