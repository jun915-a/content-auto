# 25G Thunderbolt Ethernet

Get 25G Ethernet on your Mac Studio with Thunderbolt for faster networking, learn how to set it up and its benefits

## The Core of This Topic
To get 25G Thunderbolt Ethernet on your Mac Studio, you need a Thunderbolt 3 port and a compatible Ethernet adapter. 
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Hardware Requirement**: Thunderbolt 3 port
- **Software Requirement**: macOS support
- **Speed**: 25G Ethernet
## Detailed Breakdown
**Thunderbolt 3 Port**
A Thunderbolt 3 port is required to connect the Ethernet adapter, it supports speeds of up to 40Gbps. 
**Ethernet Adapter**
An Ethernet adapter that supports 25G Ethernet is needed, it should be compatible with your Mac Studio.
