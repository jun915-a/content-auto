## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Cookie Tracking**
Cookie trackers are small pieces of code that follow your online activities, gathering valuable information about your browsing habits. These trackers are often used for targeted advertising, but they also pose a significant risk to your online security.

**Online Ads**
Intrusive ads can be frustrating and annoying, but they also pose a threat to your online experience. By blocking these ads, you can enjoy a smoother and more enjoyable browsing experience.
