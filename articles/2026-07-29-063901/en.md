# First Submerged Submarine Circles the Globe

In 1960, the US Navy's USS Triton completed the first submerged circumnavigation of the globe, a groundbreaking feat that pushed the limits of submarine technology.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The USS Triton, a US Navy submarine, made history in 1960 by becoming the first submerged vessel to circle the globe. This remarkable achievement was a result of the US Navy's Operation Sandblast, a daring endeavor that tested the limits of submarine technology.

**Ballistic Missile Testings**
The USS Triton's journey was not just about breaking records; it was also a testbed for the US Navy's ballistic missile capabilities. The submarine was equipped with Polaris missiles, which were launched at targets in the Atlantic Ocean during the circumnavigation.

**Technological Advancements**
The USS Triton's success was made possible by significant technological advancements in submarine design and construction. The submarine's advanced air-independent propulsion system allowed it to remain submerged for extended periods, while its highly efficient power generation system enabled it to maintain a steady course.
