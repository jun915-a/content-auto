The Jurassic Park computer systems were designed to manage and control the park's infrastructure, including security, visitor management, and dinosaur enclosures.

- **Point 1**: The park's mainframe was an **IBM System/370**, a powerful computer system for its time.
- **Point 2**: The system used a **custom-built interface**, designed to be user-friendly for park staff.
- **Point 3**: The computer systems played a crucial role in the film's plot, often malfunctioning and causing chaos.
