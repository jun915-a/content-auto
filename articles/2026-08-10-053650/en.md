# ReMarkable 2 Revived: SSH Access for an Old Tablet

Revive your aging 4-year-old reMarkable 2! This guide unlocks SSH access, breathing new life into your e-ink tablet with advanced customization and control.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
This guide details how to enable SSH access on a reMarkable 2 tablet that is several years old. By leveraging specific tools and techniques, users can regain control and unlock advanced functionalities, effectively extending the device's lifespan and utility.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Enabling SSH Access**
This involves carefully preparing a specific file, often named `ssh`, on the tablet's internal storage. This file acts as a trigger for the device's boot process to enable the SSH server, allowing remote connections.

**Connecting via SSH**
Once enabled, you can connect to your reMarkable 2 from another computer on the same network using standard SSH clients. This opens up a world of possibilities for managing files and running custom scripts.
