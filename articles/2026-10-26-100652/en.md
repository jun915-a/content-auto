## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1: Effortless Process Management**
Proc-CLI eliminates the hassle of juggling multiple commands to monitor or kill processes. With a single command, you can **list, inspect, or terminate** any running process—even across multiple servers. The tool provides **detailed insights** like CPU usage, memory allocation, and parent-child relationships, making troubleshooting a breeze. No more digging through logs or guessing which process is causing bottlenecks.

**Element 2: Smart Port Management**
Port conflicts and misconfigurations are a nightmare for developers. Proc-CLI **automatically detects** unused or conflicting ports and lets you **reserve, release, or scan** them with minimal effort. It integrates with common services like Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud providers, ensuring smooth deployments without port-related surprises. The tool also supports **port forwarding** and **firewall rules**, making it a one-stop solution for network management.
