This course demystifies the creation of reasoning models by focusing on fundamental principles and building them from the ground up. It emphasizes understanding the underlying mechanisms rather than relying solely on high-level libraries, fostering a deeper comprehension of how these models truly function.

- **From Scratch Implementation**: Learn to build models without heavy reliance on pre-built abstractions.

The course delves into the architectural components and logical frameworks that enable machines to "reason." This involves exploring how models process information, infer relationships, and arrive at conclusions, moving beyond simple pattern recognition.

**Building Blocks of Reasoning**
It systematically introduces the essential modules and algorithms required for reasoning, such as knowledge representation, inference engines, and learning components. Each part is explained and implemented to build a cohesive system.
