# Steam Controller Auto-Charge: Magnetic Magic for Gamers

Transform your Steam Controller into a wireless charging marvel with this open-source pilot project. Using CV (current-voltage) feedback, it automates charging via a magnetic puck—no more tangled cables or missed sessions. Dive into the tech and DIY potential that could redefine gaming peripherals.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
A **pilot project** by FossPrime introduces a **Steam Controller Auto-Charge system** that leverages **magnetic charging pucks** and **CV (current-voltage) feedback** to automate the charging process. Instead of relying on traditional wired connections or bulky docks, this solution uses **wireless inductive charging** paired with a **custom control mechanism** to ensure the controller charges seamlessly when placed on a designated puck. The innovation lies in its **open-source nature**, allowing hobbyists and developers to explore, modify, and expand the functionality for future gaming peripherals.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Wireless charging**: Uses **magnetic pucks** for hands-free charging, eliminating cable clutter.
- **CV feedback**: Monitors current-voltage to ensure optimal charging efficiency and safety.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Magnetic Charging Puck Integration**
The project replaces traditional wired charging with a **magnetic puck**, a compact inductive charger that aligns seamlessly with the Steam Controller’s base. This setup eliminates the need for physical ports or docks, offering **portability and ease of use**. The puck’s design ensures **precise alignment detection**, triggering the charging cycle automatically when the controller is placed on it. This is particularly useful for **gaming setups** where cables can be cumbersome, or for **public events** where wireless charging stations are preferred.

**CV Feedback for Smart Charging**
At the heart of this innovation is the **current-voltage (CV) feedback system**, which dynamically adjusts the charging process based on real-time data. This ensures the Steam Controller charges **efficiently and safely**, avoiding overcharging or overheating. The CV loop monitors the controller’s battery status and **adjusts power delivery** accordingly, mimicking the behavior of high-end wireless chargers found in smartphones. This not only extends the **lifespan of the battery** but also provides a **smoother user experience** by preventing interruptions during gameplay.

> 💡 **Insight**: The CV feedback system could be adapted for **other wireless peripherals**, such as VR headsets or gaming mice, expanding the potential of inductive charging beyond just controllers.
