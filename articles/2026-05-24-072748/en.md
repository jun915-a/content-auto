# Trump's Green Card Rule

Green card seekers must leave the US to apply, a new Trump administration rule states, affecting many immigrants

## The Core of This Topic
The Trump administration has introduced a new rule requiring green card seekers to leave the US to apply, affecting thousands of immigrants.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **New Rule**: Green card applicants must exit the US
- **Affected Groups**: Immigrants and their families
- **Administration Goal**: To reduce immigration
## Detailed Breakdown
**Immigration Process**
The new rule changes the adjustment of status process, now requiring applicants to leave the country.
**Extraordinary Circumstances**
Only in extraordinary circumstances will USCIS grant adjustment of status within the US.
> Insight: This new rule may lead to family separations and increased hardships for immigrants.
## Real-World Impact
- Increased hardships for immigrant families
- Potential economic impact on the US
- Changes to the current immigration system
## Conclusion
The new rule may have far-reaching consequences for immigrants and the US, sparking controversy and debate.
