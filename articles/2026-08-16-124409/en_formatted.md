## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Lyme disease is a debilitating condition caused by the bacteria *Borrelia burgdorferi*, transmitted to humans through the bite of an infected blacklegged tick. Early diagnosis is crucial to prevent long-term complications, but current methods often rely on laboratory testing of tick samples, which can be time-consuming and expensive. Now, a new at-home test promises to revolutionize Lyme disease diagnosis by allowing individuals to test their ticks quickly and easily, potentially leading to earlier and more accurate diagnosis.
