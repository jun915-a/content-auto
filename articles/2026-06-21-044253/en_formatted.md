## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Community Building**
By offering sewing machines, libraries create a space for people to come together, share ideas, and learn from each other. This fosters a sense of community and belonging, which is essential for mental well-being and social cohesion.

**Skill Development**
Renting sewing machines allows people to explore their creativity and develop new skills. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced sewer, this service provides access to high-quality machines and expert guidance.
