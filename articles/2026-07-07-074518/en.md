# Rotman Lens

The Rotman Lens is a novel approach to understanding complex systems, combining elements of cognitive psychology and system dynamics to provide a more nuanced understanding of human behavior and decision-making.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Rotman Lens is a theoretical framework that seeks to explain how humans perceive and interact with complex systems. At its core, the lens posits that people's understanding of a system is filtered through a series of mental shortcuts, biases, and assumptions, which can lead to inaccurate or incomplete understanding.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Role of Cognitive Biases**
Cognitive biases play a significant role in shaping how people perceive and interact with complex systems. The Rotman Lens highlights the importance of considering these biases when designing systems or making decisions.

**The Impact of Mental Shortcuts**
Mental shortcuts, or heuristics, are another key aspect of the Rotman Lens. These shortcuts can lead to inaccurate or incomplete understanding of a system, but they also provide a means of coping with the complexity of a system.
