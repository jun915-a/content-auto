# Trump's New Green Card Rule

The Trump administration has introduced a new rule requiring green card seekers to leave the US to apply, sparking controversy and concern among immigrants

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Trump administration has announced that green card seekers must leave the US to apply, a move that could significantly impact immigration processes.
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **New Rule**: Green card applicants must exit the US
- **Application Process**: Applies to adjustment of status requests
- **Extraordinary Cases**: Some exceptions may be granted
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Eligibility**
Applicants must meet specific criteria to be eligible for a green card, including background checks and documentation.
**Application Procedure**
The new rule requires applicants to leave the US and apply from their home country or another location.
> 💡 Insight: This change may lead to longer processing times and increased uncertainty for applicants.
## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Increased wait times for applicants
- Potential economic impacts on the US
- Concerns about family separation
## ✨ Conclusion
The new rule has sparked debate and concern among immigrants and advocates, who argue that it may lead to unnecessary hardship and separation for families.
