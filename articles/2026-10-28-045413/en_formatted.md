## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The loss of firefly species in North America is a silent ecological crisis, revealing how human activity has eroded biodiversity. Fireflies, or **Lampyridae**, are not just mesmerizing nocturnal creatures but vital pollinators and indicators of environmental health. Their decline signals broader ecosystem imbalances, yet their extinction often goes unnoticed due to their small size and elusive nature. This article delves into the **specific species lost**, the **causes behind their disappearance**, and the **consequences for nature and humanity**—urging action before more vanish.
