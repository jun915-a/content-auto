## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Fifth Lateran Council, convened in 1512-1517, issued a groundbreaking decree recognizing the merit of lending as a moral act. This shift in perspective unlocked new financial theories, influencing centuries of economic development.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Interest Rates**
The council's decree allowed for the charging of interest rates, which became a cornerstone of financial theory. This change opened up new avenues for investors, enabling the growth of credit markets.

**Risk and Reward**
The council's recognition of lending as a meritorious act highlighted the importance of risk and reward in financial transactions. This led to the development of more sophisticated risk assessment methods and the emergence of new financial instruments.
