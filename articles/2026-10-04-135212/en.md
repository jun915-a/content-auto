# Man Celebrates 100th Birthday of Kidney Donated by Mom

A Webster man is marking an extraordinary milestone: the 100th birthday of the kidney his mother gifted him as a teenager. This life-saving donation continues to thrive decades later.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
This story highlights an incredible medical achievement and a profound act of maternal love. A kidney donated by a mother to her son in his youth has now reached its 100th birthday, a testament to the success of transplant surgery and the enduring bond between mother and child.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Unprecedented Milestone**: A donated kidney celebrates a remarkable 100 years.
- **Maternal Love**: A mother's selfless gift saved her son's life.
- **Medical Marvel**: A successful transplant exceeding all expectations.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Gift of Life**
At a young age, the recipient received a life-saving kidney transplant from his mother. This act of love not only saved his life but has allowed him to live a full life, celebrating an extraordinary anniversary.

**A Century of Function**
Organs, especially transplanted ones, have a typical lifespan. For this kidney to have functioned successfully for a century is an astonishing medical feat, far exceeding typical projections for transplant longevity.

> 💡 Insight: This case underscores the potential for organ transplants to offer decades of life and the incredible resilience of the human body.
