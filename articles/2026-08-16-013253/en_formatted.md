## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Eigendrum is a web application that simulates the acoustic properties of any 2D shape by solving the 2D wave equation. Users can draw custom shapes, and the tool generates the corresponding drum sound, offering a unique auditory experience.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Draw Shapes**: Create any 2D form you can imagine.
- **Hear Sounds**: Listen to how your shapes sound as drums.
- **Wave Equation**: Solves complex physics for realistic audio.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Finite Element Analysis**
The tool employs finite element analysis to discretize the drawn shape into smaller elements. This method is crucial for accurately solving the complex 2D wave equation across the entire shape's geometry.

**Eigenvalue Problem**
At its heart, Eigendrum solves an eigenvalue problem derived from the wave equation. The eigenvalues correspond to the resonant frequencies of the shape, dictating the fundamental pitches and overtones of the resulting drum sound.

> 💡 Insight: The geometry of a shape directly dictates its acoustic signature, turning visual forms into auditory experiences.
