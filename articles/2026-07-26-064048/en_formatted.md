## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJTs)**
BJTs are transistors that use both positive and negative charge carriers to control current flow. They are widely used in amplifiers and switching circuits.

**Field-Effect Transistors (FETs)**
FETs are transistors that use an electric field to control current flow. They are commonly used in digital circuits and analog amplifiers.
