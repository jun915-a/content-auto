## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
FreeCAD is a free and open-source software that allows users to create and edit 2D and 3D models in the comfort of their web browser. Developed by a community of passionate developers, this software has been designed to provide users with the tools they need to create complex designs without the need for expensive hardware or software.
