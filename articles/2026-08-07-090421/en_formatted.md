## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
When it comes to USB-C cables, the labels can be misleading. Some cables may claim to be 100W, but when you plug them in, they only deliver 50W. This can lead to slow charging and even damage your device. By testing the cables with a meter, we can get a clear picture of what we're really getting.

**Element 2**
A meter can help you identify the correct cable for your needs. For example, if you need a cable that can deliver 100W, you can use the meter to test the cable and ensure that it's meeting your requirements. This can save you time and money in the long run.
