## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Spring Boot applications can be complex and hard to manage. Monitoring them effectively is crucial for identifying issues and areas for improvement. However, existing solutions often come with their own set of complexities, which can be overwhelming.

- **Point 1**: Lightweight monitoring without the overhead of Prometheus and Grafana.
- **Point 2**: Simple and efficient way to track your application's performance.
- **Point 3**: Real-time insights for better decision-making.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: We will explore the concept of a lightweight monitoring solution, focusing on the essential components necessary for effective monitoring.
This includes metrics collection, logging, and alerting mechanisms. By leveraging the right combination of these elements, you can create a robust monitoring system without the need for complex tools like Prometheus and Grafana.

**Element 2**: A key aspect of any monitoring system is the ability to collect and store metrics. In this context, we will discuss alternative solutions to Prometheus, such as the built-in Spring Boot metrics, and other lightweight alternatives.
Furthermore, we will cover the importance of logging and how it can be effectively integrated into your monitoring system.
