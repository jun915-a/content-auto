## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
YouTube guitar lessons offer an unparalleled way to learn music. However, traditional tablature transcription services often fail to deliver accurate results, hindering the learning process. To bridge this gap, a simple CLI tool has been created to turn YouTube guitar-lesson videos into readable PDF guitar tabs.
