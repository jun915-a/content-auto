## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Polars 2.0 introduces **multi-threaded execution by default**, allowing users to harness the full power of modern CPUs. This shift eliminates the need for manual thread management, making parallel processing intuitive and efficient. Whether you're filtering, aggregating, or joining datasets, the library automatically distributes the workload across available cores, cutting processing times significantly.

**Element 2**
The **lazy evaluation framework** is one of the standout features. Instead of processing data immediately, Polars 2.0 plans operations and executes them only when needed. This approach minimizes memory usage and accelerates workflows, especially for complex pipelines. Users can now chain operations like `filter()` and `group_by()` without worrying about intermediate results clogging their system.
