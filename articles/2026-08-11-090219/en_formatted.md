## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Riemann Zeta Function**
The Riemann Zeta function, denoted ζ(s), is a function of a complex variable 's'. It's defined as the sum of the infinite series 1/1^s + 1/2^s + 1/3^s + ... for real parts of s > 1, and by analytic continuation elsewhere.

**The Riemann Hypothesis**
A central conjecture in mathematics, the Riemann Hypothesis, posits that all non-trivial zeros of the Riemann Zeta function lie on the 'critical line' with a real part of 1/2. This has deep implications for the distribution of prime numbers.
