# Make PDFs Look Scanned: CLI & WASM Tool

Transform digital PDFs into vintage scanned documents!overflowy's Make Look Scanned offers a CLI and browser-based WASM solution to add authentic paper textures and imperfections.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Make Look Scanned is a tool that applies realistic paper textures, folds, and ink bleeds to digital PDFs, simulating the look of a physical scan. It operates via a command-line interface or in the browser using WebAssembly for accessibility.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Authentic Texture**: Adds realistic paper grain and imperfections.
- **Scan Simulation**: Mimics folds, creases, and ink bleed effects.
- **Dual Functionality**: Available as a CLI tool and in-browser via WASM.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Paper Texture Generation**
The tool overlays various paper textures, from aged parchment to standard office paper, complete with subtle variations in color and density to mimic real-world paper aging and printing processes.

**Distortion and Imperfection Effects**
It simulates common scanning artifacts like slight page bends, corner folds, uneven ink saturation, and minor smudges, giving PDFs a genuine, non-digital appearance.

> 💡 Insight: This project democratizes the creation of retro or distressed document aesthetics without needing expensive software or complex editing.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- **Digital Archiving Aesthetics**: Give digital archives a vintage feel.
- **Creative Projects**: Enhance the realism of historical documents in games or films.
- **Artistic Expression**: Create unique visual styles for digital art and design.

## ✨ Conclusion
Whether you're a developer, designer, or hobbyist, Make Look Scanned provides an easy and effective way to give your PDFs that sought-after scanned document look, bridging the gap between digital and analog aesthetics.
