## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**3SUM Problem:**
The 3SUM problem asks if, given a set of n real numbers, any three sum to zero. The new algorithm employs a clever combination of hashing and divide-and-conquer techniques, achieving a subquadratic time complexity of O(n^{4/3} polylog(n)).

**APSP (All-Pairs Shortest Paths):**
APSP, a classic graph problem, is tackled with a novel approach. By utilizing fast matrix multiplication and dynamic programming, the algorithm achieves a subcubic time complexity of O(n^{2.807}). This is a significant improvement over the previous best of O(n^3).
