By keeping everything on-chip, the D270 ensures **faster iteration cycles** and **lower latency**, making it perfect for **large-language models (LLMs) and generative AI**.

Powered by Intel’s **Gaudi 2 architecture**, the D270 is engineered specifically for AI workloads.
