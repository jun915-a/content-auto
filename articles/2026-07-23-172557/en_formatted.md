# Screenpipe: Powering AI Agents with Your 24/7 Screen Recording

Screenpipe introduces a revolutionary app that records your screen and audio locally, creating a continuous, private data stream to power AI agents. This innovation promises to deliver highly personalized and context-aware digital assistance, redefining how AI interacts with users.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Screenpipe is an application designed to record a user's screen and audio directly on their local device, ensuring privacy by never sending data to the cloud. Its core purpose is to generate a rich, continuous dataset that can be utilized by AI agents, enabling them to gain a deep, real-time understanding of user context, workflows, and intentions to provide highly personalized and proactive assistance.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Local & Private**: Records screen and audio exclusively on your device.
- **AI Agent Fuel**: Provides continuous context for intelligent agents.
- **Personalized Automation**: Enables agents to learn and adapt to user workflows.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Privacy-First Approach**
Screenpipe's commitment to local-only recording is a critical differentiator. In an era where data privacy is paramount, this design ensures that sensitive screen activity and audio never leave the user's device, building essential trust for a tool that operates 24/7.

**Powering Next-Gen AI Agents**
By continuously capturing visual and auditory data, Screenpipe offers an unparalleled stream of information to AI agents. This rich context allows agents to move beyond simple commands, observing user behavior and understanding the 'why' behind actions, leading to significantly more intelligent and adaptive automation.
