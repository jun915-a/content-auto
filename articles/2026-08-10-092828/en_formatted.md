## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Tuxedo No. 2 cocktail is a classic variation of the Manhattan, made with whiskey, Aperol, and sweet vermouth. It's a unique blend of flavors that's both refreshing and complex.

- **Point 1**: A combination of whiskey and Aperol creates a distinct flavor profile.

- **Point 3**: The Tuxedo No. 2 is a versatile cocktail that can be enjoyed in various settings.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: To make a Tuxedo No. 2, you'll need 2 oz of whiskey, 1 oz of Aperol, 1 oz of sweet vermouth, and a dash of bitters. The key to this cocktail is finding the right balance between the strong flavors of the whiskey and Aperol.

**Element 2**: The Aperol adds a subtle bitterness to the cocktail, while the sweet vermouth provides a touch of sweetness. This balance of flavors is what sets the Tuxedo No. 2 apart from other cocktails.
