# Singapore’s Bold Move: Paying Readers to Ditch Phones

Singapore’s National Library Board is revolutionizing literacy with micropayments, incentivizing citizens to swap digital distractions for books—unlocking a cultural shift toward reading. Could this be the future of engagement?

**Singapore’s National Library Board (NLB) is making headlines with an innovative strategy to combat digital addiction: paying people to read books.** In a world dominated by smartphones, the NLB’s micropayment program aims to foster a reading culture by rewarding users for engaging with physical books. This bold experiment not only highlights the challenges of modern attention spans but also demonstrates how financial incentives can reshape habits. Here’s how it works and why it matters.
