## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Japanese verb conjugation is a complex yet fascinating aspect of the Japanese language. It involves changing the ending of a verb to indicate tense, mood, and politeness. With over 200 different conjugations, it's no wonder many learners find it daunting.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Understand the basics of Japanese verb conjugation, including the use of suffixes and prefixes.
- **Point 2**: Master the different tenses, including past, present, and future.
- **Point 3**: Learn how to conjugate verbs for different levels of politeness.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The present tense is one of the most common tenses in Japanese verb conjugation. It's used to describe actions that are happening now. For example, the verb 'iku' (to go) becomes 'iku' in the present tense.

**Element 2**
The past tense is used to describe actions that have already happened. It's formed by adding the suffix '-ta' to the verb stem. For example, the verb 'iku' becomes 'itta'.
