# Curl's Summer Break

Curl won't accept vulnerability reports in July 2026, a unique move to give maintainers a break, learn more about this decision and its implications

## The Core of This Topic
The Curl project has announced it will not accept vulnerability reports during July 2026, a decision to give its maintainers a well-deserved break.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **No Reports**: No vulnerability reports will be accepted
- **Summer Break**: Maintainers get a month off from security reports
- **Business as Usual**: Other aspects of the project continue uninterrupted
## Detailed Breakdown
**Maintenance Time**
The maintainers will use this time to focus on other aspects of the project.
**Community Impact**
The community is generally supportive of this decision, understanding the need for maintainers to rest.
> Insight: This move highlights the importance of maintainer well-being in open-source projects.
## Real-World Impact
- Reduced stress for maintainers
- Potential delay in vulnerability fixes
- Increased focus on other project aspects
## Conclusion
This break is a unique step towards recognizing the human side of open-source projects, and its effects will be closely watched by the community.
