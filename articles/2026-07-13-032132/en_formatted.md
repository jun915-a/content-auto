**Element 1**
The AI-generated flag would enable users to make informed decisions about the content they engage with. This feature would also encourage creators to be more transparent about their use of AI tools.

**Element 2**
By introducing this flag, HN can set a precedent for other platforms to prioritize content transparency. This move could lead to a shift in how users perceive and interact with online content.
