## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
DX Core 4 shifts the focus from **vanity metrics** (e.g., pull requests merged) to **meaningful contributions** like **time-to-resolution** and **code review quality**. **Element 2**

Each dimension is scored using a mix of **quantitative data** (e.g., build times, incident rates) and **qualitative feedback** (e.g., surveys, sentiment analysis). This dual approach ensures metrics reflect **both efficiency and well-being**.
