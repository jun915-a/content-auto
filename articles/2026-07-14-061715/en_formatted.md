## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
TFTP honeypots are designed to mimic TFTP servers, making them an attractive target for attackers seeking to exploit vulnerabilities or conduct reconnaissance. Our honeypot was set up to capture and analyze these malicious interactions, providing valuable insights into the tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) employed by attackers.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
* **Point 1**: Malicious actors used TFTP honeypots to conduct reconnaissance and exploit vulnerabilities.
* **Point 2**: Common tools used included TFTPD32 and TFTP client software.
* **Point 3**: Attackers targeted our honeypot with brute-force attacks and scanning.

**Element 1**: Our honeypot received over 10,000 connection attempts, with a significant portion being brute-force attacks. This suggests that attackers are still relying on simple, yet effective, methods to compromise systems.
