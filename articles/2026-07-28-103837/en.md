- **Point 2**: Trust exercise at company retreat
- **Point 3**: Executive was fired after the incident

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**

**Element 2**
The executive's actions were seen as a breach of trust and unprofessional, leading to their termination and a subsequent lawsuit.
