## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Just Intonation replaces the 12-tone equal temperament’s arbitrary divisions with **mathematically derived ratios** (e.g., 5:4 for a perfect fourth). This allows musicians to explore **non-octave-repeating intervals**, such as the **17:16 major third**, which sounds richer and more resonant to trained ears. Xenharmlib’s implementation includes **predefined JI scales** (e.g., Pythagorean, 1/4-comma mean tone) alongside customizable tuning systems, empowering creators to experiment with **historical and avant-garde harmonies** without sacrificing theoretical rigor.

**Element 2**
The library’s JI support extends beyond static tuning—it introduces **dynamic analysis tools** for composers. For instance, users can now **visualize beat patterns** between overtones in real time, helping to avoid dissonance in complex polyphonic works. Additionally, Xenharmlib’s **algorithmically generated JI chord progressions** assist in crafting pieces that adhere to **historical tuning systems** (e.g., medieval or Renaissance music) while remaining accessible to modern audiences.
