The proposal aims to integrate Large Language Models (LLMs) into Debian, enhancing the operating system's capabilities and user experience.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic

Debian, a popular Linux distribution, is considering the integration of Large Language Models (LLMs) to improve its performance and user experience. LLMs have revolutionized the way we interact with technology, and their potential in Debian is vast.

**Element 2**
The integration of LLMs will also enable Debian to stay competitive in the market.
