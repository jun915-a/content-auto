## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Sun's Arc**
The core of Sun Clock is its depiction of the sun's path across the sky. Unlike standard clocks, it shows sunrise, solar noon (when the sun is highest), and sunset as key markers, providing a visual representation of daylight hours and the sun's daily journey.

**Daylight Hours**
Sun Clock accurately calculates and displays the duration of daylight for any given location and date. This highlights seasonal variations and provides a clear understanding of how much natural light is available throughout the year.

**Time Zones and Local Noon**
While acknowledging standard time zones, Sun Clock emphasizes local solar time. This can reveal discrepancies between clock time and true solar noon, offering a unique perspective on timekeeping and its historical evolution.
