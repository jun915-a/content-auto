## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
US beef prices have hit record highs, yet cattle farmers are struggling financially. The disconnect stems from an uneven distribution of profits across the supply chain, where processors and retailers capture most gains while farmers bear rising costs and volatile market swings.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Record prices, no profits**: Retail beef prices surged 30% since 2020, but farmers’ incomes fell 12% in 2023.
- **Middlemen win**: Meatpacking giants like Tyson and Cargill dominate, controlling 85% of the market and dictating prices.
- **Feed costs crush farmers**: Droughts and high corn prices in 2022-23 added $200+ per head in expenses, eroding margins.
