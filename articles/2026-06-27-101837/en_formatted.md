Critics argue this sacrifices **universal accessibility** for efficiency, particularly for **rural or low-income listeners** who lack smartphones.\n\n> 💡 Insight: **Longwave’s shutdown mirrors broader tech transitions**—like the decline of vinyl records or landlines—where convenience often trumps legacy utility.\n\n## 🎯 Real-World Impact\n- **Emergency Services**: Longwave’s **no-battery-needed receivers** (like the **BBC Radio 4 Longwave**) were lifelines during storms or grid failures.
