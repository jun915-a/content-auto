# Preserving Fragile Floppy Disks with Copy That Floppy

Discover how to rescue data from fragile floppy disks using the Cambridge guide's 'Copy That Floppy' technique, protecting your digital heritage.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Preserving fragile floppy disks is crucial for safeguarding irreplaceable data. The 'Copy That Floppy' method helps you rescue your digital heritage, even when the original disks are on the verge of failure.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Identify the fragile floppy disks and their contents.
- **Point 2**: Prepare the necessary equipment and software for data transfer.
- **Point 3**: Perform the data transfer using the 'Copy That Floppy' technique.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: Start by locating the floppy disks and assessing their condition. Check for any physical damage, dust, or wear that might affect the data transfer process.

**Element 2**: Next, gather the necessary equipment, including a floppy disk drive, a computer with a compatible operating system, and specialized software for data transfer.
