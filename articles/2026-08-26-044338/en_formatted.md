**M6 Processor**
The M6 chip offers a substantial upgrade over its predecessors, delivering faster processing speeds and improved graphics performance. It's designed to handle demanding professional applications and next-generation gaming with ease, pushing the boundaries of mobile computing.

**M5 Ultra**
The M5 Ultra is Apple's most powerful chip to date, built for the most intensive workloads. With its expanded core count and specialized accelerators, it provides unparalleled performance for tasks like video editing, 3D rendering, and advanced scientific simulations.
