# ADHD-Proof Your AI: Stop Agents Burying Answers

Tired of AI agents burying the crucial information you need? Discover 'i-have-ADHD,' a clever technique to ensure AI responses are direct and actionable, saving you time and frustration.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
This technique involves subtly instructing AI agents to mimic the 'I have ADHD' mindset, forcing them to prioritize and present the most critical information upfront, avoiding lengthy introductions or tangential details.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Directness**: Demands immediate answers.
- **Conciseness**: Eliminates fluff and unnecessary context.
- **Actionability**: Focuses on what you need to know or do.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Prompting Strategy**
By framing requests with phrases like "I have ADHD and need the answer directly," you signal to the AI that brevity and immediate relevance are paramount. This overrides its default tendency to provide comprehensive, often overwhelming, explanations.

**Information Prioritization**
The AI learns to identify and surface the core answer, solution, or data point first, before offering any supplementary details. This ensures the essential information is never missed, even with a quick scan.

> 💡 Insight: This method leverages AI's pattern recognition to encourage a more efficient and user-centric output.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Faster information retrieval for busy users.
- Reduced frustration with verbose AI responses.
- Improved productivity by getting straight to the point.

## ✨ Conclusion
Embrace the 'i-have-ADHD' approach to reclaim your time and ensure AI works *for* you, delivering answers with the speed and clarity you deserve.
