# OccupySaaS: The Radical Shift in Cloud Software Ownership

OccupySaaS is redefining how businesses interact with cloud software by championing open-source alternatives and user sovereignty. This movement empowers enterprises to break free from vendor lock-in and regain control over their digital infrastructure. Discover how it’s reshaping the SaaS landscape and why it matters for the future of tech.
