# Show HN: CheapFoodMap Finds Delicious Meals Under $10 Locally

Discover affordable, local eateries with CheapFoodMap! Built after a layoff, this crowdsourced map highlights great meals under $10, steering clear of franchises.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
CheapFoodMap is a community-driven platform dedicated to finding delicious and affordable meals. It focuses exclusively on local restaurants offering tasty dishes priced under $10, providing an alternative to generic chain establishments.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Affordable Eats**: Find meals consistently under $10.
- **Local Focus**: Excludes franchises, champions local businesses.
- **Crowdsourced**: Relies on user submissions for accuracy.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Community Driven Discovery**
This map thrives on user contributions. Anyone can submit their favorite local spots that offer great value, creating a dynamic and ever-growing resource for budget-conscious foodies.

**Beyond the Chains**
The project intentionally bypasses large franchise restaurants. The goal is to shine a spotlight on hidden gems and unique culinary experiences offered by independent, local establishments.

> 💡 Insight: Supporting local businesses while finding affordable food is a win-win.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Empowers users to find budget-friendly dining options.
- Supports and promotes local, independent restaurants.
- Fosters a sense of community around shared food discoveries.

## ✨ Conclusion
CheapFoodMap offers a valuable service for anyone seeking delicious, affordable meals while supporting the heart of their local communities. It's a testament to building something useful from personal challenges.
