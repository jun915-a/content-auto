# Astronauts Told to Return to ISS After Sheltering Over Air Leak Repairs

Astronauts on the International Space Station (ISS) have been ordered to return to their spacecraft after they sheltered from a potentially life-threatening air leak.

- **Point 2**: Repairs are underway, but astronauts must return to their spacecraft.
- **Point 3**: Safety of astronauts remains the top priority.

The air leak was detected in one of the station's Russian modules, and the astronauts were moved to a safe location. The exact cause of the leak is still unknown, but repairs are underway.

**Element 2**
The astronauts were in no immediate danger, but the situation was being taken seriously by mission control. The decision to return them to their spacecraft was made as a precaution, to ensure their safety.
