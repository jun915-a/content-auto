## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
A film photographer's DIY project turns an iPhone and a $20 smart plug into a functional f-stop timer, providing an affordable alternative to costly equipment.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1: The iPhone's Role**
The iPhone serves as the brain of the operation, running a custom app that communicates with the smart plug. This app allows for precise control over exposure times, offering a range of options for different photographic needs.

**Element 2: Smart Plug Integration**
The smart plug acts as the muscle, physically controlling the exposure process. It receives commands from the iPhone app, triggering the camera's shutter release at precise intervals. This integration ensures accurate and repeatable exposures, a crucial aspect of film photography.

> 💡 Insight: The key to this setup's success is the seamless communication between the iPhone app and the smart plug, providing a reliable and affordable solution for film photographers.
