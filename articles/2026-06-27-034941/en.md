# The Finances of Pre-Modern Armies: Funding the Fury

In our continuing series on pre-modern armies, we tackle the complex issue of funding and sustaining large-scale military endeavors.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic

Pre-modern armies were expensive to maintain, with costs including military equipment, personnel, and logistics. Funding these armies came from a variety of sources, including taxes, tribute, and plunder.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Taxes were a primary source of funding for pre-modern armies.
- **Point 2**: Tribute and plunder were also common sources of funding.
- **Point 3**: Military leaders often had to be creative in securing funds for their armies.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown

**Taxes**
Taxes were a primary source of funding for pre-modern armies. Governments would impose various taxes on their citizens to fund military endeavors. These taxes could be in the form of direct taxation, where citizens were required to pay a fixed amount, or indirect taxation, where taxes were imposed on goods and services.

**Tribute and Plunder**
Tribute and plunder were also common sources of funding for pre-modern armies. Tribute involved the payment of money or goods by conquered territories to their rulers. Plunder involved the seizure of wealth and resources from defeated enemies. Military leaders often had to be creative in securing funds for their armies, as taxes and tribute may not have been enough to cover the costs of military operations.

> 💡 Insight: Pre-modern armies were often funded through a combination of taxes, tribute, and plunder.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- The financial strain of maintaining a large army could lead to economic instability and social unrest.
- Creative funding solutions, such as tribute and plunder, could have significant impacts on the relationships between rulers and their subjects.
- The financial constraints of pre-modern armies often limited their ability to engage in large-scale military campaigns.

## ✨ Conclusion
Understanding the finances of pre-modern armies is crucial for worldbuilders looking to create realistic and engaging military scenarios. By considering the various sources of funding and the financial constraints of pre-modern armies, worldbuilders can create more nuanced and believable military stories.
