## 📈 Detailed Breakdown

**Element 1: The Riemann Hypothesis**
The Riemann Hypothesis, proposed by Bernhard Riemann in 1859, concerns the distribution of prime numbers. It states that all non-trivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function lie on a specific critical line. This hypothesis, if proven, would have profound implications for number theory.

**Element 2: Generalized Riemann Hypothesis**
The GRH is an extension of the Riemann Hypothesis, applying to a broader class of functions. It proposes that all non-trivial zeros of certain L-functions lie on a critical line. This generalization is crucial for understanding the distribution of prime numbers and has applications in cryptography and number theory.
