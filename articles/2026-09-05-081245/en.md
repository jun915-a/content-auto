## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Theorem Itself**
Fermat's Last Theorem states that no three positive integers a, b, and c can satisfy the equation aⁿ + bⁿ = cⁿ for any integer value of n greater than 2. This was famously conjectured by Pierre de Fermat.

**The Proof's Complexity**
Andrew Wiles' proof, completed in 1994, is exceptionally intricate, spanning hundreds of pages and relying on advanced concepts from number theory and algebraic geometry, making manual verification challenging even for human mathematicians.

**AI's Role in Verification**
