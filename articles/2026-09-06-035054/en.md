## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Ergonomic Split Design**
The Terpstra Keyboard’s **split, staggered layout** is its defining feature. By separating the left and right halves, it aligns naturally with your hands, reducing the **uncomfortable inward reach** of traditional keyboards. This design is backed by **ergonomic research**, making it ideal for long typing sessions. The **adjustable angles** let you fine-tune the keyboard’s fit, ensuring optimal comfort for users of all sizes.

**Hot-Swappable Switches**
One of the most **user-friendly innovations** is its **hot-swappable PCB**. No soldering required—simply pop out switches and replace them with your preferred type (linear, tactile, or clicky). This flexibility allows for **endless customization**, whether you’re tweaking for gaming, typing, or macro-heavy workflows. The keyboard supports **common switch profiles** like Cherry MX, Gateron, and Kailh, ensuring broad compatibility.

> 💡 Insight: **The hot-swap feature lowers the barrier to experimentation**, making it easier for users to refine their ideal typing experience without permanent commitment.

**Modular and Open-Source**
Beyond switches, Terpstra’s keyboard thrives on **open-source firmware**. Users can **remap keys, create macros, or even design custom layouts** via QMK or ZMK. This level of control appeals to **tech-savvy users** who want to tailor their keyboard to specific needs—whether it’s programming shortcuts, gaming combos, or accessibility tweaks. The **modular design** also extends to **custom keycaps**, allowing for a truly personalized build.

**Aesthetic and Build Quality**
Visually, the Terpstra Keyboard exudes **modern minimalism** with its **aluminum frame** and **RGB backlighting**. The **compact footprint** (60% layout) makes it perfect for small desks, yet the **spacious key arrangement** ensures ample finger movement. The **durable materials**—including **PN30 brass stabilizers**—promise longevity, while the **smooth PCB** enhances reliability.
