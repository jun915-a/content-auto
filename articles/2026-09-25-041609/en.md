The temperature varies: **Alaska’s** can exceed **100°C (212°F)**, while **California’s** often hover around **40–50°C (104–122°F)**—ideal for soaking. As climate change threatens some thermal sites, preserving them ensures future generations can experience Earth’s **natural saunas**—one soak at a time."
