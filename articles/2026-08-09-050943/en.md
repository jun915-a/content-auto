# Witness the Total Solar Eclipse of a Lifetime

Get ready to witness one of the rarest celestial events - the total solar eclipse on August 12. With an open-source interactive map, you can track the eclipse's path and experience the breathtaking beauty of this phenomenon.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
An open-source interactive map, available at https://eclipsefan.org, allows users to track the path of the total solar eclipse on August 12. This map is equipped with various layers, including the eclipse path, Besselian elements, umbra, and shadow 3D projections.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Interactive Map**
The interactive map at eclipsefan.org is the central feature of this event. It allows users to zoom in and out, pan across the map, and explore various layers to gain a deeper understanding of the eclipse.

**Layers and Features**
The map includes several layers, each providing unique insights into the eclipse. The Besselian elements layer displays the critical points of the eclipse, while the umbra layer shows the area where the sun is completely obscured by the moon. The shadow 3D projection layer creates a 3D representation of the eclipse, allowing users to visualize the moon's shadow on the Earth's surface.
