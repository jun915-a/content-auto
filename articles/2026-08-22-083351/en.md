# Kagi Search Filters Out Paywalled Links

Kagi, the privacy-focused search engine, now lets users hide paywalled content from their search results, offering a cleaner, more accessible browsing experience.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Kagi has introduced a new setting allowing users to filter out links to websites that require a subscription or payment to access their content. This feature aims to provide a more efficient and frustration-free search experience by prioritizing freely available information.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Hide Paywalls**: Users can now opt-out of seeing paywalled content.
- **Focus on Free Info**: Search results will prioritize accessible articles.
- **Enhanced Browsing**: Reduces friction and saves time when searching.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**New "Hide Paywalled Sites" Setting**
A new toggle has been added to Kagi's settings. When enabled, Kagi's search algorithm will actively deprioritize or remove links from sites known to have hard paywalls, ensuring users are less likely to click on content they cannot access.

**Improved User Experience**
This feature directly addresses a common frustration for internet users who encounter paywalls after clicking a search result. By proactively filtering these links, Kagi enhances the overall usability and value of its search engine.

> 💡 Insight: This move highlights Kagi's commitment to user-centric features that solve real-world browsing annoyances.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Users will save time and avoid the frustration of hitting paywalls.
- Search results will be more consistently useful for those seeking free information.
- Kagi further differentiates itself by offering a unique solution to a widespread online problem.

## ✨ Conclusion
Kagi's new paywall filter is a significant step towards a more user-friendly and efficient search experience, demonstrating their dedication to putting users first.
