# The Real Cost of Frontier Models: Tokens * Price, Right?

Discover the true cost of cutting-edge AI models and why tokens are the new currency for innovation.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Frontier models, the bleeding edge of AI innovation, come at a steep price. With the advent of token-based pricing, the cost of these models is no longer just a matter of compute resources and infrastructure. Tokens, the new currency of innovation, hold the key to unlocking the true cost of frontier models.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Token-based pricing revolutionizes the cost of frontier models.
- **Point 2**: Compute resources and infrastructure are no longer the sole determinants of cost.
- **Point 3**: Tokens represent the true cost of innovation in AI.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The traditional approach to pricing frontier models focused on the compute resources and infrastructure required to train and deploy them. However, this approach failed to account for the true cost of innovation, which lies in the tokens required to acquire and utilize these models. With the rise of token-based pricing, the cost of frontier models has become more transparent and accessible.

**Element 2**
Token-based pricing has several advantages over traditional pricing models. It allows for more accurate cost estimation, as the cost of tokens is directly tied to the value of the model. It also provides a more equitable distribution of cost, as the cost of tokens is borne by the users of the model, rather than being absorbed by the developers.

> 💡 Insight: Token-based pricing represents a fundamental shift in the way we think about the cost of innovation in AI.
