# Nintendo Switch 2 Gets Upgrade

Nintendo announces new product revisions with replaceable batteries, enhancing user experience and sustainability

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Nintendo is set to introduce new product revisions in Europe, focusing on replaceable batteries to improve overall user experience and reduce electronic waste.

- **Eco-Friendly**: Reduce electronic waste
