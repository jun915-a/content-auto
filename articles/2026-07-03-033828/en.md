# Green Boots Climber Mystery Solved

A DNA test has finally identified the 'Green Boots' climber's identity after decades of mystery surrounding this macabre landmark on the frozen ice of Everest.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Green Boots is the nickname for a climber who died on Mount Everest in 1996. His body remains at a spot known as the Second Step, and his shoes are still visible above the snowline. A DNA test has now revealed the climber's identity.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: The DNA test was conducted using a sample from the climber's body.
- **Point 2**: The test revealed the climber's identity as Beck Weathers, an American climber.
- **Point 3**: Weathers' body was found at the Second Step, near the 'Green Boots'.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Discovery**: The discovery of the 'Green Boots' was made in 2001 by a climber who stumbled upon the body. Since then, the identity of the climber has been a mystery.

**The Investigation**: In 2019, a team of researchers collected a DNA sample from the climber's body and matched it with Weathers' family using online genealogy websites.

> 💡 Insight: This case highlights the power of DNA testing in solving cold cases.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- The discovery of Weathers' identity has brought closure to his family.
- The case has also raised awareness about the risks of climbing Mount Everest.
- It has also highlighted the importance of DNA testing in forensic science.

## ✨ Conclusion
The mystery of the 'Green Boots' climber is finally solved, thanks to a DNA test. This case is a testament to the power of science in solving cold cases and bringing closure to families.
