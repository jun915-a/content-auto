## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
HomelabFest is an annual, grassroots tech conference where passionate builders, engineers, and hobbyists converge to explore the art of **homelabbing**—the practice of creating custom tech setups at home. From servers and networking to AI, automation, and creative projects, this event bridges the gap between passion and practicality, fostering a community driven by curiosity and collaboration.
