# 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Silpheed is a vertically scrolling shooter developed by Game Arts and released on the Sega CD in 1994. It stands out for its 3D-like graphics and immersive storyline.

**Storyline**: The game's storyline is heavily focused on character development and player choice, setting a new standard for storytelling in games.
