## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Protecting your 1983 synthesizer's delicate electronics is crucial when integrating Web MIDI. This involves carefully selecting the MIDI interface and adjusting settings to avoid overloading the device.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Choose a MIDI interface with a low latency and robust circuitry.
- **Point 2**: Set the MIDI buffer size to minimize data loss and optimize processing.
- **Point 3**: Regularly update your Web MIDI software to ensure compatibility with your synthesizer.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Buffer Size Matters**: When setting the MIDI buffer size, it's essential to strike a balance between data loss and processing power. A larger buffer can help prevent data loss but may cause latency issues.

**MIDI Interface Selection**: Opt for a MIDI interface with a low latency and robust circuitry to ensure stable communication with your synthesizer. This will help prevent crashes and data corruption.

> 💡 Insight: By prioritizing your synthesizer's safety and choosing the right MIDI interface, you can enjoy seamless integration with Web MIDI while preserving the integrity of your vintage instrument.
