# How Zinc Creep Doomed Arecibo’s Iconic Telescope

The Arecibo Observatory’s collapse in 2020 wasn’t an act of nature—it was a slow-motion failure of science. Discover how zinc creep, a hidden enemy of metal, brought down one of humanity’s greatest listening posts to the cosmos.

**The Arecibo Observatory, once the world’s largest radio telescope, collapsed in dramatic fashion on December 1, 2020. The cause? Not a hurricane or an earthquake, but a subtle, insidious process called zinc creep—an overlooked failure of engineering that brought down one of science’s most iconic structures.**
