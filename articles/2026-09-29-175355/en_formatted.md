**## 🔑 The Core of This Topic**

Conversational AI agents—whether embedded in messaging apps, voice assistants, or standalone chatbots—are designed to learn, adapt, and engage in human-like interactions. However, their ability to **process, store, and analyze user data** often comes at the cost of **unintended privacy violations**. These agents don’t just respond to queries; they **track behavior patterns, extract personal details, and sometimes share data with third parties**, all while operating under vague terms of service that few users scrutinize.**
