**Exascale Computing Breakthrough**

This achievement relies on the **Aurora supercomputer**, one of the most powerful systems in the world, capable of performing **1.2 exaflops** of computational power. By simulating fusion reactions at this scale, the team aims to refine our understanding of plasma behavior, a critical component in achieving **net-energy-positive fusion**.

The simulation’s success hinges on advanced algorithms and **machine learning techniques**, which optimize computational efficiency while maintaining precision. This hybrid approach allows researchers to explore previously inaccessible parameters, such as **high-energy-density plasmas**, which are essential for inertial confinement fusion (ICF) and magnetic confinement fusion (MCF) strategies.
