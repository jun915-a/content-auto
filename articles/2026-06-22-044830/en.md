# JSON-LD for Personal Websites

Boost your website's search engine ranking with JSON-LD, a simple way to add structured data to your site, making it more discoverable and improving user experience

## The Core of This Topic
JSON-LD is a method of adding structured data to your website, helping search engines understand your content better. It's a simple way to make your site more discoverable and improve user experience.

## 5-Second Key Points
- **Improved Search Engine Rankings**: Increase your website's visibility
- **Enhanced User Experience**: Provide users with more accurate and helpful information
- **Easy Implementation**: Add JSON-LD to your site with minimal coding knowledge

## Detailed Breakdown
**What is JSON-LD?**
JSON-LD is a lightweight data interchange format that is easy to read and write, making it simple to add structured data to your website.

**How Does it Work?**
JSON-LD works by adding a script tag to your website's HTML, containing the structured data, which is then read by search engines and other web crawlers.

> Insight: By adding JSON-LD to your website, you can help search engines understand your content better, leading to improved search engine rankings and a better user experience.
