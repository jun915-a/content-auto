## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Link Rot**
Link rot occurs when links to online content become broken due to website updates, removal, or other factors. This can result in lost historical data, cultural significance, and online identity.

**The Role of the Internet Archive**
The Internet Archive is a digital library that aims to preserve the old web by archiving websites and making them accessible. However, even the Internet Archive is not immune to link rot.
