It’s not just ignoring a problem—it’s convincing yourself it doesn’t matter. This response is common in high-stakes situations, like financial mismanagement or relationship breakdowns, where the truth feels too overwhelming to face. While denial provides temporary comfort, it erodes trust and opportunities for growth.\n\n> 💡 Insight: Problems don’t disappear when ignored; they fester.
