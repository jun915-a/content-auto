# Trump's Green Card Rule

Green card seekers must leave the US to apply, says Trump administration, sparking controversy and concern among immigrants

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Trump administration has introduced a new rule requiring green card seekers to leave the US to apply, affecting thousands of immigrants.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **New Rule**: Green card seekers must exit the US
- **Application Process**: Applies to adjustment of status
- **Exception**: Extraordinary circumstances only

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Immigration Impact**
The new rule will significantly affect immigrants who have been living in the US, forcing them to leave the country to apply for a green card.

**USCIS Guidance**
The US Citizenship and Immigration Services will provide guidance on the new rule, including exceptions for extraordinary circumstances.

> 💡 Insight: The new rule may lead to increased uncertainty and hardship for immigrant families.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Longer wait times for green card applicants
- Increased stress for immigrant families
- Potential economic impact on US businesses

## ✨ Conclusion
The Trump administration's new rule will have far-reaching consequences for green card seekers, emphasizing the need for clear guidance and support for affected immigrants.
