# Ancient Peace Treaty Fragment Reveals 3,300-Year-Old Diplomacy

Archaeologists in Turkey uncovered a fragment of the world’s oldest known peace treaty, dating back to 1300 BCE. The discovery sheds light on ancient Egyptian-Hittite relations and offers rare insights into early diplomatic practices. A groundbreaking find for history enthusiasts and scholars alike.
