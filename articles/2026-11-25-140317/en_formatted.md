## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
This project resurrects classic, resource-intensive tech demos from Nvidia and ATI, originally designed for powerful hardware of the past. By leveraging modern web technologies like WebGPU and WebAssembly, these demanding graphical experiences are now accessible and runnable directly within your web browser.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Classic Demos Reimagined**
Explore a curated gallery of visually stunning tech demos that once pushed the boundaries of graphics cards. These are not mere screenshots but interactive showcases of groundbreaking visual effects from the late 90s and early 2000s.

**Web Technologies Powering Revival**
Using WebGPU for high-performance graphics and WebAssembly to run native code, these demos achieve impressive fidelity and performance within the browser environment, making them accessible to a wide audience without specialized hardware.
