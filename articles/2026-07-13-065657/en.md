# Flag AI-Generated Articles

Should HN add a flag for AI-generated articles to promote transparency and trust in the community?

## The Core of This Topic
The discussion revolves around adding a flag for AI-generated articles on HN to indicate their origin and promote transparency.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Transparency**: Flagging AI-generated content
- **Trust**: Building trust within the community
- **Indicator**: Showing the indicator without deranking
## Detailed Breakdown
**Community Feedback**
The community's feedback is crucial in deciding whether to implement this feature, as it affects the overall user experience.
**Content Origin**
Knowing the origin of the content helps users make informed decisions about the credibility of the information.
> Insight: Transparency is key to building a trustworthy community.
## Real-World Impact
- Improved transparency
- Enhanced trust
- Better decision-making
## Conclusion
Adding a flag for AI-generated articles can significantly impact the HN community by promoting transparency and trust.
