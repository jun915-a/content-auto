# AWS: A Reminder Why I Left

A personal account of returning to AWS and being reminded of the reasons for initially leaving, highlighting key points and real-world impacts

# The Core of This Topic
The author's return to AWS was a reminder of the platform's complexities and challenges, prompting a reflection on the reasons for initially leaving.

# 5-Second Key Points
- **Steep Learning Curve**: AWS has a complex architecture
- **Cost Management**: Difficult to manage and optimize costs
- **Service Overlap**: Overlapping services cause confusion

# Detailed Breakdown
**Complexity**
AWS has a vast array of services, making it hard to navigate and find the right tool for the job.

**User Experience**
The platform's user interface can be overwhelming, leading to frustration and decreased productivity.

> Insight: Understanding the core services and simplifying the workflow can improve the overall experience.

# Real-World Impact
- Increased costs due to incorrect service usage
- Decreased productivity from navigating the complex interface
- Difficulty in finding the right talent to manage AWS services

# Conclusion
The author's experience serves as a reminder to carefully evaluate the needs of a project before choosing a platform, considering factors such as complexity, cost, and user experience.
