## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1: **
The atlas organizes systems by **category** (e.g., search engines, social networks) and **component** (e.g., caching, databases). Each entry includes a **diagram** with labeled services, APIs, and data flows. For instance, the **Uber ride-matching system** diagram visualizes how requests are routed, drivers matched, and payments processed—all with annotations explaining latency, load balancing, and fault tolerance.

**Element 2: **
Beyond static images, the atlas emphasizes **interactivity**. Users can **hover over components** to see tooltips, click to expand sub-systems, and even **navigate between related systems** (e.g., from Uber’s architecture to Lyft’s). This approach mimics real-world debugging or design review sessions, making complex flows digestible.
