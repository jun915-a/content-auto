## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
AlphaGenome Atlas is a groundbreaking predictive map of the human genome. It forecasts the functional impact of every possible single DNA letter change, offering unprecedented insight into genetic variation and its implications for health and disease.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Predictive Power**: Forecasts the effect of any DNA letter change.
- **High Resolution**: Maps the genome at an unprecedented level of detail.
- **Disease Insights**: Aids in understanding genetic contributions to disease.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Functional Impact Prediction**
AlphaGenome Atlas utilizes advanced AI models to predict how altering a single DNA base pair will affect gene function. This allows researchers to pinpoint potentially harmful mutations with greater accuracy.

**Comprehensive Genome Coverage**
The atlas covers the vast majority of the human genome, including both coding and non-coding regions, which are crucial for understanding gene regulation and overall genomic health.

> 💡 Insight: This tool moves beyond simply identifying variations to understanding their functional consequences.
