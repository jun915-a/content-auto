# Indexing a Year of Video Locally

A comprehensive guide on how to efficiently index a year's worth of video data on a 2021 MacBook with Gemma4-31B (50GB swap).

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Indexing a year's worth of video data locally can be a daunting task, especially when dealing with limited storage space. However, with the right approach and tools, it's achievable on a 2021 MacBook.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Utilize a powerful GPU like Gemma4-31B for efficient processing.
- **Point 2**: Optimize storage space by leveraging swap memory (50GB).
- **Point 3**: Choose the right indexing tool for the job.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Video Data Preparation**
Preprocessing video data is crucial for efficient indexing. This involves trimming unnecessary frames, converting formats, and resizing videos to reduce storage space.

**Indexing Tool Selection**
Selecting the right indexing tool can make a significant difference in performance. Consider tools that leverage GPU acceleration and are optimized for video data.

> 💡 Insight: The right combination of video data preparation and indexing tool selection can significantly reduce indexing time.
