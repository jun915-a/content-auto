# Rare Medieval Bookmark Fetches Stunning Price at Auction

A remarkably preserved medieval bookmark, believed to be from the 15th century, has shattered auction estimates, highlighting the enduring value of historical artifacts and their connection to everyday life.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
This story centers on a rare, intricately decorated medieval bookmark that far surpassed its expected auction price. Such items offer a tangible link to literacy and personal devotional practices in the late medieval period.

- **Auction Success**: Sold for significantly more than anticipated.
- **Historical Significance**: Provides insight into medieval reading habits and artistry.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Artifact**
This exquisite bookmark, crafted from vellum, features vibrant illustrations and calligraphy. Its survival through centuries is remarkable, offering a glimpse into the personal lives and artistic sensibilities of people from the late medieval era.

**Auction Performance**
Despite initial conservative estimates, the bookmark's unique historical value and rarity captivated collectors, driving the final sale price to astonishing heights. This demonstrates a strong market interest in tangible pieces of history.

> 💡 Insight: The high price reflects not just the object's age but its rarity and the emotional connection it fosters with the past.
