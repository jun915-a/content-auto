# Lost Coltrane Tapes: Frank Tiberi’s Hidden Jazz Treasure

Unheard recordings of John Coltrane’s genius, captured by Frank Tiberi, are finally released—offering fresh insight into the saxophonist’s evolution and legacy. A must-listen for fans and historians alike.

**John Coltrane’s legacy is vast, but even legends have untold stories.** Recently, a trove of previously unheard recordings—captured by jazz enthusiast Frank Tiberi—has emerged, offering a raw, intimate glimpse into the saxophonist’s creative process. These ‘Tiberi Tapes,’ spanning the 1950s and early 1960s, reveal Coltrane’s raw energy, experimental spirit, and the early seeds of his revolutionary sound. Now, Impulse Records has reissued these recordings, giving listeners a chance to hear history unfold in real time.
