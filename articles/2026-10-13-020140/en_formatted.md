Experience PS2 nostalgia on the web! This project statically recompiles PS2 games into WebAssembly, offering a unique way to play classics like Metal Gear Solid 2 and Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas in your browser.

The PS2-Web-Recomp project is an innovative endeavor, bringing classic PlayStation 2 games to modern web browsers. By statically recompiling PS2 game code into WebAssembly, it opens up a new avenue for gaming enthusiasts to relive their favorite PS2 titles.
