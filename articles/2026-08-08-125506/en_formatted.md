# Physicist Uploads Hamster's Runs to Strava

*Insert header image here*

A physicist has ingeniously rigged his pet hamster's wheel to track its activity and upload the data to Strava, the popular running app. Get ready for some surprisingly intense rodent workouts!

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
A physicist modified his pet hamster's exercise wheel with sensors and a microcontroller. This setup collects data on the hamster's running speed, distance, and duration, then transmits it wirelessly to Strava, turning tiny paws into digital fitness achievements.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Hamster Power**: A physicist's pet hamster is now on Strava.
- **Tech Integration**: The hamster wheel is rigged with sensors and a microcontroller.
- **Data Upload**: Activity data is wirelessly sent to the popular Strava app.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Setup**
The core of the project involves a small microcontroller and sensors attached to the hamster's wheel. These components detect the wheel's rotation, translating movement into quantifiable data that mimics human exercise metrics. It's a clever blend of pet care and technological innovation.

**Strava Integration**
Once the data is collected, it's wirelessly transmitted to a server and then uploaded to Strava. This allows the hamster's 'runs' to appear alongside human athletes' activities, complete with distance, pace, and even heart-rate zones (though simulated for the hamster).

> 💡 Insight: This project highlights how everyday objects and even pets can be integrated into digital fitness ecosystems with a bit of ingenuity.
