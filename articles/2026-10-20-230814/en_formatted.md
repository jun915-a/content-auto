## 📈 A Deeper Dive

**Element 1: The Viral Effect**
Publishing false statistics can create a ripple effect, with the data being shared and cited by numerous sources. This rapid spread is often due to the eye-catching nature of the stats.

**Element 2: Impact on Language Models**
Language models, including LLMs, are trained on vast amounts of text data. If fake stats are included, these models may learn and repeat the misinformation, further propagating it.
