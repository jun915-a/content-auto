## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
FlattenSF.com is a web application that visualizes the elevation changes on San Francisco streets. It helps users find the most level paths for cycling, walking, or any activity where steep hills are a deterrent, revolutionizing urban navigation.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Visualize Elevation**: See how hilly or flat a street is at a glance.
- **Plan Your Route**: Find the easiest paths between any two points.
- **Enjoy SF More**: Make your journeys less strenuous and more pleasant.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Street Color Coding**
Streets are color-coded to represent their steepness. Green indicates flatter routes, while red signifies the steepest inclines. This intuitive system allows for quick identification of easy-to-navigate paths.

**Interactive Map**
The platform features an interactive map of San Francisco. Users can click on any two points to generate a suggested route, prioritizing minimal elevation gain. This makes planning a trip effortless.

> 💡 Insight: By making elevation data accessible and actionable, FlattenSF empowers residents and visitors to explore the city more comfortably.

**Route Calculation**
The underlying algorithm calculates the best route by considering the gradient of each street segment. It aims to minimize the total elevation climbed, offering a practical solution for everyday travel.

**User Contributions**
While not explicitly detailed on the site, community input and data accuracy are crucial for such a tool to remain effective and up-to-date with the city's ever-changing landscape.

> 💡 Insight: The tool democratizes route planning, moving beyond simple distance to incorporate a crucial factor for physical activity: effort.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- **Improved Cycling Experience**: Makes cycling accessible to more people, regardless of fitness level.
- **Easier Navigation for All**: Benefits pedestrians, families with strollers, and those with mobility challenges.
- **Encourages Exploration**: Promotes discovering new parts of the city without the dread of steep climbs.
