# Passkeys Flawed

*Insert header image here*

Passkeys, meant to simplify security, may confuse consumers due to lack of user-centric design, hindering widespread adoption and potentially creating more issues than they solve.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Passkeys are designed to replace traditional passwords, offering a more secure way to authenticate online. However, their complexity may overwhelm average users.
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Simplified Security
- **Point 2**: User Confusion
- **Point 3**: Adoption Challenges
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: Passkeys rely on public key cryptography, which, while secure, can be daunting for non-technical users.
**Element 2**: The process of setting up and managing passkeys requires a level of technical savvy that not all users possess.
> 💡 Insight: For passkeys to be effective, they must be intuitive and user-friendly.
## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Increased Support Requests
- Lower Adoption Rates
- Potential Security Risks
## ✨ Conclusion
The future of passkeys hinges on making them accessible and easy to use for all consumers, not just the tech-savvy.
