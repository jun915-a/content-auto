Linum.ai has developed a text-to-image generative model, trained at a cost of approximately $20,000. The key innovation lies in making the trained model weights publicly available under an Apache 2.0 license, lowering the barrier to entry for AI-powered creative tools.

- **Open Source Weights**: Apache 2.0 licensed weights available for public use.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Model Training Cost**
The project highlights that a capable text-to-image model can be trained for a fraction of typical costs, around $20,000. This makes advanced AI generation more accessible to smaller teams and independent developers.

**Open Access Weights**
By releasing the trained weights under the permissive Apache 2.0 license, Linum.ai encourages widespread adoption and further development. This fosters a collaborative environment for AI innovation.
