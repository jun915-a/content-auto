Flock-powered police chiefs stalking women raises concerns about warrantless tracking, highlighting the need for stricter regulations and oversight

## The Core of This Topic
The issue at hand is the misuse of Flock-powered systems by police chiefs to track women without warrants, sparking debates about privacy and accountability.

## 5-Second Key Points
- **Lack of Oversight**: Police chiefs are using Flock systems to track individuals without proper authorization.
- **Privacy Concerns**: This practice raises significant concerns about personal privacy and the potential for abuse.
- **Need for Reform**: There is a growing need for stricter regulations and oversight to prevent such misuse.
