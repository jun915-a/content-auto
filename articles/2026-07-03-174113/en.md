## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Local reasoning is a fundamental concept in computer science and mathematics that allows us to analyze and understand complex systems by breaking them down into smaller, manageable components. By focusing on local properties, we can gain insights into the global behavior of a system, making it easier to reason about and predict its behavior.
