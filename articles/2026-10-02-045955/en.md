These organisms, including archaea and bacteria, produce methane and other compounds that sustain life in environments where oxygen is scarce or absent. Their survival strategies—such as using sulfur or iron instead of oxygen—echo processes that may have dominated Earth’s primordial seas.\n\n**Element 2**\n> 💡 **Insight**: The discovery suggests that life’s early evolution might not have required oxygen at all. This research not only honors the resilience of microbial life but also opens doors to exploring life’s potential in the most unexpected corners of our universe. As we peer deeper into these forgotten ecosystems, we may uncover the very origins of life itself—and perhaps, the blueprint for its survival across the cosmos."
