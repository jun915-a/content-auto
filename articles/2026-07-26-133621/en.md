Debian is poised to integrate Large Language Models (LLMs) to enhance its infrastructure, user experience, and developer capabilities. This move seeks to bridge the gap between AI-driven innovation and the timeless reliability of the Debian ecosystem.

- **Point 2**: Leveraging LLMs for personalized user support and AI-assisted coding tools.
- **Point 3**: Utilizing LLMs for enhanced security features and real-time threat detection.
