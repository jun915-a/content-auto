# DeepSeek V4 Pro Price Drop

DeepSeek makes the V4 Pro price discount permanent, adjusting to 1/4 of the original price after May 31, 2026, promoting AI accessibility

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
DeepSeek's V4 Pro model API pricing will be officially adjusted to 1/4 of the original price, making the 75% discount permanent.
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Permanent Discount**: 75% off the original price
- **New Price**: 1/4 of the original price
- **Effective Date**: After May 31, 2026, 15:59 UTC
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**New Pricing Structure**
DeepSeek's decision to make the discount permanent aims to increase AI adoption.
**Accessibility**
The reduced price will make the V4 Pro model more accessible to a wider range of users.
