## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Understanding the Technology**: The live facial recognition technology uses advanced algorithms to match passengers' faces with those on a database of wanted individuals. The system can process images in real-time and identify individuals across different locations.

**How it Works**: The process is straightforward – passengers' faces are scanned for up to 5 seconds, and the system checks for a match with the database. If a match is found, the authorities are notified immediately.
