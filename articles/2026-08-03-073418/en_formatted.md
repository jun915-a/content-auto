## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Philosophy**
Germany has given the world some of the most influential philosophers in history, from Kant to Nietzsche. Their ideas continue to shape our understanding of the world and our place in it.

**Music and Art**
From Bach to Beethoven, Germany has produced some of the most iconic composers in history. The country's art scene is equally impressive, with artists like Monet and Picasso drawing inspiration from German culture.
