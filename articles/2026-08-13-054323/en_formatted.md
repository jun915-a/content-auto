Woxi is a groundbreaking open-source interpreter for the Wolfram Language, meticulously crafted in Rust to rival Mathematica’s capabilities. Offering both a GUI akin to Mathematica and seamless integration with CLI tools, Jupyter, and Python, it empowers developers and researchers to harness symbolic computation without vendor lock-in.
