## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Leaves is a text-UI disk usage treemap visualizer designed for containers and remote servers. Traditional GUI disk analyzers are great for figuring out what's filling up your laptop/desktop drive, but they're limited when working with containers or remote servers. In these cases, users are often left with purely text-based utilities like 'du' to help them make sense of their disk usage.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points

* **Point 3**: Easy to use and understand.
