**There Will Come Soft Rains** is a haunting exploration of humanity’s fragility in the face of technological advancement and apocalyptic annihilation. Written by Ray Bradbury in 1950, this short story unfolds in a deserted American suburb where a fully automated house, oblivious to its empty surroundings, carries out its daily rituals. The narrative masterfully blends dystopian horror with poetic melancholy, questioning whether progress and automation can replace human warmth and resilience. It remains a timeless cautionary tale about the consequences of unchecked technological dependence and the emotional cost of survival.
