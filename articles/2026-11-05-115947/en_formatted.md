**Detection Method**
Security researchers utilized advanced network monitoring tools to identify the presence of traffic originating from what appeared to be residential IP addresses, routed through the White House network. This method is crucial for uncovering hidden or anomalous network behaviors.

**Nature of Residential Proxies**
These proxies leverage legitimate home internet connections, making them harder to detect than traditional data center proxies. Their use within a government network is highly irregular and warrants immediate attention from security teams.
