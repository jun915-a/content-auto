# Warsaw in Voxels: A Playful 3D City Adventure

*Insert header image here*

Dive into an interactive voxel-based reimagining of Warsaw’s historic center—where Minecraft-inspired charm meets real-world exploration. Adjust time, weather, and even control pedestrians in this immersive digital playground.

**Warsaw in voxels** is more than just a digital toy—it’s a vibrant, interactive 3D map of Warsaw’s city center, blending the blocky aesthetics of *Minecraft* and *MagicaVoxel* with real-world charm. This project transforms the Polish capital into a sandbox where users can wander, experiment, and even control virtual pedestrians, all while tweaking the environment with ease. It’s a creative fusion of art, technology, and urban exploration, inviting players to experience Warsaw in a whole new way.
