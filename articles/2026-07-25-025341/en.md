Discover the innovative new feature in Firefox that's revolutionizing online security and browsing. Learn how to use Firefox Containers Preview to separate your online activities and keep your identity safe.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Firefox Containers Preview is an innovative feature in Firefox that allows users to separate their online activities into individual containers. This means that each container can have its own set of bookmarks, history, and add-ons, making it easier to keep your online identity safe.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Containerization**
Firefox Containers Preview uses containerization to separate your online activities. This means that each container is a separate, isolated environment that can't access or share data with other containers.

**Security Benefits**
By using containers, you can significantly improve your online security and identity protection. Each container can have its own set of permissions, making it easier to restrict access to sensitive information.
