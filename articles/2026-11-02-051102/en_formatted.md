# Outcrop.Rocks: AR Turns Landscapes Into Geology Classrooms

*Insert header image here*

Imagine pointing your phone at a mountain and instantly learning its geological secrets. Outcrop.Rocks uses AR to overlay maps and insights—turning hikes into interactive lessons.

**A groundbreaking AR app transforms how we explore Earth’s history.** Outcrop.Rocks overlays geologic maps and rock descriptions onto real landscapes, letting users identify formations, read their stories, and even visualize hidden layers. No more guessing what that outcrop means—just point, tap, and learn. This tool bridges the gap between curiosity and expertise, making geology accessible to everyone, from casual hikers to educators. It’s the future of fieldwork, redefined through augmented reality.
