# Tokens: The New Era of 'Too Cheap to Meter'?

Exploring the concept of tokens becoming so abundant and accessible that their cost becomes negligible, reminiscent of the 'too cheap to meter' energy debate. Is this the future of digital assets?

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The idea of "tokens too cheap to meter" suggests a future where digital tokens, often representing value or access, become so abundant and easily generated that their individual cost or utility becomes practically insignificant. This mirrors the historical debate about electricity generation costs.
