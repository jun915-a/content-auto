## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The FCC has approved an experimental space mirror designed to collect and reflect sunlight, providing a constant source of renewable energy on demand.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
* **Point 1**: Harnessing solar energy in space for use on Earth.
* **Point 2**: The mirror's ability to beam sunlight at night could disrupt astronomical observations.
* **Point 3**: The project's focus on low Earth orbit is sparking debate among space enthusiasts.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Space-Based Solar Energy**
With the space mirror, solar energy can be collected and concentrated in space, eliminating the need for terrestrial solar panels and their associated environmental impacts. This technology has the potential to provide a constant and reliable source of renewable energy.

**Disruptions to Astronomical Observations**
However, the mirror's ability to beam sunlight at night could disrupt astronomical observations. The increased brightness could interfere with telescopes and other astronomical equipment, potentially hindering our understanding of the universe.
