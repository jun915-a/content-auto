# Jurassic Park Computers: A Glimpse into the Future

Step into the iconic world of Jurassic Park and explore the fictional computers that brought dinosaurs to life. Delve into the design and functionality of these groundbreaking systems.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The computers in Jurassic Park, designed by Dennis Muren, are a testament to 90s computer graphics. These systems used proprietary software and hardware to render photorealistic dinosaurs.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: The computers in Jurassic Park were designed to mimic real-world systems, but with futuristic interfaces.
- **Point 2**: The system's user interface was inspired by Unix and early graphical user interfaces.
- **Point 3**: The team used a combination of hardware and software to achieve realistic effects.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Unix Interface**
The Unix-inspired interface was a nod to the early days of computer graphics. The team wanted to create a system that felt familiar, yet futuristic.

**The Graphics Workstation**
The graphics workstation was the heart of the system, responsible for rendering photorealistic dinosaurs. It was a custom-built machine, designed to push the limits of what was possible at the time.
