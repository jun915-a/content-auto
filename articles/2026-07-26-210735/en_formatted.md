## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Technical Challenge**
Creating an immersive experience like the Grace Cathedral tour required a deep understanding of 3D modeling, lighting, and physics. The team used PlayCanvas's powerful features to build a highly detailed and realistic environment that simulates the actual cathedral.

**The Artistic Vision**
The experience was designed to transport users into the heart of the cathedral, with a focus on storytelling and atmosphere. The team worked closely with the client to ensure that every aspect of the tour, from the lighting to the sound design, was carefully crafted to evoke a sense of wonder and awe.
