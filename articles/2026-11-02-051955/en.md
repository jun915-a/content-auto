# AI Wikis Distorting Gaming Culture with Errors

Fan-made AI-driven wikis are flooding gaming communities with inaccuracies, blurring lines between fact and fiction and eroding trust in games culture. Here’s how the problem spreads—and why it matters.

**AI fan wikis are drowning gaming culture in misinformation, turning once-reliable sources into chaotic hubs of errors. From misattributed lore to fabricated lore, these AI-generated platforms are reshaping how fans engage with games—often for the worse.**
