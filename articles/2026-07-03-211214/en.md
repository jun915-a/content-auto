# Farmer, Marketer at Odds Over Sales of White Nectarines

A California farmer and a marketer are locked in a dispute over the sales of white nectarines, with the marketer alleging that the farmer has infringed on a patent.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
California farmer Al Van Amburgh is at the center of a heated dispute with a marketer over the sales of white nectarines. The marketer, who has a patent for the variety, claims that the farmer's sales of the fruit infringe on their intellectual property rights.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: A California farmer is accused of patent infringement over white nectarine sales.
- **Point 2**: The disputed fruit is a popular variety, sought after for its unique taste and appearance.
- **Point 3**: The patent holder is seeking damages from the farmer and an injunction to stop sales.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
The dispute centers around the 'White Beauty' variety of nectarine, which is prized for its sweet flavor and smooth skin. The marketer, who holds the patent for the variety, claims that the farmer's sales of the fruit infringe on their intellectual property rights.

According to the patent holder, the farmer has been selling the 'White Beauty' nectarines without permission, which is a clear infringement of their rights. The marketer is seeking damages from the farmer and an injunction to stop sales of the disputed fruit.
