## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
WebKit's recent vulnerability in its IP leak detection mechanism has far-reaching implications for proxy browsers and iCloud Private Relay users. The issue arose when WebKit mistakenly identified legitimate, encrypted connections as leaks, thereby exposing users' IP addresses.

- **Point 3**: Users' IP addresses are exposed due to the vulnerability.
 
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The issue stems from WebKit's inability to accurately identify legitimate, encrypted connections. This led to the misidentification of secure connections as IP leaks. As a result, users' IP addresses were inadvertently exposed.
 
**Element 2**
The vulnerability poses a significant threat to users of proxy browsers and iCloud Private Relay. These services rely on secure, encrypted connections to maintain user anonymity. However, the WebKit bug compromises this security, putting users at risk of IP address exposure.
