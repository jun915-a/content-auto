## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
JSON-LD, or JavaScript Object Notation for Linked Data, is a format used to represent data on the web in a way that's easily readable by machines and humans alike. For personal websites, JSON-LD offers a simple yet effective way to provide additional context to search engines and other services, enhancing the overall user experience and online visibility.
