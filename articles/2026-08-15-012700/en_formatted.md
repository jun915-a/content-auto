# Stop Sending Me Huge PRs; A Rant

*Insert header image here*

Tired of massive pull requests that overwhelm and intimidate? It's time to rethink your approach to code sharing.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Stop sending me huge PRs. It's not about being a contributor, it's about being a collaborator. The difference lies in your approach and understanding of the project's needs.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Don't overwhelm the team**: Break down your code into smaller, manageable chunks.
- **Respect the project's velocity**: Don't slow down the team's progress with enormous PRs.
- **Collaborate, don't dominate**: Work together to solve problems, not impose your solutions.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Breaking down code**: Imagine you're explaining a complex concept to a friend. Would you dump all the details at once, or break it down into smaller, more digestible pieces?

**Respecting project velocity**: A project's pace is like a river's flow. You can't just dump a massive stone into it and expect it to continue flowing smoothly.

> 💡 Insight: Collaboration is key to successful projects. By working together and respecting each other's time, you'll achieve more in less time.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- **Improved code quality**: Smaller PRs lead to more thorough reviews and better code quality.
- **Increased team morale**: Collaborative approaches boost team motivation and job satisfaction.
- **Faster project completion**: By working together efficiently, you'll reach project milestones sooner.

## ✨ Conclusion
Let's ditch the massive PRs and work together towards a common goal. By doing so, we'll create better code, stronger teams, and more successful projects. It's time to collaborate, not dominate.
