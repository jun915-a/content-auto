# HN’s November 2026 Hiring Roundup: Tech Jobs & Where to Find Them

From FAANG to startups, November 2026’s HN hiring thread reveals the hottest roles, remote vs. onsite splits, and where to apply—plus insider tips for landing your dream job in tech.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The November 2026 Hacker News ‘Who is hiring?’ thread is a snapshot of the tech job market, aggregating postings from top companies, startups, and niche firms. It highlights **remote flexibility trends**, **high-demand roles**, and **geographic hotspots**, offering job seekers a real-time pulse on where opportunities lie—whether you’re targeting FAANG, bootstrapped startups, or specialized domains like AI/ML or cybersecurity.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Remote work is still king**: Over 60% of postings allow **REMOTE (US)** or **REMOTE** (global), but some roles (e.g., hardware, biotech) require **ONSITE**.
- **AI/ML and cloud dominate**: Roles in generative AI, DevOps, and Kubernetes are oversubscribed, with **$200K+ salaries** common for senior hires.
- **Startups vs. corps**: Startups offer equity but fewer benefits; FAANG prioritizes stability but may push for **onsite 2-3x/year**.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1: Remote vs. Onsite Split**
The thread shows a clear divide: **REMOTE (US)** roles (e.g., backend dev at Stripe, data scientist at Palantir) dominate, but **ONSITE** is non-negotiable for hardware engineering (NVIDIA, Qualcomm) or lab-based biotech (Moderna). Hybrid roles (e.g., **REMOTE (US) with 1x/quarter onsite**) are growing, especially in finance tech (JPMorgan, Revolut).

> 💡 Insight: **Location flexibility is a dealbreaker**. Candidates with **H-1B sponsorship** or **green cards** have a 30% edge in onsite-heavy roles, per comments.

**Element 2: Salary & Equity Trends**
- **Entry-level**: **$120K–$150K** for software engineers (REMOTE), with **$100K+** for specialized roles (e.g., Rust devs at Mozilla).
- **Mid-level**: **$180K–$220K** for senior engineers, with **$250K+** for AI/ML leads (e.g., at Anthropic).
- **Startups**: Equity packages vary wildly—**0.001%–0.01%** for Series A, but **$50K–$100K** in signing bonuses can offset lower base pay.

> 💡 Insight: **Negotiation leverage lies in niche skills**. Roles like **quantitative research (finance) or embedded systems** command premiums even in remote settings.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- **Job seekers**: Prioritize **remote-first companies** (e.g., GitLab, Automattic) if location flexibility is critical, but **onsite roles** often lead to faster promotions.
- **Companies**: Expect **higher attrition** in fully remote roles—**40% of postings** mention hybrid as a future shift.
- **Industries**: **AI/ML and cybersecurity** are the fastest-growing fields, with **50%+ of postings** requiring prior experience in these areas.

## ✨ Conclusion
November 2026’s HN hiring thread underscores a **polarized but dynamic** tech job market. Remote work remains the norm, but **onsite roles** (especially in hardware/biotech) offer unique growth paths. For candidates, **specialization + negotiation** are key—whether you’re chasing **FAANG stability** or **startup equity**. Companies, meanwhile, must adapt to **hybrid expectations** to retain talent. The message? **Skills matter, but flexibility wins.**
