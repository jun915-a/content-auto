# Ask HN: Who wants to be hired? Share your information.

Calling all job seekers: share your details and get noticed by potential employers.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
This post is for individuals looking for work, whether it's a new job, a career change, or a freelancing opportunity. Share your information and connect with potential employers in the tech industry.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
* **Point 1**: Share your location, remote work options, and willingness to relocate.
* **Point 2**: List the technologies you're proficient in and your relevant experience.
* **Point 3**: Include your resume/CV, email address, and any relevant links.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Location**
Please share your preferred location, including cities or regions you're willing to work in. This will help employers identify potential matches for their job openings.

**Remote Work Options**
Do you have experience with remote work or are you open to it? Share your thoughts on this to help employers understand your flexibility.

**Technologies**
List the technologies you're proficient in, including programming languages, frameworks, and tools. This will help employers identify potential matches for their job openings.

**Résumé/CV**
Share a link to your online resume or upload your resume/CV directly. This will give employers a quick way to review your experience and qualifications.

**Email**
Include your email address so employers can easily contact you to discuss potential opportunities.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Potential employers can find and connect with job seekers who match their needs.
- Job seekers can increase their visibility and get noticed by potential employers.
- The tech industry can benefit from a more streamlined and efficient hiring process.

## ✨ Conclusion
By sharing your information and connecting with potential employers, you can take the first step towards finding your next career opportunity. Don't be shy – share your details and get noticed!
