# WebRTC Skimmers Hijack Shopping Sites for Card Data

Cybercriminals are exploiting WebRTC on popular shopping sites to steal credit card details. This sophisticated attack bypasses traditional security measures, putting shoppers at risk.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Attackers are leveraging WebRTC, a technology enabling real-time communication in browsers, to inject malicious scripts onto e-commerce websites. These scripts intercept payment details entered by unsuspecting customers, effectively creating a digital skimmer.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **WebRTC Exploitation**: Malicious scripts use WebRTC to capture payment data.
- **Popular Sites Targeted**: E-commerce platforms are the primary victims.
- **Data Theft**: Credit card numbers and personal information are stolen.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Malicious Script Injection**
Attackers find vulnerabilities in e-commerce sites to inject JavaScript code. This code then utilizes WebRTC to establish a connection and exfiltrate sensitive payment information directly from the user's browser.

**WebRTC Functionality**
WebRTC is designed for peer-to-peer communication, but attackers repurpose its capabilities to create a covert channel for data exfiltration, making it difficult to detect.

> 💡 Insight: The legitimate use of WebRTC for communication is being weaponized by cybercriminals.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- **Financial Loss**: Victims may suffer direct financial losses from fraudulent transactions.
- **Identity Theft**: Stolen data can be used for broader identity theft schemes.
- **Erosion of Trust**: Compromised sites lose customer trust and face reputational damage.

## ✨ Conclusion
This new wave of WebRTC-based skimming highlights the evolving threat landscape. Vigilance from both consumers and businesses is crucial to combat these sophisticated attacks and protect sensitive financial information.
