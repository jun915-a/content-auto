Four former CoScreen founders launch Petal, a free, open-source remote collaboration tool, reviving the spirit of ScreenHero after Datadog's service sunset.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Petal is a new, open-source remote collaboration tool created by the original founders of CoScreen. It aims to provide the same powerful screen sharing and remote control capabilities that users loved, but now completely free and community-driven.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Open Source**: Built on an open foundation for transparency and community contributions.
- **Free**: No cost for users, removing the barrier to powerful remote collaboration.
- **CoScreen Legacy**: Continues the work of the beloved CoScreen, offering familiar functionality.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Petal's Mission**
Petal aims to fill the void left by CoScreen's discontinuation. The team is committed to fostering a collaborative environment where users can contribute to the development and ensure the tool's longevity.

**Key Features**
Expect features reminiscent of CoScreen, including seamless screen sharing, remote control, and potentially new integrations. The open-source nature allows for rapid feature development based on user needs.

> 💡 Insight: The open-source model democratizes access to advanced collaboration tools and fosters innovation.

**Community Focus**
The project emphasizes community involvement, inviting developers and users to participate in shaping Petal's future. This collaborative approach ensures the tool evolves to meet real-world remote work demands.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Enables affordable, high-quality remote collaboration for individuals and small teams.
- Fosters innovation in the remote work space through open-source development.
- Provides a reliable alternative for users who relied on CoScreen's functionality.

Petal represents a significant step forward for open-source remote collaboration, offering a powerful, free, and community-backed solution for modern teams.
