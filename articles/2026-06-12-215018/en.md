# Stop FCC's KYC Regime

The FCC's proposed KYC regime threatens digital privacy, learn how to take action and protect your rights in the face of excessive regulation

## The Core of This Topic
The FCC's Know Your Customer (KYC) regime is a proposed regulation that could severely impact digital privacy and freedom. 
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Invasion of Privacy**: Excessive data collection
- **Overregulation**: Stifling innovation
- **Security Risks**: Increased vulnerability to hacks
## Detailed Breakdown
**Excessive Regulation**
The proposed regime would impose strict identification requirements, potentially limiting access to digital services.
**Impact on Innovation**
Overregulation could stifle the growth of new technologies and startups.
> Insight: The FCC's regime could have far-reaching consequences for the digital landscape.
## Real-World Impact
- Increased surveillance
- Decreased digital security
- Limited access to services
## Conclusion
It is crucial that we take action to stop the FCC's KYC regime and protect our digital rights.
