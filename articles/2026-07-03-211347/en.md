# International Chess Federation Sanctions Kramnik

The FIDE Ethics and Disciplinary Commission has issued a decision in a case involving Grandmaster Vladimir Kramnik, resulting in sanctions.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
FIDE's Ethics and Disciplinary Commission has found Grandmaster Vladimir Kramnik guilty of breaching FIDE's Code of Ethics. The commission has issued a decision, imposing sanctions on the Russian chess player.

The FIDE Ethics and Disciplinary Commission has the authority to investigate and hear cases involving FIDE members who are accused of breaching FIDE's Code of Ethics.
