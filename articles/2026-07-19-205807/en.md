# Why Cheap Clones Are Winning the Market

From UGGs to Fender guitars, generic knockoffs are crushing original brands. Here’s why—and how it’s reshaping shopping forever.

{
  "## 🔑 The Core of This Topic": "The rise of dupe culture—cheap, mass-produced clones of premium products—has reshaped consumer habits, flooded marketplaces, and forced even iconic brands to adapt or die. Social media and e-commerce algorithms now favor affordability over authenticity, accelerating this trend across industries."
}
