# Distilling DeepSeek into GPT-OSS Reduces Censorship Transfer

Researchers explore the effectiveness of distilling DeepSeek into GPT-OSS, finding reduced censorship transfer in finance tasks.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Censorship transfer is a significant concern when fine-tuning large language models, as it can result in the propagation of biases and misinformation. Recently, researchers have investigated the use of distillation to reduce censorship transfer in GPT-OSS, a powerful language model.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Distillation can help reduce censorship transfer by filtering out unwanted information.
- **Point 2**: The researchers used DeepSeek V4 Flash as a teacher for finance tasks with GPT-OSS-120B.
- **Point 3**: Self-distilled 120B scores 83.61% on the constrained 8k token budget.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The researchers' approach involved using DeepSeek V4 Flash as a teacher model to fine-tune GPT-OSS-120B on finance tasks. This allowed them to leverage the strengths of both models and reduce censorship transfer.

**Element 2**: The team conducted experiments with a constrained 8k token budget and found that the self-distilled 120B model scored 83.61%. This result indicates that distillation can be an effective way to reduce censorship transfer while maintaining performance.

> 💡 Insight: The study highlights the potential of distillation in reducing censorship transfer and improving the overall performance of language models.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
