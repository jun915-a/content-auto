# Build Your Own Mechanical TV: The Scanwheel

Discover the fascinating world of mechanical television and learn how to build your own Scanwheel, a drum-style mechanical TV.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Scanwheel is a mesmerizing drum-style mechanical television that can be built by enthusiasts. This innovative project combines mechanical and electronic components to create a unique display system.
 
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: A mechanical TV that uses a drum to display images.
- **Point 2**: The drum is covered in a reflective surface to enhance visibility.
- **Point 3**: The Scanwheel is a DIY project that can be built by anyone with basic electronics knowledge.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Drum**: The drum is the heart of the Scanwheel, where the mechanical images are displayed. It's a rotating cylinder covered in a reflective surface, which enhances visibility and creates a unique viewing experience.
 
**Electronics**: The electronics of the Scanwheel are straightforward and can be built using basic components. The system uses a series of transistors and resistors to control the drum's rotation and image display.
