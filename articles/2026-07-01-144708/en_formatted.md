Pine64 has unveiled its latest product, the PineVoice, a $50 RISC-V-based smart speaker tailored for Home Assistant enthusiasts.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Pine64's PineVoice smart speaker is a budget-friendly, RISC-V-based device designed specifically for users who have Home Assistant set up in their homes. This speaker combines a compact form factor with the ability to control and interact with various smart devices using voice commands.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Hardware Specifications**
The PineVoice smart speaker boasts a robust set of hardware features, including a quad-core RISC-V processor, 1 GB of RAM, and 8 GB of internal storage. These capabilities make it an ideal choice for users who require a reliable and efficient smart speaker.

**Software Features**
One of the standout features of the PineVoice is its seamless integration with Home Assistant. Users can easily set up and control their smart home devices using voice commands, making it a game-changer for those who value a hassle-free smart home experience.
