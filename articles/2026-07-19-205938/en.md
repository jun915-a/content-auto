# The Core of This Topic
Stability in deep learning models is crucial for achieving good performance. However, training a deep neural network can be challenging due to exploding gradients, which can cause the model to diverge and become unstable. This is where Muon and Gradient Clipping come in – two techniques that can help stabilize your model and improve its overall performance.

## Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Muon is a technique that can help prevent exploding gradients by clipping the norm of the gradient. This is done by adding a penalty term to the loss function, which encourages the model to produce smaller gradients. By doing so, Muon helps stabilize the model and prevents it from diverging.

**Element 2**
Gradient Clipping is another technique that can help prevent exploding gradients. This technique involves clipping the gradient at a certain threshold, preventing it from becoming too large and causing the model to diverge. By clipping the gradient, Gradient Clipping helps stabilize the model and improves its overall performance.
