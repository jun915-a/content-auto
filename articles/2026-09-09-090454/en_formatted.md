The ‘board’ isn’t a single dataset but a **living graph of associations**, where each output refines the next. For developers, this means designing systems that **nurture emergence**, not just efficiency. For ethicists, it forces a reckoning: **Can we trust systems that ‘learn’ in ways we don’t fully understand?** And for users, it’s a promise: the next breakthrough might not come from a dataset, but from the **unscripted dialogue between AI and the world**.\n\nThe future of AI isn’t just in the models—it’s in the **blackboard they leave behind**."
