## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Product Analysis Tools**
These tools analyze product listings for common red flags associated with fakes. This includes checking seller reputation, reviewing customer feedback for suspicious patterns, and scrutinizing product images and descriptions for inconsistencies that often indicate counterfeit merchandise.

**Community-Sourced Data**
Knockoff.shopping leverages a community of users who report and flag suspicious products. This crowdsourced intelligence creates a dynamic database, continuously updated with real-world examples of knockoffs, offering a valuable layer of protection beyond automated checks.

**Educational Resources**
The platform offers guides and tips on how to spot fakes yourself. Understanding the tactics used by counterfeiters empowers shoppers to make better choices and protect themselves from scams and low-quality imitations.
