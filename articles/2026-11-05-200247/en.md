# Window Sweaters: AI-Powered Privacy for Your Browser

Discover **Window Sweaters**, an open-source tool that transforms your browser into a privacy fortress by isolating tabs with AI-driven security. Protect sensitive data effortlessly—no extensions needed.

**Window Sweaters** is an open-source browser extension that redefines privacy by isolating individual tabs within your browser. Unlike traditional extensions that run in the background, Window Sweaters creates **sandboxed windows** for each sensitive tab, ensuring that malicious scripts or trackers can’t escape their container. This approach leverages modern browser APIs to provide granular control over data leakage, making it ideal for journalists, activists, or anyone concerned about online surveillance.
