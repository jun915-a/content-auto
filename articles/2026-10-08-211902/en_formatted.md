## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Step 5 Preview from StepFun is a **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model** with a **1-million-token context window**, marking a leap forward in AI’s ability to handle vast, complex inputs. Unlike traditional large language models (LLMs), MoE architectures dynamically route inputs to specialized submodels (experts), optimizing performance and efficiency. This innovation enables **scalable, high-capacity reasoning** without compromising speed or cost, making it a game-changer for applications requiring deep contextual understanding.
