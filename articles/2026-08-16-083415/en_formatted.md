## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Modularity and Maintainability**
A well-structured software system with clear modularity and maintainability is easier to understand, modify, and extend. This leads to faster development cycles, reduced bugs, and improved overall system quality.

**Scalability and Performance**
Scalability and performance are critical in today's fast-paced digital landscape. A software system that can scale with growing demands and optimize performance under heavy loads is essential for businesses to remain competitive.
