## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Fermat’s Last Theorem states that **no three positive integers *a*, *b*, and *c* satisfy the equation *aⁿ + bⁿ = cⁿ* for any integer value of *n* greater than 2**. Proven by Andrew Wiles in 1994 after a 358-year pursuit, it reshaped number theory—but its formalization in AI systems like Anthropic’s is redefining mathematical rigor.
