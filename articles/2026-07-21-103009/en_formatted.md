## 📈 Detailed Breakdown

Bloomy's AI engine continuously assesses student understanding, adjusting the difficulty level and content to meet their individual needs. This ensures students are challenged but not overwhelmed, fostering a deeper understanding of the material.

The platform's mastery-based approach allows students to progress at their own pace, celebrating small victories along the way. This motivates students to take ownership of their learning, building confidence and a growth mindset.
