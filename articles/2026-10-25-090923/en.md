# Neural Chip Reveals Brain’s Hidden Communication Networks

EPFL researchers unveil a groundbreaking neural chip that decodes real-time interactions between brain regions, offering unprecedented insights into neural dynamics. This innovation could revolutionize neuroscience and brain-machine interfaces.

Scientists at the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL) have developed a cutting-edge neural chip capable of decoding the intricate interactions between different regions of the brain. This breakthrough, published in *Nature*, marks a significant leap forward in understanding how neural circuits communicate and process information. The technology holds transformative potential for both fundamental neuroscience and clinical applications, including brain-machine interfaces and treatments for neurological disorders like epilepsy or Parkinson’s disease.
