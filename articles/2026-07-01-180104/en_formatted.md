## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Sony has deleted 551 movies from PlayStation owners' accounts, citing a deal with StudioCanal that expired in 2022. This move has left many customers without access to films they paid for, prompting widespread criticism.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
* **Point 1**: Sony deleted 551 StudioCanal movies from PlayStation accounts due to expired licensing deals.
* **Point 2**: The deletion affects PlayStation owners who paid for the movies, leaving them without access.
* **Point 3**: The move has sparked a class-action lawsuit against Sony.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Expired Licensing Deals**
The deleted movies were part of a licensing deal between Sony and StudioCanal, which expired in 2022. Sony claims that it was not obligated to continue offering the movies after the deal expired.

**Customer Outrage**
The deletion of the movies has sparked widespread outrage among PlayStation owners, who argue that they paid for the content and should still have access to it. Many customers feel that Sony is unfairly taking away something they purchased.
