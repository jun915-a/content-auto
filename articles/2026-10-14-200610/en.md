# How Kid Pix Became a Digital Art Revolution

From a humble prototype to a cultural phenomenon, discover the untold story behind *Kid Pix*—the game that taught millions how to create digital art. Dive into its origins, innovation, and lasting legacy.

**Kid Pix** wasn’t just a game; it was a gateway to creativity for an entire generation. Launched in 1989 by **Broderbund Software**, it transformed how kids (and adults!) approached digital art with its intuitive tools and vibrant interface. Before tablets and touchscreens, *Kid Pix* made coding-free design accessible—proving that technology could be both fun and empowering. Its legacy endures in modern digital art education, proving that simplicity and imagination can change the game forever.
