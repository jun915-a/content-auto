## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**System One Models: A New AI Architecture**
System One Models redefine AI by integrating **probabilistic reasoning** (handling uncertainty) with **deterministic constraints** (ensuring logical consistency). This duality allows the system to **adapt to ambiguity** while maintaining **strict adherence to rules**—a critical balance missing in traditional AI. For example, in medical diagnostics, the model can weigh probabilistic risks while enforcing non-negotiable clinical guidelines, reducing errors caused by over-reliance on statistical outputs alone.

**Jev: The Explainable AI Framework**
Jev stands for **Jurisdictional Evidence-Based Validation**, a framework that **traces AI decisions back to formal reasoning**. Unlike black-box models, Jev provides **transparent, auditable logic** for every output. This means stakeholders—whether regulators, developers, or end-users—can **verify why** an AI made a specific decision, not just **what** it decided. Imagine an autonomous vehicle system where Jev doesn’t just say, *“Stop at the red light,”* but also explains, *“The light’s sensor data (probability: 92%) aligns with traffic law X, and edge case Y was ruled out.”*
