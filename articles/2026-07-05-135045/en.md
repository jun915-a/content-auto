## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Microwaves are a form of electromagnetic radiation, and researchers have found a way to convert them into a usable form of energy.
This technology has the potential to provide a new source of renewable energy, reducing our reliance on fossil fuels and mitigating climate change.
