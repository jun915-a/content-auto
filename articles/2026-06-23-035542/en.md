Polymarket, a prediction market, has faced criticism for hiring paid creators to produce deceptive videos that spread misinformation on social media.

- **Point 2**: The videos were designed to spread misinformation on social media.
- **Point 3**: Polymarket's actions have raised concerns about the integrity of online information.
