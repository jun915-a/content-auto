## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Understanding Texas' Reproductive Health Laws**
Texas' reproductive health laws are complex and often contradictory. The ban on abortion after six weeks of pregnancy, for example, does not provide exceptions for cases of rape, incest, or fetal anomalies. This means that many Texans will be forced to carry unwanted pregnancies to term, regardless of their circumstances.

**Mayday Health's Impact Surveillance**
Mayday Health's impact surveillance is a system that tracks and monitors individuals who seek abortion care. This can include tracking online activity, social media posts, and even in-person interactions. The goal of this surveillance is to identify and deter individuals who may be seeking abortion care, effectively restricting access to reproductive health services.
