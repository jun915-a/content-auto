# Molly (Signal Fork) Left Vulnerable for 3 Months—What You Need to Know

Molly, a prominent fork of Signal, has been abandoned for months, leaving users exposed to critical security risks. Discover why this matters, the risks involved, and what alternatives exist to protect your privacy.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Molly is a privacy-focused messaging app built as a fork of Signal, designed to offer enhanced features like end-to-end encryption, decentralized identity, and open-source transparency. However, its **inactivity since March 2024** has raised alarm bells—users are left with unpatched vulnerabilities, no updates, and a growing risk of exploitation by malicious actors.
