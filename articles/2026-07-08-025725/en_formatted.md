# EU Mandates Driver Monitoring

The European Union now requires all new cars to include a driver monitoring camera to reduce distracted driving, enhancing road safety

## The Core of This Topic
The European Union has introduced a new regulation that makes it compulsory for all new cars to be equipped with a driver monitoring camera. This system is designed to detect and prevent distracted driving, a major cause of accidents on European roads.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Improved Safety**: Reduces accidents caused by distracted driving
- **Driver Monitoring**: Cameras track the driver's attention and alertness
- **EU Regulation**: Mandatory for all new cars sold in the EU
## Detailed Breakdown
**Driver Attention**
The system continuously monitors the driver's face and eyes to detect any signs of distraction or drowsiness.
**Alertness Detection**
It can identify if the driver is not paying attention to the road and issue warnings to refocus.
> Insight: This technology has the potential to significantly reduce the number of accidents caused by distracted driving.
## Real-World Impact
- Reduced number of accidents on European roads
- Increased safety for all road users
- Potential for similar regulations in other regions
## Conclusion
The introduction of mandatory driver monitoring cameras in new cars is a significant step towards enhancing road safety in the European Union, and it is expected to have a positive impact on reducing accidents.
