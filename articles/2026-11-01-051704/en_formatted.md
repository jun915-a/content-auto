This tactic—common in cybersecurity—demonstrates how **AI systems without robust input validation** can become tools for deception.

**Element 2**

> 💡 Insight: This incident serves as a **warning to legal tech adopters**—AI integration must include **input sanitization, audit trails, and user authentication** to prevent abuse. Courts worldwide should treat AI vulnerabilities as **operational risks**, not just technical ones.
