# NYC May Require AI Disclosure

*Insert header image here*

NYC considers requiring landlords and realtors to disclose AI use in listings to ensure transparency and fairness in the real estate market

## The Core of This Topic
The NYC government is looking into requiring landlords and realtors to disclose the use of AI in property listings to prevent misleading potential buyers.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Transparency**: Ensuring buyers know what they're getting
- **AI Usage**: Disclosure of AI-generated images and descriptions
- **Fairness**: Preventing deceptive advertising practices
## Detailed Breakdown
**AI-Generated Images**
AI-generated images can make properties look more attractive than they really are, which can be misleading to potential buyers.
**Realtor Responsibilities**
Realtors have a responsibility to provide accurate information to their clients, and disclosing AI use is a part of that responsibility.
> Insight: Buyers have the right to know what they're getting into
## Real-World Impact
- Increased transparency in the real estate market
- Reduction in deceptive advertising practices
- Protection of buyers' rights
## Conclusion
The proposed requirement for landlords and realtors to disclose AI use in listings is a step towards a more transparent and fair real estate market.
