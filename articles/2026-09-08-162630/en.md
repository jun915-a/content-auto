## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Delve into the world of antiquated HTML snippets, where we uncover the remnants of early web development. These snippets, often forgotten, provide a fascinating insight into the evolution of HTML and web design.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Legacy Tags**: Explore the use of now-obsolete tags like <font> and <center>.
- **Deprecated Elements**: Understand the impact of deprecated elements like <frame> and <applet>.
- **Coding Practices**: Discover the shift from table-based layouts to CSS-driven designs.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Legacy Tags**: Tags like <font> and <center>, once commonly used for text styling and alignment, are now obsolete. These tags, while functional, lacked the precision and versatility of modern CSS.

**Deprecated Elements**: Elements like <frame> and <applet> were once popular for creating complex layouts and embedding media. However, their usage has been deprecated due to security concerns and the rise of more efficient alternatives.

> 💡 Insight: While these elements are no longer recommended, understanding their history provides valuable context for modern web development practices.
