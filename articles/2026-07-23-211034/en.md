# Safeguarding AI Secrets: Introducing OneCLI

OneCLI is an open-source vault for AI Agents, keeping secrets out of AI agents. Learn how it revolutionizes AI security.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
OneCLI is a groundbreaking open-source credential gateway designed to keep sensitive information, or "secrets," out of AI agents. By separating AI credentials from the rest of the code, developers can ensure that confidential data remains secure and is not accidentally exposed during the development process.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Decentralized credential storage for AI agents.
- **Point 2**: Easy integration with various AI frameworks.
- **Point 3**: Enhances overall AI security and privacy.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Traditional Vault Limitations**
Traditional vaults often rely on centralized storage, which can be a single point of failure and may not be suitable for large-scale AI deployments. OneCLI addresses these limitations by providing a decentralized credential storage solution.

**OneCLI Advantages**
OneCLI's distributed architecture ensures that sensitive information is not stored in a single location, making it more difficult for unauthorized parties to access. Additionally, OneCLI's flexibility allows for seamless integration with various AI frameworks, making it an attractive solution for developers.

> 💡 Insight: By separating AI credentials from the rest of the code, OneCLI empowers developers to build more secure and private AI systems.
