# Meet JBR-001: The Open-Source 3D-Printable Arduino-Powered Robot

Unlock creativity with JBR-001, a fully customizable, open-source desktop robot built for Arduino Uno Q. Free 3D models, code, and assembly instructions await—perfect for makers and hobbyists alike!

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
JBR-001 is an open-source, **3D-printable desktop robot** designed as a companion for your workspace. Powered by the **Arduino Uno Q**, it combines modularity, affordability, and creativity, allowing users to build, customize, and expand its functionality with ease. The project includes **fully open-source files**—from 3D models to firmware—making it ideal for DIY enthusiasts, educators, and hobbyists looking to bring automation or interactive elements into their daily routines.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Open-source hardware & software**: All designs, code, and instructions are freely available on Arduino Project Hub.
- **Arduino Uno Q-powered**: Leverages the compact yet powerful **Arduino Uno Q** for seamless integration.
- **3D-printable**: Entire robot is printable at home, reducing costs and enabling full customization.
