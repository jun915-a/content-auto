A recent discussion on the Codex GitHub repository points to a potential issue with GPT-5.5's reasoning capabilities. **Token Clustering Mechanism**
This refers to how the model groups and processes sequences of tokens. When 'reasoning tokens' cluster too closely, it might limit the model's ability to explore alternative logical paths or consider distinct pieces of information independently, impacting its problem-solving depth.

**Observed Performance Degradation**
Users have noted a decline in Codex's effectiveness, particularly for intricate coding challenges that demand nuanced understanding and multi-step reasoning. This suggests the underlying model architecture or training process may have introduced this unintended clustering effect.
