# Game Ownership Matters

*Insert header image here*

The debate between physical and digital games is overshadowed by a more pressing issue: ownership and control over the games we buy

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The heart of the issue lies in the concept of ownership, as gamers are concerned about having control over the games they purchase, whether physical or digital.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Ownership gives players control over their games
- **Point 2**: Digital rights management can limit access
- **Point 3**: Physical games provide a sense of tangible ownership

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Digital games are often tied to the platform they were purchased on, making it difficult to transfer or resell them. 
**Element 2**
Physical games, on the other hand, can be lent, sold, or traded without restrictions, giving players more freedom.

> 💡 Insight: The ability to resell or lend games is a key aspect of ownership that is often lacking in digital purchases.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Gamers may be locked out of their digital libraries if a platform shuts down
- The resale value of physical games can be a significant factor in purchasing decisions
- Digital game ownership can be affected by server shutdowns or license revocations
