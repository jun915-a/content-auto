## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Dithered QR codes leverage digital halftoning to replace the standard black and white modules with intricate patterns, creating a visual image while maintaining the underlying data. This process cleverly manipulates pixel density to simulate shades, making the QR code an artistic canvas without losing its scannability.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Transforms standard QR codes into visually rich, dithered images.
- **Point 2**: Achieves artistic effects while preserving full scannability.
- **Point 3**: Utilizes advanced digital halftoning algorithms.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The core technique involves replacing each black or white QR module with a small dithered pattern. This pattern is carefully designed to average out to the original module's color (black or white) when viewed from a distance, ensuring readability.

**Element 2**
Advanced error correction capabilities inherent in QR codes are crucial. They allow for a significant amount of visual noise and artistic alteration without data loss, making dithered designs robust against the visual modifications.

> 💡 Insight: Dithering exploits human perception and QR error correction to embed art into data, making information visually engaging.
