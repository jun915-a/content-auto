# Sendov’s Conjecture: A Decades-Old Problem Finally Resolved

After nearly 70 years, mathematicians have cracked Sendov’s conjecture—a puzzle about polynomials and their roots. Here’s how the proof reshapes number theory and analysis.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Sendov’s conjecture, posed in 1958, posits that for any polynomial with roots inside the unit disk, at least one root of its derivative lies within a unit distance from *each* root of the original polynomial. This deceptively simple claim has baffled mathematicians for generations.
