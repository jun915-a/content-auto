# Telstra’s 2006 Time Warp: The Night Australia Froze in Time

When Telstra’s network glitch turned 2023 into 2006, millions faced frozen services, delayed payments, and chaos. Here’s how a single misconfiguration unraveled Australia’s digital infrastructure—and what it reveals about our reliance on flawless tech.

**The night Australia’s digital clock broke.** On a seemingly ordinary evening in 2023, Telstra’s network suffered a catastrophic outage—not from a cyberattack or hardware failure, but from a bizarre time warp. Servers, apps, and payments suddenly believed it was **2006**, plunging millions into a digital blackout. What followed was a night of frustration, financial hiccups, and a stark reminder of how fragile our interconnected world truly is.**
