# Meta Pauses Employee-Tracking Program Over Data Leak

Meta, the parent company of Facebook, has put its employee-tracking program on hold due to an internal data breach. The program, which was designed to monitor employee productivity, has raised concerns over employee surveillance.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Meta's employee-tracking program was designed to monitor employees' productivity by tracking the time spent on various tasks and activities. However, the program's reliance on employee data has raised concerns over employee surveillance.
