# Stenchill: 3D Print Your Custom Solder Paste Stencils Instantly

Stenchill revolutionizes electronics prototyping with its online generator for custom 3D-printable solder paste stencils. Design and print your own stencils in minutes, saving time and cost.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Stenchill is an online tool that allows users to generate custom 3D printable solder paste stencils directly from PCB design files. It automates the creation process, making it accessible and fast for hobbyists and professionals alike.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Instant Generation**: Create stencils in minutes from your PCB design.
- **3D Printable Focus**: Optimized for FDM 3D printers.
- **Cost-Effective**: Significantly cheaper than traditional stencil manufacturing.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Stencil Design Automation**
Stenchill analyzes your Gerber files or schematics to automatically determine the correct aperture sizes and placement for solder paste application. It handles complex layouts efficiently.

**3D Printing Optimization**
The generated STL files are specifically tailored for FDM 3D printing, considering layer height, nozzle size, and material properties to ensure accurate and usable stencils for prototyping.

> 💡 Insight: This tool democratizes stencil creation, enabling rapid iteration in PCB development.

**Ease of Use**
Simply upload your design files, adjust parameters like paste thickness and aperture shape, and download the printable 3D model. No complex CAD software or specialized knowledge is required.

- Enables small-batch production and custom PCB assembly.
