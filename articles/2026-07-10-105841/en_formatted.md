## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1: Real-Time 3D Reconstruction**
Hy3 leverages advanced neural rendering and deep learning to reconstruct 3D scenes from minimal input—whether a single image or a video stream. Unlike traditional methods that require hours of processing, Hy3 delivers results in real time, making it ideal for dynamic applications like live AR experiences or robotic navigation.

Beyond reconstruction, Hy3 incorporates generative AI to synthesize 3D objects or environments from text descriptions or sparse data.
