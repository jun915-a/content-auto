Discover how air quality in the room can significantly impact our ability to make sound decisions, and the science behind it

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The air we breathe has a profound effect on our cognitive abilities, particularly decision-making. High levels of CO2 can impair our judgment and reduce our productivity.
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: CO2 levels above 1000 ppm negatively affect decision-making
- **Point 2**: Poor ventilation leads to increased CO2 levels
- **Point 3**: Cognitive functions decrease with high CO2 levels
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The human brain requires oxygen to function efficiently. When CO2 levels are high, the brain's ability to make sound decisions is compromised.
**Element 2**: Ventilation systems play a crucial role in maintaining low CO2 levels. Natural ventilation or air purifiers can help reduce CO2 levels in a room.
> 💡 Insight: Even minor reductions in CO2 levels can significantly improve cognitive functions.
## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Reduced productivity in workplaces with poor ventilation
- Impaired decision-making in high-stakes meetings
- Decreased learning capabilities in classrooms with high CO2 levels
## ✨ Conclusion
Maintaining low CO2 levels is essential for making sound decisions. By recognizing the impact of CO2 on our cognitive abilities, we can take steps to improve ventilation and create a healthier environment.
