# Introducing Rowboat

Rowboat is an open-source, local-first alternative to Claude Desktop, offering a full-fledged work app experience

## The Core of This Topic
Rowboat is a local-first, open-source alternative to Claude Desktop, designed to provide a more comprehensive work app experience. It includes features that make it more like a full-fledged work app, rather than just a chat app.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Local-first approach**: Data is stored locally for security and privacy
- **Open-source**: Community-driven development and transparency
- **Full-fledged work app**: More features and functionality than a traditional chat app
## Detailed Breakdown
**Key Features**
Rowboat offers a range of features that make it an attractive alternative to Claude Desktop, including data storage and open-source development.
**Community Involvement**
The Rowboat community is encouraged to contribute to the development process, ensuring the app meets the needs of its users.
> Insight: By being open-source and local-first, Rowboat provides a unique solution for those looking for a more private and customizable work app experience.
## Real-World Impact
- Increased productivity through a more comprehensive work app experience
- Improved data security and privacy with local-first approach
- Community-driven development for a more tailored user experience
## Conclusion
Rowboat is an exciting new alternative to Claude Desktop, offering a local-first, open-source, and full-fledged work app experience. With its range of features and community-driven development, Rowboat is set to make a significant impact in the world of work apps.
