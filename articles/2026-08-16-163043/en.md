# Moire Patterns for Ship Guidance

A fascinating exploration of applying moire patterns to guide ships, leveraging the principles of interference and wave manipulation.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Moire patterns, a result of the superimposition of two grids, have been used to create mesmerizing visual effects. But did you know that these patterns can also be used to guide ships? By manipulating the wave patterns, ships can be directed towards their desired destination.
