# Mullvad Shuts Down Public DNS, Partners with Quad9

*Insert header image here*

Mullvad is decommissioning its public encrypted DNS servers, shifting its support to Quad9. Learn why this change benefits users and how it enhances DNS privacy and security.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Mullvad is discontinuing its public encrypted DNS servers. Instead, they are now sponsoring Quad9, a non-profit DNS service. This strategic shift aims to provide a more robust, secure, and privacy-focused DNS experience for users by leveraging Quad9's established infrastructure and expertise.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **DNS Server Shutdown**: Mullvad's public encrypted DNS servers are no longer available.
- **Quad9 Sponsorship**: Mullvad now supports and recommends Quad9 for encrypted DNS.
- **Enhanced Privacy & Security**: This partnership brings improved security features and privacy protections.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Transition to Quad9**
Mullvad's decision stems from a desire to offer a superior DNS solution. Quad9 provides advanced security features, including blocking malicious domains, which aligns with Mullvad's commitment to user protection.

**Benefits of Quad9**
Quad9 operates as a non-profit, focusing on privacy and security. It offers DNSSEC validation and blocks known malicious domains, adding a crucial layer of defense against phishing and malware.

> 💡 Insight: By consolidating efforts and sponsoring Quad9, Mullvad can contribute more effectively to a widely accessible, secure, and privacy-respecting DNS infrastructure.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Users will benefit from Quad9's enhanced security features, including malware and phishing protection.
- The move supports a non-profit initiative, promoting a more decentralized and privacy-focused internet.
- Mullvad can focus resources on core VPN services while ensuring a high-quality DNS offering.

## ✨ Conclusion
While Mullvad's public DNS servers are gone, the commitment to user privacy and security remains strong. The partnership with Quad9 ensures users have access to a top-tier, privacy-focused DNS service.
