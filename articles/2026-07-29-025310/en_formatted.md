## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
HNewhere is a userscript designed to simplify your experience on Hacker News (HN). I created it to address a common frustration: the need to open multiple tabs to read an article and its accompanying discussion. With HNewhere, you can access both the article and its comments in a single tab.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Eliminate the need for multiple tabs.
- **Point 2**: Enhance your productivity on HN.
- **Point 3**: Enjoy a seamless browsing experience.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
HNewhere achieves its goal through a simple yet effective approach. When you click on a link, the script automatically opens the article and comments in the same tab. This eliminates the need to manually switch between tabs or open a new window.

The script is lightweight and doesn't interfere with your browsing experience. You can install it on your browser and start enjoying the benefits right away.
