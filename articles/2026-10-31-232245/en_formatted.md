## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
A USB-C LTE modem called **Koppar** bridges the gap between your computer and cellular networks. Unlike traditional modems, this device plugs into a USB-C port and provides a **phone line connection** via LTE, enabling voice calls, SMS, and even data sharing over cellular. It’s an open-source project designed for developers, hackers, and tech enthusiasts who want to leverage cellular connectivity without relying on proprietary hardware.
