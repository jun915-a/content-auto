- **Trade Secret Theft**: Apple claims ex-employees took sensitive info to OpenAI
- **Lawsuit Filed**: Apple takes action against OpenAI for the alleged theft
- **Ex-Employees Involved**: Former Apple staff are at the center of the trade secret controversy
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The lawsuit centers around the alleged theft of trade secrets by ex-Apple employees who joined OpenAI.
**Element 2**: Apple believes these secrets could give OpenAI an unfair competitive edge, prompting the legal action.
