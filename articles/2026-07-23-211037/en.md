# Revolutionize Your Mobile Productivity with Remux

Experience the power of tmux on your iPhone with Remux, an open-source workspace designed to streamline your development workflow.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Remux is an open-source tmux workspace built specifically for iPhone users. It's designed to provide a seamless and efficient development experience, allowing you to stay productive on-the-go.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
* **Point 1**: A tmux workspace tailored for iPhone users.
* **Point 2**: Streamline your development workflow with Remux.
* **Point 3**: Open-source, customizable, and free.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Tmux Workspace**
Remux brings the power of tmux to your iPhone, enabling you to manage multiple terminals, windows, and sessions with ease. Its intuitive interface and customizable layout allow you to focus on your code without distractions.

**Customization**
With Remux, you can personalize your workspace to suit your needs. Choose from a variety of themes, customize keyboard shortcuts, and even create your own layouts.
