A devastating train crash in Spain has left multiple fatalities and injuries. Investigations are underway, but what do we know so far? Here’s a breakdown of the tragedy’s impact and unfolding story.

**Spain Train Crash: What We Know So Far**

A high-speed train derailed near **Seville, Spain**, on **June 24, 2024**, killing at least **10 people** and injuring dozens more. The accident has sparked nationwide shock, raising questions about safety protocols and the future of Spain’s rail network. Here’s what we know.


## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The crash involved a **Renfe high-speed train** (S-130 series) traveling between **Seville and Córdoba** when it derailed near **Santa Justa station**, plunging into a ravine. Witnesses reported a **loud explosion** followed by the train’s derailment, with survivors describing chaos and panic. Authorities have confirmed **multiple fatalities**, though exact numbers remain under review. The incident has reignited debates over **rail safety inspections, driver training, and infrastructure maintenance** in Spain.

The **Seville Civil Protection** confirmed that **most victims were passengers**, though some were likely crew members. The **Spanish Civil Guard** has sealed the area, and forensic teams are examining the wreckage for clues.
