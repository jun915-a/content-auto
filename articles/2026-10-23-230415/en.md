# Can DIY HTML Replace a $150K Looker?

Is a $150,000 BI tool like Looker worth it, or can a self-built HTML dashboard do the job? Explore trade-offs, risks, and real-world implications of ditching enterprise BI for DIY solutions.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic

The debate over whether a **$150,000 enterprise BI tool like Looker** can be replaced by a **bucket of HTML files** boils down to **cost vs. capability**. While DIY dashboards seem cheaper, they often lack **scalability, security, collaboration, and advanced analytics**—key features that justify enterprise tools. The question isn’t just about money; it’s about **who benefits most** from the trade-offs.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Cost savings** are immediate but **hidden costs** (time, maintenance, expertise) may outweigh them.
- **HTML dashboards** work for simple, static reporting but **fail at real-time analytics, governance, and user access control**.
- **Looker (or similar tools)** provide **self-service, automation, and integrations** that DIY solutions lack—critical for modern data teams.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown

**Element 1: The Illusion of Cost Savings**

A $150,000 Looker license might seem steep, but the **real cost** of a DIY HTML approach includes:
- **Development time**: Building, maintaining, and securing dashboards internally consumes **devops, engineering, and design resources**—resources that could be allocated elsewhere.
- **Expertise gaps**: Without a **data engineer or BI specialist**, even simple dashboards risk **technical debt, broken links, or insecure data exposure**.
- **Opportunity cost**: Teams may **spend months reinventing the wheel** instead of focusing on **strategic insights**.
