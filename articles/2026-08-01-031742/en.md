# Deprecating LLM Routers

Discover why Manifest.build deprecated their LLM router and what this move means for the future of AI development

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The deprecation of Manifest.build's LLM router marks a significant shift in the company's approach to AI development. At its core, this decision reflects a change in strategy towards more innovative and efficient solutions.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Rapidly Evolving Tech**: LLM routers are being built by everyone, making them less unique.
- **Shifting Priorities**: Companies are focusing on more cutting-edge AI technologies.
- **Efficiency Over Novelty**: The need for streamlined processes outweighs the appeal of pioneering technologies.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The rise of LLM routers has led to a saturated market, reducing the competitive advantage they once offered.

**Element 2**: As AI continues to advance, companies are now prioritizing the development of more sophisticated and integrated technologies.

> 💡 Insight: The deprecation of LLM routers is not a step backward but a strategic move towards embracing more innovative and efficient AI solutions.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Reduced redundancy in AI development
- Increased focus on groundbreaking technologies
- Streamlined processes for better productivity

## ✨ Conclusion
The decision to deprecate LLM routers signifies a forward-thinking approach, acknowledging that true innovation lies in pushing the boundaries of what AI can achieve, rather than clinging to pioneering but now common technologies.
