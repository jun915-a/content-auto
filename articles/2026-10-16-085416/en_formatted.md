# Libreboot 26.10 "Ominous Olive" Adds UEFI & Chromebook Support

*Insert header image here*

Libreboot 26.10 "Ominous Olive" ushers in major advancements, including full UEFI support and expanded Chromebook compatibility, empowering users with greater freedom and control over their hardware.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Libreboot 26.10 "Ominous Olive" marks a significant leap forward by introducing full UEFI boot support, a long-awaited feature that enhances compatibility with modern hardware and operating systems. This release also brings expanded support for various Chromebook models, further democratizing the use of free and open-source firmware.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **UEFI Support**: Full UEFI boot capability is now integrated.
- **Chromebook Focus**: Expanded support for a wider range of Chromebook devices.
- **Security Enhancements**: Ongoing improvements to firmware security and stability.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**UEFI Integration**
The inclusion of full UEFI support is a game-changer, allowing Libreboot to boot on hardware that relies on UEFI firmware, a standard on most modern computers. This opens up Libreboot to a much broader audience and enables compatibility with a wider array of operating systems.

**Chromebook Expansion**
Libreboot 26.10 significantly boosts its compatibility with Chromebooks, providing a free and open alternative to the proprietary firmware found on these devices. This empowers users to reclaim control over their hardware.

> 💡 Insight: The addition of UEFI support is crucial for Libreboot's long-term relevance, bridging the gap between legacy BIOS systems and the modern computing landscape.

**Performance & Stability**
Beyond major feature additions, this release includes general performance optimizations and stability improvements across supported hardware, ensuring a smoother and more reliable user experience.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Increased hardware compatibility, especially for newer machines.
- Greater freedom for Chromebook users to install open-source operating systems.
- Enhanced security and user control over their computing devices.

## ✨ Conclusion
Libreboot 26.10 "Ominous Olive" is a monumental release, breaking down barriers to free firmware adoption with its UEFI and Chromebook advancements. It reaffirms the project's commitment to user freedom and hardware liberation.
