## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The recent Xbox outage has highlighted a significant issue: gamers are unable to play games they own on disc when the service is down.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Games on disc require online verification
- **Point 2**: Xbox Live outage affects game ownership
- **Point 3**: Digital rights management sparks controversy

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The Xbox outage has raised questions about the true meaning of game ownership in the digital age.

**Element 2**: Gamers are calling for greater transparency and control over their gaming libraries, particularly when it comes to games they have purchased on disc.

> 💡 Insight: The line between physical and digital game ownership is becoming increasingly blurred.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Gamers are locked out of their games
- The outage has sparked a wider debate about digital rights
- Concerns over the reliability of online gaming services

## ✨ Conclusion
The Xbox outage serves as a reminder that gamers need to be aware of the terms and conditions of their game ownership, and the potential risks associated with digital rights management.
