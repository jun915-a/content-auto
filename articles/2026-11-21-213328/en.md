# Riemann Hypothesis Verified in Lean Prover

A monumental achievement in mathematics: the Riemann Hypothesis, a conjecture about prime number distribution, has been formally verified using the Lean proof assistant. This marks a significant step in computational mathematics.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Riemann Hypothesis, a century-old conjecture, links the distribution of prime numbers to the zeros of the Riemann zeta function. Its formal verification in Lean Prover means a computer has rigorously checked every step of the proof, ensuring its absolute correctness.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Riemann Zeta Function**
This function, defined for complex numbers, has a deep connection to prime numbers. Its non-trivial zeros are conjectured to lie on a specific line in the complex plane.

**The Hypothesis**
It states that all non-trivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function have a real part equal to 1/2. Proving this would have profound implications for number theory.

> 💡 Insight: This verification is not a discovery of new mathematics but a rigorous, machine-assisted confirmation of existing, complex reasoning.

**Lean Proof Assistant**
A sophisticated tool designed for formalizing and verifying mathematical proofs, ensuring logical soundness and preventing errors.
