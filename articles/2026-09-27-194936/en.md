# Impulse Unveils Coltrane’s Lost Tiberi Tapes on Centenary

In a groundbreaking move, Impulse Records marks John Coltrane’s centenary by releasing the long-lost Tiberi Tapes—unheard recordings from 1963. This rare treasure offers a glimpse into the genius’s experimental phase, redefining jazz history.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
John Coltrane’s centenary celebrations reached a monumental milestone with Impulse Records’ release of the **Tiberi Tapes**, a collection of unreleased recordings from 1963. These tapes, discovered decades later, provide an intimate look into Coltrane’s creative process during his most radical period, bridging his transition from hard bop to avant-garde innovation. The tapes feature rare performances, spontaneous jams, and early explorations of modal jazz and free improvisation—offering fans and scholars a direct window into his artistic evolution.
