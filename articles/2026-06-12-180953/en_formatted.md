When asking for human attention, it's essential to demonstrate human effort, showing that you value the other person's time.

- **Point 2**: Show you've done your research
- **Point 3**: Provide unique insights
## Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Human effort is about being authentic and transparent in your interactions, helping build trust with others.
**Element 2**
It involves taking the time to understand the other person's needs and interests, tailoring your approach accordingly.
> Insight: People are more likely to engage with you if they feel seen and understood.
