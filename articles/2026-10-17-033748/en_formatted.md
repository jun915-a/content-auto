By integrating Muse Spark—Apple’s experimental AI model—MLX enables developers to train and deploy models **without GPU limitations**. The framework’s **distributed inference** capability splits workloads across multiple Mac Studio cores, optimizing speed and efficiency. This is particularly impactful for **large-scale models** that traditionally require cloud infrastructure.
