# Kagi Now Lets You Block Paywalled Search Results

Tired of hitting paywalls in search results? Kagi, the privacy-focused search engine, has introduced a new setting to filter out content behind subscription barriers. Get ready for a cleaner, more accessible search experience.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Kagi has introduced a new user-configurable setting that allows users to automatically remove links to websites that are behind a paywall from their search results. This aims to provide a more seamless and efficient search experience by avoiding frustrating encounters with subscription-required content.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Filter Paywalls**: Kagi now offers a setting to hide paywalled links.
- **Save Time**: Avoids clicking into content you can't access.
- **Cleaner Results**: Focuses on freely available information.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**New Paywall Setting**
A dedicated option within Kagi's settings allows users to toggle the visibility of paywalled content. This empowers users to curate their search experience based on their access preferences, ensuring they only see results they can freely read.

**Enhanced User Experience**
By proactively filtering out links that require payment, Kagi enhances the overall user experience. This reduces the time spent navigating to inaccessible pages and minimizes user frustration, leading to more productive search sessions.

> 💡 Insight: This feature directly addresses a common pain point for web users, demonstrating Kagi's commitment to user-centric design and efficient information retrieval.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Users can now discover information without the constant interruption of paywalls.
- Researchers and students can access a broader range of freely available academic and news content.
- Casual web surfers can enjoy a less cluttered and more direct path to information.

## ✨ Conclusion
Kagi continues to innovate by offering practical solutions to everyday online frustrations. This new paywall filtering feature is a significant step towards a more accessible and user-friendly internet search.
