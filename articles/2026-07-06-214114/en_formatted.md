CoMaps is an open-source project that enables users to create, edit, and share maps without relying on internet connectivity. This innovative approach has far-reaching implications for communities worldwide, particularly in areas with limited or no access to online resources.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
CoMaps allows users to contribute to map creation and editing, fostering a sense of community and collective ownership. This collaborative approach ensures that maps are accurate, up-to-date, and relevant to local needs.

**Element 2**
The platform's offline capabilities make it an ideal solution for areas with limited internet access, such as rural communities, disaster zones, or remote work sites. CoMaps empowers users to navigate and explore their surroundings without relying on external resources.
