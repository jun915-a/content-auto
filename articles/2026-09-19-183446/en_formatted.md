## 📈 Detailed Breakdown

The San Francisco Onion Futures Company leverages **agricultural commodity markets**, where onions are traded like soybeans or coffee. **Element 2**
Why onions? The company taps into a **$10+ billion global onion market**, where supply shocks (e.g., droughts in India) can spike prices overnight. Futures allow farmers and traders to **lock in profits or avoid losses**, creating a safety net in volatile markets. The company’s platform aggregates data from farms, auctions, and export markets to set fair prices.
