## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The Commodore 128's graphics chip, the VDC (Versatile Commodore Graphics), was a key component in managing multiple monitors. This chip was capable of handling complex graphics and was designed to be highly flexible. By utilizing the VDC, the Commodore 128 was able to divide its graphics output into multiple monitors, each with its own unique display.

**Element 2**
To accommodate the demands of multiple monitors, the Commodore 128's memory was expanded to a staggering 512 KB. This was a significant increase from earlier models and provided the necessary capacity for smooth operation with multiple monitors. The computer's memory was carefully managed to ensure that each monitor received the necessary resources to function properly.
