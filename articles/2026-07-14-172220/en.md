# S&P Downgrades Oracle

S&P downgrades Oracle to BBB, one notch above junk level, citing concerns over debt and cash flow

## The Core of This Topic
S&P downgraded Oracle to BBB due to high debt levels and declining cash flow. 
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Debt Increase**: High debt levels
- **Cash Flow**: Declining cash flow
- **Rating**: One notch above junk
## Detailed Breakdown
**Debt Concerns**
High debt levels are a major concern for Oracle, affecting its credit rating.
**Cash Flow Issues**
Declining cash flow is another factor contributing to the downgrade.
> Insight: Oracle's financial health is a concern for investors.
## Real-World Impact
- Increased borrowing costs
- Reduced investor confidence
- Potential impact on stock price
## Conclusion
The downgrade serves as a warning for Oracle to address its financial issues.
