# Google Breaks reCAPTCHA

De-googled Android users face issues with reCAPTCHA, highlighting Google's control over services

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Google's reCAPTCHA service is facing issues for de-googled Android users, affecting their ability to access various online services.
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: reCAPTCHA not working for de-googled Android users
- **Point 2**: Google's control over services is evident
- **Point 3**: Alternative solutions are being explored
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
De-googled Android users rely on alternative app stores and services, which can lead to compatibility issues with Google's services.
**Element 2**
Google's reCAPTCHA service is widely used across the web, making it a crucial component for online security and verification.
