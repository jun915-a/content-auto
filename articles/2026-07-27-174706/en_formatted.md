## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Email has come a long way since its inception. However, the underlying architecture remains largely unchanged. This article explores the idea of building modern email by borrowing and recombining existing components.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Break free from traditional email constraints
- **Point 2**: Leverage existing technologies for innovation
- **Point 3**: Create a more efficient and user-friendly email experience

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Email Infrastructure**
The traditional email infrastructure consists of a client-server model, where the client is the email client and the server is the email server. This model has been around for decades and is showing signs of aging.

**Web Technologies**
Modern web technologies such as web assembly, service workers, and PWA's can be used to build a more efficient and user-friendly email experience. By leveraging these technologies, email can be reimagined as a more dynamic and interactive experience.

> 💡 Insight: By borrowing and recombining existing components, modern email can be built from scratch, allowing for a more innovative and user-friendly experience.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Improved email productivity and efficiency
- Enhanced user experience through interactive features
- Increased adoption of modern web technologies

## ✨ Conclusion
The future of email is not about throwing out the old and starting from scratch, but about borrowing and recombining existing components to create something entirely new and innovative. By embracing this approach, we can create a more efficient, user-friendly, and dynamic email experience.
