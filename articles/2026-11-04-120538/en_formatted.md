## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1: Firmware Encryption**
The encryption of firmware is a security measure aimed at protecting the integrity and authenticity of the system. However, in the case of Nvidia RTX Spark, this encryption has led to a substantial slowdown in inference speed, particularly on Linux platforms.

**Element 2: Impact on Performance**
The inference speed on Linux systems with Nvidia RTX Spark has dropped to 35% of its potential. This slowdown can have a significant impact on the performance of AI applications and tasks, especially in time-sensitive scenarios or those requiring high computational power.
