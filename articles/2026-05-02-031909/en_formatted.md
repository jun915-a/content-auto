# USB-C Cable Inspector

*Insert header image here*

WhatCable helps you identify USB-C cables' capabilities, solving the mess of identical-looking cables with different charging speeds and features

## The Core of This Topic
WhatCable is a tiny menu bar app that reads the cable data your Mac provides to identify the capabilities of your USB-C cables.
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Identify Charging Speed**: Know if your cable can charge at 5W or 100W
- **Detect Thunderbolt 4**: Find out if your cable supports Thunderbolt 4
- **Simplify Cable Management**: Easily manage your cables based on their features
## Detailed Breakdown
**Cable Inspection**
WhatCable sits in your menu bar, providing easy access to cable information.
**Cable Management**
With WhatCable, you can organize your cables based on their capabilities, making it easier to find the right cable for the task.
> Insight: This can save you time and reduce frustration when working with multiple devices.
## Real-World Impact
- Reduce cable clutter
- Increase productivity by quickly finding the right cable
- Make informed purchasing decisions when buying new cables
## Conclusion
WhatCable is a simple yet powerful tool for anyone struggling with USB-C cable management, providing a convenient way to identify and organize your cables.
