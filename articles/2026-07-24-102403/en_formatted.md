## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Fields Medal, often called the Nobel Prize of mathematics, is awarded every four years to mathematicians under 40. The 2026 ceremony will once again celebrate groundbreaking contributions, inspiring future generations and advancing the frontiers of mathematical science globally.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Prestigious**: The highest international award in mathematics.
- **Eligibility**: Awarded to mathematicians under 40 years old.
- **Frequency**: Presented every four years at the International Congress of Mathematicians (ICM).

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
The unique age restriction for the Fields Medal emphasizes future potential and significant contributions made early in a mathematician's career. This criterion ensures the medal celebrates vibrant, active researchers who are likely to continue shaping the field for decades.

**Element 2**
The selection process is meticulously managed by the International Mathematical Union (IMU), involving a committee of distinguished mathematicians. Their rigorous review ensures that only truly exceptional and innovative work receives this unparalleled global recognition.

> 💡 Insight: The Fields Medal not only honors individual brilliance but also highlights emerging areas and breakthroughs in mathematics, guiding future research directions.
