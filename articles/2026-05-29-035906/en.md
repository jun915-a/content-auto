# Building Durable Workflows on Postgres

Unlock the full potential of Postgres for building robust and reliable workflows. Discover the secrets to creating systems that can withstand failures and scale with ease.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Postgres is a powerful database management system that offers a wide range of features and tools for building durable workflows. At its core, a durable workflow is one that can withstand failures and recover seamlessly. This means that even if a component of the system fails, the overall workflow remains intact and continues to function as expected.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
* **Point 1**: Postgres provides robust transactional support for ensuring data consistency and integrity.
* **Point 2**: Its advanced locking mechanisms prevent data corruption and ensure that concurrent updates are handled correctly.
* **Point 3**: Postgres also offers robust support for asynchronous processing and message queues.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: When building durable workflows on Postgres, it's essential to design your system with transactional support in mind. This means using transactions to group related operations together and ensuring that either all operations in the transaction succeed or none of them do. This approach helps maintain data consistency and integrity.

**Element 2**: Another critical aspect of building durable workflows on Postgres is understanding its locking mechanisms. By using the correct locking strategies, you can prevent data corruption and ensure that concurrent updates are handled correctly. This is particularly important when dealing with multiple users accessing and updating the same data.

> 💡 Insight: The key to building durable workflows on Postgres is to design your system with robust transactional support and advanced locking mechanisms.
