# AI Outperforms Doctors

AI diagnoses 67% of ER patients correctly, outdoing doctors' 50-55% rate in a Harvard trial

OpenAI's o1 AI has shown impressive results in a Harvard trial, correctly diagnosing 67% of ER patients.
