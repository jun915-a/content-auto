# Cutting the Tracker That Follows You Even Under VPN

Learn how to evade online trackers and maintain your anonymity even when using a VPN.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Online trackers and VPNs are often seen as opposing forces, but what if you could cut the tracker that follows you even under a VPN? This is exactly what we will explore today, as we delve into the world of digital anonymity and the tactics used to maintain it.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: Trackers operate by collecting data about your online activities, which is then used to create a profile of your behavior. This information is often shared with third parties, compromising your anonymity. VPNs can mask your IP address, but they do not prevent trackers from collecting data about your activities.

**Element 2**: To cut trackers, you need to use a combination of tools and techniques. This includes using a VPN that is specifically designed to block trackers, as well as implementing browser extensions that prevent tracking. You also need to be mindful of the websites you visit and the data you share online.

> 💡 Insight: Maintaining your anonymity online requires a holistic approach that involves understanding how trackers operate, using the right tools and techniques, and being mindful of your online behavior.
