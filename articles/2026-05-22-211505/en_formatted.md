## The Core of This Topic
The unique business culture in Japan allows companies to diversify and innovate. 
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Diversification**: Japanese companies often expand into unrelated markets
- **Innovation**: They focus on cutting-edge tech and products
- **Long-term strategy**: Prioritizing growth over short-term gains
## Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: Large corporations, known as keiretsu, dominate the Japanese economy, driving innovation and growth.
**Element 2**: Smaller companies, or SMEs, also play a crucial role in the diversification of Japanese businesses.
> Insight: The combination of keiretsu and SMEs fosters a dynamic business environment.
## Real-World Impact
- Increased competition drives innovation
- Diversified portfolios reduce financial risk
- Global market expansion boosts economic growth
## Conclusion
Japanese companies' diverse business models are a key factor in their success, offering valuable lessons for businesses worldwide.
