Kimi Linear introduces a novel attention mechanism that achieves linear time complexity, a significant leap from the quadratic complexity of traditional attention. - **Linear Complexity**: Achieves O(N) attention computation, unlike O(N^2).
- **Enhanced Expressiveness**: Maintains or improves model expressiveness despite efficiency gains.
- **Scalability**: Enables processing of much longer sequences efficiently.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Efficient Attention Mechanism**
The core innovation lies in a new formulation of the attention calculation, moving away from dense matrix multiplications. This allows for processing sequences of arbitrary length with computational cost that scales linearly with the sequence length.

**Preserved Expressiveness**
Despite the architectural shift for efficiency, Kimi Linear carefully designs its components to retain the rich representational power of standard attention mechanisms. This ensures that performance on downstream tasks is not compromised.
