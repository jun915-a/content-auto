Learn how to control data collection from your Rivian vehicle and understand the implications of doing so, including impacts on features and services.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Rivian vehicles collect various data to enhance performance, safety, and user experience. However, you may have concerns about data privacy.
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Data collection can be managed through settings
- **Point 2**: Certain features may be affected by disabling data collection
- **Point 3**: Rivian prioritizes data security and user privacy
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: Disabling data collection may limit the functionality of certain features that rely on data analysis, such as navigation and vehicle health monitoring.
**Element 2**: Users can access and manage their data collection preferences through the vehicle's settings or the Rivian app, allowing for a degree of control over what data is shared.
> 💡 Insight: Understanding how data collection affects your vehicle's capabilities is crucial for making informed decisions about your privacy settings.
## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Impacts on vehicle performance and maintenance
- Effects on personalized user experiences and recommendations
- Considerations for data security and privacy laws
## ✨ Conclusion
Controlling data collection from your vehicle is a balance between privacy and functionality. Rivian aims to provide users with options to manage their data while ensuring the vehicle operates safely and efficiently.
