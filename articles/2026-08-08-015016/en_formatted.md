- **Operator Fusion**: Combines operations for efficiency

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Batching**
Batching involves grouping similar operations together to reduce overhead and improve efficiency, leading to faster query execution times.

**Operator Fusion**
Operator fusion combines multiple operations into a single step, minimizing the need for intermediate results and thereby enhancing overall performance.
