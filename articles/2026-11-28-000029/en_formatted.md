## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Bouncemarket is a live prediction market game where users bet on the exact landing spot of a simulated falling ball. The game streams ball physics server-side, allowing real-time trading on its descent.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Predictive Betting**: Wager on a ball's landing zone.
- **Live Simulation**: Server-side physics for real-time action.
- **Dynamic Market**: Trade predictions as the ball falls.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Server-Side Simulation**
The entire physics simulation of the ball's drop occurs on the server. This ensures consistency and fairness, as every player experiences the same simulated reality, unaffected by client-side variations.

**Real-Time Streaming**
Physics updates are streamed to clients using server-sent events. This technology provides a low-latency, efficient way to push live data, making the game feel responsive and immediate as the ball progresses.

> 💡 Insight: Server-side simulation and SSE are crucial for maintaining a fair, real-time prediction market experience.

**Prediction Market Mechanics**
Players can buy and sell predictions for different landing zones. As the ball falls and probabilities shift, the market price for each zone fluctuates, creating a dynamic trading environment.

**User Interface**
The interface visualizes the ball's trajectory and displays live market data. It's designed for clarity, allowing players to quickly assess risks and make informed trading decisions during the drop.

> 💡 Insight: The combination of visual feedback and market data empowers strategic decision-making.
