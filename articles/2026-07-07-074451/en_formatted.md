Small AI models have been gaining traction in various applications, particularly in areas with unreliable networks. **Element 2**: The use of small AI models in areas with unreliable networks has several benefits. They can provide critical services such as health monitoring, financial transactions, and emergency response systems, even in areas with limited connectivity.
