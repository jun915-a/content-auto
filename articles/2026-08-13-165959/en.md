**The ES-30 Aircraft**
The ES-30 is a 30-seat regional electric aircraft designed for short-haul flights, promising zero operational emissions and reduced noise pollution. Its development is a testament to advancements in battery technology and electric powertrain integration.

**Flight Test Success**
The successful first flight validates the aircraft's aerodynamic design, control systems, and electric propulsion. It represents months of rigorous testing and engineering, bringing the vision of sustainable regional air travel closer to reality.
