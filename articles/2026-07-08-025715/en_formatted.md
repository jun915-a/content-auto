StreetComplete is an innovative app designed to improve the accuracy and completeness of OpenStreetMap. It does so by presenting users with small, manageable tasks or 'quests' that can be completed on the go.

## Detailed Breakdown
**How it Works**: The app identifies areas of OpenStreetMap that require improvement and presents users with specific tasks to complete, such as adding street names or verifying building locations.
**Benefits for Users**: By contributing to OpenStreetMap, users can enhance their navigation experience and support a wide range of applications that rely on accurate map data.
