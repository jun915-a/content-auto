# Japan Recovers 90% Lithium

Japan develops innovative method to recover up to 90% of lithium from used EV batteries, reducing waste and increasing sustainability

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Japan has made a groundbreaking discovery, creating a method to recover a significant amount of lithium from used electric vehicle batteries. This breakthrough has the potential to greatly impact the environment and the EV industry.
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **High Recovery Rate**: Up to 90% of lithium can be recovered
- **Sustainable Solution**: Reduces electronic waste and minimizes the need for new lithium mining
- **Cost-Effective**: Could lower the cost of producing new EV batteries
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Lithium Extraction Process**
The new method involves a complex process of dismantling and treating the batteries to extract the valuable lithium. This process is still being refined for mass production.
**Environmental Benefits**
The recovery of lithium from used batteries reduces the demand for newly mined lithium, thereby decreasing the environmental impact of mining operations.
> 💡 Insight: This innovation could significantly reduce electronic waste and support a more circular economy.
## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Reduced electronic waste
- Increased supply of recycled lithium
- Potential to make EVs more affordable
## ✨ Conclusion
This breakthrough in lithium recovery has the potential to transform the EV industry and contribute to a more sustainable future.
