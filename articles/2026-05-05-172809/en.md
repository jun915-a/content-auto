# AI: The Scapegoat

Discover how human error, not AI, is often the culprit behind database deletions and learn to protect your data

## The Core of This Topic
The core issue lies in misunderstanding AI's role in data management. 
## 5-Second Key Points
- **Human Error**: Mistakes made by users
- **Lack of Oversight**: Inadequate monitoring of AI systems
- **Insufficient Training**: Poorly trained AI models
## Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: Human error can lead to devastating consequences, such as data loss.
**Element 2**: Inadequate oversight of AI systems can exacerbate the problem.
> Insight: Proper training and oversight are key to preventing data disasters.
## Real-World Impact
- Financial losses
- Reputation damage
- Legal consequences
## Conclusion
Take responsibility for your data's safety and security.
