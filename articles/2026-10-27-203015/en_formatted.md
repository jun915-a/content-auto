## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The FISA Court's Authority**
The Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court (FISC) operates largely in secret, issuing warrants for surveillance under FISA. This specific ruling, however, is so sensitive that even its existence is being shielded from public view, creating a significant information gap.

**Secrecy and National Security**
While the government cites national security concerns for the secrecy, critics argue this prevents proper oversight and public debate. The ruling could define the boundaries of lawful surveillance, impacting millions of Americans without their knowledge.
