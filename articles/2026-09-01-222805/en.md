# Google Play Blocks AnkiDroid's Donation Link, Sparks Outcry

AnkiDroid users are facing issues donating to the popular flashcard app as Google Play has removed its Open Collective donation link, citing policy violations. This move impacts vital funding for the open-source project.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Google Play has disallowed AnkiDroid from including an Open Collective donation link within its app description. This decision, reportedly due to policy violations, directly impacts the project's ability to receive community support and funding.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Donation Blocked**: Google Play removed AnkiDroid's Open Collective donation link.
- **Policy Dispute**: The removal is linked to alleged policy violations by Google.
- **Funding Impact**: This hinders crucial community financial support for the open-source app.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Google Play's Stance**
Google Play has flagged the Open Collective link as non-compliant with their policies. While the exact nature of the violation is not fully detailed, it appears to relate to how third-party donation platforms are presented or integrated.

**AnkiDroid's Response**
AnkiDroid developers are frustrated, noting that the link was a primary method for users to contribute financially to the app's development and maintenance. They are seeking clarification from Google and exploring alternative solutions.

> 💡 Insight: This situation highlights the challenges open-source projects face when relying on app stores for distribution and community engagement.

**Open Collective**
Open Collective is a widely used platform that helps open-source projects manage their finances transparently, allowing users to contribute recurring donations or one-time gifts.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Reduced financial contributions to support AnkiDroid's development and server costs.
- Potential decrease in volunteer developer motivation due to funding uncertainty.
- Users who wish to donate may be deterred or unable to find alternative, convenient methods.

## ✨ Conclusion
This unfortunate situation underscores the importance of direct community support for open-source software. AnkiDroid is actively working to resolve this with Google and find alternative ways for users to contribute, ensuring the app's continued success.
