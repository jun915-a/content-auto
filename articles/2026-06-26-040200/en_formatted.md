## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Herculaneum scrolls are ancient Roman texts that were carbonized during the eruption of Mount Vesuvius in 79 AD. Recently, a team of researchers successfully deciphered an entire scroll, providing a unique glimpse into the past.
