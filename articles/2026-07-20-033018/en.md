**Goblin Movement Patterns**
Goblins in the C64 Basic dungeon crawler move randomly, but they tend to follow corridors and avoid open spaces. Knowing this can help you predict their movements and plan your strategy accordingly.

**Terrain Awareness**
The terrain can be a powerful ally in a goblin attack. Use walls, pillars, and other obstacles to block goblin movements and funnel them into kill zones. This can give you a significant advantage in combat.
> 💡 Insight: Knowing the goblin's movement patterns and using terrain to your advantage can greatly improve your chances of survival.

**Combat Tactics**
When engaging goblins in combat, use your surroundings to your advantage. Lure goblins into kill zones or use ranged attacks to pick them off from a safe distance. Also, be mindful of your own movement and avoid getting cornered or trapped.
