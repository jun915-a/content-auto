## The Core of This Topic
OpenWrt One is an open hardware router designed to provide users with a highly customizable and secure networking experience. It is based on the OpenWrt operating system, which offers a wide range of features and extensions.

- **Highly Customizable**: Users can modify the router's firmware and settings to suit their needs
- **Secure**: OpenWrt One offers advanced security features to protect users' networks
- **Open Source**: The router's hardware and software are open source, promoting community involvement and development
## Detailed Breakdown
**Hardware Features**
OpenWrt One features a powerful processor, ample storage, and a variety of connectivity options, making it suitable for a wide range of applications.
**Software Features**
The router runs on the OpenWrt operating system, which provides a user-friendly interface and a wide range of features, including network management, security, and quality of service control.
