A DIY robotics research setup that defies convention, built for under 10% the cost of its predecessor.

Building a cutting-edge robotics research setup that fits next to your desk, costing a fraction of the price of its predecessors, is a groundbreaking achievement. As someone who's experienced the benefits and challenges of robotic manipulation research firsthand, I've created a setup that's not only affordable but also efficient and effective.

* **Point 2**: Utilizes off-the-shelf components to significantly reduce costs.
* **Point 3**: Customizable to suit various research needs and applications.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
This setup is designed with modularity in mind, allowing researchers to easily add or remove components as needed. Each module is crafted to perform a specific function, making it simple to swap out or upgrade individual components rather than the entire system.

**Element 2**
By leveraging off-the-shelf components, we've managed to keep costs significantly lower than traditional setups. This not only makes the setup more accessible to researchers but also reduces the environmental impact of production and disposal.
