# Visionary Artist Charles Ross Completes Star Axis

Charles Ross has spent 50 years building the Star Axis, a massive naked-eye observatory in New Mexico. The project embodies Ross's artistic vision and commitment to astronomical exploration.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The Star Axis is a monumental land art installation created by Charles Ross, which aims to recreate the celestial patterns of the stars in the night sky. Spanning over 70 feet in diameter, this massive naked-eye observatory is designed to provide an immersive experience for viewers, allowing them to contemplate the vastness of the universe.
