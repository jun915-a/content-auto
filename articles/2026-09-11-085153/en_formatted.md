## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Science Behind It**
The device works by generating low-frequency sound waves. These waves create pressure variations in the air that effectively push the oxygen away from the fire source, starving it of the fuel it needs to burn.

**Portability and Design**
Unlike bulky traditional extinguishers, this acoustic version is designed to be compact and easily portable. The student has focused on making it user-friendly and effective for small to medium-sized fires.
