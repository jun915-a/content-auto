**Unsupervised Learning at Scale**
These AIs learn from vast datasets without explicit human guidance, discovering patterns and relationships that humans might miss. This allows for emergent intelligence that isn't constrained by our preconceived notions.

**Exploration of Latent Spaces**
By navigating complex 'latent spaces,' these systems can generate novel solutions and creative outputs. This exploration is key to their ability to discover unexpected insights and strategies.
