Google discloses a staggering $94.1 billion investment in SpaceX, marking a 6% stake in the private aerospace company.

Google's investment in SpaceX is a significant move, demonstrating the tech giant's interest in space exploration and commercialization.

- **Point 2**: This investment highlights Google's growing focus on space technology.
- **Point 3**: The move positions Google as a major player in the private aerospace industry.
