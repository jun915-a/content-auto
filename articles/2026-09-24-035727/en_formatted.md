Explore the Z80 REPL, a browser-based emulator that brings the iconic 8-bit Zilog Z80 CPU to life. Dive into assembly, debug, and experiment with retro computing—all in your web browser.

**The Z80 REPL (Read-Eval-Print Loop) is an innovative web-based tool that emulates the legendary Z80 microprocessor, allowing users to interactively code, debug, and experiment with assembly language in real time. Originally released in 2018 by Abagames, this tool bridges the gap between modern browsers and retro computing, offering a hands-on way to explore the foundational architecture of 8-bit systems.**
