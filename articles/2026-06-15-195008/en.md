# ePub Disputes

Kobo and Adobe disagree on ePub validity, causing issues for users

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
The core issue lies in the differing opinions between Kobo and Adobe on what constitutes a valid ePub file. 
## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Kobo has its own set of validation rules
- **Point 2**: Adobe's rules may not align with Kobo's
- **Point 3**: Users are caught in the middle
## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**
Kobo's validation rules are designed to ensure that ePubs work seamlessly on their devices. 
**Element 2**
Adobe's rules, on the other hand, focus on the technical aspects of the ePub format.
> 💡 Insight: The key to resolving these issues lies in understanding the differences between these two sets of rules.
## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Incompatibility issues arise when ePubs are not validated correctly
- Users may experience formatting problems or errors
- The dispute affects the overall user experience
## ✨ Conclusion
The disagreement between Kobo and Adobe highlights the need for a standardized approach to ePub validation
