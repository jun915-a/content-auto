## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**The Porting Journey**
Converting *Lemmings* to PalmOS required overcoming significant technical barriers. The original game relied on high-resolution sprites and vibrant colors, while PalmOS devices of the era had **160x160-pixel screens** with a monochrome or limited-color palette. Developers had to **simplify graphics** while preserving gameplay fluidity—balancing visual fidelity with performance constraints. The project also involved rewriting input handling, as PalmOS lacked traditional controllers, relying instead on stylus taps and menu navigation.

**Open-Source Collaboration**
This project thrives on **GitHub**, where contributors from around the world pitch in with code, bug fixes, and optimizations. Unlike commercial ports, there’s no corporate oversight—just passionate developers experimenting with **assembly, C, and PalmOS SDK tools**. The lack of official support from either Dendy (the game’s original publisher) or Palm, Inc., makes this effort all the more remarkable. It’s a rare example of **pure community-driven retro gaming**, where fans take the reins.

> 💡 Insight: The project highlights how **open-source retro gaming** can revive classics without corporate involvement, proving that passion often trumps resources.
