# Optimizely Breach: Malware Delivered Via Top Websites

*Insert header image here*

Major brands like Zoom and GitLab were affected when Optimizely, a popular web optimization platform, was compromised, serving malware to unsuspecting users.

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
A severe security incident occurred when Optimizely, a widely used A/B testing and personalization platform, was compromised. Attackers injected malicious JavaScript into Optimizely's service, which was then served to users visiting websites that utilize Optimizely, including prominent platforms like Zoom and GitLab.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Compromised Platform**: Optimizely, a trusted service, was the vector.
- **Malware Delivery**: Malicious scripts were injected and served.
- **High-Profile Victims**: Websites like Zoom and GitLab were affected.

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Optimizely's Role**
Optimizely is integrated into thousands of websites to test and personalize user experiences. Its widespread adoption meant a compromise had significant reach.

**Malicious Script Injection**
Attackers managed to insert malicious code into Optimizely's infrastructure. This code was designed to exploit vulnerabilities or deliver further malware payloads to visitors of Optimizely-enabled sites.

> 💡 Insight: The trust placed in third-party services like Optimizely makes them attractive targets for attackers seeking broad impact.

**Targeted Websites**
Websites leveraging Optimizely for their operations, such as communication giant Zoom and software development platform GitLab, were unknowingly distributing the malware to their user base.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Users visiting compromised sites were at risk of infection.
- Reputational damage to Optimizely and affected brands.
- Potential for data theft or further system compromise.

## ✨ Conclusion
This incident highlights the critical need for robust security measures and supply chain vigilance, even with trusted third-party providers.
