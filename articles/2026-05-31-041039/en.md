# Microsoft Limits Offline Products

Microsoft degrades functionality of perpetually-licensed offline products, affecting user experience and productivity

## 🔑 The Core of This Topic
Microsoft has started to limit the functionality of its offline products, including Microsoft Office 2019 and 2021 for Mac, by converting them to view-only mode.

## ⚡ 5-Second Key Points
- **Point 1**: Limited editing capabilities
- **Point 2**: No new feature updates
- **Point 3**: Affected products include Office 2019 and 2021 for Mac

## 📈 Detailed Breakdown
**Element 1**: The change affects users who purchased perpetual licenses, expecting full functionality without subscription-based models.

**Element 2**: Microsoft's decision may push users towards subscription-based services like Office 365, which offer more features and updates.
> 💡 Insight: This shift highlights the company's focus on cloud-based services and recurring revenue streams.

## 🎯 Real-World Impact
- Users may need to upgrade to newer versions or subscription services
- Small businesses and individuals may be disproportionately affected
- The change may lead to increased costs for users who rely on offline products

## ✨ Conclusion
Microsoft's decision to limit offline products may have significant implications for users, emphasizing the importance of understanding licensing terms and the potential benefits of cloud-based services.
